So I wanted to talk a little bit about concept to console, or vibe-shipping games; I'm Peter Danenberg.

Game development doesn't have to start with a doc anymore; you can start with a vibe. In other words, as soon as you start ideating something, boom, go to Veo 3 and start playing around. At this stage, don't worry about things like continuity, etc.; just see if it has the right vibe.

And, as soon as you see something you like, a character maybe; grab a still and head over to Nano-Banana. The beautiful thing about Nano-Banana is that, given some still, it will happily generate things like orthographic projections at ninety degrees.

And the reason why this is interisting is that, given orthographic projections at ninety degrees, tools like Meshy can create a more or less faithful 3D projection; fully articulated; ready for animation.

This is great! What about the world, though? One of the most exciting new developments are world models like Genie 3, Odyssey; which, given a description, can generate a fully playable world.

What's a playable world without a soundtrack? That's where things like Google's Lyria come in: again, can vibe-describe a soundtrack; even on the fly during a game.

What's missing, though? Orchestration is missing. Let's say you're grappling with 7+ AI generated assets (haven't even gone into things like Gemini Live for NPCs, Gemini for code, there are also things like in-game LLM companion to help you solve puzzles, etc.).

This is a source of friction and complexity; but it's also a huge opportunity. Some of the big engines like Unity and Roblox have been working on pieces of this; but my gut says that we have six to twelve months to put something together.

Rare opportunity for an enterprising startup to get a crack at this billion dollar opportunity while all the pieces are in place; just needs someone to come put them together.
