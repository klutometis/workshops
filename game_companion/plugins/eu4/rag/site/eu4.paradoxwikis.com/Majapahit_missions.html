      National missions[edit | edit source]

      The Fall of Majapahit[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Available only with the Leviathan DLC enabled.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Demand Loyalty
      Majapahit once ruled a vast network of vassal and tributary states. We must ensure the loyalty of those kingdoms that remin under our grip and attempt to restore some part of our old empire.
      		 Majapahit has at least 5 subjects with less than 50% Liberty desire The event "Disloyalty to Majapahit" will no longer occur

      Majapahit gets “Loyalty to Majapahit” for 20 years, giving:

      • −10% Liberty desire in subjects
      Restore Order
      Faith in the monarchy is waning as the succession is constantly challenged. We must restore order to the realm and assure the people that the danger is over.
      		 Majapahit:
      • Has at least +2 Stability
      • Has at least 90 Legitimacy, if is a monarchy
      • Has no ongoing rebel army
      		 The events "The Rise of Demak", "The Rise of Mataram" and "Spread of Islam in [maj_sunni_province.GetName]" will no longer occur

      Majapahit gets “Legitimacy Restored” for 20 years, giving:

      • +1 Yearly legitimacy
      Protect the Faith
      Majapahit's traditional Hindu-Buddhist faith is weakening as merchants and conquerors of the Islamic faith become more prevalent in Nusantara. We must ensure that the people have a strong faith to follow.
      		 Majapahit:
      • Has at least 80% Religious unity
      • All owned provinces unrest is less than 2 local unrest
      • Has had the disaster "The Fall of Majapahit" for at least 10 years
      		 The event "[Root.maj_pretender.GetName] Challenges the Throne" will no longer occur

      Majapahit gets “Religious Triumph” for 20 years, giving:

      • −33% Missionary maintenance cost
      Prevent Collapse
      Our dramatic decline must be dramatically reversed. Once we have secured the throne, our faith, and the loyalty of our subjects we can emerge from the darkness.
      		 Majapahit owns the following provinces:
      • All provinces in the Central Java area
      • All provinces in the Surabaya area
      • Kediri (2691)
      • Malang (4794)
      		 The disaster "The Fall of Majapahit" ends.

      Majapahit:

      • Gains 25 prestige
      • Gets “Majapahit Revival” until the end of the game, giving:
        • +1 Diplomatic relation

      Restore Order
      Demand Loyalty
      Protect the Faith

      Enforce Majapahit[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Available only with the Leviathan DLC enabled.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Negara Agung
      To expand the borders of our empire and bring new peoples under our rule is a great endeavor. But we must also look to the good of our core territories, the negara agung. Our capital must be a great city, a hub of commerce and culture from all over Nusantara.
      		 Majapahit:
      • Has a capital of at least 50 Development
      • Either:
        • Has improved the capital at least 5 times
        • Has concentrated development at least 2 times
      		 Majapahit gets “Negara Agung” until the end of the game, giving:
      • −5% Development cost
      		 Prevent Collapse
      Chakravarti
      An ideal monarch rules an ideal realm, the material and spiritual center of the world. Majapahit shall become this realm.
      		 Majapahit:
      • Has at least 500 total development
      • Has at least 75 Absolutism, if is not revolutionary
      • Is not a subject nation

      Any great project in Kotagede (2690) is at least  magnificent

      		Majapahit:
      • Allows "Chakravarti" government reform
      • Becomes an Empire
      • Gains 10 prestige
      • Gets the event “Majapahit Absolutism”, if is not revolutionary
      		 Negara Agung
      Candi Shrines
      Scattered throughout Nusantara are a number of ruined or abandoned Candi temples of mixed Hindu and Buddhist worship. We must restore these temples to renew our faith.
      		 Majapahit:
      • Owns at least 15 provinces with a Cathedral if unlocked, else Temple
      • Has at least 65 clergy loyalty, if Clergy estate exists
      • Has any ally which:
        • Has at least 100 total development
        • Follows a Buddhist religion ( Theravada, Mahayana or Vajrayana)
      		 If Majapahit is Hindu and DLC 'Wealth of Nations' is active:
      • Unlocks the "Buddha" personal deity

      Else:

      • Majapahit gets “Religious Peace” until the end of the game, giving:
        • +10% Religious unity
      		 Prevent Collapse
      The Porch of Mecca
      The Aceh region has become the staging point for the spread of Islam in Nusantara. We must stamp out this new faith and restore Aceh to Majapahit.
      		 All provinces in the Aceh area:
      • Are owned by Majapahit or its non-tributary subjects
      • Follow Majapahit's religion

      If Majapahit is not Muslim:

      • No country with a capital in the Malaya[2] or Indonesia regions are Muslim

      Else,

      • No country with a capital in the Malaya[2] or Indonesia regions are not Muslim

      Note: The Maldives (just southwest of India) are considered part of the Malaya region in-game and begin the game under Muslim control

      		If Majapahit is Hindu and DLC 'Cradle of Civilization' is active:
      • Unlocks the "Propagate Religion" trade policy
      • Owned Hindu provinces become immune to the "Propagate Religion" trade policy

      Else, Majapahit:

      • Gains 20 prestige
      		 Candi Shrines
      Enforce Loyalty
      It is no longer enough that our subjects are just barely kept from defying us. It is time to enforce true loyalty.
      		 Majapahit has at least 6 subjects with less than 20% Liberty desire Majapahit:
      • Enables the Majapahit Campaigns casus belli against countries with 'Malay' primary culture
      • Vassalizes every Tributary subjects which also gets “Enforced Loyalty” for 15 years, giving:
        • −100% Liberty desire
      		 Prevent Collapse

      Expand Majapahit[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Available only with the Leviathan DLC enabled.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Rein in Malacca
      Our rivals of old, Malacca, rose to prominence through the dreaded treasure fleet of Zheng He that also ended our hegemony in Nusantara. We must crush our rivals and return the peninsula to the fold.
      		 Majapahit:
      • Is the strongest trade power in Malacca trade node
      • Has Malacca as a subject, or Malacca does not exist

      Majapahit or its non-tributary subjects own at least 12 provinces in the following areas:

      • Malacca
      • North Malaya
      • Malaya
      • Central Tenasserim
      • Johor
      		 Majapahit:
      • Gains 20 navy tradition
      		 Enforce Loyalty
      Rein in Brunei
      Brunei once loyally submitted to Majapahit before they defied us and forged their own empire. We must bring them to their knees once more.
      		 Majapahit:
      • Has Brunei as a subject, or Brunei does not exist

      Majapahit or its non-tributary subjects own at least 15 provinces in the following areas:

      • Kutai
      • Brunei (all provinces must be owned)
      • Kalimantan
      • Banjar
      • Sabah
      		 Majapahit:
      • Gains 20 navy tradition
      		 Enforce Loyalty
      Gajah Mada's Oath
      The great statesman Gajah Mada made an oath that all of Nusantara shall be conquered by Majapahit. We remember this oath, and we will fulfill it.
      		 The country:
      • Is Malaya
      		 Malaya:
      • Enables the Majapahit Campaigns casus belli against countries in the Chinese technology group
      		 Rein in Brunei

      Rein in Malacca

      Subjugate the Mainland
      Even Gajah Mada never dreamed that Majapahit might one day dominate the Thai and Khmer realms. We will expand the empire to heights never before imagined.
      		 Malaya or its non-tributary subjects own at least 50 provinces in the following regions:
      • Indochina
      • Burma
      		 Malaya:
      • Enables the Majapahit Campaigns casus belli against all countries
      		 Gajah Mada's Oath

      Economic expansion[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Available only with the Leviathan DLC enabled.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Eastern Colonies
      Majapahit once collected tribute from the numerous petty kingdoms of Nusa Tenggara. It is time that we ruled over them more direcetly. We should assemble a fleet and the capacity to colonize these islands to prepare for our campaign in the east.
      		 Majapahit:
      • Is the strongest trade power in The Moluccas trade node
      • Has at least 1 colonist
      • Has at least 10 light ships
      		 Every potential or currently owned colonial provinces in the following areas gets “Potential Majapahit Colony” ( +50 Local settler increase) until the end of the game:
      • Lesser Sunda Islands area
      • Timor area
      • Belitung (2679)
      		 Enforce Loyalty
      Subjugate Makassar
      Our rule on Sulawesi has collapsed entirely, and we must rectify that. The Makassar Kingdom in particular has grown too powerful for our liking.
      		 Majapahit:
      • Has Makassar as a subject, or Makassar does not exist

      Majapahit or its non-tributary subjects own the following area:

      • Makassar
      		 If Majapahit is not Animist
      • Every Animist subject with its capital in Makassar area:
        • Adopts Majapahit's religion

      Every potential or currently owned colonial provinces in the following areas gets “Potential Majapahit Colony” ( +50 Local settler increase) until the end of the game:

      • South Sulawesi area
      • North Sulawesi area
      		 The Eastern Colonies
      Dominate the Moluccas
      The trade in cloves is among the most profitable in the world. Majapahit once controlled this trade when our hegemony was absolute, and we must once again achieve this dominance.
      		 Majapahit:
      • Controls at least 85% of the trade in The Moluccas trade node
      • Is the leading producer of Cloves
      		 Majapahit gets “Majapahit Trade Empire” until the end of the game, giving:
      • +5% Trade efficiency
      • +25% Colonial range

      Every potential or currently owned colonial provinces in the following areas gets “Potential Majapahit Colony” ( +50 Local settler increase) until the end of the game:

      • Spice Islands area
      • Moluccas area
      		 Subjugate Makassar

      Cultural expansion[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Available only with the Leviathan DLC enabled.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Relations with Sunda
      Majapahit once enjoyed a strong relationship with the neighboring Sunda Kingdom, but it was sullied by Gajah Mada at the Battle of Bubat. We should either seek to repair this relationship or end the strife by bringing Sunda under our rule.
      		 At least one of:
      • Sunda is allied to Majapahit with at least +100 opinion of us
      • Majapahit or its non-tributary subjects own the following areas:
        • Banten
        • West Java
      		 Majapahit gains 20 prestige

      Sunda:

      • If is our ally:
        • Majapahit and Sunda become mutual historical friend
      • If is our subject:
        • Changes culture to 'Javanese'
        • Gain Sundanese as an accepted culture. If Sundanese is already an accepted culture, or if there are not enough slots, gain 100 diplomatic power instead.

      The following province change culture to 'Javanese':

      • Banten (624)
      • Cirebon (4791)
      • Sumedang (2688)
      • Kalapa (630)
      Restore the Fleet
      The fleets of Majapahit were once a glorious sight to behold, our mighty djong ships dominating the horizon. The fleet must be restored.
      		 Majapahit:
      • Has at least 60 Navy tradition
      • Has a navy of at least 80% of its force limit
      • Has at least 10 heavy ships
      • Has at least 20 light ships
      		 Majapahit gets “Disrupting Chinese Trade” for 20 years, giving:
      • +33% Privateer efficiency
      Disrupt Chinese Trade
      When Zheng He and his treasure fleet entered Nusantara, he disrupted the balance of power and commerce in our empire. We should repay the favor by sending our new fleet to disrupt trade in China.
      		 Majapahit has at least 20% trade power from privateering in Hangzhou and Canton trade nodes Majapahit gets “The Great Blockade” until the end of the game, giving:
      • +10% Morale of navies
      • +1 Blockade impact on siege
      • +25% Blockade efficiency
      		 Restore the Fleet
      The Great Blockade
      We have conconcted our ultimate revenge against the Ming dynasty: a great blockade that will completely cut off their ports from all maritime commerce. This will take every bit of our naval prowess to achieve, but we are confident that we have the numbers and skill to carry out the Great Blockade.
      		 If Majapahit is not the Emperor of China and the Emperor of China has at least 1 port:
      • The Emperor of China is 100% blockaded

      Else, if Ming is not a subject nation and has at least 5 ports:

      • Ming is 100% blockaded

      Else, if any country with 'Chinese' culture is not a subject nation and has at least 10 ports:

      • This country is 100% blockaded

      Else:

      • Majapahit or its non-tributary subjects own Province Canton (667)
      		 Majapahit gains a permanent claim on all coastal provinces in the China Subcontinent Disrupt Chinese Trade

      Regional missions[edit | edit source]

      Exploit local resources[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.32.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Palopo Iron
      Sulawesi is named after its rich iron deposits. It would be foolish for us not to exploit these resources by building a weapons manufactory.
      		 Province Palopo:
      • Is owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects
      • Has Weapons Manufactory building
      		 Province Palopo gains permanent province modifier “Palopo Iron” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +2 Local goods produced
      Banjar Textiles
      Banjar's textile production is booming. We should encourage this industry by constructing a textile manufactory.
      		 Province Banjar:
      • Is owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects
      • Has Textile Manufactory building
      		 Province Banjar gains permanent province modifier “Banjar Textiles” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +25% Local production efficiency
      Lampung Peppers
      The southern region of Sumatra is famous for producing a huge portion of the world's peppers. We should ensure maximum yields by establishing factories in Lampung.
      		 Province Tulangbewang:
      • Is owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects
      • Has Trade Station building
      		 Province Tulangbewang gains permanent province modifier “Lampung Pepper” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +2 Local goods produced
      Moluccan Cloves
      Cloves are among the most highly prized goods in the world. If we could gain a monopoly on cloves, we could be rich beyond our dreams.
      		 Provinces Ternate and Tidore:
      • Are owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects
      • Have Trade Station building
      		 Provinces Ternate and Tidore gain permanent province modifier “Clove Boom” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +20% Local production efficiency
      Sea Cucumbers
      Traders from Sulawesi tell of an island to the south where people harvest sea cucumbers. We should discover this island and either trade for this valuable commodity or else take over its production by force.
      		 Province Tiwi:
      • At least one of:
        • Province is owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects
        • Province owner is allied to our country
      • If produces Gold
        • Province Development is at least 15
      • Else:
        • At least one of:
          • Has Naval Equipment Manufactory building
          • Has Trade Depot building
          • Has any manufactory
      		 Province Tiwi gains permanent province modifier “Sea Cucumbers” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +3 Local goods produced
