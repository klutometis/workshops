      National missions[edit | edit source]

      Control the Black Sea[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Emperor's Legion
      To reverse the downfall of our nation, we must first see to the restoration of our military. We must establish a fighting force capable of not only protecting our state from its foes but also of overcoming our enemies on the battlefield and expanding the influence of the Komnenid realm.
      		 Trebizond:
      • Has at least 1 general
      • At least one of:
        • Has an army of at least 100% of its force limit
        • Has at least 100% of its manpower reserves
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains “Martial Resurgence” for 25 years, giving:
        • −10% Land maintenance modifier
      • Gains a permanent claim on all neighbouring provinces
      The Goths and the Romans
      The Princes of Gothia share a long history with the Komnenoi. Having once been a Perateia of Trebizond, our peoples have a lot in common. Our shared struggle should make the unification of our nations a mutual interest of both parties.
      		 Trebizond:
      • At least one of:
        • Owns province Theodoro (2410)
        • Has Theodoro as a vassal
        • Theodoro
          • Is our ally
          • Has a royal marriage with us
          • Is not a subject nation
          • Has at least 70 trust with us
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains a claim on every coastal province in the Black Sea area

      If we complete this mission via an alliance with Theodoro:

      • Theodoro:
        • Gets “An Offer from Trebizond” event

      If we complete this mission via vassalization of Theodoro:

      • Theodoro:
        • Loses 20% liberty desire
        • Gains 1 ruler diplomatic skill

      If we complete this mission via conquest of Theodoro:

      • Province Theodoro (2410):
        • Gets its Fort upgraded[2]
        • Gains “Kalamita Fortress” for the rest of the game, giving :
          • +25% Local defensiveness
          • −75% Local fort maintenance
      		 Emperor's Legion
      Recover Perateia
      Trebizond once possessed vast overseas territories called the Perateia. As we are slowly rebuilding our nation from the ruin, the reclamation of Perateia colonies should be our utmost priority.
      		 Trebizond:
      • Owns at least 4 provinces coastal province in the Black Sea area which:
        • Is not Trebizond (330) nor borders Trebizond (330)
        • Has any Trade building
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains 3% convertible[3] mercantilism
      • Gains a permanent claim on every coastal province in the Black Sea area

      Every province that fulfills the requirements:

      • Gains “Perateia Colonies” for 25 years, giving:
        • +25% Local trade power modifier
        • −10% Local development cost
      		 The Goths and the Romans
      Ties with the Latins
      In the aftermath of the Fourth Crusade, the Latins have gained footholds in the Black Sea and have been dominating its trading naval routes ever since. If we are to rid ourselves of their negative influence, we must either make them our allies or banish them from the region for good.
      		 At least one of:
      • Trebizond owns core provinces Matrega (2447), Caffa (285) and Azov (286)
      • Genoa has at least +75 opinion of Trebizond
      		 Trebizond:
      • Removes the “Italian Coastal Influence” privilege
      • If we complete this mission via conquest:
        • Creates a level 1 center of trade in Azov (286)
      • If we complete this mission by improving relations with Genoa:
        • We and Genoa becomes mutual historical friends
        • Gains 25% progress towards the Renaissance institution in the capital
      Monopolize the Black Sea
      The Black Sea is a vital trade route that has been underutilized by our nation. By establishing a monopoly over its waters, we can ensure the prosperity of Trebizond and weaken our rivals who also depend on these trade routes.
      		 Trebizond:
      • Controls at least 50% of the trade in the Crimea trade node
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains 2 years worth of income from the home trade node
      • Gains “Black Sea Domination” ( +50 Trade power) in the Crimea trade node for the rest of the game
      		 Recover Perateia

      Ties with the Latins

      The Komnenian Restoration[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Scorn the Ottomans
      The Ottomans are a scourge that has descended upon the Roman Empire. We must show these heathens that we are not afraid of them, that we do not recognize their rule over Asia Minor, and that we can expand our alliances and realm despite their threats!
      		 Trebizond:
      • Has increased its total development by at least 30
      • At least one of:
        • Has “Insulted” or “Scornfully Insulted” the Ottomans
        • The Ottomans do not exist
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains “Komnenian Restoration” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −5% Core-creation cost
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Erzurum
        • Karadeniz
        • Rum
      • If we complete this mission while we have at least 3 alliances:
        • Gains 5 favors with all of our allies
      		 Emperor's Legion
      The Turkoman Question
      The Turkoman tribes pose a significant threat to our nation, and it is important that we find a solution to this problem as soon as possible. Our previous rulers have established a tradition of marrying Turkomen in exchange for military guarantees. Perhaps, we should consider seeking to resolve this problem via diplomacy, else we are left with military action as the only solution.
      		 At least one of:
      • Aq Qoyunlu has at least +75 opinion of Trebizond
      • All of:
        • Trebizond or non-tributary subjects owns the Erzurum area
        • At least one of:
          • Trebizond rivals Aq Qoyunlu or vice versa
          • Trebizond has won a war against Aq Qoyunlu during the last 10 years
          • Aq Qoyunlu does not exist
      		 Trebizond:
      • If we complete this mission by improving relations with Aq Qoyunlu:
        • We and Aq Qoyunlu becomes mutual historical friends
        • Gains “Turkoman Aid” for 15 years, giving:
          • −20% Cavalry cost
          • +10% Cavalry combat ability
      • If we complete this mission militarily:
        • Gains 100 administrative power
        • Gains “Turkoman Conquest” for 15 years, giving:
          • −10% Aggressive expansion impact
      Pontic Reconquest
      Despite being dominated by the Turks and Turkomen, the lands of Asia Minor are still home to many Hellenic peoples. Their unification under our rule must be the first step in our journey to turn destiny in our favor and reclaim what once rightfully belonged to us.
      		 Trebizond or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 10 provinces in Anatolia (region)
      		 Trebizond:
      • Provinces in Anatolia will slowly begin converting to Greek, Pontic or Cappadocian culture via an event
      • Loses 3 war exhaustion
      • Empowers the “Komnenian Restoration” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −5% Core-creation cost
        • +0.5 Yearly army tradition
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Karaman
        • Dulkadir
        • Çukurova
        • Ankara
      		 Scorn the Ottomans
      Avenge Manzikert
      The Battle of Manzikert represents a turning point in the history of Asia Minor and a great tragedy for the Greek people. Following the Byzantine defeat, the Turkish domination over the region was confirmed, and the Roman Empire went into a decline from which it has never recovered.
      		 Trebizond or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 20 provinces in Anatolia (region)
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gets the “Vengeance for Manzikert” event
      • Empowers the “Komnenian Restoration” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −10% Core-creation cost
        • +0.5 Yearly army tradition
      • Gains a permanent claim on Anatolia (region)
      		 Pontic Reconquest

      The Turkoman Question

      Hellenic Resettlement
      The Hellenic peoples have been scattered across the lands. We must encourage their resettlement in our territories to strengthen our cultural unity and claim our rightful place as the successors of the Roman Empire.
      		 Trebizond:
      • Has at least 100 development in 'Byzantine' provinces in Anatolia (region)
      		 The "Hellenic Resettlement" event that converts Anatolian provinces to Byzantine cultures will now fire more often

      Ten randomly owned provinces in the Byzantine culture group:

      • Gain 1 base manpower
      		 Pontic Reconquest
      March of the Vengeful
      Having avenged the humiliating defeat at Manzikert, it is time our triumphant armies march towards the ultimate goal - Constantinople. Soon, those who have caused the downfall of Rome shall pay for it, and a new empire will rise from their ashes.
      		 Trebizond:
      • Has at least as much total development as the Ottomans
      • Has an army at least as large as that of the Ottomans
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 30 provinces in Anatolia (region)
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains 100 military power
      • Empowers the “Komnenian Restoration” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −10% Core-creation cost
        • +1 Yearly army tradition
      • Gains a permanent claim on the Thrace area
      • If we complete this mission while we have humiliated the Ottomans:
        • Gains 25 convertible[4] army tradition
      		 Avenge Manzikert
      Komnenian Restoration
      With Constantinople under our control, it is time we proclaim ourselves the Emperors of the Romans and restore the empire to its former glory. The time has come for a new Komnenian Restoration!
      		 Trebizond:
      • Owns core province Constantinople (151)
      		 Trebizond:
      • Forming Byzantium will grant us 100, administrative, diplomatic and military powers
      • Gets “The Komnenian Restoration” event
      • If we complete this mission while Constantinople is of Turkish culture and Sunni:
        • Province Constantinople (151):
          • Adopts Greek culture
          • Converts to our religion
      		 March of the Vengeful
      Liberate Cilicia
      The lands of Cilician Armenians have fallen to the Turks. Let us liberate our brethren and let them rejoice under the rule of the Cross once again.
      		 Trebizond or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns provinces Adana (327), Marash (332) and Ayntab (4298) which:
        • Follows our religion
        • Has less than 1 year of separatism
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains 'Armenian' as an accepted culture[5]
      • Provinces with Armenian culture will no longer be subjected to the Hellenization via events
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Aleppo
        • Syria
        • Al Jazira
        • Syrian Desert
        • Palestine
        • Transjordan

      Province Adana (327), Marash (332) and Ayntab (4298):

      • Adopt 'Armenian' culture
      		 March of the Vengeful
      Legacy of the Crusades
      Historically, the Komnenoi have had strong ties with the Latin crusaders of the Levant - both diplomatically and militarily. As we now once again possess control over much of the former Byzantine lands, perhaps, it is time to rekindle our crusader legacy and set our eyes on the liberation of the Holy Land.
      		 Trebizond or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 15 provinces in the following areas:
        • Aleppo
        • Syria
        • Al Jazira
        • Syrian Desert
        • Palestine
        • Transjordan
      		 Trebizond:
      • Unlocks the “Crusader Nobility” Tier 2 government reform
      • Allows us to release Jerusalem even after the Age of Reformation and regardless of religion. Releasing them will turn them into our vassal.
      		 Liberate Cilicia

      Caucasian conquest[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Caucasian Kingdoms
      The kingdoms of the Caucasus are rich in resources and strategic locations. We must establish alliances with these kingdoms, or bring them under our rule, to secure our position in the region.
      		 At least one of:
      • Georgia:
        • Has at least +190 opinion of Trebizond
        • Has a royal marriage with Trebizond
      • Trebizond:
        • At least one of:
          • Has at least 2 allies in Caucasia (region)
          • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 5 provinces in Caucasia (region)
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains a claim on all provinces in Caucasia region that are not owned by our allies
      • If we complete this mission diplomatically:
        • Gets a −50% cost level 2 Diplomat with our culture and religion
      • If we complete this mission via conquest:
        • Gets a −50% cost level 2 Army reformer with our culture and religion

      We can earn both rewards of this mission by fulfilling both requirements

      Emperor of the Iberians
      Our rulers formally claim the title of Emperors and Autocrats of all the East, of the Iberians and of Perateia. It is time we press these claims and establish a permanent rule over the region of the Caucasus; be it via diplomacy or by force.
      		 At least one of:
      • Any ally or non-tributary subjects owns at least 15 provinces in Caucasia (region)
      • Trebizond or non-tributary subjects owns at least 15 provinces in Caucasia (region)
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Azov
        • Astrakhan
      • If we complete this mission by having an ally own 15 provinces in Caucasia:
        • Gains 1 ruler diplomatic skill[6]
        • Gains a permanent claim on Caucasia (region) not owned by an ally
      • If we complete this mission by directly owning 15 provinces in Caucasia:
        • Gains 1 ruler military skill[7]
        • Gains a permanent claim on Caucasia (region)
      		 Caucasian Kingdoms
      Georgian Union
      Our Georgian neighbors share much of our history and culture. It is time we unite under a single banner and face the challenges of this world together.
      		 Trebizond:
      • At least one of:
        • Is the senior partner of Georgia who:
          • Owns all 'Georgian' provinces
          • Has at least 100 development in 'Georgian' provinces
        • All of:
          • Owns all 'Georgian' provinces
          • Has at least 100 development in 'Georgian' provinces
      		 Trebizond:
      • Changes the 'Georgian' culture to 'Greco-Georgian,' moving it to the 'Byzantine' culture group
      • Gains 'Greco-Georgian' as an accepted culture
      		 Emperor of the Iberians
      Chastise the Hordes
      The hordes to the north of Caucasus pose a mortal threat to our realm. We have seen the devastation that was caused by the Mongols, and so in order to prevent it, we must pacify the steppe nomads and secure our northern borders.
      		 Trebizond:
      • At least one of:
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own province Sarai (466)
        • Controls province Sarai (466)
        • Has won a war against the Great Horde during the last 10 years
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains a new general with:
        • 60 army tradition
        • +2 shock
      • Gains “Pacified the Hordes” for 20 years, giving:
        • −2 National unrest
        • +1 Yearly army tradition
      		 Caucasian Kingdoms

      Emperor of the Iberians

      Development mission[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Bolster the Defenses
      Our nation's defenses are in a dire state. Although the mighty walls and citadel of Trebizond have not failed us yet, we cannot underestimate the safety of our beloved capital.
      		 Trebizond:
      • At least one of:
        • Is employing a Military engineer advisor
        • Owns province Trebizond (330) which has at least 7 base manpower
        • Owns province Trebizond (330) which has at least Fort level 4
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains 1.5 years' worth of manpower

      Province Trebizond (330):

      • Gains “Bolstered the Defenses” for 10 years, giving:
        • +25% Local defensiveness
        • +1 Local defender dice roll bonus
      Economic Revitalization
      The economic strength of Trebizond has been in decline for years. It's time to revitalize our economy by promoting trade, encouraging innovation, and attracting foreign investors.
      		 Trebizond:
      • Has increased its starting monthly income by at least 200%
      • Has less than 10% average autonomy above the minimum
      • Owns at least 8 provinces which either:
        • Has been developed at least 2 times
        • Has at least 1 building
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains “Economic Revitalization” for 25 years, giving:
        • −10% Development cost
        • +12 Tax income
        • Developing provinces decreases their autonomy by −5%
      		 Bolster the Defenses
      Chaldian Deposits
      Since about the sixth century AD, the Pontic mountains of Chaldia have been known for their rich mineral deposits of lead, silver, and gold. For centuries, there were few attempts to mine them as the mountains are very inaccessible, but we should make efforts to open new mines in the mountains and extract these valuable raw materials for ourselves.
      		 Trebizond:
      • Owns core provinces Trebizond (330) and Erzurum (331) which:
        • Have at least 8 base production
      • At least one of:
        • Is employing a Natural scientist advisor
        • Has at least 25% of the income is from production
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains ducats equal to 0.25 years of income

      Province Trebizond (330):

      • Now produces Gold
      • Gains 2 base production

      Province Erzurum (331):

      • Now produces Copper
      • Gains 2 base production
      		 Economic Revitalization
      Trapezuntine Pride
      Trebizond, our capital, is the heart and soul of our nation. We must invest in its development, making it a beacon of culture, commerce, and military might. As we transform Trebizond into a city that is the envy of the Black Sea, we will instill a sense of pride in every Trapezuntine.
      		 Trebizond:
      • Owns province Trebizond (330) which:
        • Has at least 20 development
        • Has any Tax building
        • Has any Trade building
        • If does not produce Gold:
          • Has any Production building
        • Else:
          • Has at least 12 base production
      		 Province Trebizond (330):
      • Gains 100 progress towards prosperity
      • Increases its center of Trade level by 1

      If we complete this mission while no coastal province in the Black Sea area has more development than Trebizond:

      • Trebizond:
        • Gains 200 splendor
      		 Bolster the Defenses

      Religious mission[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Seek Religious Support
      In these trying times, we must seek the support of the religious community. Their influence and guidance can help us navigate through the challenges we face.
      		 Trebizond:
      • Has at least 10% patriarch authority
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 5 provinces following our religion

      The Clergy estate, if exist:

      • Has at least 60 loyalty
      • Has at least 3 privileges
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains 10% patriarch authority
      • Gains “Rally Behind the Cross” for 20 years, giving:
        • +10% Manpower in true faith provinces
      Sacred Architecture
      The grandeur of our sacred architecture is a testament to our faith and devotion. We must continue to build magnificent holy places to inspire our people and demonstrate our commitment to the [Root.Religion.GetName] faith.
      		 Trebizond:
      • Has any Tax building in its capital
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 5 provinces following our religion with any Tax building
      • Has at least 50 development in owned provinces following our religion with any Tax building
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gets a −50% cost level 2 Inquisitor with our culture and religion

      The capital:

      • Gets its Tax building upgraded[8]
      		 Seek Religious Support
      Metropolitan of Trebizond
      The Metropolitan of Trebizond is a key figure in our religious and political life. We must ensure that this position is held by a person of great wisdom and piety, who can guide our people in faith and serve as a beacon of Orthodoxy.
      		 Trebizond:
      • Has at least 50% patriarch authority
      • Has at least 200 development in owned Orthodox provinces
      • Is employing an Inquisitor or Theologian advisor of at least level 3
      • Has at least 80 clergy loyalty, if they exist
      		 Trebizond:
      • Gains “Metropolitan of Trebizond” for 20 years, giving:
        • +1% Missionary strength
        • +0.15 Prestige per development from missionary
      • If we complete this mission while we are the Defender of the Faith, the "Metropolitan of Trebizond" modifier will be permanent
      • If is Orthodox:
        • Consecrating a metropolitan now gives the affected provinces a province modifier giving −10% state maintenance and −0.05 monthly devastation
      • Else:
        • Gains 200 diplomatic power
      		 Seek Religious Support
