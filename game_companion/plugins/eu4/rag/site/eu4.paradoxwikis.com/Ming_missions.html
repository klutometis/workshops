National missionsEdit

The missions Tumu Crisis and Restore the Royal Road are only available if   Ming has a   culture in the Chinese culture group which is neither Manchu, Sino-Tibetan, Sino-Altaic, Sino-Vietnamese, nor Sino-Korean. This condition is fulfilled at the start of the game, but   Ming might will access to them if they unlock their branching missions while having different culture. The other missions don't have a culture requirement.
