      National missions[edit | edit source]

      Arabian conquest[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Twilight of Oman
      We have been in conflict with the Sultanate of Oman for many years. Their influence is now reduced to the fort of Nizwa, and their defeat is imminent. Let us strike at them one last time, and wipe out their influence from our lands.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns the Oman area
      • Owns province Nizwa (403)
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 1 tier to the “Nizwa Fort” great project
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Bahrain
        • Al-Yamamah
        • An Nafud
        • Al-Qasim
        • Dhofar
        • Mahra

      Every owned Omani culture province:

      • Gains “Omani Influence Curtailed” for 15 years, giving:
        • +3% Local missionary strength
        • −25% Local missionary maintenance
        • −2 Local unrest
      Subduing the Mahri People
      Southern Arabia is home to the Mahri people, a culture distinct from other Arabian tribes. They are also renowned seafarers like us, but that only makes them our competitors. Let us march into their land and make them submit to our rule.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 5 provinces in the following areas:
        • Dhofar
        • Mahra
      		 Every owned province in the required areas:
      • Loses 25% local autonomy
      • Loses 5 years of separatism

      Province Socotra (1099):

      • If is owned by us or our non-tributary subject:
        • Creates a level 1 center of trade
        • Gets its Shipyard building upgraded[2]
        • Adopts our religion
      		 Twilight of Oman
      The Land of Bahrain
      Bahrain is home to some of the most precious gems in Arabia. They are however controlled by foreigners, not making use of them to their full potential. Perhaps it is time we took over and traded these gems for our own benefit.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns the Bahrain area
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 2% mercantilism
      • Gains 8 years of production income from Gems provinces in Bahrain area and capital

      Province Qatar (395) and Bahrain (396):

      • Gains “Exploiting the Gems of Arabia” for 15 years, giving:
        • +1 Local goods produced
      		 Twilight of Oman
      Consolidating Central Arabia
      Central Arabia is home to many hostile tribes, all much more accustomed to desert warfare than us. However, what they lack is the wealth to oppose us. Our gold will buy a thousand warriors, and with a thousand warriors we shall subdue them.
      		 Hormuz:
      • Owns province Yabrin (2339)
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects owns at least 6 provinces in the following areas:
        • al-Yamamah
        • al-Qasim
        • An Nafud
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 10 convertible[3] army tradition
      • Builds 5 cavalry regiments for free, taking 50% of normal time in Yabrin
      • Gains “Tribal Allegiance” for 20 years, giving:
        • −20% Cavalry cost
      		 Twilight of Oman
      Custodian of the Cities
      The Holy Cities of the Islamic world are controlled by weaklings. We, the merchants of [Root.GetName], backed up by gold and steel are a much better fit for the title of Custodian. Mecca and Medina will be in our hands soon.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 6 provinces in the following areas:
        • Medina
        • Mecca
      		 Hormuz:
      • The "Religious Diplomats" Clergy privilege will now double its bonuses, as well as give +10% improve relations
      • Gains a permanent claim on Egypt (region)
      		 Subduing the Mahri People
      Fall of Baghdad
      The House of Wisdom is perhaps the most famous library in the Islamic world. Created in Baghdad, it strengthened the Islamic Golden Era. However, since the Mongol Invasion it has lost its once former glory. We will help rebuild it to even greater heights, and it will start by seizing Baghdad for ourselves.
      		 Hormuz:
      • Owns province Baghdad (410)
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects owns at least 6 provinces in the following areas:
        • Basra area
        • Iraq Arabi
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 5 innovativeness
      • Gains a permanent claim on Mashriq (region)

      Province Baghdad (410):

      • Gets a University building[4]

      The Land of Bahrain
      Consolidating Central Arabia
      Subduing the Mahri People

      Conquest of Yemen
      Yemen stands as the crossroad between Arabia and Africa. They are also producers of some of the most famous coffee in the world. They are but enemies to us however, and they need to be dealt with. We will conquer them like we've conquered the rest of Arabia.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 8 provinces in the following areas:
        • Asir
        • Upper Yemen
        • Tihama al-Yemen
        • Lower Yemen
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 8 years of production income from Coffee in the required areas and capital
      • Gains 1 ruler military skill
      • Gains “Conquest of Yemen” for 15 years, giving:
        • +10% Morale of armies
        • −2 National unrest
      		 Consolidating Central Arabia
      Exploiting the Coffee's Secret
      The coffee of Yemen is one of the most profitable goods of Arabia. As traders, it would only be fair if we were to exploit this, and tried to increase the production of this good.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns provinces Taiz (4280), Zabid (4279) and Mokha (387) which:
        • Has at least 6 base production
        • Has at least 2 buildings
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains the 'Industrious' ruler personality trait}}

      Provinces Taiz (4280), Zabid (4279) and Mokha (387):

      • Build the manufactory for the trade good of this province

      Provinces Taiz (4280) and Zabid (4279):

      • Gain “Coffea Arabica” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +3 Local goods produced
      		 Conquest of Yemen
      Our Peninsula
      At last the lands of Arabia have been united. Ever since the fall of the Caliphate, and the Caliph becoming but a mere puppet, the Islamic world had been divided. Now that we have united the land of the prophet, we may now declare ourselves the new Caliph.
      		 Hormuz owns the following core provinces:
      • Alexandria (358)
      • Cairo (361)
      • Aleppo (377)
      • Jerusalem (379)
      • Damascus (382)
      • Medina (384)
      • Mecca (385)
      • Aden (388)
      • Sana'a (390)
      • Al-Arid (392)
      • Al-Qatif (394)
      • Muscat (400)
      • Nizwa (403)
      • Baghdad (410)
      		 Hormuz:
      • "Form Arabia" decision will no longer require administrative technology
      • Distributes 10 manpower development among random owned provinces
      • Gains 5 permanent power projection

      Conquest of Yemen
      Fall of Baghdad
      Custodian of the Cities

      Persian expansion[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Across the Hormuz Strait
      The Persian Gulf may be under our control, but across it lies the land of Persia, where we once had our holdings. Let us prepare an invasion force and try to reclaim what has been lost in the past.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns the following provinces:
        • Mogostan (431)
        • Bandar Langeh (2219)
        • Siraf (4332)
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 50 diplomatic power
      • Gains “Reclamation of Persian Lands” for 10 years, giving:
        • −10% Core-creation cost
        • +10% Movement speed
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Kerman
        • Makran
        • Farsistan
        • Gulf Coast

      Province Mogostan (431):

      • Gets its center of trade upgraded to level 2[5]
      Persian Beachhead
      With our foothold established in Persia, we need to start making preparations for securing inland fortifications. The Persian kingdoms may try to oppose us, but we will make sure that we will not be defeated again.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns province Bam (2220)
      • Owns at least 5 provinces in the following areas:
        • Kerman
        • Makran
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 1 tier to the “Bam Citadel” great project
      • Distributes 5 manpower development among random owned provinces
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Sistan
        • Herat
        • Birjand
      		 Across the Hormuz Strait
      Farsi Campaign
      The rulers of Fars have always been a threat to our realm. Launching campaigns against us, as well as competing for control over the Persian Gulf. We must take them out in order to remove this threat once and for all.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns the following areas:
        • Farsistan
        • Gulf Coast
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gets “Our Holdings in Fars” event
      • Gain a permanent claim on Persia (region)
      		 Across the Hormuz Strait
      Timur's Puppetmaster
      Perhaps our most trustworthy yet untrustworthy partners would be the Timurids, which aided us in the past. Now however their realm is splintered, and perhaps we can act pull some strings behind their backs in order to gain control of the Timurid Empire.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 7 provinces in the following areas:
        • Sistan
        • Herat
        • Birjand
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 5 permanent power projection
      • Gains “End of the Timurid Reign” until ruler's death, giving:
        • −0.05 Monthly autonomy change
        • −10 Years of separatism
      • If has subjugated the Timurids:
        • The Timurids lose 50% liberty desire
        • Gains 1 ruler administrative skill
        • Gains 1 ruler diplomatic skill
        • Gains 1 ruler military skill
      		 Persian Beachhead

      Farsi Campaign

      Persian Domination
      The lands of Persia serve as a great base for our traders to reside in. Being the crossroad between Islam, China, and India, securing it can allow us to more effectively steer any trade towards our realm.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 20 provinces in Persia (region)
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 25 “Arabian Mercantilism” for “Arabian Mercantilism”
      • Gains 200 splendor
      • Gains 1% mercantilism for every owned center of trade in Persia (region)
      • If is the strongest trade power in the Persia trade node
        • Gains 2 years worth of income from the Persia trade node
      		 Farsi Campaign

      Naval mission[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Extending Our Reach
      If we are to ever establish a trade network outside of Arabia, we must prepare to seize and fortify overseas outposts. In order to even establish them, we must prepare a fleet that will support our merchants in any mercantile activities outside of our homeland.
      		 Hormuz:
      • Has at least 10 light ships
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least 20 ships
        • Has a navy of at least 100% of its force limit
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains “Beyond Arabia” for 20 years, giving:
        • +50% Trade range
        • +35% Ship trade power modifier
      • Gains a permanent claim on every coastal provinces with a center of trade of at least level 2 in the following regions:
        • Deccan
        • Coromandel
        • Western India
        • Bengal
      Connections in India
      Our merchants have outposts as far as in the lands of India. Many of them however are currently oppressed by the rulers of the many Indian kingdoms. Let us set sail and help them gain their rightful influence in India.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 4 coastal provinces with a center of trade in the following regions:
        • Deccan
        • Coromandel
        • Western India
        • Bengal
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 25 “Arabian Mercantilism” for “Arabian Mercantilism”
      • Gains a permanent claim on:
        • All Spices provinces in Malaya (region)
        • Canton (667)
        • Macau (668)
      		 Extending Our Reach
      Shores of Malay
      Although our merchants influence ends at the ports of India, the lands of South East Asia have always been influenced by Islamic traders. Should we sail there and seize their trade routes, we can extend our reach even further into Asia.
      		 Hormuz:
      • Is the strongest trade power in the Malaccan trade node
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 2% mercantilism
      • Gains +0.5 piety when propagating religion on provinces in the Malacca, Philippines and Moluccas trade nodes
      • Gains a permanent claim on all Spices provinces in Indonesia and the Moluccas (region)
      		 Connections in India
      Chinese Riches
      China is perhaps the most wealthy region our merchants have encountered. From tea, to chinaware, to any goods the Islamic world has a shortage of. By establishing an influence on their ports, we may be able to create a new company that will bring us profit beyond imagining.
      		 Hormuz:
      • At least one of:
        • Controls at least 55% of the trade in the Canton trade node
        • Owns or its non-tributary subjects owns the following provinces:
          • Macau (668)
          • Canton (667)
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gets “The Far East Company” event
      		 Connections in India
      The Spice Must Flow
      Spices may not have a direct benefit to our nation, but the Spice Trade itself has proven to be profitable for us. We must make sure to secure the spices, whilst keeping away any competitors, if we are to monopolize on this valuable good.
      		 Hormuz:
      • At least one of:
        • Produces at least 65 Spices
        • Has access to at least 30% of the global Spices market
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 15 months of trade income in Home trade node
      • If the “Spice Trade” has not yet happened:
        • Gets the “Spice Trade” event
      • Else:
        • Gains 5% mercantilism
        • Gains 20 “Arabian Mercantilism” for “Arabian Mercantilism”
      		 Shores of Malay

      Chinese Riches

      Commercial venture[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Hormuz Toll
      Our control over the Persian Gulf has allowed us to control trade between Asia and the Middle East, as any ship wishing to make its way to Mesopotamia must sail through our glorious city first. If we place a sound toll for any fleet passing by, perhaps we can further increase our profits from trade any foreigner may conduct.
      		 Hormuz:
      • Controls at least 40% of the trade in the Hormuz trade node
      • Owns or non-tributary subject owns province Hormuz (2999) that:
        • Has at least 10 development
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains 10% burghers loyalty
      • Gains 1 ruler diplomatic skill

      Province Hormuz (2999):

      • Gains “Hormuz Sound Toll” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +20 Local trade power
        • −10% Local development cost
        • As long as we controls at least 40% of the trade in the Hormuz trade node
      The Overseas Outposts
      If our merchants are to stand a chance whilst they're far away from Arabia, we need to aid them as well. By establishing new outposts and building them up in order to secure new trade routes, they will be able to further strengthen their influence on foreign territory.
      		 Hormuz:
      • Has at least 35 trade value in its capital trade node
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects owns at least 5 provinces which:
        • Has a center of trade of at least level 2
        • Has at least 20 development
        • Belongs to a trade company
      		 Hormuz:
      • The “Strengthen Overseas Outposts” government interaction now give an extra 10% local trade power and −5% local center of trade upgrade cost
      • Gains 25 diplomatic power for every owned province that meets the requirements, up to 10 provinces
      		 Extending Our Reach

      The Hormuz Toll

      Gems of Arabia
      Arabian Gems are known all around the Islamic world. However, our desire of wealth grows ever stronger. We must ensure that our production of these gems is increased further, so that we may all live like kings.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns provinces Qatar (395) and Bahrain (396) that:
        • Has at least 4 base production
        • Has any Production building
      		 Provinces Qatar (395) and Bahrain (396):
      • Get their Production building upgraded[6]

      Every owned province that produces Gems:

      • Gains 100 progress towards prosperity
      		 The Hormuz Toll
      Fortifying the Island
      Hormuz may be an island, but it is still vulnerable to being attacked by anyone wishing to destroy us. This, combined with the added benefit of further controlling trade in the region, makes many of our merchants request that we take care of this problem.
      		 Hormuz or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns province Hormuz (2999) that:
        • Has any Fort
        • Has any Coastal Defense building
      		 Province Hormuz (2999):
      • Increases its Center of Trade level by 1
      • Gets its Fort upgraded[7]

      Every owned and future owned province with a Fort:

      • Gains “Fort Building” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +25% Local trade power modifier
      		 The Hormuz Toll
      Arabia's Finest Merchants
      The people of [Root.GetName] are the most renowned merchants of Arabia. Their influence extends to many ports of the Indian Ocean, and we have reaped the benefits of that influence. Perhaps if we give some of these merchants more power in our country they will be willing to help us even more.
      		 Hormuz:
      • Has at least 50 “Arabian Mercantilism” in “Arabian Mercantilism”
      • Is employing a Trader of at least level 3
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least 20% mercantilism
        • Gains at least 10 diplomatic power per month
      		 Hormuz:
      • Unlocks the "Arabian Traders" Burghers privilege
      • Gets the “Strengthening Our Merchants” event
      		 The Overseas Outposts

      Gems of Arabia

      Indian Ocean Supremacy
      The Indian Ocean is vast, but that will not stop us from becoming its master. The trade routes will be secured, and our ships will master its waves, all in the name of profit.
      		 Hormuz:
      • Has at least 90 trade value in its capital trade node
      • Has at least 6 strong trade companies[8]
      • At least one of:
        • Has completed the Trade Ideas
        • Has at least 5 ruler diplomatic skill
      		 Hormuz:
      • The "Arabian Traders" Burghers privilege will now grant +15% Arabian mercantilism gain modifier
      • Gains “Far East Company” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +1 Merchant
        • −25% Trade company investment cost
        • +15% Trade steering
      		 Chinese Riches

      Arabia's Finest Merchants

      Arabian Thalassocracy
      Our dreams of mercantile and seafaring dominance over the Indian Ocean and the rest of Asia has been achieved. Our merchants are free to buy and sell their riches from every corner of our overseas empire. Let this be a new age for any opportunistic merchant of Islam.
      		 Hormuz:
      • Gains at least 250 ducats per month
      • Is the strongest trade power in the capital trade node, which is the highest valued trade node in the world
      • Controls at least 50% of the trade in the following trade nodes:
        • Coromandel
        • Malacca
        • Bengal
      		 Hormuz:
      • “Arabian Mercantilism” will give an extra 10% scaling trade efficiency
      • Unlocks the “Thalassocracy” Tier 8 government reform
      • Gains “Thalassocracy” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +0.25 Yearly navy tradition
        • −10% Light ship cost
        • +1 Merchant
        • −5% Diplomatic technology cost
      		 Indian Ocean Supremacy

      The Spice Must Flow

      Handle the Ibadi[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Ibadi Sect
      The lands of Oman are home to the Ibadi school, a faith that has been a nuisance ever since our increased influence in the Oman area. We must deal with them, either by tolerating them, converting them, or perhaps accepting their teachings and spreading them...
      		 Hormuz:
      • At least one of:
        • Owns no Ibadi province
        • Is Ibadi and has at least 100% religious unity
        • Is not Ibadi and has at least 35 development in owned Ibadi provinces
      		 Hormuz:
      • If owns no Ibadi province:
        • Gains 15% clergy loyalty
        • Gains “Wiped Out the Ibadi Sect” for 20 years, giving:
          • +2 Tolerance of the true faith
          • +0.1 Monthly piety accelerator
      • Else, if has developed Ibadi provinces:
        • Unlocks the “Enforce Interfaith Dialog” burghers privilege
        • Gains 10% burghers loyalty
      • Else, if is Ibadi:
        • Gains “Spreading the Ibadi Sect” for 20 years, giving:
          • +2% Missionary strength
          • −25% Missionary maintenance cost
      The Ways of the Prophet
      If there's anything merchants of the Islamic world have accomplished, it's spreading their faith across Asia through trade. They are able to show our teachings by firstly introducing them to the new shores they land on, and then allowing the population to convert over time. We shall follow this tradition, so we can spread our faith to every corner of the world.
      		 Hormuz:
      • Has converted at least 30 provinces through "Propagate Religion" trading policy
      		 Hormuz:
      • “Religious Culture” Ulema privilege will now give an extra 10% local trade power and −10% province governing cost
      • When we Propagate Religion, the converted province:
        • Loses the "Religious Society" modifier
        • Gains “Religious Culture” until end of conversion, giving:
          • −1 Local unrest
          • +10% Local tax modifier
          • +10% Local production efficiency
          • +10% Local manpower modifier
          • +10% Local trade power modifier
          • −10% Province governing cost
      		 Arabia's Finest Merchants
