      English Civil War
      England is being torn apart by a conflict between Parliamentarians and Royalists.

      The English Civil War[1] is a disaster in Great Britain, Angevin Kingdom or England which usually happens during the ‘Age of Absolutism’.

      Prerequisites[edit | edit source]

      This country:

      • is either Great Britain, Angevin Kingdom or England if Great Britain and Angevin Kingdom do not exist.
      • did not have this disaster before.
      • has one of the following government reforms: English Monarchy, British Monarchy, Angevin Monarchy
      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not the lesser partner in a union.
      • one of the following:
        • owns any provinces in Britain except in Munster, Connacht, Leinster and Ulster areas.
        • had the event The Conflict Escalates

      If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

      Monthly progress starts if Monthly progress halts if
      Great Britain, Angevin Kingdom or England:
      • does not have an ongoing disaster.
      • has any of the following:
      • has less than 1 stability.
      • has less than 2 stability and at least 20 seats in parliament.
      • has less than 3 stability and at least 20 absolutism
      • has less than 3 stability and had the event The Conflict Escalates

      It is currently the Age of Absolutism or had the event The Conflict Escalates.

      Monthly progress Great Britain, Angevin Kingdom or England:
      +1.0% Has less than 50 legitimacy.
      +1.0% Has less than 0 stability.
      +1.0% Has at least 30 absolutism.
      +1.0% Has at least 35 absolutism.
      +1.0% Has at least 40 absolutism.
      +1.0% Has at least 45 absolutism.
      +1.0% Has at least 50 absolutism.
      +1.0% Has “Support Ruling Dynasty” as the current debate.
      +1.0% Has at least 5 war exhaustion.
      +0.5% Has the “Star Chamber” modifier.
      		Great Britain, Angevin Kingdom or England:
      • has any of the following:
      • 1 stability and did not have the event The Conflict Escalates.
      • 3 stability and had the event The Conflict Escalates.
      • an ongoing disaster.
      • does not have one of the following government reforms: English Monarchy, British Monarchy, Angevin Monarchy.

      Start[edit | edit source]

      If the progress reaches 100%, the disaster will start with the following event

      id

      The English Civil War

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      Rising tensions between the [Root.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName] monarchs and parliamentary hardliners following disputes about taxation and the centralization of royal power have boiled over. The Parliamentarians now openly defy the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] and his Royalists, and seek to establish republican government. For their part, the Royalists hope to rout Parliament and establish an absolute monarchy. The two sides have taken up arms against each other and are ready to fight for control of the state.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by
      • the start of the ‘English Civil War’ disaster.
      For Parliament and Liberty!

      Great Britain or England:

      • loses manpower equals to 2.5 years of manpower.
      • loses the modifier “Star Chamber".
      • remove government reform Parliamentary-Monarchy Struggle
      • heir gets exiled.
      • becomes a Republic with Protectorate Parliament government reform.
      • gets a new ruler named 'Oliver' of the 'Cromwell' dynasty with the following attributes:
        • Fixed '4 Administrative skill
        • Fixed '4 Diplomatic skill
        • Fixed '5 Military skill
        • Has strict personality

      Pretender rebels (size 3) led by the previous monarch rise in revolt in one random owned province of Britain (except Ireland) without stationed units that is not the capital (it is not possible to prevent rebel spawning by having troops in every province). If the country has more than 30 provinces, another rebel stack of size 2 spawns in a province which fulfills the same conditions.
      The AI ​​chooses this option in 50% of cases.

      For the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]!

      Great Britain or England:

      • loses manpower equals to 2.5 years of manpower.
      • remove government reform Parliamentary-Monarchy Struggle

      Parliamentarian rebels (size 3) with the leader Oliver Cromwell rise in revolt in one random owned province of Britain (except Ireland) without stationed units that is not the capital (it is not possible to prevent rebel spawning by having troops in every province). If the country has more than 30 provinces, another rebel stack of size 2 spawns in a province which fulfills the same conditions.
      The AI ​​chooses this option in 50% of cases.

      Modifiers[edit | edit source]

      While the disaster is ongoing the country gets:

      • +10 national unrest
      • −1 Yearly legitimacy
      • −0.5 Yearly republican tradition

      Also the following events are triggered by a monthly pulse.

      Monthly events[edit | edit source]

      The weight for each monthly event is 100 while the weight that no event is triggered is 1000.

      id

      Pride's Purge

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      Dissenters against the course [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] [Root.Monarch.GetName] has charted for the future of [Root.GetName] form a large and troublesome faction in Parliament. If we cannot silence them quickly, they could cause significant problems for the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]. Colonel Thomas Pride of the New Model Army has volunteered his regiment to "deal with" them, if we are willing to resort to such measures.

       
      Trigger conditions

      This country

      • did not have this event before
      • has the modifier “New Model Army”
      • owns a province in Britain which is not in Ireland and not the capital
      		 Is triggered only by

      the monthly pulse of ‘English Civil War’ disaster.

      Execute those not loyal

      Pretender rebels (size 2) rise in revolt in a random owned province of Britain (except Ireland) that is not the capital.
      The AI ​​chooses this option in 75% of cases.

      Pardon them

      This country:

      • loses 25 legitimacy
      • loses 20 republican tradition
      • loses 1 stability

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 25% of cases.

      id

      Self-denying Ordinance and New Model Army

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      A proposal to improve the quality of the army is being debated in Parliament. It has long been the practice to give command of the armies to members of the aristocracy, who often lack military experience. Under the proposed Self-denying Ordinance, Members of Parliament would be barred from holding command in the army or the navy for the duration of the civil war. Instead, the armed forces would be re-organized into the New Model Army under the command of Sir Thomas Fairfax.

       
      Trigger conditions

      This country:

      • chose the option “For Parliament and Liberty!” in the event ‘The English Civil War’.
      • did not have this event before

      the monthly pulse of the ‘English Civil War’ disaster.

      This country:

      • gets the modifier “New Model Army” for 10 years, giving:
        • +10% morale of armies
        • +10% morale of navies
        • −10% national manpower modifier
        • +1 national unrest
      • gets access to a general named 'Sir Thomas Fairfax' with the following skill:
        • '4 Fire
        • '4 Shock
        • '3 Maneuver

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 90% of cases.

      It is not needed

      This country:

      • gains manpower equals to 0.5 years of manpower.

      • then it gains 1 stability,
      • else it gains 50 administrative power.

      Finishing conditions[edit | edit source]

      If this country:

      • has at least 2 stability.
      • has less than 1 average unrest.
      • has no rebel army.
      • does not own rebel controlled provinces.

      or

      • One of the following rebels enforce their demands:
        • pretender rebels
        • Parliamentarian rebels
        • Renegade Pretender rebels[2]

      then the disaster ends and the country loses the above-mentioned effects and the following event is triggered.

      id

      End of the English Civil War

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The English Civil War is finally over as [Root.Monarch.GetName] and [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] supporters have crushed the opposing faction and entrenched their preferred type of government.

      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      the end of the ‘English Civil War’ disaster.

      • If is not a monarchy
        • becomes a monarchy

      • gains 10 absolutism.
      • if has less than 2 stability
        • gains 2 stability.
      • else, gains 20 legitimacy.

      • rebels did not enforced their demands

      • gains 50 prestige.
      • if has less than 2 stability
        • gains 2 stability.
      • else, gains 20 republican tradition.

      If Oliver Cromwell is not the ruler then this country gets the event ‘Parliamentarian Victory’.

      • rebels have enforced their demands
      The Royalists have won. Alas for our liberties!

        • gets Absolute British Monarchy government reform.

      • rebels have enforced their demands
      Parliament has won. Alas for the King!

      • becomes a republic
      • gets the Protectorate Parliament government reform

      Else, gets the event ‘Parliamentarian Victory’.

      Further events[edit | edit source]

      id

      Parliamentarian Victory

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The description below is one of several available for this event.
      The following description is used if the country had an heir which was exiled at the start of the english civil war disaster:

      As the civil war that has plagued our land has come to an end, we stand at a crossroad. Some feel that this would be the ideal time to re-establish a monarchy in our lands and call for the reinstatement of the old heir to the throne, albeit under the condition that [Root.GetName]'s governance is regulated by a strict constitution and laws to prevent the situation that led to our recent turmoil. Others argue that the time has come to do away with the monarchy altogether and establish a modern republic built around the old principles of the English Parliament.

      Otherwise following description is used:

      As the civil war that has plagued our land has come to an end, we stand at a crossroad. Some feel that this would be the ideal time to re-establish a monarchy in our lands, albeit one governed by a strict constitution and laws to prevent the situation that led to our recent turmoil. Others argue that the time has come to do away with the monarchy altogether and establish a modern republic built around the old principles of the English Parliament.''
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
