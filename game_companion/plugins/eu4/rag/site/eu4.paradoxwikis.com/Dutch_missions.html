      Dutch independance[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Raise the Militia
      We have lived under the Burgundian banner for far too long. It is time for our people to rise up and seize control of our destiny for ourselves.
      		 The country:
      • At least one of:
        • Has an army of at least 100% of its force limit
        • Has an army of at least 10 regiments
      		 The country:
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • North Brabant
        • Holland
        • Frisia
        • Flanders
        • South Brabant
      • If is a subject nation:
        • When we declare war for Independence against our overlord, we will not lose stability.
      • Else:
        • Gain a new general with:
          • 40 army tradition
          • +1 shock
      In Flanders Fields
      Strength, solidarity and a common goal are but some of the qualities that hold our people together. As we struggle to expand our territory, our next goal shall be none other than Flanders.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns province Antwerpen (1744)
      • Owns at least 4 provinces in the following areas:
        • Flanders
        • South Brabant
      		 The country:
      • If has 'Flemish' primary culture:
        • Gains 5 years of production income from Cloth provinces in Flanders and South Brabant areas
        • Increases center of trade level by 1 in Antwerpen (1744)
      • Else:
        • Gets “Our Flemish Brethren” event
      		 Raise the Militia
      Unification of Holland
      The heartland of the Low Countries is home to ample trade and wealth. Adding it to our domain would only further increase our influence and posture in the region.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 5 provinces in the following areas:
        • Holland
        • North Brabant
      		 The country:
      • Loses 10 aggressive expansion with all Imperial princes
      • If is or was Holland:
        • Gets “From Trade Cities to State” event
      • Else:
        • The most developed province in the required areas:
          • Gains 1 base production
          • Gains 2 base manpower
      		 Raise the Militia
      Frisian Freedom
      The Frisian people have long demanded their independence from outside influences and fought for their autonomy. Let us provide them with freedom under our banner, as a compromise.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns the Frisia area
      		 The country:
      • If has 'Frisian' primary culture:
        • Gains “Protecting Frisian Freedom” for 15 years, giving:
          • +0.3 Yearly republican tradition
          • −2 National unrest
        • Provinces Friesland (100) and Groningen (4382):
          • Gain 1 base production
      • Else:
        • Gains 10 convertible[2] army tradition
        • Gains 10 nobility loyalty
      		 Raise the Militia
      Je Maintiendrai
      The time has come for us to launch an attack against all those who wish to harm our people and control our lands. We will be subjects no longer!
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 15 provinces in Low Countries region
      		 The country:
      • Gains a permanent claim on the region Low Countries
      • Forming the Netherlands no longer requires to have administrative technology 10
      • Gains “Je Maintiendrai” for 30 years, giving:
        • +5% Discipline
        • −10% Development cost
        • If completes the mission whilst being the Netherlands, this modifier is extended by 10 more years

      Baltic trade[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Hansa Relations
      Begining in 1441, our naval Forces clashed numerous times with the Hansa, a dominant group of cities that controlled the lucrative trade in the Baltic Sea. Having beaten and humiliated them we signed the Treaty of Copenhagen which allowed us unlimited access and sizeable presence in their ports. It is time we demand the treaty be reinstated, lest they risk going to war.
      		 The country:
      • At least one of:
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own province Lübeck (45)
        • Controls at least 5% of the trade in the Lübeck trade node
        • Province Lübeck (45) owner:
          • Has at least +125 opinion of us
          • Is our ally
      		 The country:
      • Gains 2% convertible[4] mercantilism
      • If the mission was completed diplomatically:
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
        • Gains “Hanse Alliances” ( +60 Trade power) in the Lübeck trade node for 15 years
      • Else, if the mission was not completed diplomatically:
        • Gains a permanent claim on Lübeck (45) and Gotland (25)
        • Gains “Challenging the Hansa” ( +30 Trade power) in the Lübeck trade node for 15 years
      Ending the Sound Toll
      The goods that flow through the domain of Sjælland do so under a tariff and a toll. This is very bad for our bottom line and must be dealt with.
      		 The country:
      • Is the Netherlands

      Province Sjælland (12):

      • At least one of:
        • Is owned by the Netherlands or its non-tributary subjects
        • Is owned by a country with at least +150 opinion of the Netherlands
      		 If the mission was completed diplomatically:
      • Province Sjælland (12):
        • Gains “Dutch Traders in the Sound Toll” for the rest of the game, giving:
          • −10% Local development cost
          • As long as its owner maintains +125 opinion of the Netherlands
      • As long as we maintain +125 relations with the owner of Sjælland:
        • Gains +70 trade power in the Lübeck trade node

      Else, if the mission was not completed diplomatically:

      • Gets the “Gateway Into the Baltic” event
      		 Hansa Relations
      Support the Bulk Trades
      The grain of the Baltic has traditionally been shipped west by our merchants, but there are still so many opportunities there for us to take advantage of. If we were to expand our presence in the Baltic markets, we could earn enough money to start thinking about funding an expedition to find even more places to do business.
      		 The country:
      • Has at least 30 light ships
      • Controls at least 15% of the trade in the Lübeck trade node
      		 The country:
      • Gains 3 months worth of income from the home trade node
      • Gains a permanent claim on every province in the Baltic Sea trade node that has a center of trade
      		 Hansa Relations
      Baltic Trade Supremacy
      Northern currents and vessels with cargo from the Eastern seas are rich in fish, goods, and supplies. We must maintain a strong presence all across the Baltic
      		The country:
      • Is the strongest trade power with at least at least 25% of the trade in the Lübeck trade node
      		 The country:
      • Gains 10 burghers loyalty
      • Gains 1 ruler diplomatic skill[5]
      • If Lübeck is not a trade league leader:
        • Gains “Connection to the Baltic” until the end of the Age of reformation, giving:
          • +10% Trade efficiency
      		 Support the Bulk Trades
      Securing Baltic Grain
      The Low Countries have always been supplied by grain imports, specifically those from the Baltic. If we manage to secure that grain supply for ourselves, we will no longer be required to pay for it.
      		 The country:
      • Has the trading in bonus for Grain
      • Is the strongest trade power with at least at least 30% of the trade in the Baltic Sea trade node
      		 The country:
      • Distributes 5 production development among random owned provinces

      Every owned Low Countries province that is Farmlands:

      • Gains “Baltic Grain Supply” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +25% Local manpower modifier
        • +15% Local garrison size
      		 Support the Bulk Trades


      Dutch production[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Cloth Trading
      Workshops across our provinces are famed for the quality of their cloth and string. Various techniques and local artisans lend their expertise to create some of the finest garments known to Europe.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Produce at least 25 cloth
      		 Every province in the Low Countries region that are NOT Flemish and produce Cloth:
      • Gains “Dutch Cloth Trading” for 15 years, giving:
        • +20% Trade value modifier

      Every Flemish provinces in the Low Countries region that produce Cloth:

      • Gains “Flemish Cloth Trading” for 15 years, giving:
        • +30% Trade value modifier
      Dyke Works
      Robust engineering and a culmination of strong materials are some of the factors that will allow us to erect strong dykes and master the water element.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • The "Dutch Polders" great project is magnificent
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 6 provinces which:
        • Have been developed at least 3 times
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Gain 100 diplomatic power
      • Gain 10 convertible[6] prestige
      • The "Dutch Polders" now grants:
        • +5% Goods produced modifier
        • +20% Provincial trade power modifier
      		 Cloth Trading
      Land Reclamation
      Despite the sea providing for us through trade and coin, it has become apparent that to ensure more living space for our people, we must reclaim it from the sea.
      		 The Netherlands or non-tributary subjects:
      • Own at least 6 coastal provinces in Low Countries region which:
        • Has been developed at least 1 time by its owner
        • Has at least 1 building
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Distribute 5 production development among random owned provinces fulfilling the requirements

      Every owned province fulfilling the requirements:

      • Gains “Reclaiming the Coast” for 20 years, giving:
        • −20% Local construction cost
        • +10% Local production efficiency
      Cities of the Low Countries
      The progress of man may not be hindered or stalled by the architectural deficiencies of our forefathers. To that end, let us erect and expand our urban centers to facilitate a growing demand for goods and living quarters.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Gain at least 200 ducats per month
      • Own or non-tributary subjects own at least 10 provinces owned in Low Countries region which:
        • Has at least 25 development
        • Has at least 4 buildings
      		 The Netherlands:
      • If have 'Flemish' primary culture:
        • Every owned and future province in the Low Countries that meets the requirements:
          • Gains “Flemish Urbanization” for the rest of the game, giving:
            • +15% Local goods produced modifier
            • −20% Province governing cost
            • −20% Local construction cost
      • Else:
        • Every owned and future province in the Low Countries that meets the requirements:
          • Gains “Dutch Urbanization” for the rest of the game, giving:
            • +10% Local goods produced modifier
            • −10% Province governing cost
            • −10% Local construction cost

      Dutch army[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Founding the Staatse Leger
      As an emerging nation in need of defending itself, we must establish a fast-moving army that is well-equipped and ready to defend our home provinces.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Have an army at least 30 regiments
      • At least one of:
        • Have at least 25 army tradition
        • Have at least 10% army professionalism
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Gain “Found the State's Army” for 25 years, giving:
        • +30% Army drill gain modifier
        • +10% Morale of armies
      Mauritian Reforms
      The rise of Army Professionalism in Europe is slowly changing how warfare is conducted. Instead of a king leading his nobles and their militia into battle, the army is now an institution of itself. Despite our lack of population, we can make use of mercenaries in order to protect our state.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Have an army at least 75 regiments
      • Have at least 30% army professionalism
      • At least one of:
        • Are employing an Army reformer advisor of at least level 3
        • Have the Martial Educator ruler personality trait
        • Have at least 4 ruler military skill
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Get the “Dutch Military Reforms” event, if did not had it before
      • Gain a new general named “Wilhelm Ludwig von Nassau” with:

      Fixed Garrisons
      Our homelands are not large, which means it is all the more important to defend what we do have. We must establish a strong garrison system that can protect our beloved country.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Are employing a Military engineer advisor of at least level 2
      • Own or non-tributary subjects own at least 7 provinces in the Low Countries region which:
        • Has any Fort building of the highest level available
      		 Every owned and future Fort provinces in the Low Countries region:
      • Gains “Fort Building” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −5% Local development cost
        • +10% Local production efficiency
        • +10% Local defensiveness
        • The above bonuses will be doubled should we ever complete Defensive ideas
      		 Founding the Staatse Leger
      Army of the State
      There is one thing the army should accomplish, and that is protect the state. Protect it from foreigners and from insurrectionists. It is why we must strengthen our army to be the most glorious in Europe, so that we may be able to protect ourselves.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Have an army at least 200 regiments
      • Have won at least 200 battles
      • Own or non-tributary subjects own at least 5 provinces in the Low Countries region which:
        • Has any Manpower building
        • Has at least 6 base manpower
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Distribute 15 manpower development among random owned provinces fulfilling the requirements
      • The “Staatse Leger” government reform now grants:
        • +15% Army drill gain modifier
        • +2.5% Discipline
      		 Mauritian Reforms

      • Own at least 40 artillery regiments
      • At least one of:

      • Get the “Pioneers of Modern Siege Warfare” event

      Statists vs Orangists[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Statists and Orangists
      The dispute between those who support the state, the 'Statists', and those who support the monarchy, the 'Orangists', has been plaguing our nation since its foundation. We must pick a side in this conflict if we are to end it once and for all.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Have at least +2 stability
      • Have at least 4 government reforms
      • If use Statists vs Orangists mechanics:
        • Either Statists or Orangists have at least 33 power

      The "Constitutionalism and the General Estates" event has happened

      		The Netherlands:
      • Get the “Shaping the State” event
      Handling the Stadtholders
      Every province has the right to appoint its own stadtholder, who acts as both a political leader and a military leader. As our country grows larger and the provinces continue to unite, however, a multitude of stadtholders pose certain problems, particularly for the army. We need a single commander in charge of the overall military effort, and the simplest way to achieve that is to appoint a single man to the stadtholderate of multiple provinces at the same time.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Have at least 80 legitimacy (or equivalent)
      • Are employing every type of advisor of at least level 2
      • If use Statists vs Orangists mechanics:
        • Either Statists or Orangists have at least 75 power
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Gain 50 government reform progress
      • Our rulers will no longer gain the trait Embezzler
      • If the Statists are in power:
        • Gain “Prelude to True Freedom” for 20 years, giving:
          • +0.3 Yearly republican tradition
          • −2 National unrest
      • Else, if the Orangists are in power:
        • Gain 1 ruler administrative skill[7]
        • Gain 1 ruler diplomatic skill[5]
        • Gain 1 ruler military skill[8]
        • Gain 200 splendor
      		 Statists and Orangists
      Lands of the Generality
      It is important to administer our country soundly, given the fact that our country is home to many regional differences. To that end, the central government sometimes needs to directly administer lands to prevent one regional faction from gaining too much influence in national affairs.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Have at least 50% crown land ownership
      • Have less than 10% average non-overseas core province autonomy
      • If have a parliament:
        • At least one of:
          • Have completed the Administrative ideas
          • Have the Lawgiver ruler personality trait
          • Have at least 10 seats in parliament
      • Else:
        • At least one of:
          • Have completed the Administrative ideas
          • Have the Lawgiver ruler personality trait
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Distribute 10 tax development among random owned provinces
      • When we "Seize Crownland", all estates will only lose 10% loyalty
      		 Statists and Orangists
      Fate of the Confederation
      The Confederation that has founded our Republic is now completed. The Orangist and Statist dispute is now settled, with one side emerging victorious. It is time we now shape the Confederation to how our rulers imagined it from the start.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • Have enacted one of the highest tier government reforms
      • Have at least 500 total development
      • If use Statists vs Orangists mechanics:
        • Either Statists or Orangists have at least 90 power
      		 The Netherlands:
      • If the Statists are in power:
        • Gain “True Freedom” for the rest of the game, giving:
          • −2.5% All power costs
          • −10% Stability cost modifier
      • Else, if the Orangists are in power:
        • Gain “Orangist Triumph” for the rest of the game, giving:
          • +20 Maximum absolutism
          • +10% Max effect of absolutism
      • Else, if are not a "Dutch Republic":
        • The Estate Land Rights privileges will give +20 more governing capacity
      		 Lands of the Generality

      Handling the Stadtholders

      Religion & Humanism[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Re-Organizing the Clergy
      The branch of the clerics and men of faith that mingle in court affairs is a most dangerous precedent to set. We must weed out their corrupt elements and assure their loyalty.
      		 The Netherlands:
      • If the Clergy estate exists:
        • Have at least 60 clergy loyalty
        • At least one of:
          • Have less than 50 clergy influence
          • Have at least 4 clergy privileges
      • Else:
        • Own or non-tributary subjects own at least 5 provinces with any Tax building
      		 The Netherlands:
