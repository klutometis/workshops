      National missions[edit | edit source]

      Centralization or Decentralization[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Secure Electors
      Ever since Rudolph, Count of Habsburg, was crowned Holy Roman Emperor and maneuvered his family into the Duchy of Austria, it has been the goal of all its Dukes to become Emperor. We must do what we can to ensure that the Empire has the stability it needs, an Austrian Emperor.
      		 The country:
      • If the Holy Roman Empire exists:
        • Is the Holy Roman Emperor
        • Has at least 4 electors backing us to become Emperor and having at least +75 opinion of us
      • Else:
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 20 provinces in South Germany region
      		 The country:
      • Enables branching missions
      • Gains “Successful bid for the Imperial Crown” for 15 years, giving:
        • +1 Yearly prestige
        • +1 Diplomatic reputation
      • If both we and Bohemia are independent Christian monarchies:
        • Gains "Restoration of Union" casus belli against Bohemia for 25 years
      • Else:
        • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
          • Bohemia
          • Erzgebirge
          • Moravia

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Imperial Border
      Some territories of the Empire have lapsed from our rule and are governed by those who do not recognize the Emperor's authority. This situation must be rectified.
      		 The country:
      • Is the Holy Roman Emperor

      • At least one of:
        • Is owned by a country not in the HRE
        • Is owned by a country that is subject to a country not in the HRE

      • Gains 15 imperial authority

      Every HRE member state that is NOT a rival of or rivaled by us:

      • Gains 10 trust towards us

      • Gains “Imperial Capital” for 20 years, giving:

      Tensions between member states have been aggravated by religious differences. We must restore religious unity if we are to regain the moral authority we claim as the "Holy" Roman Empire.

      • Is the Holy Roman Emperor
      • All concessions in the Council of Trent have been taken
      • At least one of:
        • The religion of the Holy Roman Empire is fixed

      There are those among the Princes who would have ambitions to challenge us and seize control of the Empire for themselves. A show of diplomatic and military strength will be necessary to quell these ambitions and ensure that the predominance of our power is undisputed.
      		 The country:
      • Is the Holy Roman Emperor

      At least one of:

      • The last Centralization imperial reform Revoke The Privilegia has been passed
      • All of:
        • No HRE member state below +100 opinion of us has 10 provinces in the Holy Roman Empire
        • At least 10 members of the HRE have “Free City” government reform
      		 The country:
      • Gains 50 diplomatic power
      • Gains “Ascendancy within the Empire” for 20 years, giving:
        • −10% Diplomatic annexation cost
        • +10% Land force limit modifier

      • Gains 50 administrative power
      		 Secure Electors

      		 The country:

        • Owns at least 5 provinces which:

      • Is the Holy Roman Emperor

      At least one of:

      • The last Decentralization imperial reform Reichskrieg has been passed
      • The last Centralization imperial reform Revoke The Privilegia has been passed

      Every HRE member state:

      • Has at least +1 opinion of us

      No province of the HRE:

      • At least one of:
        • Is owned by a country not in the HRE
        • Is owned by a country that is subject to a country not in the HRE
      		 The country:
      • Gains 25 power projection
      • If the last Centralization imperial reform Revoke The Privilegia has been passed:
        • Gains 15 convertible[2] prestige
        • The "Strong Duchies" nobility privilege now grants:
          • +25% Vassal force limit contribution
          • +10% Income from vassals
      • Else, if the last Decentralization imperial reform Reichskrieg has been passed:
        • Gains 50 administrative power
        • Gains 50 diplomatic power
        • Gains 50 military power
        • Unlocks the "Imperial War Leader" nobility privilege, granting:
          • +5% Morale of armies
          • +50% Army tradition from battles
          • −10 Opinion of Same Culture Group

      German Unification branch[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Gessler's Revenge
      We have attempted to annex the Swiss before but were thwarted on every occasion - by a simple farmer with a bow and arrow, in one case. We must overcome the generations of humiliation we have suffered and bring these rebellious farmers under our rule.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 5 provinces in the following areas:
        • Switzerland
        • Romandie
      		 The country:
      • Gains 10 military power per province of 'Swiss' culture

      Province Breisgau (72):

      • If is connected to our capital:
        • Gets its Fort upgraded[4]
        • Gains “Fort of Breisgau” for the rest of the game, giving:
          • −50% Local fort maintenance
          • +15% Local defensiveness
      		 Secure Electors
      Duchy of Bavaria
      The Bavarians have long been a troublesome presence on our north-western border. Their wealthy duchy has the potential to be a great thorn in our side. We should secure our borders - and our preeminence - by claiming it for ourselves.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns province Salzburg (76)
      • Owns at least 6 provinces in the following areas:
        • East Bavaria
        • Upper Bavaria
        • Lower Bavaria
      		 The country:
      • Gain 50 military power
      • Gains 10 convertible[2] prestige

      Province Salzburg (76):

      • Gains “Salt; A Way of Life” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +1 Local goods produced
      		 Secure Electors
      Dealing With Brandenburg
      Brandenburg has the potential to become the power base of a state in northern Germany strong enough to challenge us. This challenge must be neutralized if our dominance in Germany is to remain unquestioned.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns province Berlin (50)
      • Owns at least 5 provinces in the following areas:
        • Neumark
        • Mittelmark
      		 The country:
      • Gains a permanent claim on North Germany (region)
      • Gains 10 convertible[5] army tradition
      • Adds 1 tier to the great project "Brandenburg Gate"
      		 Secure Electors
      South German Cities
      Southern Germany is the home to many great German families. By developing their lands, we may gain favors with them.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 5 provinces in South Germany (region) which:
        • Has at least 20 development
        • Has at least 3 buildings
      		 The country:
      • Gains 50 government reform progress

      Five random provinces that meet the requirements:

      • Gets its Tax building upgraded[6]
      • Gains “South German Cities” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −15% Local development cost
      		 Gessler's Revenge
      Prussian Styled Armies
      Prussia is known to have one of the most professional armies in Europe. Many military theorists have studied how their armies function, and wish to implement them for our armies.
      		 The country:
      • Has an army of at least 100 regiments
      • Has at least 30% army professionalism
      • At least one of:
        • Gains at least 10 military power per month
        • Has the Martial Educator ruler personality trait
        • Has completed the Quality Ideas
      		 The country:
      • Whenever we go to war, the "Nobility in Officer Corps" nobility privilege will grant +10% morale of armies
      • Gets a −75% cost level 3 Army Reformer advisor named “Friedrich Wilhelm von Haugwitz”
      • Gains “Von Haugwitz's Reforms” for 20 years, giving:
        • +0.5 Yearly army tradition
        • +25% Army drill gain modifier
      		 Dealing With Brandenburg
      Unify Germany
      The disunity in Germany has lasted for far too long. The Holy Roman Empire has only split the land rather than unify it. By force, we will unite the numerous German states under our banner.
      		 The country:
      • Owns the following core provinces:
        • Berlin (50)
        • Brunswick (57)
        • München (65)
        • Augsburg (1868) or Stuttgart (70)
        • Nürnberg (67) or Frankfurt (1876)
        • Niederhessen (1762) or Köln (85)
        • Königsberg (41) or Straßburg (75)
        • Hamburg (44) or Lübeck (45)
        • Dresden (61) or Erfurt (63)
      • Owns at least 50 core provinces which:
        • Is in North or South Germany (region)
        • Is not in Erzgebirge, Bohemia nor Moravia areas
      		 The country:
      • Gains 10 permanent power projection
      • Removes the administrative technology requirement from the "Form German Nation" decision

      Western expansion[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Reclaim Burgundy
      The Burgundian territories have lapsed from the Holy Roman Empire. We must ensure that they are brought under our authority again, one way or another.
      		 The country:
      • At least one of:
        • Is the senior partner in a personal union with Burgundy
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 4 provinces in the following areas:
          • Eastern Burgundy
          • Lorraine
      		 The country:
      • Gains a permanent claim on the Low Countries region
      • If is the Holy Roman Emperor:
        • Gains 5 imperial authority (scaled)
      • If Burgundy is our junior partner:
        • Gets “The Lands of Lothair” event
      • Else:
        • Gets a −50% cost level 2 Diplomat advisor
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
      Weaken France
      The French are our main rivals for hegemony over Germany and Europe as a whole. We must weaken them to the degree that they can never again pose a threat to our imperial ambitions.
      		 The country:
      • If France exists and is not a subject nation:
        • Has at least as much total development as France
        • Has won a war against France during the last 20 years
      • Else:
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 3 provinces in France (region)
      		 The country:
      • Gains 10 power projection
      • Gains “War Against the French” for 10 years, giving:
        • +5% Morale of armies
        • −5% Province warscore cost
      • Gains a permanent claim on France (region)
      		 Reclaim Burgundy
      Austrian Netherlands
      The Dutch lands are home to many prosperous cities. If we can make them our own, we will be able to give our treasury a significant boost - and put us in a position to entertain ambitions of an overseas empire.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 16 provinces in the Low Countries (region)
      		 The country:
      • Gains ducats equal to 1 year of income
      • Gains “Conquest of the Netherlands” ( +35 Trade power) in the English Channel trade node for 20 years
      • Unlocks the "Great Council of Mechelen" Tier 6 government reform which disables the Dutch Revolt
      		 Reclaim Burgundy
      Defeat the French
      With the French weakened, their realm now lies in chaos. It is time to deliver a coup de grace, and end them once and for all.
      		 The country:
      • At least one of:
        • Has France as our subject
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 25 provinces in France (region)
        • All of:
          • Is the Holy Roman Emperor
          • At least 25 provinces in France (region) are part of the Holy Roman Empire
      		 The country:
      • If has completed this mission by conquest or subjugation:
        • Gets the “Conquest of France” event
      • Else, if has completed this mission by adding provinces in the Holy Roman Empire:
        • Gains 5 imperial authority (scaled)
        • Gains 1 ruler diplomatic skill[7]
      		 Weaken France
      Iberian Relations
      We may not share a lot in common with the Iberian nations, but if there is one thing that can unite us, it is the French. Our hatred for them may be enough to convince them to place a [Root.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName] on their throne.
      		 The country:
      • At least one of:
        • All of:
          • Has at least twice the development of Spain (else Castile)
          • Shares the same dynasty with Spain (else Castile)
          • Has 100 legitimacy (or equivalent)
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 4 provinces in Iberia (region)
      		 The country:
      • If both we and Spain (else Castile) are independent Christian monarchies:
        • Gains "Restoration of Union" casus belli against Spain (else Castile) for 10 years
      • Unlocks the "Unite with the Iberian Crown" decision
      • Gains a permanent claim on Iberia (region)
      Crown of Spain
      With the establishment of the Universal Empire, all that is left is to conquer the rest of Iberia.
      		 The country:
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 55 provinces in Iberia (region)
      • Is a great power of at least rank 4
      		 The country:
      • Gains 10 permanent power projection
      • If Spain (and Castile) are our junior partner:
        • Castile and Spain will be turned into Incorporated Personal Unions costing us no diplomatic upkeep.
      • If it is the Age of Reformation:
        • Gains “Onwards! For Glory!” until the end of the Age of Reformation, giving:
          • +1 Diplomatic reputation
          • −10% Aggressive expansion impact
      • Else:
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
      		 Iberian Relations
      Hegemon of Europe
      With the fall of France and Iberia, there is no more competition for us in the West. Let us declare ourselves as the hegemons of Europe.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 140 provinces in the following regions:
        • France
        • Iberia
        • Britain
      		 The country:
      • Triples the chance to get the Charismatic Negotiator personality
      • Gains “Hegemons of Western Europe” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +25% Chance to inherit personal union subjects
        • −10 Years for personal union integration
      		 Defeat the French

      Italian ambition[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Italian Ambition
      The rich lands of Northern Italy have long been defying Imperial decrees. These cities must not be tolerated so close to the Austrian heartlands.
      		 At least one of:
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 4 provinces in Italy (region)
      • All of:
        • Is currently dealing with the "Shadow Kingdom"
        • Is the Holy Roman Emperor
      		 The country:
      • If has completed this mission via "Shadow Kingdom":
        • Gets the “Bringing Light to Shadows” event
      • Else, if has completed this mission via conquest:
        • Gains “Italian Wars” for 10 years, giving:
          • −15% Mercenary cost
          • +2.5% Mercenary discipline
        • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
          • Venetia
          • Po Valley
          • Lombardy
      Duchy of Milan
      The [MLO.GovernmentName] of Milan is a troublesome but important part of the Empire. We should make sure that its riches cannot be used against us by bringing it under our closer control.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns province Milan (104)
      • Owns at least 5 provinces in the following areas:
        • Lombardy
        • Po Valley
      		 The country:
      • Gains 20 convertible[2] prestige
      • Adds 1 tier to the "Duomo di Milano" great project
      • If both we and Naples are independent Christian monarchies:
        • Gains "Restoration of Union" casus belli against Naples for 25 years
      		 Italian Ambition
      Venetian Ports
      Venice is known for its trade fleets, its wealth, and its maritime traditions. By conquering their ports in the Italian mainland, we may be able to start creating a navy to dominate the Mediterranean.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 4 provinces in Venetia area
      		 The country:
      • Gains 3% convertible[8] mercantilism

      Every owned or non-tributary subjects owned port province in the Venetia area:

      • Gets its Shipyard building upgraded[3]
      • Gains “Protected Italian Interests” for 10 years, giving:
        • +1 Tax income
        • +10% Local production efficiency
      		 Italian Ambition
      King of Naples
      By adding the Kingdom of Naples to our collection of titles, we can solidify our control of Italy and move one step closer towards bringing the entire peninsula under our rule.
      		 The country:
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own province Napoli (121)
      • At least one of:
        • Has Naples as its subject
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 8 provinces in the following areas:
          • Campania
          • Apulia
          • Calabria
      		 The country:
      • Gains “Italy Subjugated” for 20 years, giving:
        • −10% Unjustified demands
        • −5% Aggressive expansion impact
      • Gains a permanent claim on Italy (region)

      Province Napoli (121):

      • Increases its center of trade level by 1
      • Gains 2 base production
      		 Italian Ambition
      One True King of Italy
      Our conquest of Italy is finalized. From Lombardy to Naples, the crown we now wear rules all of it.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns every non-island province in Italy (region)
      		 The country:
      • Gains 1 ruler administrative skill[9]
      • Gains “Iron Crown of Lombardy” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +15% Improve relations
        • −20% Envoy travel time

      Eastern conquest[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Crown of Bohemia
      The fate of the Kingdom of Bohemia has long been of interest to the Dukes of Austria. We must take a more active hand in this and bring the Kingdom more firmly into our orbit.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns province Prague (266)
      • Owns at least 8 provinces in the following areas:
        • Bohemia
        • Erzgebirge
        • Moravia
      		 The country:
      • Gains a permanent claim on the Silesia area
      • If is the senior partner in a personal union with Bohemia:
        • Bohemia integrate its Silesian subjects ( Glogow, Opole and Silesia)
        • Gains “Crown of Bohemia” for 15 years, giving:
          • +1 Diplomatic relation
          • −10% Liberty desire in subjects
      • Else:
        • Gains 5 imperial authority (scaled)
        • Gains “Kingdom of Bohemia” for 15 years, giving:
          • +10% Imperial authority growth modifier
          • +1 Yearly legitimacy
      Surpass the Poles
      The Holy Roman Empire has been into several conflicts with the Polish crown. Perhaps now it is time to surpass them and conquer their lands.
      		 The country:
      • At least one of:
        • Has an army at lest 2 times stronger than the Commonwealth (else Poland)
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 3 provinces in Poland (region)
      		 The country:
      • Gains a permanent claim on Poland (region)
      • If Poland or the Commonwealth and their subjects have less than 400 development:
        • If both we and the Commonwealth (else Poland) are independent Christian monarchies:
          • Gains "Restoration of Union" casus belli against the Commonwealth (else Poland) for 25 years
      		 Crown of Bohemia
      Recover Silesia
      The province of Silesia is a legitimate part of the Habsburg inheritance but has been lost. We must recover it for the sake of the majesty of the Habsburg dynasty.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 3 provinces in Silesia area which:
        • Has at least 15 development
        • Has at least 3 buildings
      		 Every owned or non-tributary subjects owned province in the Silesia area:
      • Gains “Silesian Industry” for 20 years, giving:
        • +10% Local goods produced modifier
        • −10% Local development cost

      A random owned or non-tributary subjects owned province in Silesia with at least 15 development:

      • Gains 2 base production
      • Gets its Production building upgraded[3]
      		 Crown of Bohemia
      King of Poland
      Our north-eastern frontier will never be truly safe until we can count the whole of Poland as subject to the Austrian [Root.GetStateForm].
      		 The country:
      • At least one of:
        • Has the Commonwealth (else Poland) as our subject
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 15 provinces in Poland (region)
      		 The country:
      • If has the Commonwealth (else Poland) as our subject:
        • Unlocks the "Lisowczycy" mercenary company that cost no army professionalism to recruit nor Forcelimit to maintain
        • Gains 15 convertible[5] army tradition
      • Else:
        • Gains 'Polish' as an accepted culture[10]
        • Gets the “Cities of Poland” event
      		 Surpass the Poles
      Conquer Galicia
      Poland is a weak state led by weak rulers. The relevant nations of the world, and our own nation foremost, should carve up their nation at the first opportunity.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 6 provinces in the following areas:
        • Malopolska
        • Red Ruthenia
        • Sandomierz
      		 The country:
      • Gets the “Founding a New Empire” event
      		 Surpass the Poles

      Southern expansion[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Decline of Hungary
      Hungary's relative decline in relation to the Duchy of Austria is a cause for concern. We must tighten our relationship with Hungary to ensure its lands do not fall into hostile hands.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Has at least 200 development
      • If Hungary exists:
        • Has at least as much development as Hungary
      		 The country:
      • Gains 5 convertible[5] army tradition
      • If both we and Hungary are independent Christian monarchies:
        • Gains "Restoration of Union" casus belli against Hungary for 25 years
      • Else:
        • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
          • Slovakia
          • Transdanubia
          • Alföld
          • Slavonia
          • Croatia
          • Dalmatia
      Secure Croatia
      Let us create a military frontier in the old kingdom of Croatia. This way we can fend off anyone who would encroach on the Christian lands of South East Europe.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 9 provinces in the following areas:
        • Dalmatia
        • Croatia
        • Slavonia
      		 The country:
      • Gains 0.8 years' worth of manpower

      Province Rijeka (4752):

      • Increases center of trade level by 1
      • Gains 2 base manpower
      		 Decline of Hungary
      Royal Hungary
      The Kingdom of Hungary is threatened by dangerous enemies on all sides. We must step in and protect the Hungarian people directly, unifying their lands under one ruler.
      		 The country or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 11 provinces in the following areas:
        • Slovakia
        • Transdanubia
        • Alföld
        • Northern Transylvania
        • Southern Transylvania
      		 The country:
      • If Hungary is our subject:
        • Makes Hungary an Incorporated Personal Union
        • Gets a −50% cost level 2 Diplomat advisor with Hungarian culture
