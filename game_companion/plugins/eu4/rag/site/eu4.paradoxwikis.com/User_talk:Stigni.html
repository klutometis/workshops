      Tuscany's Goverment[edit source]

      If you go through the historic dates tuscany is at first a despotic monarchy and later an enlightened despotism. --Gsueso (talk) 19:16, 1 July 2015 (CEST)

      They become a Despotic Monarchy in 1532. The "first" government is Oligarchic Republic, so we take that. Stigni (talk) 20:07, 1 July 2015 (CEST)
      No, florence starts as an oligarchic republic and later becomes a feudal monarchy, tuscany itself starts as a despotic monarchy. --Gsueso (talk) 20:11, 1 July 2015 (CEST)
      Chances are you're both correct. In 1444, Tuscany is an Oligarchic Republic. But it doesn't actually exist in 1444.
      In 1532 it becomes a Despotic Monarchy. This is presumably when, or before, it actually shows up in-game.
      The government form actually used in the first moment in time the country shows up in-game should be used. Alternatively, "Feudal Monarchy" should be used as that's what the formation decision forces. ~ Meneth (talk) 20:16, 1 July 2015 (CEST)
      The first time TUS shows up is in 1569, and as I said in 1532 it become a Despotic Monarchy. But I am for Oligarchic Republic for two reason:
      1. Coherence with Countries
      2. As Tuscany is primary nation for Tuscan culture it could show up early as Oligarchic Republic.
      Stigni (talk) 20:24, 1 July 2015 (CEST)

      Italy[edit source]

      Hi. I saw you reverted my change on Italy's primary culture. As noted in my edit, I recently (1.14.3) formed Italy with Venice and the primary culture didn't change to Lombard, but stayed Venetian. The changelog for 1.14.4 didn't mention changing this, so I am quite confident that the current page is incorrect. Gorath99 (talk) 10:19, 21 January 2016 (CET)

      In the country page we insert the default culture for the country. For Italy you can see in /Europa Universalis IV/history/countries/ITA - Italy.txt that there is primary_culture = lombard. So for historical start in 1800ca where Italy exist it has lombard culture. Of course if you form Italy as Venice you will keep your primary culture, as you do if you form GBR as Scotland or Spain as Aragon/Portugal; or look at Bharat/Hindustan any indian culture (and they are in 5 different group) can form them or Germany where only Westphalian, Pommeranian, Rhenish, Saxon, Bavarian, Prussian, Swabian and Franconian nation can form it. If we insert for each Formable Nation a note in the infobox as you have done for Italy some infobox (Indian Nation/Germany/...) will be too much complicated; so if you want insert a note about the culture in the Decision section but not in the infobox. Stigni (talk) 10:35, 21 January 2016 (CET)
      Ah, that makes sense. Thanks for explaining! Gorath99 (talk) 10:59, 21 January 2016 (CET)
