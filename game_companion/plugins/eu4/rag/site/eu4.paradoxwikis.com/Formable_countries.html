      Formable countries

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
      At least some were last verified for version 1.31.

      See also: Formation decisions

      A formable country is one that can be formed by decision if certain conditions are met. A reformable country is a formable country that exists at the 1444 start; if it ceases to exist other countries with similar culture may transform into it, adopting their flag, ideas, and identity. All the relevant formation decisions can be found in their respective country pages (unless otherwise stated).

      Forming a country through decision[edit | edit source]

      Generally, a nation must meet the following requirements to form most formables:

      • Target formable does not exist
      • Current nation is not an end-game tag
      • At peace
      • Is not a non-tributary subject
      • Is not a Steppe Horde
      • Own and core certain provinces (Some provinces can also be owned by non-tributary subjects)

      And forming a country will have the following effects:

      • A new name and color; often some prestige, permanent claims and the "Increased Centralization" modifier.
      • Most formable countries have unique national ideas. An event triggered by the formation decision gives a choice between switching ideas or keeping the existing set. Note that custom nations do not get the event this way.
      • Formation of a new country changes "historical rival" attitudes regarding other countries. For example, if Christian Ottomans form Roman Empire, they will no longer be historical rivals with Austria. This also applies inversely; if Burgundy form France, they will become historical rivals with Austria.
      • Forming another nation while being the Emperor of China resets all reforms made as the previous nation. This is important to keep in mind if looking to form Qing or Yuan.
      • Forming a country may require your capital to be in a region, or it may move your capital. This may void your trade company investments, and if you don't have a main trade port it may move your home node.
      • Forming a country resets any expanded infrastructure done in any provinces. Centralized states remain centralized however.
      • All separatism in provinces owned at the time of formation is removed as soon as they are cores - instantly if they already are cores. This is very useful to get rid of rebels when expanding rapidly.

      End-game tags[edit | edit source]

      Some countries are end game tags. These countries are not allowed to form other countries even if they otherwise meet the prerequisites.[1] Nine exceptions are forming Aotearoa, Hawai'i, Roman Empire or Viti, the last imperial reform creating the Holy Roman Empire, Yuan forming the Mongol Empire, Ethiopia forming Aksum, Persia forming Eranshahr and Byzantium forming Latin Empire or Morea. However, none of these options are available to the Holy Roman Empire or the The Papal State. Forming a non-endgame-tag as an end-game tag does not lift the end-game tag restrictions. The end-game tag restrictions can be lifted by disabling the option Limited Country Forming, although this will disable achievements.

      Former colonial nations[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.36.

      The following nations represent new identities in the New World, and are usually formed by colonial nations who have achieved independence. Unlike other formable nations, these can be formed in games using the random setup. A country can only form one of these nations during a scenario, with exceptions of forming United States and Canada. Around half of these nations don't have unique ideas or events, so the formation decisions are mostly cosmetic, although they usually provide prestige and permanent claims on their respective regions.

      It is possible for an Old World nation that has moved its capital to a colonial region to form these nations as well. However, if it does so it will lose all owned land and subjects in Africa, Asia and Europe and inherit its colonial nations. Any personal unions lost via this action will be regainable via a restoration of union CB that is granted upon loss of the old world subject.

      Federation countries[edit | edit source]

      These countries can be formed by uniting a federation with the federation advancement “Federal Constitution”. Which country gets formed depends on the primary culture of the federation leader (in parenthesis).

      • Creek (Creek)
      • Huron (Huron)
      • Illiniwek (Illini)
      • Iroquois (Iroquois)
      • Potawatomi (Anishinabe)
      • Shawnee (Shawnee)
      • Sioux (Dakota)

      If the country already exists or the federation leader has a primary culture which is not in the list, a new federation tag with a custom name and flag is formed.

      Event countries[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.34.

      These nations can be formed and played as via special events.

      • Tibet mission event Meeting with the Khans: the country can become Khalkha, Khoshuud or Dzungar. Note that this changes primary culture and government type, but not national ideas or missions.
      • Teutonic Order event The End of the Teutonic State during the Prussian Confederation disaster: the country becomes Danzig.
      • When Pest is taken by the Ottomans, Hungary can become Transylvania in the fall of Hungary event.
      • Sunda mission event A New Capital, A New [Root.GovernmentName]: gives the choice to become either Banten, Cirebon or stay the current country.
      • Dai Viet Divided: splits up Dai Viet into Tonkin and Annam one of which can be played depending on previous decisions.
      • Civil war events for Lan Xang: allows to become Champasak, Luang Prabang or Vientiane in some circumstances.
      • Byzantium can become Morea in the event Ottomans Advance on Morea if the Ottomans control Constantinople.
      • Dali can become Zhou through the Qing mission The Three Feudatories.
      • Eranshahr can be formed by Persia after converting to Zoroastrian in the event [[Persian events#A [Root.Religion.GetName] Identity|A [Root.Religion.GetName] Identity]].

      Releasable countries[edit | edit source]

      These nations cause you to abandon your current nation to the AI to take over the newly spawned nation.

      • Habsan can be spawned and played as if a player meets the conditions to trigger the The Siddi state of Habsan event
      • / mission event The East India Company: after conquering a port province in the India subcontinent, a choice is given to possibly create either an AI controlled or player controlled East India Company.
      • Ashanti can be released in uncolonized provinces by a country bordering the provinces of Kumasi or Denkyira in the event The Ashanti.
      • Netherlands can be released by a country with the Dutch Revolt disaster in the event Netherlands Declare Independence!
      • Algiers can be released by Tunis in the event Hayreddin Barbarossa after completing the mission Sponsor Piracy.
      • Fulo can be released in uncolonized provinces by the owner of the province of Kantor in the event Emergence of the Fulani.
      • Shun, Dali, Yue, and Wu can be released by Ming during the Crisis of the Ming Dynasty disaster in the events Li Zicheng's Rebellion and the Shun Dynasty, Yunnan Protectorate, and Losing Control of the South, respectively.
      • Funj can be released in uncolonized provinces by any country that owns one of its cores in the event New state emerges in the Sudan!
      • Tungning can be released in uncolonized provinces by Ming in the event Koxinga Flees to Taiwan.
      • Zulu can be spawned and optionally played through an event after the Militarize the Nguni decision.
