      Policies

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
      At least some were last verified for version 1.30.

      Policies are a set of bonuses unlocked by completing idea groups. Each possible pair of ideas from different types (i.e. administrative and diplomatic, administrative and military, or diplomatic and military) unlocks a policy. Since there are 7 administrative, 7 diplomatic, and 11 military idea groups, there are 7 * 7 + 7 * 11 + 7 * 11 = 203 different policies. Since nations may have up to 8 idea groups, they can have up to 21 possible policies available.

      Policies are visible when the requisite idea groups are chosen, but cannot be activated until both idea groups have been completed.

      Once activated, a policy cannot be deactivated for ten years.

      Possible policies[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.30.

      A nation may only have up to 3 policies active in each monarch point type i.e. 3 military, 3 administrative and 3 diplomatic. This limit can be modified by the following modifiers.

      Free policies[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.30.

      The first policy of each type is free. The subsequent policies cost 1 monarch point of the appropriate type each month.

      General modifiers[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Dharma DLC enabled.

      Additional free policies can be gained through the following:

      Conditions
      +1 with ‘Autonomous Swiss Cantons’ government reform (tier 1 republic and tier 1 theocracy)
      with ‘The Two-Chamber System’ government reform (tier 5 monarchy and tier 6 republic)
      with ‘Church and State’ government reform (tier 7 theocracy)
      with Innovative Idea 7 'Expanded Policies'

      Note: Without ‘Dharma’ DLC these modifiers are replaced by −10% advisor cost per free policy.
