      Japan missions

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

      		Available only with the Domination DLC enabled.

      This is a list of all missions for Japan[1]. For missions of Daimyos and the Shogun see ”Japanese missions”.

      The following mission branches can be unlocked by:

      • Christian/non-Christian branch: Completing ”A New Buddha?” mission depending on the outcome of the ”Spread of Christianity” Shinto incident.
      • ”Port of Dejima”: Completing the ”Nanban Trade” mission depending on the outcome of the ”Nanban Trade”' Shinto incident.
      • Army/Navy branch: Completing the ”Bushido” mission triggers an event which gives the choice between an army or navy focus which unlocks the respective branching mission.
      • Japanese version of the Emperor of China missions: Added to the bottom of the tree when becoming the Emperor of China.

      Focus on Japan missions[edit | edit source]

      Trade missions[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Balance the Shinokosho
      Japanese society has traditionally been organized around the existence of four groups of people: warriors, farmers, artisans and merchants. Maintaining this structure in a well-balanced manner will bring great stability to our nation.
      		 Japan:
      • Has at least +2 stability
      • Has at least 60% clergy loyalty, if they exist
      • Has at least 60% burghers loyalty, if they exist
      • Has at least 60% nobility loyalty, if they exist
      • Has no province controlled by rebels
      		 Japan:
      • Gains “Stability of the Edo Period” until the end of the current age, giving:
        • +10% All estates' loyalty equilibrium
        • No stability loss on monarch death
        • −1 National unrest
      Nanban Trade
      The arrival of westerners to our shores have clearly shown the importance of trade. Increasing our market capabilities is sure to bring us great profit.
      		 Japan:
      • If is Shinto:
        • Has concluded the ”Nanban Trade” incident
      • Owns at least 8 provinces with any Trade building
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least 5 ruler diplomatic skill
        • Has the 'Entrepreneur' ruler personality trait
        • Has completed the Trade ideas
        • Gains at least 12 diplomatic power per month
      		 Japan:
      • If completes the mission via the Open outcome:
        • Unlocks the 'Port of Dejima' mission
        • If has less than 3 ruler personality traits:
          • Gains the 'Scholar' ruler personality trait
        • Else:
          • Gains 1 ruler diplomatic skill[2]
        • Gains “Exchange of Ideas” for 25 years, giving:
          • −10% Technology cost
          • +25% Innovativeness gain
      • Else, if completes the mission via the Balanced outcome:
        • Unlocks the 'Port of Dejima' mission
        • Every owned province with a Trade building:
          • Gets their trade building upgraded
          • Gains 1 base production
      • Else, if completes the mission via the Isolationist outcome:
        • Every owned province in Japan (region):
          • Gains “Enforced Sakoku” for the rest of the game, giving:
            • +1 Possible number of manufactories
            • −5% Local development cost
      • Else, if we do not have access to the Nanban Trade incident:
        • Gains “Mercantile Growth” for 30 years, giving:
          • +10% Trade efficiency
          • −10% Diplomatic technology cost
      		 Balance the Shinokosho
      Expand the Gokaido

      		 Japan:
      • Has at least 8 government reforms
      • Owns the following province which have been developed at least 5 times and have at least 3 buildings:
        • Kyoto (1020)
        • Mino (1835)
        • Shinano (4188)
        • Kozuke (1843)
        • Musashi (1028)
        • Ise (4359)

        • Mikawa (4360)
        • Suruga (1839)
        • Sagami (4189)
        • Kai (1029)
        • Rikuzen (1026)
        • Shimotsuke (4191)
      		 Japan:
      • Gains 50 government reform progress
      • Enacts the “Gokaido Reform“ Tier 8 monarchy reform
      • If has less than 3 ruler personality traits:
        • Gains the 'Great Engineer' ruler personality trait
      • Else:
        • Gains 1 ruler administrative skill[3]
      		 Nanban Trade

      		 Japan:
      • Owns province Hizen (4182):
