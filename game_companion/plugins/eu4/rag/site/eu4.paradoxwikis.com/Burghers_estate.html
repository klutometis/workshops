      The Burghers estate represents the urban population of your country such as artisans, merchants or others that reside inside city walls. Their traditional responsibility is to work to keep the economy going and in order to do so efficiently they expect to be granted special rights and privileges for themselves and the cities they represent.

      Availability[edit | edit source]

      General availability:

      • This estate belongs to the base estates group which are available to all nations unless supplemented/replaced by special estate groups added via expansions.

      Specific conditions:

      • Does not use other mechanics
      • Does not have the Vaishyas or Jains estates
      • Is not nomad
      • Has enacted the "Empower the Quartermasters" Reform if country is Pirate Republic
      • Has enacted the "Reestablish the Burgher class" Reform if country is Revolutionary Empire
