Advisor events[1] are one-time events which may fire when a country has a level 3 advisor hired. Each type of advisor has a specific advisor event. Every event triggers only once for a country.

List of advisor eventsEdit

All advisor events are broadly similar, as they provide an option to choose between   stability and   prestige. Except for   the philosopher, both options will also add a country modifier which has an effect related to the advisor's bonus.

In the game, the advisor's name is used as the event's title and in place of the $ADVISORTYPE$ in the text. $MONARCH$ is replaced with the current ruler's name.

 $PHILOSOPHER$

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.32.

$PHILOSOPHER$ must certainly be one of the foremost philosophers of our time. He/She is probably best known for his/her philosophical empiricism and skepticism. He/She have concluded that no theory of reality is possible - there can be no knowledge of anything beyond experience.

 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has a philosopher of level 3 as advisor.
  • did not have this event so far.
 Mean time to happen

300 months

Modifiers

The country:

  • has at least 4 innovative ideas: ×0.9
  • has at least 6 innovative ideas: ×0.9
  • has less than 4 innovative ideas: ×1.1
  • has less than 2 innovative ideas: ×1.1
 
His/Her efforts will be admired throughout the nation
If the country has a stability of less than +3,
  • then it gains   1 stability,
  • else it gains   50 administrative power.

If this country is the Emperor of China,

  • then it gains  5 meritocracy.
 
His/Her reputation will reach further than our borders

The country:

  • gains   50 prestige.

If this country is the Emperor of China,

  • then it gains  5 meritocracy.

To our enemies the effort we put into defending our fortresses seems cowardly. However $FORTIFICATION_EXPERT$ gets the last laugh as attrition slowly eats our enemies away while we sit snugly inside our fortresses.

 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has a military engineer of level 3 as advisor.
  • did not have this event so far.
 Mean time to happen

300 months

Modifiers

The country:

  • has at least 4 innovative ideas: ×0.9
  • has at least 6 innovative ideas: ×0.9
  • has less than 4 innovative ideas: ×1.1
  • has less than 2 innovative ideas: ×1.1
 
His/Her efforts will be admired throughout the nation

The country gets:

  • the modifier “Great defensive thinking” for 6 years giving the following effect:
    •   +20% fort defense,
    •   −25% fort maintenance.
If the country has a stability of less than +3,
  • then it gains   1 stability,
  • else it gains   50 administrative power.

If this country is the Emperor of China,

  • then it gains  5 meritocracy.
 
His/Her reputation will reach further than our borders

The country:

  • gains   50 prestige.
  • gets the modifier “Great defensive thinking” for 6 years giving the following effect:
    •   +20% fort defense,
    •   −25% fort maintenance.

If this country is the Emperor of China,

  • then it gains  5 meritocracy.

 $INQUISITOR$

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.32.

Always knowing just the right questions to ask, $INQUISITOR$ strikes fear into the hearts of those that would spy upon us. $INQUISITOR$’s presence alone is enough to plant the seed of failure before the mission has even begun.

 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has an inquisitor of level 3 as advisor.
  • did not have this event so far.
 Mean time to happen

300 months

Modifiers

The country:

  • has at least 4 innovative ideas: ×0.9
  • has at least 6 innovative ideas: ×0.9
  • has less than 4 innovative ideas: ×1.1
  • has less than 2 innovative ideas: ×1.1
 
His/Her efforts will be admired throughout the nation

The country gets:

  • the modifier “National security” for 6 years giving the following effect:
    •   +10% foreign spy detection.
If the country has a stability of less than +3,
  • then it gains   1 stability,
  • else it gains   50 administrative power.

If this country is the Emperor of China,

  • then it gains  5 meritocracy.
 
His/Her reputation will reach further than our borders

The country:

  • gains   50 prestige.
  • gets the modifier “National security” for 6 years giving the following effect:
    •   +10% foreign spy detection.

If this country is the Emperor of China,

  • then it gains  5 meritocracy.

AI behaviourEdit

An AI country chooses:

  • if its stability is less than 1:
    •  
      His/Her efforts will be admired throughout the nation
  • if its stability is greater than 1:
    •  
      His/Her reputation will reach further than our borders
  • if its stability is 1:
    • then the chance for both options is 50%.
