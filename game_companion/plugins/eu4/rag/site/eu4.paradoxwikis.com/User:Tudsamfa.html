      Weird Oddities

      • The AI considers Amsterdam Bourse to be +350 Reason to sell Amsterdam, as opposed to the -350 Reason for Dutch polders, making Amsterdam as easily buyable as any other province
      • The "Unite the Hansa" Mission of Bremen and Hamburg (the "Queen of the Hansa" mission of Lübeck probably as well) only checks that you are in a league, not that you lead the league. If you are a one province great power, you can vassalize your trade leader.
      • The "crowning of a new league" event is bugged, not only do you do have access to option 1 if you previously were Hamburg (in my run at least, I got renamed to hanseatic league before forming the hanseatic league, but I also counted as hamburg to get the bonuses in option 2), but if you then get new traditions and ambitions, you get the generic German ideas.
      • Raiding coasts can be done from unfinished colonies. You can extend your raiding range by colonizing a province for 1 month, then abandoning it. This works better with close by colonies like in Indonesia.

      -90% Idea costs

      My favourite run at the moment. But way too slow and methodical to play in most situations.

      Things I have noticed because of this run, but couldnt quite figure out where to mention:

      • Removing Courts idea 7 "Monarch +1 diplomatic skill" while an event that chooses a new ruler is up (states general) can both increase and decrease said rulers' diplomatic power, but the number not rerolled every time the idea is dis-/enabled: the ruler(s) stat flip-flops between the one calculated with and the one without the +1, but the rng is already set in stone.
      • English Culture provinces in Asia can have east india company rebels which can create east india company cores, even without domination
      • Having the option to "separate clergy from state" can be exploited to sell infinite monopolies if you enable/disable religious ideas
      • You can force the game to disable government reforms without time passing by spawning events like "invite religious scholar"
      • There is a cap on how much fabricating a claim can cost, always between 2 and 90 spy network. I assumed that the "claim state" interaction then costs 12 at minimum, but its 10, no idea if 90 remains the max or if it's 100.
      • Spy action modifier is applied after these caps. So with -20%, fabricating a claim costs between 1.6 and 72.

      Livonian Order

      Enjoying a nice playthrough with the bois, where I almost quit for being reduced to 3 provinces - luckily I bounced back. I then found out that if Riga is already part of the HRE (like if riga advances their missions while you are in russian exile), when you complete the mission "make riga our capital" that you join the HRE - even if you are empire rank yourself. You also stay Empire Rank...until you reload the save.

      Too many Bureaus

      Wanted to play a chill game as EoC Korea, but got felt the need to culture shift and form yuan. Then I realized that all the Emperor of China Missions can be done multiple times. I assume the Country and province modifiers work as intended and do not stack, but a fair few have multiple rewards depending on how they are finished, and anything that is not a permanent modifier still benefits you. This is a theorhetical list of what could be done with this.

      • You can unlock the EoC missions at least 3 times by switching from Korea to Manchu to another tag and then to Yuan
      • You can unlock all 3 Bureaus from the Form the Depots
      • Cornerstone of an Empire can permanently power up the decree "Expand palace beureaucracy" once to give 20% advisor cost. Otherwise, temporary boni:
      • The Censorate +0,025 monthly Mandate is the best out of these temporary options
      • Reform Civil Registration has 2 different permanent outcomes, powering up decree "population census" or +0.1 reform per month while all estates gain permanent influence. Also, improves current ruler
      • Examination System was not well documented. There were a lot of interestig modifiers listed on Manchu's mission page, but it's only Keju we can get from this.
      • Mutual_Trade_System should only stack better tributairies once, but merchant influx from allies is temporary and could be done multiple times.
      • The Jing Hang Grand Canal Will give you more great architects and increse base producion further
      • Outer City of 1816.GetName Should stack once with and once without the manufactury bonus, giving + 4 fort level in total...
      • Pure Land could upgrade confucius palace on it's own.
      • Yongle Dadian could upgrade newly acquired paper, but doesn't stack
      • Seongse & Chise while the big all power cost reduction shouldn't stack, I believe the power projection and the extended golden era do
      • Grand Coordinators,Single Whip Law, Tame China's Sorrow, Expand the Bogue, Fortify the Coast shouldnt stack

      Trade Protectorate Stacking

      I definitely underutilized Trading cities as Oman, should have used them as colonizers, but found a good use for them in late game anyways. After confirming Thalassocracy, you can create them on a different continent, offer to make them a trade protectorate instead, and then create another trade city (you could do this with client states as well, I suppose). Anyways, after giving up your 20 worst provinces, you have +100% global trade power and +40% goods produced. The trade power can at that point completely negate any malus from collecting outside the home trade node, just send your good Omani trade fleet to the English channel, place a merchant there and profit (Maybe charter some trade companies first so they don't sink to attrition).

      More Notes on trade protectorates I don't know where to put.

      • Trade Protectorates are obviously better as many small ones
      • As there is no truce, and with diplomatic ideas no penalty, upon cancelling, you can just conquer your protectorate after it's no longer useful
      • There is no relation penalty for mistreating your protectorates except for involving them in your wars, but relation is all they care about. You can enforce religion on them, have them break the overlordship (or break it yourself with diplomatic ideas), and then there is still a good chance they will accept your protectorate right after. the -100 liberty desire just vanishes this way
