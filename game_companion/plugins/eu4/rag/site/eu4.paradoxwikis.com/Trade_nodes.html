      Trade nodes

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

      See also: Trade

      Trade nodes are 80 static "locations" on the map where trade can be conducted utilizing merchants. The province containing a trade node must be discovered before merchants can be sent there, but trade nodes are not otherwise tied to specific provinces. Instead, nodes are tied to a collective of provinces, each contributing its trade value to determine the overall local wealth generated for the node. The node value can be modified by improving base production wealth, trade goods price changes, constructing manufactories or steering trade to and from the node.

      As a general rule, controlling more provinces in a node helps in gaining high trade power within that node, although this could also be achieved by controlling only provinces with trade modifiers (i.e. estuary or center of trade). Provinces which are added to states or trade companies are more useful for trade power due to their low autonomy floor. Other means of increasing trade power involve sending merchants or a fleet of light ships to the node. Picking certain idea groups or opportunities to increase mercantilism will improve the effectiveness of taking land for its trade power.
