      The Ottomans in 1444 are a regional power, in the process of securing control over Anatolia and the Balkans. In the east, Ottoman control has been reimposed in the aftermath of the Timurid invasion, but several beyliks remain independent. These Beys jockey for a position between the expanding power of the Ottomans on one side, and the Mamluk Empire of Egypt and Syria, still the strongest state in the Middle East, on the other.

      In Europe, the Ottomans have defeated the Crusaders in the Battle of Varna, killing Wladyslaw, the Polish and Hungarian king. Byzantium now lies exposed and Hungary has been forced on the defensive, but with the fortress of Belgrade, still serves as a strong bulwark against further Ottoman expansion. The lesser princes in between can survive only by playing the great powers off one another. Further south, Skanderbeg leads Albania in revolt against the Ottomans, supported by Venice, which still dominates the seas.

      The realm ruled by the Ottoman dynasty is not yet a centralized state in 1444. The sultan's authority is enforced only by his small household of servants, recruited from slaves captured in war and from the 'devshirme', the periodic conscription of Christian youths from the Balkan countryside. These are the famous Janissaries, who will in time grow into a powerful standing army. The Ottomans have come to power with the military backing of numerous marcher-lords, still highly autonomous on the Balkan frontier, while the administration lies in the hands of the Turkish aristocracy, literate in the ways of statecraft, but largely independent of the sultan's authority.

      All this is about to change. Having defeated the Crusader armies, the old sultan Murad II is now on his way back to his estates in Manisa, leaving the government in the hands of the young Mehmed II. It is he who will go on to transform the Ottoman state into an empire, acquiring Constantinople as an imperial capital and establishing a centralized administration to rein in the marcher-lords and old Turkish families. For 'The Grand Turk', there can be no compromise – the House of Osman is to be the supreme authority in the land. The only question is this: where will his armies turn to first?

      Europa Universalis IV starts on November 11th 1444, the day after the Ottomans victory over the Christian alliance at Varna. They begin owning most of Anatolia and the southern Balkans. For new players, the early game will likely be focused on consolidating the country's position in those regions. Internally, the country is divided between the Orthodox and Sunni faiths. Economically, they hold the majority of the Constantinople node trade power with very easy access to the rest.

      In the Balkans, the important trade center of Constantinople and the region of Morea are held by the declining nation of Byzantium and its vassal, Athens. The Aegean Sea is controlled mainly by Venice and Genoa hindering control of the Constantinople trade node. The Anatolian region is a home to several Turkish Beyliks. Further east, in the Taurus and Caucasus mountains, the tribes of Aq Qoyunlu and Qara Qoyunlu control some outlying Ottoman territory, and shield the Ottomans from their historical rival, the Timurids. To the south, historical and trade rival the Mamluks control a sprawling trade empire that extends into Africa, while their vassal Hejaz controls the holy cities of Medina and Mecca. To the west, the Balkan nations are ripe for the taking - Moldavia is the only safe nation due to it being guaranteed by Poland which blocks expansion. Further expansion into the west is halted by Austria and Hungary, while across the Adriatic, Italy is mostly protected by the Holy Roman Emperor and Aragon.

      The Ottomans have a unique government type named the Ottoman Government. This government is in many ways synonymous with the royal household, but with a harem feature that ensures that there will never be a lack of heirs for the Ottoman ruler. The Ottomans follow the Hanafi school of Islam.

      Ottoman Government[edit | edit source]

      The Ottoman Government is a unique government type with fixed dynasty and the following benefits:

      • +0.5 Yearly legitimacy
      • −10% Unjustified demands
      • +150 Governing capacity
      • +5 Maximum absolutism

      This government is permanently lost if the country is no longer Muslim or if the government type is changed during the Revolution disaster or by revolutionary rebels enforcing their demands.

      Harem[edit | edit source]

      The harem helps ensure that there will never be a lack of heirs for the empire. An event triggers if the current ruler has reached 30 years of age and has no heir which allows the player choose an heir for the throne from a random list of three choices, being able to view their monarch stats prior to the choice.

      Pashas[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Cradle of Civilization DLC enabled.

      This government may assign a pasha to one of its states from the state interface, and the following modifiers will be applied to each province in this state:

      −20% Local governing cost
      +20% Minimum local autonomy
      −33% State maintenance
      −0.1% Local monthly autonomy
      +10% Local tolerance of heathens

      Removing the Pasha causes all the provinces in the state to suffer a +5 local unrest penalty for 10 years.

      Janissaries[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Cradle of Civilization DLC or the Domination DLC enabled.

      Janissaries are a special unit category available only to countries with the Ottoman Government, Modernized Ottoman Government, Reorganized Ottoman Government, Sultanate of Rûm, Modernized Turkoman Federation and Janissary Stratocracy government types. You can also get 10 Janissary units as a reward from Persian mission "End the Turkish Threat" or the Hisn Kayfan mission "A Sublime Alliance".

      Janissaries are displayed with a light green background in the army view.

      They are infantry troops.

      Their modifiers are :

      −10% Fire damage received
      −10% Shock damage received
      +50% Army drill gain modifier
      +50% Assault fort ability
      +100% Reinforcement cost

      Janissaries can be recruited using the Macro-builder or from the state interface of states. This is only possible in states that have provinces that are heathen.

      They costs 3 military power per regiment raised but cost neither manpower nor money to recruit.

      They start with 10% Morale and 10% Regiment strength.

      Units available[edit | edit source]

      The number of Janissaries available to a country is a percentage of the force limit equal to the percentage of heathen province development of the country. So if 50% of the total development of a country is made up of heathen provinces, it can recruit up to 50% of its force limit as Janissaries.

      ( force limit ⋅ heathen development total development ) ⋅ ( 100 % + special force limit ) {\displaystyle ({\text{force limit}}\cdot {\frac {\text{heathen development}}{\text{total development}}})\cdot (100\%+{\text{special force limit}})}

      Janissary Stratocracies also base their Janissary limit on true faith provinces:

      ( force limit ⋅ heathen + true faith development total development ) ⋅ ( 100 % + special force limit ) {\displaystyle ({\text{force limit}}\cdot {\frac {\text{heathen + true faith development}}{\text{total development}}})\cdot (100\%+{\text{special force limit}})}

      See above for a list of all possible modifiers that increase special unit forcelimit.

      Eyalets[edit | edit source]

      Main article: Subject_nation#Eyalet
      Available only with the Domination DLC enabled.

      Eyalets are special subjects available to the Ottomans and Rûm.

      Generic Eyalets[edit | edit source]

      Normal Vassals can be turned into Eyalets via the "Promote to Eyalet" diplomatic action. Additionally, the "Ottoman Campaign of Conquest" Casus Belli enables the "Establish Eyalet" peace treaty, which will turn the target nation into a Eyalet subject. This CB can be gained by The "Justify Invasion" covert action against all nations, as well as via Missions against certain nations.

      Event Eyalets[edit | edit source]

      After conquering certain territories, special events will fire which allow the Ottomans to turn these territories into a special subject called a "Eyalet".

      The event will always have at least two options, to either release an Eyalet or to keep the land for yourself.

      Eyalets that are released this way will keep all the cores of the nation they are released as, aswell as gaining cores on all the provinces they get released with. They do not gain cores on any provinces the nation does not currently own, even though the tooltip says so.

      Missions[edit | edit source]

      Main article: Ottoman missions

      Practically all of the Ottomans missions revolve around expanding their borders. In the early stages of the game, many of the missions revolve around reclaiming the lands of Anatolia, as well as the iconic mission to conquer the City of the World's Desire: Constantinople or Conquer the Crusader's Last Bastion (Rhodes). Further missions will request the Ottoman Empire to expand and conquer the regions of Balkans, North Africa, Syria, Egypt, Iraq, as well as individual provinces - all to increase trading power in the spice trade and to unite lands against Christendom.

      Events[edit | edit source]

      Main article: Ottoman events

      Many historical events are the aftermath from the expansion and government reforms of the Ottoman Empire - such events can revolve around the relationship between the Ottoman Empire and the trade states of Venice and Genoa, the left-over Greek Orthodox Patriarchate, enacting reforms that might not be popular with everyone, or suppressing nationalistic rebels.

      Strategy[edit | edit source]

      The below is one of many player suggested strategies for Ottomans. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

      Overview[edit | edit source]

      The Ottomans begin the game in 1444 in the Anatolian military technology group at level 3 for Administration, Diplomacy and Military with Feudalism embraced. If you rush to Military level 5, Ottomans should have the strongest infantry units, comparing to Western, Muslim and Eastern units.

      Because they have very strong missions, easy avenues of expansion and some very good bonuses, events and decisions, they usually become the strongest and the most developed nation in the world very fast. At this stage the question becomes how big can the Ottomans grow. Other than Austria, Rivals may include Commonwealth, Russia, Great Britain, France, Ming and Spain.

      • 60% Ratio. As a Sunni nation, Cavalry numbers may be up to 60% of a total army size, excluding artillery, before the Insufficient Support penalty is incurred. Note that this will be reduced to the standard 50% Cavalry ratio upon conversion to another religion.
      • Anatolian units: see Anatolian land units. Early game Anatolian units (up to military tech 15) are superior to Western by 1 pip. Notably, Janissaries (infantry at military tech 9) have 1 offensive fire pip; the only other tech groups to get fire pips this early are Indian and Chinese (who have a much harder time embracing institutions). Conversely, Anatolian cavalry has no fire pips until tech 18, compared to 10 for Western. However, at higher techs cavalry has very little use, so it might be best to simply cut the cav out of your armies in favor of infantry, Janissary infantry, and of course more cannons.

      Before Starting the Timer[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Domination DLC enabled.

      When starting a new game with the Ottomans, you should hire ten Janissary regiments (costing 3 military mana each), get a general, give out al least two privileges to the new Janisaries estate ("Sultan Appointed Aghas" and "Ensure Disciplined Training" are recommended) and build as many galleys as you can to kicksart your mission tree and unlock three missions from the get-go and open two more immediately. Only three galleys are enough to unlock the Ottoman Navy mission, but it is recommended to build more, since you will need them against naval superpowers like Venice, Aragon and Castile.

      You will start with an available cheaper level two Natural Scientist ( administrative advisor), Ali al-Qushji, so hire him. You are free to hire a diplomatic advisor too, preferably a Diplomat or a Statesman, but do not hire a military advisor at the start, since an early event will make the cheaper level two Commandant Urban available. Hire him as soon as the event happens, and you will get four cannons for free from the unlocked mission. Keep in mind that Ottomans and France are the only two nations that can get cannons before Military technology 7.

      Not changing religion and staying Sunni is strongly recommended. Ottomans start with Dhimmi Autonomy privilege already granted at the start, so capturing non-Muslim land will not create religious disunity problems. Changing the religion will also lose you so many advantages of the Ottoman government, such as Janissaries, Dhimmi estate, pashas, eyalets, harem system and extra governing capacity.

      Alliances[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Domination DLC enabled.

      Although Ottomans are strong enough that they do not really need any allies, they also have lots of great choices in that regard. You should ally Crimea from the get-go, because if you complete the "Crimean Destiny" mission before "the Fate of the Crimean Khanate" event happens, they will become your march as a result of that event, instead of a tributary. Also, your guarantee on Ragusa is unnecessary; revoke it to open another relation slot. Because you are a Muslim nation, royal marriages are perfectly safe (you will not fall under a personal union) and you may use them to secure alliances with any Muslim nation.

      Bohemia is a good possible ally, who will join your wars against Austria, Hungary and Venice. If they are not rivaled to you, you can improve relations with Poland to ally them, although they usually pick Bohemia as a rival, so you may need to choose only one of them if that happens. France will often ally you and help your wars with strong nations like Castile, Aragon and Austria, but be careful not to ally them before they finish their war with England, becuase you want to make Constantinople your capital as soon as possible and you need to be at peace to take that decision. But it is recommended that you ally France as soon as possible after their war and to not let them become your rival. You may also ally Muscovy as soon as you discover them, although do not expect a loyal ally. You will later clash with them and they will become a formidable enemy after they form Russia.

      But none of these alliances are going to last, because you will start to desire their lands. Instead, it is much better to use your diplomatic relation slots for the nations you can diplomatically vassalize and promote to eyalets. Eyalets are better than vassals because they do not occupy diplomatic relation slots (unless there is a royal marriage) and they give bonus manpower and money. But keep in mind that promoting a vassal significantly stalls the annexation. So do not promote any vassals which might block your expansion avenues to eyalets (to annex eyalets, you need to turn them into core eyalets first and wait for another ten years after that), but annex such vassals as soon as the option becomes available.

      Early Moves[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Domination DLC enabled.

      When you start the game, wait for the "Crusade of Varna" event (which will also happen very quickly), which gives you permanent claims on Byzantium in addition to some bonuses through a mission. Attack Byzantium immmediately and full annex both Byzantium and Athens and white peace any allies in war. They will not get strong allies and you want to capture these allies' lands too, later. Do not core Constantinople/Kostantiniyye, as a very strong decision called "Make Kostantiniyye Your Capital", which will core it for free, turns it into a Sunni Turkish city and promotes you into an empire becomes available as soon as you end your war. Do not start any wars before taking that decision, because these bonuses are very important for you.

      After that war, I would recommend chaining a war against Epirus immediately after the war against Byzantium and annexing them too. This allows the player to unlock a mission which gives claims on all of the Balkans. You can also attack either Candar, Dulkadir and Ramazan to consolidate Anatolia and gain further claims. Since these are very weak nations which will not get any strong allies, the wars with Epirus and the Anatolian beyliks are easy enough to pull off at the same time.

      Fighting in two fronts is challenging for new players, but Ottomans are strong enough to do that. Therefore, a new player should focus on one war at a time. But you should try it out at some point, because trying challenging things is the best way to improve. Keep in mind that your number one limit will be the aggressive expansion (AE). During these battles and any of the following, be sure to assign at least one diplomat to improve relations with outraged countries to prevent a coalition. Do not take land from non-cobelligerents unless your strategy demands it (for example, to walk around strong alliances), and even then, not much. From cobelligerents, only take money, war reparations and/or prestige or just take a white peace (give or take nothing) for a short truce. And most importantly, try to spread out your AE. Alternate taking land from Christians in Europe and Muslims in Asia to spread out your AE. Make sure not to take too much for your self in order to keep the costs of coring down, and don't be afraid to make a vassal or two for reconquest wars.

      After that, wait for your truce with Karaman to expire and full annex them. After that, attack Serbia and full annex them. Serbia owns Kosovo/Kosova, which is gold mine and capturing it will improve your economy significantly. After that, Trebizond is a very easy conquest. If they are allied to Theodoro, call Crimea into your battle and feed it to them, since you will gain Theodoro region as a free core in the Crimean Succession event if they own it. If they are guaranteed by Aq Qoyunlu, take Urfa and Rakka from them and take nothing else. Rakka/ar-Raqqah is a core of Syria, which could be released to start a reconquest war against Mamluks. Since Aq Qoyunlu is usually allied with Ajam and guaranteed by Qara Qoyunlu at the start, they will be very hard to fight on their own. Don't use vassal-feed strategy if you don't plan to take influence idea group in early game since Ottomans' first national idea gives −25% core creation cost thus conquering those provinces are better than vassal-feed strategy.

      Alternatively, you may start building a spy network on Mamluks and fight them on their own (which will be easier to fight than Aq Qoyunlu and its allies/protectors). The war with the Mamluks wwill be your first big war either way. If you choose that path, just take the only province you claimed from the Mamluks, full money and war reparations, then release Syria from that province, then attack Cyprus immediately. Mamluks will come in to protect them and you want a white peace with them for a shorter truce. They will be easier to fight this time. You may conquer their lands in Syria directly for a faster game, but a Syrian reconquest would be easier for a newer player, since it a reconquest war will create less AE and you may take more lands for cheaper.

      Then your target should be Qara Qoyunlu itself. Take the Iraqi provinces until you get a border with Mushasha and make sure to get Baghdad too. Other than that, try to get as much as you can. White peace Mushasha (as you can guess, they will very soon be on the chopping list) and take only money from any other allies. Since they are a Shia nation, taking land from them will not cause too much AE. Expansion into the Caucasus region should be very easy also, and the player should either full annex or vassalize small Caucasian nations like Imereti, Georgia, Circassia, Shirvan and Gazikumukh

      Venice guarantees Albania, and although Ottomans has two cores on them, fighting Venice for only two provinces might not be worth it. Instead, after doing these wars and getting a larger army and navy and becoming more powerful, attack Venice directly and cobelligerent the The Knights. Their Balkan holdings are essential, otherwise Venice will steal the trade income from Constantinople and Alexandria. The player should be careful and have a bigger navy than Venice and its allies together. The player can also wait until Venice's rivals such as Austria and Hungary attack them. This makes them even easier to take down. You also want to full annex the The Knights to stop them from raiding your coasts and take as much land from Venice as your AE allows while removing the guarantee over Albania in order to attack them in a separate war. Alternatively, if Albania did not ally anybody strong like Hungary, declare your reconquest war during your war with Venice.

      Goals After Early Moves[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Domination DLC enabled.

      From here, the possibilities for the Ottomans are endless. Some players may try to Unify Islam, which involves conquering holy sites (provinces) and converting all of the player's provinces to Muslim. Some players may seek the "Definitely the Sultan of Rum" or "Parisian Pasha" achievements. They are not that difficult, even for a new player. All the player will need to do is to manage aggressive expansion well, keep up on tech and institutions, and keep expanding. The Ottoman mission tree provides a huge amount of permanent claims and many places are worth taking later on.

      After getting at least 6 provinces from Mamluks, you gain an invasion casus belli on them. No matter the case, always attack the Mamluks as soon as your truce expires. When you use the invasion CB, you need to siege down Cairo/Al-Qahirah and keep it sieged for two years. If you can get %99 warscore, they will automatically turn into an eyalet. Do not sign a peace them before the event which makes Mamluks your eyalet happens. Your missions will give you another invasion CB on Tunis, which is recommended to be used immediately if there is no truce. In that war, if they joined your war, vassalize Granada for a mission and white peace Morocco, against whom you will get a new invasion CB if you have successfully subjugated Tunis. Feed Fezzan's provinces to Tunis and build a marketplace in Tripoli in order to open a Tunisian mission which gives them (and in proxy, to their overlord, you) a free province in Sahara, which will help with your later expansion into Sahel region.

      All the nations in the Arabian peninsula will accept diplomatical vassalization except most probably Hormuz and Yemen, but you want a directly owned coast to the Indian Ocean for further expansion opportunities. You may want to attack a few coastal ones like Haasa and Hormuz to allow you further expansion, but try to diplo-vassalize poorer nations in the desert and promote them to eyalets as much as possible. Keep rivalries in mind when doing so and try to avoid royal marriages to not unnecessarily clog your diplo relation slots. If you or your subjects own enough land in the Arabian Peninsula, you will gain an invasion CB on Yemen.

      Genoa owns some of the Greek islands and a few provinces near what is today's southern Ukraine. You want their lands in these regions, but it is advised to fight them directly only after the Shadow Kingdom event happens, otherwise the Emperor of the HRE (usually Austria) will protect Genoa. Because the Black Sea provinces (Caffa/Kefe, Matrega/Hunkala and Azov, to be exact) will also become yours in the same Crimean Succesion event, if Genoa ever joins a war of yours, feel free to feed these regions to Crimea.

      A single successful war with Hungary ending by transferring their Balkan lands and possibly also transferring Croatia for yourself will unlock a mission which lets you make Hungary your eyalet. However, because Hungary usually tends to ally Austria, a strong European ally will probably be required for this war and any later wars which could invovle the Emperor. France, Poland, Bohemia or even Muscovy can decide to ally the Ottomans and help in the wars against the common enemy Austria. If Austria declares a Restoration of Union war, take that mission before Austria finishes that war, but this depends on RNG. If Austria wins after you take the mission, can make Austria your eyalet, instead of Hungary. Be careful not to take anything else in that war to keep the truce short and to be able to still make Hungary an eyalet. If you lose the invasion CB, you can use the old strategy against Hungary and release Transylvania, Nitra and Croatia as vassals from them for possible reconquests. If that does not happen, you make Hungary your eyalet and get claims on Austria's lands, which can be used to dismantle the HRE.

      It is possible to get Italian states after the The Shadow Kingdom events by either diplo-vassalizing small Italian states and conquering Naples if Aragon loses it. There is an early event which gives you a claim on Salento if Naples is freed. Owning land in Italy could get some easier institution process, as the colonization and printing press might be slow if the player conquer too much Asian or African lands. On the other hand, the lands in Europe are more highly developed and there are lots of nations of the same faith, so these lands create more AE and the player should not be as greedy when taking land there.

      After getting these possessions, the player will be much more powerful than any nation in the world and you may even go on to conquer the world. You will have many expansion routes now. You may attack Poland and Lithuania using your claims on Moldavia and Crimea's cores and release vassals like Galicia-Volhynia, Kiev and Chernigov to later make into eyalets, thus preventing a strong Commonwealth from appearing. You may attack Castile and Aragon early to prevent a strong Spain to challenge you later. You may expand into Great Horde and other European hordes early to attack Muscovy and stop them from forming Russia and colonizing Siberian lands, while also expanding into the Central Asia with the help of strong vassals like Kazakh and Sibir. You may expand further south towards the Eastern Africa through the Horn of Africa. Both Ethiopia and Kilwa start with tons of gold mines. But the most profitable area to expand into is into India through Persia. You will gain a great deal of trade and lots of merchants in Asia and Africa if you give any trade node east and/or south of Adana into trade companies. Be very careful not to give any gold mines to trade companies, though, and be careful not to block your own expansion paths when giving land to vassals and/or eyalets.

      Converting to Orthodox[edit | edit source]

      Ottomans can convert to Orthodox in several ways:

      • The quickest way occurs prior to annexing any territory by sending a missionary to an Orthodox province triggers enough unrest to create a possible rebellion. Ottomans are sufficiently Orthodox to be able to convert by accepting the rebels demands. However, if this is done the Ottomans lose out on the decision to make Constantinople the capital missing 11 development points. The upside is that Constantinople will not need to be converted.
      • A second way to convert and securing the extra 11 development from the make Constantinople the capital decision is by conquering Byzantium and at least two of the four nations of Athens and Albania, Theodoro, or Trebizond. After Constantinople is secured and the decision clicked sending a missionary to any Orthodox province with unrest below 0 will create a rebellion that will allow accepting demands to convert to Orthodox. Religious rebels generated in the province of Trebizond have the advantage of being able to force convert Anatolia while completing sieges on provinces. A rebel generated in Edirne or Kirkkilise are the closest to Constantinople and can force convert that province - which otherwise can not be converted until a combination of patriarch authority, an inquisitor and Religious ideas are taken.
      • Both methods should be done prior to the annexation of the Anatolian Beyliks as the extra provinces will reduce the percentage of Orthodox.

      Note, there are two handicaps to this as reduced religious unity incurs a corruption penalty and initially Orthodox are unable to convert Sunni provinces.

      Available only with the The Cossacks DLC enabled.

      If the Dhimmi estate has low loyalty, revoking the heathen provinces granted to them will spawn zealots of the province's religion. As soon as the game starts,the Impose New Religious Tax interaction from the Estate tab can be taken, reducing Dhimmi loyalty to 35. Many Orthodox provinces start out as belonging to the Dhimmi estate, and can be revoked after a year has passed. The Zealots spawned will convert every non-Orthodox province they occupy to Orthodox if the occupied province is not within a fort's Zone of Control, including Konstantiniyye after Make Constantinople the Capital decision has been taken, if the fort in Edirne is destroyed.. Zealots will not be able to cross the strait to Anatolia as there are almost always AI ships in the Sea of Marmara. They instead will march all the way around the Black Sea until they are defeated in battle by a nation along the way, or arrive back into Ottoman lands. The player can annex Trebizond and send a missionary to the province (as outlined above) after granting it to the Dhimmi estate, then revoke the estate ownership after a year if they wish to force convert Sunni Anatolia prior to switching to Orthodox.

      But staying Sunni is also an option, because you will lose the amazing Ottoman Government form if you do choose to convert.

      Converting to Coptic (outdated)[edit | edit source]

      Conversion to Coptic is done fastest by taking the 3 Coptic provinces Van, Yerevan and Nakhchivan from Qara Qoyunlu. Converting to Coptic removes the unique government type from Ottomans.

      Give those provinces to the Dhimmi estate, and spawn 3 Coptic rebel stacks by revoking those provinces a year later, after making the Dhimmi disloyal by asking for gold. However, all Coptic holy sites are easily conquered, so the Ottomans will get the benefit of the five Coptic blessings very early on. (Does not work in 1.30 onward, as estates were changed drastically.)

      Converting to Catholic[edit | edit source]

      Conversion to Catholic is also possible as the Ottomans. This can be done in an many ways however, the easier ways include securing either a Catholic province from Bosnia, Ragusa, Sayuda from the Mamluks or waiting for Athens to convert its province and then securing it. However, as the amount of Catholic is small relative to the empire it will take a several years of waiting as a rebel stack forcibly converts provinces. However, this can be shortened by utilizing Dhimmi revolts. Note, there are two handicaps to this as reduced religious unity incurs a corruption penalty and initially Catholics are unable to efficiently convert Sunni provinces.

      Form Holy Roman Empire (Outdated)[edit | edit source]

      After becoming Catholic the Ottomans can be elected and can gain a lot of Imperial authority by adding all of their European provinces to the HRE, and passing several reforms. Call for Reichsreform may already have been passed before the Ottomans become emperor, so Institute Reichsregiment and Reform the Hofgericht may be passable. The Imperial authority tick from all of the new provinces in the empire will also allow you to pass the following reforms quickly. (Not Possible after 1.30 Patch)

      Ideas (outdated)[edit | edit source]

      The choice of Ottomans are fairly diverse and can be approached in a number of ways. Given that the Ottomans can release and do re-conquest for Syria//Iraq and also can diplo-vassalize Fezzan and feed it north African land, taking Influence ideas that has reduced diplomatic annexation cost. Later Diplomatic ideas can be taken to more effectively reduce aggressive expansion by giving −20% province warscore cost and Improve Relations so any AE quickly cools down.

      Administrative ideas are recommended as the −25% core reduction cost can stack with the −20% from Kanunnames. The lowered cost and maintenance of mercenaries will help the Ottomans to field a large force of mercenaries.

      If the Ottomans choose to go Orthodox, Coptic, or Catholic, Religious ideas may be a good choice as Deus Vult yields the Holy War casus belli making the acquisition of territories east or south of the Ottomans free from the use of diplomatic power. This is especially strong with the Coptic faith as it will yield even more coring cost reduction, and a casus belli with nearly all neighbors (among other bonuses). Administrative ideas may be required to manage the administrative power cost of coring all that land. The bonus +1 missionary, +3% missionary strength and +2% missionary strength vs heretics will be of great help in maintaining Ottomans' religious unity.

      If Ottomans stay Sunni, Humanist idea group is usually the best option for handling rebels and fits well with an aggressive conquest style where Ottoman don't have time to convert all heathen provinces. The Ottoman traditions gives a +3 tolerance of heathens you can stack this with the +2 tolerance of heathens in this idea group. With that, you don't have any penalty to religious unity in heathen provinces. It also gives provinces for the Dhimmi estate, which can be quite lucrative.

      It has the be noted however that a military idea group isn't important in the early game because of the Anatolian tech group, which gives The Ottomans extra unit pips compared to the Western nations. In the late game, at military tech 19 (for infantry) and tech 18 (for cavalry) The Ottoman units will have less unit pips compared to the western tech group. Ottomans should get more military idea for further aggression.

      Offensive ideas shine with good generals and siege ability with Ottomans' tendency to often be at war and having high military tradition Aristocratic idea with Anatolian troops could be strong early game, helpful in putting military points into other areas, such as extra manpower, extra diplomat to annex vassals, Monthly autonomy change to counterbalance Lighten Dhimmi Tax Burden, keeping military traditions high and getting an extra siege pip are very good to keep the Ottomans as an unbeatable military force. Quantity idea could solve later on manpower problem while Quality idea solves the each single battle.
