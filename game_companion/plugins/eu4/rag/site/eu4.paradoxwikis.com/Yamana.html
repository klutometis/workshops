      The rise of the Yamana

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.30.

      Few have profited more than us from the fall of the Akamatsu clan after their assassination of Shogun Ashikaga Yoshinori. As the Akamatsu were stripped of their important lands and belongings many of them were given to reward our loyalty, and as a sign that we have atoned for the rebellion of 1391. This sudden rise to power has made some suspicious however. The Hosokawa especially view our influence as troubling and might well act to limit our influence in the future.

      Unite Japan!

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

      The time has come to unite Japan by force! The time for diplomacy is over and all these warring daimyo lords must be conquered.

       
      Potential requirements

      The country:

      • was never an end-game nation.
      • has enacted the ‘Shogunate’ government reform.
      • has at least 1 subject daimyo.
      		 Allow

      Japan does not exist.
      The country:

      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not at war.
      • is not a nomad nation.
      • owns at least 25 cities.
      • owns Kyoto (1020) and Musashi (1028).
      Effects

      The country:

      • becomes Japan.
      • gets new missions.
      • gets the event ‘New Traditions & Ambitions’, unless it is a custom nation.
      • becomes an empire.
      • abolishes the ‘Shogunate’ government reform.
      • loses its cores on every provinces owned by the subject daimyos of the country.
      • gains a permanent claim on over province in the Japan region, not owned by themselfes or their subjects
      • if Domination is active :
        • Gain country modifier “New Era Samurai” for the rest of the game, giving the following effects:
          • Country allows samurai infantry
          • +10% Samurai force limit

      All subject daimyos of the country:

      • become independent.
      • abolish the ‘Daimyo’ government reform.
      The AI will not take this decision with less than 30 cities.

      Strategy[edit | edit source]

      The below is one of many player suggested strategies for Yamana. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

      Yamana starts in 1444 as one of the strongest Daimyo , and it is a fine candidate for uniting Japan.

      Early game

      Your first goal should be consolidating he south of Japan. The key to victory is balance between aggressive expansion, conquest, and switching alliances. The first conquest should be Amago to connect your lands and your main rival Hosokawa. To do this search for their rivals and ally them. Take any loans or hire any mercenaries you need and going vastly over Force Limit just to beat enemy. Exploit "Indebted to the Bourgeoisie" privilege from the Burghers, you can remove the privilege and reinstate it again.

      Further expansion

      After securing the south you should rival stronger clans on the north. Depending on yours army strength you can first beat other rivals or taking Kioto. After uniting Japan expand in Korea and Ming. At the same time colonize as much Syberia and Indochina you can, which will provides you more manpower and money.

      Recommended Idea Groups

      Yamana has very good traditions and national ideas. Beginning with +20% National manpower modifier and +1 Diplomatic reputation, Yamana has an advantage over its neighbors.

      If player’s goal is uniting Japan as quick as possible recommended ideas are military ideas such as Defensive, Aristocratic or Quantity.

      First 3 ideas, depending on monarch points and situation

      If player thinks about arranging ideas in a way that will give you bonuses in further game and after uniting Japan recommended ideas are

      Innovative, Exploration, Quantity. It is also recommended to take Economic, Quality, Trade, Offensive, Maritime.
