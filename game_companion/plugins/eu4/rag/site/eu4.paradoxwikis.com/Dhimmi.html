      Originally a term reserved for Christians and Jews the Dhimmi are the non-Muslim subjects in a Muslim realm. Their status and privileges can vary greatly between different states as do both their organization and numbers. Traditionally it is the responsibility of a just ruler to protect these people while keeping them apart from the Muslim majority. These principles are not always as easy to keep in parts of the world where the Dhimmi may constitute the majority of the population themselves however.

      Availability[edit | edit source]

      General availability:

      • Available to any Muslim nation that does not have the Brahmins estate (mostly those with Indian tech group), or that has enacted the decision Establish the Dhimmi Estate

      Specific conditions:

      • Does not use other mechanics
      • Is not nomad
      • Does not have Celestial Empire government reform

      Influence[edit | edit source]

      Influence – The estate has a base influence of 0%, modified by:

      • +20% Has a non-Muslim dominant religion
      • +10% Has a province with non-Muslim religion
      • +5% Has enacted the Livonian Elective Monarchy tier 1 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the Expand Royal Court tier 3 government reform
      • +10% Has enacted the "Strengthen the Dhimmi" tier 4 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the General Estates tier 6 government reform
      • −5% Has enacted the Royal Fovoritism tier 3 government reform
      • −10% Has enacted the "Strengthen the Ulema" tier 4 government reform
      • -15% Has enacted the Abolish the Castes tier 4 government reform
      • −10% Has enacted the Dynastic Administration tier 5 government reform
      • −5% Has enacted the Subservient Bureaucrats tier 5 government reform
      • −5% Has enacted the Les Six livres de la République tier 8 government reform
      • −40% If another estate has seized control of the nation (via disaster)

      They gain half as much influence from land share (0.25:1 ratio).

      Loyalty[edit | edit source]

      Loyalty – The estate's loyalty equilibrium is modified by:

      • +10% Has completed the Humanist idea group
      • +10% While not being at war with heathens
      • +10% For Corruption at 100% (scaled)
      • +5% Has enacted the Decentralized Bureaucracy tier 3 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the Maintain Balance of power tier 4 government reform
      • +10% Has enacted the "Strengthen the Dhimmi" tier 4 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the Tribal Unity tier 5 government reform
      • +10% Has enacted the General Estates tier 6 government reform
      • +10% Has enacted the Supremacy of the Diet tier 6 government reform
      • -5% Has enacted the Centralized Bureaucracy tier 3 government reform
      • −10% Has enacted the "Strengthen the Ulema" tier 4 government reform
      • -5% to +5% with current Prestige
      • -10% to +10% with current Piety (inversely proportional) (doesnt scale negatively if the Timurids "Sufi Support" mission was completed)
      • −20% If another estate has seized control of the nation (via disaster)

      Rebels: If the Dhimmi are disloyal after land has been seized, Particularist rebels may rise in revolt in a few provinces.

      Disaster: If the Dhimmi estate reaches 100 influence, their estate disaster will begin ticking and - unless lowered - will eventually trigger the Dhimmi Independence disaster.
