      March

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
      At least some were last verified for version 1.25.

      See also: Subject_nation#March

      A march is a military orientated subject nation, which receives bonuses to its army, navy and forts at a cost of paying no taxes to its overlord and being more difficult to annex. Marches are more helpful subjects in wars, and can make an excellent buffer state between an overlord and its enemies.

      Benefits of a march[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.25.

      A march doesn't pay taxes to their suzerain and cannot be annexed as long as they are designated a march. In exchange, they have −15% Liberty desire and receive the following military bonuses:

      Military bonuses[edit | edit source]

      +25% National manpower modifier
      +30% Land force limit modifier
      +30% Naval force limit modifier
      +20% Fort defense
      −20% Fort maintenance
      +20% Manpower recovery speed
      −20% Land maintenance modifier
      −20% Naval maintenance modifier

      A march only receives these bonuses as long as it has less than 25% of the development of its overlord.

      If its overlord has enacted the T4 government reform Fiqh Laws (available for Ottomans and Rûm) and is of same religion:

      • +25% Manpower in true faith provinces
      • +25% National sailors modifier
      • +15% Production efficiency

      Creating a march[edit | edit source]

      Marches are created from a vassal via the "Designate March" button under the "Influence actions" section of the vassal's diplomacy window. Vassals are usually keen to become marches, and will sometimes ask their overlord to make them one. There is a −1000 malus towards an overlord accepting a request from a vassal to become a march if the vassal is next to colonizable territory, not bordering any of the overlord's rivals or enemies, or the vassal is in the overlord's culture group.

      Revoking a march[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.25.

      The status of a march can be revoked for the price of −1 stability and −50 relations, decaying at a base rate of 2 per year. Revoking a march returns the subject to vassalage, allowing diplomatic annexation by the overlord 10 years after the revocation. Note that the stability cost penalty can be negated by completing the Diplomatic idea group.

      Subject interaction[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.25.
      Main article: Subject nation#Subject interactions

      In addition to the generic subject interactions, marches have the following unique options:

      Icon Interaction Effect
      Fortify March Build a fort, paid for by the overlord
      Send Officers
      • −10% Liberty desire
      • Overlord pays 33% of subject's army maintenance
      • Subject gets +20% morale of armies and +5% discipline
      Subsidize Armies
      • 10% of the overlord's manpower is sent to the subject
      • +50 Opinion from subject

      Achievements[edit | edit source]

      Full House
      Have 3 Vassals and 2 Marches at the same time.
      Holy Trinity
      As the Papal State, own Jerusalem and have Livonian Order, Teutonic Order, and The Knights as Marches.
      Marshy March
      Have a march with at least two marsh provinces.
