      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Raise an Army
      Traditionally, the Parliament opposes any attempt of a standing army for the royal house. Instead, our [Root.GovernmentName] relies on the effort of militia, mercenaries and the levies of the Nobility. As the Hundred Years' War is going through its last phases and the Scots are posing a threat to our security, it is only natural that we must raise an army one way or another.
      		 The country:
      • Has an army of at least 80% of its force limit
      • At least one of:
        • The Nobility estate has at least 2 privileges
        • Has at least 6 mercenary regiments
      		 The country:
      • Gain "Subjugation" casus belli against Scotland for 25 years
      • Gains a permanent claim on the Britain region

      Edward I, Scottorum Malleus, saw the wisdom of securing our kingdom by subjugating the Scots. We must do the same.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns the following provinces:
      • Perth (250)
      • Argyll (4363)
      • Inner Hebrides (4364)
      • Inverness (252)
      • Sutherland (4110)
      • Outer Hebrides (253)
      • All provinces in the Lowlands area
      		 The country:
      • Gains 75 administrative power
      • If Orkney (369) is not owned by the country or its non-tributary subjects:
        • Gets the “Purchase of Orkney” event
      • Else:
        • Gains 75 diplomatic power
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following provinces:
        • Shetland (1978)
        • Färöarna (1979)
        • Reykjavik (370)

      		 Raise an Army
      Conquer Ireland
      Historically known as 'His Majesty's Irish Enemies', the Gaelic populace of Ireland have long been outside English rule. It is time they were brought to heel.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns the following areas:
      • Munster
      • Connacht
      • Ulster
      • Leinster
      		 The country:
      • Gains 75 administrative power
      • Unlocks the “Crown of Ireland Act” parliament issue

      Every province in the Munster, Connacht, Ulster, and Leinster areas:

      • Gains “The Irish Choler” for 15 years, giving:
        • +35% Local goods produced modifier
      		 Raise an Army
      Unify the Isles
      The British Isles are home to the two kingdoms of England and Scotland as well as the many petty realms of Ireland. It is up to us to unify the isles under the English rule.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 50 provinces the following region:
      • Britain
      		 The country:
      • Gains 25 convertible[2] prestige
      • If has not chosen the Angevin mission path:
        • Unlocks the “Acts of Union” parliament issue

        • +1 Monthly splendor

      		 Subjugate Scotland

      Conquer Ireland

      The Acts of Union
      The legislation of the many kingdoms of the Isles is separated and convoluted. Although each of these states share the same monarch, their laws and parliaments are not unified under a single constitution. We cannot let this continue.

      • Is Great Britain or the Angevin Kingdom

      • Every province in the Britain region:

          • −1 Local unrest

      Else, if the country is the Angevin Kingdom:

      • 20 random owned provinces in the Britain and France regions:
        • Gains 1 base production
      		 Unify the Isles

        • Gains 100 church power

        • Gains 50 fervor

        • Gains 100 church power

          • +2 Tolerance of the true faith

          • −10% All estates influence modifier

      Piety of the State
      We are a land blessed by the Lord himself. But the clerical institutions have a wildly different idea how the religious matters are handled. We must secure the piety of the state and assume control over the church.

      • Has enacted at least 4 government reforms
      • At least one of:
        • All of:
          • Has enacted the “Separate the Clergy from State” or “Curtail Clerical Privileges” government reform

            • Has less than 50 clergy influence
            • Owns less land than the crown
        • All of:
          • Has not enacted the “Separate the Clergy from State” nor “Curtail Clerical Privileges” government reform
          • If the Clergy estate exists:
            • Has completed at least 4 Clergy agendas
          • Else:
            • Owns at least 10 provinces with a Tax building
      		 The country:
      • Unlocks the “Anglican Aspect” and “Spread the Book of Common Prayer” aspects of faith
      • Gains 50 government reform progress
      • If is Protestant or Anglican:
        • Gains 100 church power
      • Else:

          • +1 Yearly papal influence
          • +0.5 Yearly patriarch authority

        • The Clergy estate loses 3% share of land
      • Else:
        • Unlocks the “Conciliarism of the Churches” government reform
        • Gains 20 clergy loyalty
      		 Handle the Reformation

      Crown and Parliament branch[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.36.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Strengthen the [Root.GovernmentName]
      The authority of the crown has always been a subject of contention. While the ruler sees his reign as absolute and of divine nature, the Parliament tries to limit the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]'s power. As the idea of Absolutism grows in the courts of Europe so does it in ours. We must follow the trend and strengthen the [Root.GetAdjective] [Root.GovernmentName].
      		 The country:
      • Has no rebel armies present
      • Has no province controlled by rebels
      • If the current age does not have the absolutism mechanic:
        • Has at least 90 Legitimacy (or equivalent)
        • At least one estate has less than 50 influence
        • All estates have at least 60 loyalty
        • Has at least 5 subjects
        • All subjects have less than 20% liberty desire
      • Else:
        • Has at least 50 Revolutionary zeal or 50 Absolutism
        • Has less than 20% average autonomy in non-overseas core province
      		 The country:
      • Gains 50 government reform progress
      • Unlocks the “Direct Royal Administration” government reform
      Acts of the Parliament
      The Parliament is a tool of the state. As powerful as any tool can be, the potential of the Parliament can only be utilized properly if it is guided wisely.
      		 The country:
      • Has completed at least 3 Burghers agendas, if the estate exists
      • If has a Parliament:
        • Has passed at least 6 Parliament issues
      • Else:
        • Has at least +2 stability
      		 The country:
      • Gains 50 government reform progress
      • Unlocks the “Parliamentary Administration” government reform
      The Three Kingdoms Wars
      The rule of the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] and the power of the Parliament are in stark contrast. Conflict between the Royalists and the Parliamentarians is inevitable.
      We must make sure that [Root.GetUsableName] will prevail these trying times and come forth as a state of greater power and authority.
      		 The country:
      • At least one of:
        • Has completed the “English Civil War” disaster
        • Has reached the Age of Revolutions without triggering it
      		 If completes this mission after the “English Civil War” has happened:
      • Unlocks the “Adjust British Laws”, “Adjust Angevin Laws”, and “Adjust English Laws” parliament issues
      • Gains “Restored Order” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +0.5 Yearly legitimacy
        • +0.3 Yearly republican tradition
        • +0.5 Yearly devotion
        • +5% All estates' loyalty equilibrium
        • +1 Number of possible parliament issues

      Else:

      • Gains 50 administrative power
      • Gains 50 diplomatic power
      • Gains 50 military power
      • Gains “Weathered Absolutism” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −10% Stability cost modifier
      		 Strengthen the [Root.GovernmentName]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Hundred Years' War
      The Treaty of Tours has brought an uneasy period of truce between [Root.GetName] and France. Both sides are aware that this is unlikely to last, and we must decide whether to honor their treaty and bide our time, or prepare for war. The fate of [Root.GetUsableName] depends on this very action.
      		 The country:
      • If France exists, at least one of:
        • Is at war with France
        • Has returned Maine (177) to France in the "Surrender of Maine" event
        • Has France as a subject
        • Does not own directly or through subject province Maine (177)
      • Else, at least one of:
        • Is at war with the owner of Paris (183)
        • Does not own directly or through non-tributary subject province Maine (177)
        • Owns directly or through non-tributary subject province Paris (183)
      		 The country gets the “Destiny of [Root.GetUsableName]” event, which:
      • Allows to decide between keeping the British missions, which focus on the New World and the colonization of Asia, and the Angevin missions, which focus on continental dominance on Europe.


      End the French Conflict
      Be it through victory over the French and claiming their throne, or by abandoning continental Europe and taking a bigger a focus on the navy: our conflict with France and the continent must find an end one way or another.
      		 The country:
      • At least one of:
        • Owns directly or through non-tributary subject at least 20 provinces in France region
        • Does not own any province other than Calais (87) in France region
        • Owns directly or through non-tributary subject provinces Cotentin (4385) and Caux (167), which:
          • Have any Trade building
      		 The country:
      • If has France as our subject:
        • Gains opinion modifier “English Diplomats” ( +50 opinion / −1 yearly decay) towards France
        • France loses 25% liberty desire
      • Else, if France does not exist:
        • Gains 150 administrative power
        • Gains 150 diplomatic power
        • Gains 150 military power
      • Else:
        • If has a stability of less than +3,
          • then it gains 1 stability,
          • else it gains 50 administrative power.
        • Gains “Recovering Prestige” for 20 years, giving:
          • −1% Prestige decay
          • +1 Yearly prestige
      		 The Hundred Years' War
      [Root.GetAdjective] Superiority
      Our rivals are many and powerful, but also arrogant. They assume they can compete with us on the sea, contest our claim in foreign lands and pose a threat to us. We must show them how fatally wrong they are.
      		 The country:
      • Receives war reparations from at least 3 countries
      • At least one of:
        • Has humiliated a rival
        • Has no rivals and is not rivaled
      		 The country:
      • Gains +25 power projection
      		 The Hundred Years' War
      Develop British Identity

Naturally, such collection of different cultures will lead to problems if left untended. We shall forge a British Identity in order to establish a British hegemony over the isles.
      		 The country:
      • Has at least +50 prestige
      • At least one of:
        • Owns at least 50 provinces with a culture from our culture group
        • All of:
          • Accepts 'Irish' and 'Highlander' cultures
          • Owns at least 10 Irish provinces
          • Owns at least 5 Highlander provinces
      		 The country:
      • If has a stability of less than +3,
        • then it gains 1 stability,
        • else it gains 50 administrative power.
      • If completes this mission while accepting the 'Highlander' culture and having Ireland as a subject or while having 'Irish' as an accepted culture and owning at least 10 Irish provinces:
        • If Ireland is our subject:
          • Gains 'Irish' as an accepted culture
        • Gains “Unified British Identity” until either Highlander or Irish are demoted, giving:
          • +2 Max promoted cultures
      • If completes this mission while owning 50 provinces with a culture from our culture group:
        • Unlocks the “Act of Cultural Unity” parliament issue
        • Gains 150 diplomatic power
      		 End the French Conflict
      Abolish Villeinage
      The villein labor service has proven its worth in the past. However, it is outdated. No longer adequate for a [Root.GovernmentName] of our prestige, it is time to replace this system by promoting copyhold tenure.
      		 The country:
      • Has no rebel armies present
      • Has no province controlled by rebels
      • Does not have the “[Root.GetAdjective] Villeinage” Nobility estate privilege
      		 The country:
      • Unlocks the “Expanded Copyhold Tenure” Nobility estate privilege
      • Gains “Villeinage Ended” for 25 years, giving:
        • +0.25 Global prosperity growth
        • −10% Development cost
      		 End the French Conflict
      Issue the Royal Warrant
      The royal warrants of appointment is an issue granted to merchants of magnificent goods for the royal court and the royal personages. We should choose our trade partners wisely and grant them these warrants in exchange for a favorable trade.
      		 The country:
      • Has a Trader of at least level 3
      • Has at least 3 government reforms
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least 8 government reforms
        • Has at least 40 mercantilism
      		 The country:
      • Unlocks the “Autonomous Trade Companies” Burghers estate privilege
      • Unlocks the “Royal Charters” government reform
      • Gains 50 government reform progress
      		 End the French Conflict

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Royal Court
      A [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] is nothing without [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] advisors. No matter how the future of [Root.GetUsableName] will look like, competent consultants are always required.
      		 The country:
      • Does not have an ongoing disaster
      • If is a monarchy:
        • Has at least 95 Legitimacy
      • Total ruler's skill are at least 5
      • Is employing any Administrative advisor
      • Is employing any Diplomatic advisor
      • Is employing any Military advisor
      		 The country:
      • Unlocks the “Appointment of State Minister” parliament issue

      Mainland Alliances
      Although our priorities lie beyond the sea, we should not forget the threat our home continent could pose to us if left unattended. We should establish alliances with the enemies of our enemies.
      		 The country has at least 3 allies who:
      • Is either Portugal or has at least 200 total development
      • Has its capital on the same continent as us
      • Has at least +150 opinion of us
      		 The country:
      • Gains 25 prestige
      • For every ally, which fulfils the mission's requirement:
        • Gains 10 trust towards them, and vice versa
        • Gains 10 favors with them
      		 The Hundred Years' War

      The Royal Court

      Influence the Lowlands
      The Dutch and the Flemish of the Lowlands are in many ways similar to us: they seek wealth from profitable trade, maintain an impressive naval force and are opposed to French and Spanish dominance. Influencing the Lowlands in our favor might bring us an invaluable ally or subject in this region.
