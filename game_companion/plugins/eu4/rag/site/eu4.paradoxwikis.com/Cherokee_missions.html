Cherokee missions is a list of missions for Cherokee. It requires the Leviathan expansion for the mission tree to be full.[1]

Clicking on a mission icon leads to the appropriate table row.

National missionsEdit

Stable governmentEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.32.
Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Principal People
The name of our people in our own language is Aniyunwiya, the principal people. We hold that name because our tribe is destined for greatness and, if we wish to fulfill that destiny, we need to start increasing our prestige.
   Cherokee:
  • Has at least   +30 Prestige
  • Has less than   1 Corruption
   Cherokee gains country modifier “The Seven Clans” for   20 years, giving the following effects:
  •   +1 Diplomatic reputation
  •   −10% Advisor cost
 
The Council House
A Council House is the center of each of our villages. Our leader rules from there, weddings and meetings are held inside it and it works as a symbol of our diplomatic power. We should invite some allies to come and see it with their own eyes.
   Cherokee:
  • Has at least   2 allies
  •   Country's capital
    • At least one of:
      • Has   Longhouse building
      • Has   Fort building of at least level 3
   Cherokee:
  • Gain   50 diplomatic power

Every   Totemist allies:

  • Gain opinion modifier “Conciliatory Views” (  +50 opinion / −2 yearly decay) towards   Cherokee
   Principal People
 
White Government
The White Government makes the decisions in times of peace. Our advisors, counselors, messengers and priests take part in it, as well as our ruler. It is their duty to ensure that such peace is maintained.
   Cherokee:
  • Has at least   +1 Stability
  • Has at least   200 ducats
  • Is employing any   Diplomatic advisor
   Cherokee:
  • Gain   100 diplomatic power
  • Gain skill 2   Treasurer advisor (50% cheaper to employ)
   The Council House
 
Red Government
The Red Government makes the decisions in times of war. Our war counselors, war speakers and great warriors take part in it, as well as our ruler. It is their duty to lead us toward victory.
   Cherokee:
  • Has at least   1 general
  • Has a   Manpower reserves of at least 60% of its maximum
  • Has an army of at least   100% of its force limit
   Cherokee:
  • Gain   100 military power
  • Gain   general with   75 army tradition
   White Government
 
Thunder and Lightning
We have achieved greatness in times of both peace and war, times of hardship and ease. The final step to fulfill the destiny that has been gifted to us is to reach total reliability.
   Cherokee:
  • Has at least   +75 Prestige
  • Has at least   4 allies
  • Has at least   +2 Stability
   Cherokee:
  • Gain country modifier “Perfect Stability” for   15 years, giving the following effect:
    •   −10% Technology cost
  • If   Stability is not +3
    • Gain   1 stability
    • Gain   50 administrative power
  • Else:
    • Gain   100 administrative power
   Red Government

Improve CherokeeEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.32.
Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Botanical Exports
Many plants grow in our fertile soil, and many of them hold interesting properties. Some may be used for healing, but others are more inclined to do harm; both types equally powerful. We should use these resources to improve our international trade.
   Cherokee is the strongest trade power in Chesapeake Bay trade node   Cherokee:
  • Gain country modifier “Black Drink” for   15 years, giving the following effect:
    •   +5% Trade efficiency
  • Gain ruler modifier “Effective Remedies” until ruler's death, giving the following effect:
    •   +10% Manpower recovery speed
 
The Cave Country
One of the many names given to our people means “those who inhabit the cave country”, and it is, in fact, a name that suits us. The land surrounding our villages is as steep as our people’s nature.
 If   Cherokee has a   Native Tribe government
  • Owns at least 2 provinces with:
    •   Great Trail building

Else,

  • Owns at least 2 provinces with either:
    •   Workshop building
    •   Counting House building
   Cherokee gains country modifier “Tunnel Construction” for   15 years, giving the following effects:
  •   −25% Envoy travel time
  •   +25% Trade range
 
The Beloved Woman
A Ghigau, or Beloved Woman, is chosen for her bravery in battle, her wisdom in life and her integrity and devotion to the truth. It would be a great honor to have one in our own court.
 At least one of:
  • Employs a female advisor
  • Has a female Ruler
  • Has a female heir
   Cherokee gains country modifier “Mother Clan” for the rest of the game, giving the following effects:
  •   +50% Female advisor chance
  •   +5% Morale of armies
  •   −5% Advisor cost

Regional missionsEdit

Southeastern developmentEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.32.
Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Birds of Prey
We must act as human raptors, as dangerous as the precise descent of the falcon. Building a powerful army and filling it with our feared warriors is an appropriate show of force.
 The country:
  • Is not a subject nation
  • Has an army of at least   100% of its force limit
  • Has no mercenary regiment

All neighbouring countries:

  • Has a smaller army than ours
 The country gains country modifier “Birdmen Hunting” for   20 years, giving the following effect:
  •   +10% Morale of armies
 
Like Thunder
We have already proved the strength of our army, but that is not enough. When a thunder strikes the earth, the soil becomes more fertile in that area; when thunder strikes our men, we shall as well raise stronger.
 Any country in the world:
  • Is at war with us
  • Has an army at least 1.5 times larger than ours
 The country:
  • Gain   75 military power
  • Gain a permanent claim on all provinces of the following areas:
    • Mississippi
    • West Florida
    • Alabama
    • Kentucky
    • Upper Louisiana
   Birds of Prey
 
Along the River
Our army is more powerful than ever, the spirits look at us and hold their breaths as we march upon the land. All that surrounds the river stream shall be ours.
 The country owns at least 15 provinces in the following areas:
  • Mississippi
  • West Florida
  • Alabama
  • Kentucky
  • Upper Louisiana
 The country:
  • If has enacted the “Martial Tradition” or “War Band” government reform
    • Gain country modifier “Our River” for   25 years, giving the following effect:
      •   +0.5 Yearly army tradition
  • Else:
    • Gain   100 military power
   Like Thunder
 
Platform Mounds
Building our houses on the top of a hill will provide us with some extra defensiveness. We could also reinforce our palisades to make sure that no one even thinks of attacking us.
 The country owns at least 2 provinces with either:
  •   Palisade building
  •   Fortified House building
 Every owned provinces with a   Palisade or   Fortified House building
  • Gain province modifier “Palisaded Village” for   20 years, giving the following effect:
    •   +25% Local defensiveness
 
Medicine Wheel
Religion is an important part of our lives not only because of the spiritual calmness that it brings to our community, but also due to the blessings it grants to our health. We should have a proper place to perform our rites.
 The country:
  • If is   Totemist
    • Owns a province with a   Sweat Lodge building
    • Has at least   +1 Stability
  • Else:
    • Owns at least 3 provinces with either:
      •   Temple building
      •   Cathedral building
 The country gains country modifier “Solar Blessing” for   15 years, giving the following effect:
  •   +1% Missionary strength
 
Eastern Agriculture
We have been blessed with a rich soil in which our crops can grow splendidly. Seeds would grow as fast as children and their liveliness would be no smaller than theirs. Taking care of our crops means taking care of our community.
 The country:
  • Owns at least 3 provinces with
    •   Irrigation building
  • Has at least   50 total development
 The country:
  • Gain   ducats equal to 1 year of income
  • Gain country modifier “Agricultural Complex” for   20 years, giving the following effect:
    •   +10% Production efficiency

ModernizationEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.35.

For some countries, this mission is located in the fifth row of the first column[2] and for other countries it is located in the first row of the second column[3].

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
New World Modernization
European colonialism threatens to wipe out our nation if we do not adapt rapidly. We must have the technology and state apparatus to challenge them.
 One of the following:
  • Government is neither   tribal nor native tribe
  • Has enacted the Steppe Nomads government reform

Any neighbouring country:

  • Has embraced the   Feudalism institution
  • Is less than   1 technology ahead
 Country gains:
  •   3 innovativeness
  •   20 prestige

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Uniting the Tribes
By forging marriages with other tribes, we are strengthening our position for future expansions. Expansion can start with a marriage.
 Country:
  • Has at least   40 Prestige
  • At least one of:
    • Has at least   3 royal marriages
    • The   federation has at least 3 members
    • Owns at least 20 non-colony provinces
 Country gains   50 government reform progress
 
Trustworthy Allies
No country can stand alone in this world. We must build lasting relationships with other countries if we are to be able to grow and prosper long term.
 Country has at least   2 allies which:
  • Have at least   +150 opinion towards our country
 Country gets “Influential Diplomats” for   25 years, giving the following effect:
  •   +1 Diplomatic reputation
   Uniting the Tribes
 
Acquire Subjects
What good is power if no one recognizes it? We must acquire subject states that pay us the kind of respect one would expect from a country like [Root.GetName].
 Country has at least 2 non-tributary subjects Country gets “Obedient Subjects” for   25 years, giving the following effect:
  •   −15% Diplomatic annexation cost
   Trustworthy Allies
 
Unite Home Area
We cannot expect others to fear us if we are not even able to control our home area. Let's unite it!
 No province in our capital's area is owned by another country Country gains:
  •   50 diplomatic power
  •   50 administrative power
  •   50 military power

Country gets “Hegemonic Ambition” for   25 years, giving the following effect:

  •   −0.05 Monthly autonomy change
   Acquire Subjects
 
Unite Home Region
The time has come to put all our hard work to build a viable military machine to the test. We must rise to be the true hegemon of our region, respected and feared by all.
 No province in our capital's region is owned by another country Country gains:
  •   50 diplomatic power
  •   50 administrative power
  •   50 military power

Country gets “Hegemonic Ambition” for   25 years, giving the following effect:

  •   −0.05 Monthly autonomy change
   Unite Home Area
 
Native Colonization
There is so much land around us, with little population and infrastructure that it would be a pity not to make it ours. We shall send our people to develop it.
 One of the following:
  • Government is neither   tribal nor native tribe
  • Has enacted the Steppe Nomads government reform
 Country gets “Colonize the Continent” for the rest of the game, giving the following effects:
  •   +1 Colonist
   Unite Home Region

Military missionsEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Build to Force Limit
Any growing country must maintain a reasonably sized army. By expanding our forces we can both further our political interests abroad and stimulate our domestic economy.
 Country has 100% of the   force limit total army size Country gets “Thriving Arms Industry” for   25 years, giving the following effects:
  •   +5% Morale of armies
  •   −5% Land maintenance modifier
 
Assemble An Army
Our territorial ambitions require a grand army. We must build and maintain a strong standing army that can defend and expand the lands under our rule.
 Country has an army with at least 80,000   troops Country gets “Army Enthusiasm” for   25 years, giving the following effects:
  •   +10% Manpower recovery speed
  •   +0.5 Yearly army tradition
   Build to Force Limit
 
Drive out the Europeans
The invading Europeans are rapidly conquering the New World, and are even expanding into our traditional homes. They must be driven back to their ships.
 One of the following:
  • Government is neither   tribal nor native tribe
  • Has enacted the Steppe Nomads government reform

Country has embraced the   Feudalism institution
No province in our capital's region is owned by   Western country

Country gains   200 military power

Country gets “Inspiring Victory” for   20 years, giving the following effects:

  •   +1 Yearly prestige
  •   +10% Manpower recovery speed
   Assemble An Army
 
Sunset Invasion
The invaders have fled from our lands. But is that enough? At any time they might return with even more terrible force. Let us pursue them back to Europe itself.
 At least 5 provinces in the Europe continent are owned by the country or non-tributary subjects
  • Change technology group to   High American
  • Change unit type to   High American
  • Gain   100 administrative power
  • Gain   100 diplomatic power
  • Gain   100 military power
   Drive out the Europeans
 
Control Unrest
Our conquests have brought some instability to our lands. We have to reduce the unrest.
 Country does not own any province with at least   2 Unrest Country gains   1 stability   Unite Home Area
 
Build Temples
Our people need guidance from our spiritual leaders and what better place to have them share their wisdom than some new religious places?
 At least one of:
  • Has at least 5 provinces with a   Temple
  • All of:
    • Has at least 1 province with a   Ceremonial Fire Pit
    • Has at least 1 province with a   Sweat Lodge
 Country gets “Building Spree” for   25 years, giving the following effect:
  •   +10 Tax income
   Control Unrest

Administrative missionsEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.35.
Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
High Income
We must increase state income by any means necessary. Money is a requirement for any endeavor of the state, from the daily expenses of the government to the maintenance of armies to invade our enemies.
 Country's   monthly income has increased by 150% compared to the starting monthly income Country gets “Growing Economy” for   25 years, giving the following effects:
  •   −10% Construction cost
  •   −25% Construction time
 
Dominate Trade
Our merchant communities have been growing stronger and stronger for a long time now. It is time we give them the help they require to truly dominate our domestic trade. No foreign power must be allowed to influence the well being of our subjects.
 Country controls at least 75% of the trade in the home trade node

Random[4] provinces gain “Dominant Trade Hub” for the rest of the game, giving the following effects:

  •   +10 Local trade power
  •   −5% Local development cost
   High Income
 
Improve Capital
The core of our nation needs to be strong so that other nations look at us in awe. We must develop it.
 Country's   total development has grown by at least 50 The   capital gains:
  •   1 base tax
  •   1 base production
  •   1 base manpower

All owned provinces within the area of the capital get “Growth of Capital” for   25 years, giving the following effects:

  •   −10% local construction cost
  •   −10% local development cost
   High Income
 
Build a Treasury
A large treasury is an imperative buffer for all things that could befall us through. If this is seen as miserly, it is because some do not realize that this vice is the very essence of what it means to reign.
 Country has at least   2 000 ducats Country gets “Strong Treasury Department” for   25 years, giving the following effects:
  •   +0.10 Yearly inflation reduction
   Improve Capital
 
Imperial Conquest
Securing wealth must always be the foremost priority of our state. Let us make use of the expensive armies and fleets that our economic prosperity has bought us to acquire even more resources and subjects for an aspiring empire.
 Country has at least   1 000 total development Country gains   30 prestige

Country gets “Imperial Ambition” for   25 years, giving the following effects:

  •   −5 Years of separatism
   Build a Treasury
