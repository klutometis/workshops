      The Nobility estate represents the families of landed aristocrats in your country. Their privileges are guaranteed by old traditions and most have originally been won through performing great services to the state. Their traditional responsibility is to fight and protect the realm against outside dangers as well as help the ruler keep the peace.

      Availability[edit | edit source]

      General availability:

      • This estate belongs to the base estates group which are available to all nations unless supplemented/replaced by special estate groups added via expansions.

      Specific conditions:

      • Does not use other mechanics
      • Does not have one of the following government reforms enacted:
        • Peasants Republic
        • Kongsi Federation
        • American Republic
        • Federal Republic
        • Self-Governing Colony
        • / kko-Ikki Republic/Ikko-Ikki Government
        • Sich Rada
        • Protectorate Parliament
        • Livonian Constitutional Monarchy
        • Parliamentarism / Parliamentarism
        • Theocratic Democracy / Regionally Elected Commanders
      • Is not nomad, unless the "Noble Tribes" Mission was completed
      • Has enacted the "Nobility of the Emperor" Reform if country is Revolutionary Empire

      Additionally, only Muslim, Sikh, and Buddhist nations may combine this estate with the Marathas or Rajputs estates.
