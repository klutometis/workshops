      Local organization

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      (Redirected from Holy order)
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
      At least some were last verified for version 1.35.

      Local organization refers to state-level modifiers, available for certain nations. Examples of local organization are Holy orders, metropolitans and other orders given out at state level. The local organization elements are located in the "State tab" of the province UI.

      Holy orders[edit | edit source]

      		 Available only with the Golden Century DLC enabled.

      Establishing a holy order in a state costs 50 administrative, diplomatic or military power and gives +1 corresponding development to each province in the state and a unique permanent modifier depending on the order. Each state can have only one holy order.

      The cost of establishing a holy order can be reduced by having Aljafería Palace at magnificent level and employing an inquisitor advisor in the court.[1] The cost is then reduced by −5% per level of the employed inquisitor.

      Ottoman orders[edit | edit source]

      Available only if both Domination and Golden Century DLCs are enabled.

      The Ottomans and Rûm have access to these orders if they enact the T4 government reform "Sufism Decree". Rûm can always enact this reform, but the Ottomans need to unlock it first via the mission "Codify Sufism", on the Mysticism path of their mission tree

      Mevlevi
      Founded in Konya by the followers of Jalaluddin Muhammad Balkhi, the Mevlevi Order is a Sunni branch of Sufism. It dedicates itself to the study of the Quaran and Rumi´s works.
      		 50 1
      • −1 Local unrest
      • −5% Local development cost
      • Move 2 towards Mysticism for every province in the state.
      • Has enacted the "Sufism Decree" government reform
      • Religion is Sunni or Ibadi
      Bektashi
      The Bektashi Order is a young, Shiite branch of Sufism. Born in Anatolia, Bektashis follow the Fourteen Innocents additionally to the twelve Imams.
      		 50 1
      • −1 Local unrest
      • +10% Local production efficiency
      • Move 2 towards Mysticism for every province in the state.
      • Has enacted the "Sufism Decree" government reform
      • Religion is Shia or Ibadi

      Pashas[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Cradle of Civilization DLC enabled.

      Certain government reforms enable the ability to assign pashas to states. These governments are: Ottoman Government, Modernized Ottoman Government, Reorganized Ottoman Government, Sultanate of Rûm, Integrated Ottoman Officials and Modernized Turkoman Federation. Pashas grant all provinces in their state the following modifiers:

      −20% Local governing cost
      +20% Minimum local autonomy
      −33% State maintenance
      −0.1% Local monthly autonomy
      +10% Local tolerance of heathens

      Removing the pasha causes all the provinces in the state to suffer a +5 local unrest penalty for 10 years.

      Metropolitans[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Third Rome DLC enabled.

      Orthodox countries may use the ‘Consecrate Metropolitan’ mechanic in states that meet the following criteria:

      • At least 30 development in provinces in the state owned and cored by this country
      • All provinces in the state must be Orthodox
      • All provinces in the state must be either of an accepted culture or part of that country's culture group.
      • No province in the state may be a territorial core

      Consecrating a metropolitan gives a one-time boost of 5% patriarch authority, and has the following permanent effects on the state:

      +10% State maintenance
      −0.05 Monthly devastation

      Turning a state which has been consecrated into a territory will revoke its consecration and remove these effects.

      If the country has consecrated a metropolitan then metropolitan events may trigger.

      Completing the Trapezuntine mission "Metropolitan of Trebizond" removes the state maintenance malus and doubles the monthly devastation reduction bonus.

      Completing the Russian mission "Appease the Patriarchate" removes the state maintenance malus and adds a +10% local tax modifier bonus to metropolitans.

      Completing the Georgian mission "Patriate of All Georgia" gives a −10% Local development cost bonus to metropolitans.

      Completing the Byzantine mission "Antiocha & Jerusalem" gives an additional +2.5 Patriarch authority when consecrating a metropolitan.
