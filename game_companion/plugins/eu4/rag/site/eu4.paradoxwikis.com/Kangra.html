      Kangra is a OPM located in the Northwest of India. In 1444, it borders Ladakh to the North, Sirhind and Sirmur to the South, and Kashmir to the West.

      Decisions[edit | edit source]

      Kangra can form Bharat.

      id

      Form Bharat (Central Indian, Eastern Aryan)

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

      Form the mighty Bharat and bring all the lands of the Indian subcontinent under your rule.

       
      Potential requirements

      Bharat does not exist.
      The country:

      • was never an end-game nation.
      • is not the Mughals
      • has never formed Bharat or Hindustan before.
      • is not colonial nation.
      • is part of the Dharmic or Buddhist religion group
      • does not have 'Steppe Horde' government reform
      • has a culture in either culture group:
        • Central Indian
        • Eastern Aryan

      If the country is AI-controlled then it:

      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not a custom nation
      • is not a former colonial nation.
      • has at least 15 cities

      Playing with normal or historical nations.

      		Allow

      The country:

      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not at war.
      • is not a nomad nation.
      • has 3 stability.
      • has embraced Feudalism
      • owns its core provinces:
        • Multan (506)
        • Surat (517)
        • Central Doab (524)
        • Madurai (536)
        • Daulatabad (545)
        • Cuttack (552)
        • Pataliputra (558)
        • Bidar (1948)
        • Halar (2052)
        • Kumari (4410)
        • Mansura (4503)
        • Delhi (522)
        • Gauda (563)
        • Assam (566)
      • owns core provinces if primary culture is Central Indian:
        • Burhanpur (527)
        • Sambalpur (553)
      • owns core provinces if primary culture is Eastern Aryan:
        • Lahore (507)
        • Tondainadu (539)
      Effects

      The country:

      • becomes Bharat.
      • gains 10 prestige.
      • gets new missions.
      • gets the event ‘New Traditions & Ambitions’, unless it is a custom nation.
      • becomes an empire.
      • removes all provinces from the Holy Roman Empire, if it is a member and not an elector or the emperor.
      • gains permanent claims on the Indian subcontinent.

      • AI will always take this decision.
