      Dominance of the Tribes
      The Tribes have grown so powerful they assumed de facto control over our country.

      Dominance of the Tribes[1] is a disaster that may occur in countries with a powerful Tribes estate.

      Prerequisites[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The country has a Tribes estate which has at least 80 influence and is not currently in the disaster Internal Power Struggle.

      If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

      Start[edit | edit source]

      If the progress reaches 100%, the disaster will start with the following event:

      id

      The $ESTATE_NOMADIC_TRIBES$ Seize Power

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      $MONARCH$ has allowed the $ESTATE_NOMADIC_TRIBES$ of $COUNTRY$ to grow too powerful and now they have made their move. Using the influence they already had over our state in terms of control over everything from tax collection to the directing and recruitment of our armies, they have effectively seized the reins of government and turned the $MONARCHTITLE$ into little more than their puppet.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      the start of ‘Dominance of the Tribes’ disaster.

      Immediate effects

      [2]

      We should have seen this coming...

      The country:

      • loses 1 stability.

      [Root.GetName] will now be controlled by the Tribes until the 'Curtail the Tribes' decision is taken.

      Modifiers[edit | edit source]

      While the disaster is ongoing the country suffers:

      • +50% idea cost
      • +50% technology cost
      • +50% development cost

      End[edit | edit source]

      The disaster can only be ended by the following decision.[3]

      id

      Curtail the Tribes

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The Tribes have made the Khan into their puppet, enforcing unwanted laws and traditions. To take the power back from them we will need to curtail their power and privileges.

       
      Potential requirements

      The country:

      • has an ongoing ‘Dominance of the Tribes’ disaster.[4]
      		 Allow

      The country:

      • has a stability of at least 1.
      • does not have rebel armies in its provinces.
      • does not have rebel controlled provinces.
      • has a Tribes estate which influence is lower than 60.
      Effects

      The country:[5]

      • loses 3 stability.

      The capital province:

      • loses 1 base tax.
      • loses 1 base production.

      Then the following event is triggered.

      id

      The $ESTATE_NOMADIC_TRIBES$ are Curtailed

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      After an arduous struggle the [Root.GetTribesOrFallbackName] have finally been brought in line again. [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] is now in control over enough land, men and institutions to once again call [Root.Monarch.GetHerselfHimself] the ruler of our people.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      the end of ‘Dominance of the Tribes’ disaster.

      Immediate effects

      [6]

      The $MONARCHTITLE$ is back in control.
