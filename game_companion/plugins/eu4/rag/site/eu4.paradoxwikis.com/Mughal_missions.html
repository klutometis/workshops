      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

      This is a list of all missions of Mughals[1]. The mission Malaccan Viceroyalty gets unlocked by either completing the mission Fall of Coromandel or the mission Subah of Ayuddhya. Then the other mission will be removed from the prerequisites.

      For the missions from the dharma DLC, see Mughal missions without winds of change.

      Conquest of India[edit | edit source]

      Southern conquest[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Fate of India
      Restoring the Empire of Timur is a vain cause. We shall return to Persia once we are stronger. Now, let our dynasty of conquerors continue conquering, establishing a new Empire not in the harsh Khorasan, but in the rich region of India. Let us seal the Fate of Hindustan.
      		 The Mughals:
      • Have at least 2 generals
      • At least one of:
        • Have won at least 25 battles
        • Have an army of at least 90% of its force limit
      		 The Mughals:
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Multan
        • Southern Sindh
        • Northern Sindh
        • Upper Doab
        • Lower Doab
        • Oudh
        • Purvanchal
        • Katehar
        • Jangladesh
        • Jaipur
      • If it is the Age of Discovery:
        • Gain “Gunpowder Pioneers” until the end of the Age of Discovery, giving:
          • +1 Artillery levels available vs fort
      • Else:
        • Gain 20 convertible[2] army tradition
      Sindh Campaign
      By tightening our grip over the upper Indus region we have acquired a good base for expansion further south. Let us follow the Indus to its mouth and add the lands of Sindh to our domain.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 10 provinces in the following areas:
      • Southern Sindh, including at least province Thatta (504)
      • Northern Sindh
      • Multan
      		 The Mughals:
      • Create a level 1 center of trade in province Thatta (504)
      • Gain “Down the Indus” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +50 Governing capacity
      • Gain “Gates of India” for 25 years, giving:
        • +1 Tolerance of the true faith
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Marwar
        • Mewar
      		 Fate of India
      Subdue Rajputana
      Rajputana is not a rich land, and the many petty princes there would never pose a danger on their own. But Rajput dynasties once ruled Northern India and their kinsmen are still very commonplace as minor rulers throughout the region. In order to build a lasting empire we must make the Rajput Kings and Princes learn that they must either bow to the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle], or face the full might of the Mughal Army.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 12 provinces in the following areas:
      • Marwar
      • Jangladesh
      • Jaipur
      • Mewar
      		 The Mughals:
      • Gain 100 military power
      • Upgrade the "Establish the Rajput Regiments" Rajputs privilege, making it give additional +15% Rajput force limit fraction and removing the Maximum absolutism penalty
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Tapti
        • Ahmedabad
        • Patan
        • Saurashtra
      		 Fate of India

      Sindh Campaign

      Secure the Hajj Ports
      The great ports of Gujarat are not only among the busiest trading centers in the world, but they are also the point of embarkation for India's thousands of Muslims on their way to the holy cities in Arabia. It is our duty to conquer Gujarat so that we can ensure the safety of our subjects as they undertake the most important journey they will ever make.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 10 provinces in the following areas:
      • Tapti
      • Ahmedabad, including at least province Khambhat (4457)
      • Patan
      • Saurashtra
      		 The Mughals:
      • Create a level 3 center of trade[3] in province Khambhat (4457)
      • Unlock the "Extensive Gujarati Privileges" Jains privilege, allowing us to gain +15% Trade efficiency scaled with our Jains's influence, but at the cost of +0.1 Monthly autonomy
      • Gain 2 years worth of trade income
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Khandesh
        • Ahmednagar
      		 Subdue Rajputana
      Expand Trade Ports
      The Gujarati Sultanate has, until recently, served as the ultimate beneficent and sponsor of a large and vast trade network centered in its Gulf of Khambhat. Let us continue serving as protectors for those merchants and have the markets flourish once more.
      		 The Mughals:
      • Controls at least 75% of the trade in the Gujarat trade node
      • Own or its non-tributary subjects own the following province with a Trade building:
        • Thatta (504)
        • Khambhat (4457)
        • Surat (517)
        • Thana (529)
      		 Provinces Thatta (504), Khambhat (4457), Surat (517) and Thana (529):
      • Get their Trade Building upgraded[4]

      The Mughals:

      • Strengthen the "Extensive Gujarati Privileges" Jains privilege, reducing the Monthly autonomy to +0.05 and reducing Jains influence modifier to +5%
      • Gain 3% mercantilism
      		 Secure the Hajj Ports
      Conquer Malwa
      With the fall of the fortresses in western Rajputana the rich Malwa plateau now lies open before us. The conquest of this region would both enlarge the Imperial treasury and open for future campaigns against Gujarat and the Deccan states. It is our fortune that the Malwa Sultans have been weak and allowed local interest groups too much free rein. This has left the area divided and easy to integrate for a more efficient state such as ours.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 15 provinces in the following areas:
      • Malwa
      • Gondwana
      • Gird
      • Bundelkhand
      • Baghelkhand
      		 The Mughals:
      • Gain 10 convertible[2] army tradition
      • Gain “Crossing the Vindhyas” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +50 Governing capacity
        • +5% Religious unity
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Khandesh
        • North Telingana
        • Berar
      		 Subdue Rajputana
      Across the Vindhyas
      The Malwa plateau is a good base of operations for expansion into Southern India and the vast region between us and the Tamil Country known as Deccan. This region is in many ways quite different from what we are used to. Many have desired the fabled diamonds of Golconda or the rich trading cities of the Coromandel Coast, but in order to get there we must scale mountains, and jungles, defeat the native kingdoms, and ultimately be ready to rule what the Delhi sultans never could. It is time we crossed the Vindhyas.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 12 provinces in the following areas:
      • Khandesh
      • Berar
      • North Telingana
      • Ahmednagar
      		 The Mughals:
      • Gain a new general with:
        • 40 army tradition
        • +2 fire
        • +1 siege
      • Merge "Down the Indus" and "Crossing the Vindhyas" into “Hegemon of the West” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +150 Governing capacity
        • +15% Religious unity
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Maidan
        • Raichur Doab
        • Konkan
        • Desh
      		 Conquer Malwa

      Secure the Hajj Ports

      Control the Desh
      The Western Ghats are home to a great number of small forts that can be used as raiding bases by Maratha cavalry against our subjects and against our armies. These high and often densely forested hills cannot be allowed to remain free from our influence!
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 12 provinces in the following areas:
      • Maidan
      • Raichur Doab
      • Konkan
      • Desh
      		 The Mughals:
      • Get the event “The Maratha Caste”
      • Gain 25 convertible[5] prestige
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Mysore
        • Malabar
        • Kanara
      		 Conquer Malwa

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Emperor of Hindustan
      The Gangetic plain is one of the most fertile places on earth and has always been the heartland of any Northern Indian empire. In this regard, we are no different than our predecessors. We must conquer the entire plains region before we can expand our Hindustani Empire.

      • Upper Doab
      • Lower Doab

      • Katehar

      Bengal Entrepot
      The Ganges Delta has been a focal point for trade and commerce for thousands of years. Here eastern traders market their wares to the Indian public, while goods from all over northern India can flow in the other direction. The recent arrival of western traders has, if anything, further accentuated this dynamic, and trade cities have begun to spring up all over the delta. We must further encourage this development and foster the growth of a major entrepot here in Bengal under our supervision.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 12 provinces in the following areas:
      • Mithila
      • Bihar
      • North Bengal
      • Gaur
      • West Bengal
      • East Bengal
      		 The Mughals:
      • Distribute 10 base production amongst owned provinces
      • Gain “Incursion into the East” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +50 Governing capacity
        • +0.25 Yearly army tradition
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Orissa
        • Garjat
        • Jharkhand
        • Upper Mahanadi
        • Assam
        • Tripura
        • Naga Hills
        • Rakhine
      		 Emperor of Hindustan
      Conquest of Orissa
      Nestled between the Garjats and the Bay of Bengal the eastern coastline of Orissa is perhaps not as rich or wealthy as Coromandel further south. Local rulers have made it their business to house Bengali landowners and potentates on the run from Mughal justice. We cannot allow such insolence, we must restore order in Orissa.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 12 provinces in the following areas:
      • Orissa
      • Garjat
      • Jharkhand
      • Upper Mahanadi
      		 The Mughals:
      • Get the event “Fall of Gajapati Empire”
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Andhra
        • South Telingana
        • Rayalaseema
        • North Carnatic
      		 Bengal Entrepot
      Riches of Golconda
      Famous for the greatest Diamond market in the world Golconda fortress has become synonymous with riches in many languages. Both Golconda and the fabled mines at Kollur now lie open before us, according to popular belief untold wealth awaits us...
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 12 provinces in the following areas:
      • Andhra
      • South Telingana
      • Rayalaseema
      • North Carnatic
      		 The Mughals:
      • Get the event “Fall of Golconda”
      • Gain “Conquest of Golconda” for 30 years, giving:
        • +10% Goods produced modifier
        • +0.1 Yearly inflation reduction
      • Upgrade “Incursion into the East” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +150 Governing capacity
        • +0.5 Yearly army tradition
        • +1 Maximum tolerance of heathens
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • South Carnatic
        • North Lanka
        • South Lanka
        • Madura
        • Tanjore
        • Kongu
      		 Conquest of Orissa
      Fall of Coromandel
      The Coromandel Coast now lies before us. It has been a major center for Indian trade for hundreds if not thousands of years. Here foreigners from east and west established trade posts and factories, bringing great amounts of the silver our economy needs. The expensive textiles, spices, gemstones, pearls, and manufactured goods that they seek to bring to their homelands are also easy to find in this region which is home to great Tamil and Telugu cities with a strong heritage of industry and trade.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 27 provinces in the following areas:
      • Mysore
      • Malabar
      • Kanara
      • South Carnatic
      • North Lanka
      • South Lanka
      • Madura
      • Tanjore
      • Kongu
      		 The Mughals:
      • Unlock the "Viceroyalty of Deccan" decision, allowing us to release Deccan in Southern India in exchange for +5% Administrative efficiency
      • Strengthen the "Grant Jains Liberties" Jains privilege, making it give +25 Trade Power in Coromandel Trade Node when active and +10% Provincial Trade Power Modifier
      • Merge "Hegemon of the West" and "Incursion into the East" into “Emperor of Hindustan” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +400 Governing capacity
        • +0.5 Yearly army tradition
        • +2 Maximum tolerance of heathens
        • +15% Religious unity
        • +10% Manpower in accepted culture provinces
      • If have not completed the mission “Subah of Ayuddhya”:
        • Unlock the "Malaccan Viceroyalty" mission
        • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
          • Malacca
          • North Malaya
          • Malaya
          • Johor

          • Aceh

      Follow the Brahmaputra
      From its sources in distant Ngari the Brahmaputra river flows through all of Tibet, Arunachal and then descends into India in Assam before joining the Ganges in Bengal. This valley is said to be a rich land, with a people adept at making rice grow even in conditions where that ought to be impossible. Let us defy the jungles of the Brahmaputra valley and launch an invasion into Assam, the final part of northern India not yet under our rule.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 12 provinces in the following areas:
      • Assam
      • Tripura
      • Naga Hills
      • Rakhine
      		 The Mughals:
      • Gain +10 power projection
      • Gain a new general with:
        • 80 army tradition
        • +1 fire
        • +1 siege
      • Gain a permanent claim on Burma (region)
      		 Bengal Entrepot
      Upper Burma Campaign
      Across the Arakanese mountains lies a land rich in minerals and precious stones. Upper Burma is also an inhospitable region, home to the warlike Shan people and fought over for centuries. However, such a source of rubies, greater than any other on earth, we could never resist.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 25 provinces in Burma (region), including at least province Taungoo (585) The Mughals:
      • Get the event “Fall of Burma”
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Indochina (region)
        • North Tenasserim
      		 Follow the Brahmaputra
      Subah of Ayuddhya
      The city of Ayutthaya - or Ayuddhya - is perhaps the greatest of the cities of South East Asia but it is not the extent of our ambition in the region. Let us expand our influence over the entire Thai and Khmer Kingdoms, and add this land of a thousand elephants to the Mughal Empire.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 45 provinces in the following areas:
      • Indochina (region), including at least province Ayutthaya (600)
      • North Tenasserim
      		 The Mughals:
      • Gain +5 permanent power projection
      • Gain 100 administrative power
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Yun-Gui Frontier
        • Yun-Gui Hinterland
        • Annam
        • Guangxi
        • West Guangdong
        • East Guangdong
        • Sông Hông
      • If have not completed the mission “Fall of Coromandel”:
        • Unlock the "Malaccan Viceroyalty" mission
        • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
          • Malacca
          • North Malaya
          • Malaya
          • Johor
          • Minangkabau
          • Aceh
          • Lampung
          • Batak
          • Riau
          • Jambi
      		 Upper Burma Campaign
      Open China
      Our recent expansion has put us at the border of the Empire of China. The Middle Kingdom was always a distant giant. A country of as much wealth and population as the entire Indian subcontinent put together but where India was often fractured, China was stable and centralized. The Chinese seem unwilling to engage with us on our terms, but we must not let them dictate rules for the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] of the mighty Mughal Empire. Let us teach this old Empire what our newly forged state can accomplish...
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 20 provinces in the following areas:
      • Yun-Gui Frontier
      • Yun-Gui Hinterland
      • Annam
      • Guangxi
      • West Guangdong
      • East Guangdong
      • Sông Hông
      		 The Mughals:
      • Gain 100 administrative power
      • Gain 100 diplomatic power
      • Gain 100 military power
      • Gain +40 power projection
      • Gain “Humiliation of China” for 30 years, giving:
        • +100% Power projection from insults
        • +10% Global trade power
      		 Subah of Ayuddhya
      Malaccan Viceroyalty
      The Malay archipelago is a thriving center of commerce. Now that we have established more direct contact with this trade network through our acquisition of Southeast Asian ports it is only natural that we also seek a local headquarters from which we might control and tax the local traffic. Malacca is in many ways the ideal base of operations for us. Let us introduce this idea to the Malaccans themselves.
      		 The Mughals:
      • Control at least 50% of the trade in the Malacca trade node
      • Own or its non-tributary subjects own at least 30 provinces in the following areas:
        • Malacca
        • North Malaya
        • Malaya
        • Johor
        • Minangkabau
        • Aceh
        • Lampung
        • Batak
        • Riau
        • Jambi
      		 The Mughals:
      • Gain ducats equal to 1 year of income
      • Gain 3% mercantilism
      • Unlock the "Support Malaccan Viceroyalty" Jains privilege, allowing us to gain benefits for our Trade Companies

      Western conquest[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Pacify Punjab
      The land of five rivers has been home to powerful Afghan landholders ever since the original conquest by Mahmud of Gazni, hundreds of years ago. These local strongmen are in many ways an obstacle to our newborn empire and if we are to last we must use a firm hand to show that they are now expected to obey our law.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own the Lahore, Sirhind and Sind Sagar areas which:
      • Has less than 25% Local autonomy above minimum
      • Has less than 5 years of separatism
      		 The Mughals:
      • If has a stability of less than +3,
        • then it gains 1 stability,
        • else it gains 50 administrative power.
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Ghor
        • Sistan
        • Herat
        • Balkh
        • Kabulistan
      Enlighten Nepal
      Traveling monks have been hard at work in their attempt to show Nepal the way of the Buddha. We must assist their efforts by incorporating the Nepali people into our state.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 5 provinces with 'Nepali' culture The Mughals:
      • Gain 100 military power
      • Gain 50% cheaper skill 3 Commandant advisor with 'Nepali' culture and Hindu religion
      • Enable the "Elite Gurkhas" Mercenary company which:
        • Cost no Army professionalism
        • +15% Infantry combat ability
        • −10% Shock damage received
        • −10% Fire damage received
      		 Pacify Punjab
      Afghan Frontier
      Kabulistan and Sistan have long been the staging areas for those that would invade India. The region is both close to the vital passes for entering the subcontinent and home to a number of clans that have specialized in raiding and looting the Punjabi plain. We must subdue this region once and for all, and place strong garrisons here to protect against foreign invaders.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 16 provinces in the following areas:
      • Ghor
      • Sistan
      • Herat
      • Balkh
      • Kabulistan
      		 The Mughals:
      • Gain +15 power projection
      • Double the chance to gain the Conqueror trait
      • Gain a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Birjand
        • Mashhad
        • Merv
        • Kerman
        • Isfahan
        • Iraq-e-Ajam
        • Khuttalan
        • Transoxiana
        • Ferghana
      		 Pacify Punjab
      Abu Said's Dream
      Even many years after the fall of Timur his empire remained a goal and the dream of his descendants. Abu Said came closer to realizing the re-unification of that empire but eventually, he fell into what our ancestors call the great disaster of Iraq-Ajemi. Let us pick up this fallen mantle and reach further than those that came before us. We are the Gurkani, heirs to the great conquerors and fearful of no living men.
      		 The Mughals or its non-tributary subjects own at least 27 provinces in the following areas:
      • Birjand
      • Mashhad
      • Merv
      • Kerman
      • Isfahan
      • Iraq-e-Ajam
      • Khuttalan
      • Transoxiana
      • Ferghana
      • Gain a permanent claim on Persia (region)
      • If it is the Age of Discovery:
        • Gain “Heir of Timur” until the end of the Age of Discovery, giving:
          • −5% Core creation cost
          • −5% War exhaustion
      • Else:
        • Gain 100 military power
        • Gain 20 convertible[2] army tradition
      		 Afghan Frontier

      Religious tolerance[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects
      Indian Syncretism
      As our rule in India continues, the aspects of our religion, especially the Sufi elements, tend to mix together with local Hindu traditions. It is becoming more common that local Hindu population begins to consider Allah as one of their gods and are trying to fit Quran into their understanding of the world.
      		 The Mughals owns at least 5 provinces which:
      • Is Hindu
      • Has been developed at least 3 times
      		 The Mughals gain “Indic Syncretism” for the rest of the game, giving:
      • +1 Maximum tolerance of heathens

      Every owned province of Hindu religion:

      • Loses 10 years of separatism
      Indian Sufi Shrines
      Sufism has been closely associated with the spread of Islam in India for centuries and the subcontinent has been home to many great Sufi sheiks. As the defenders of the faithful, it is our mission to extend our protection to all the great Shrines of India.
      		 The Mughals:
      • Owns at least 5 provinces which:
        • Follows our religion
        • Has any Tax building
        • Has at least 14 development
      • If is Muslim:
        • At least one of:
          • Has less than −33 Piety
          • Has at least +75 Piety
      		 The Mughals:
      • If is Muslim and has at least +75 Piety:
        • Strengthen the "Clerical Ministers" Clergy privilege, doubling the Monthly piety
        • Lose 1 corruption
      • Else, if is Muslim and has less than −33 Piety:
        • Strengthen the "Freedom of Interpretation" Clergy privilege, giving −1 National unrest and an additional +10% Morale damage when we are at least 50 Piety towards Mysticism
        • Gain 1 year's worth of manpower
      • Else:
        • If has a stability of less than +3,
          • then it gains 1 stability,
          • else it gains 50 administrative power.
        • Gain 3 base tax in the capital
        • Gain 67% cheaper skill 3 Theologian advisor with our culture and religion
      		 Indian Syncretism
      Tolerant Society
      Made up of many religions and even more cultures, it is imperative that the Mughal Empire remains tolerant to them all. To be permissive of other rites may not be in favor of the Ulema but it would be in favor of our growth and survival.
      		 The Mughals:
      • Have at least 70% religious unity
      • Have at least 1 Tolerance of Heathens
      		 The Mughals:
      • Gain 60% cheaper skill 3 Theologian advisor with our culture and Hindu religion
      • Modify “Indic Syncretism” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +1 Tolerance of heathens
        • +2 Maximum tolerance of heathens
      		 Indian Syncretism
      Ibadat Khana
      Ibadat Khana or "House of Worship" is an establishment created to let all religions of the realm discuss the matters of the faith. Brought together into one room, theologians of all religions ponder various philosophical aspects of our lives.
      		 The Mughals:
      • Is ahead of time in every technology
      • Has at least 40 innovativeness
      		 The capital gains “House of Worship” for the rest of the game, giving:
      • −10% Idea cost
      • As long as its owner:
        • Has at least 95% religious unity
        • Is employing any Administrative advisor of at least level 3
        • Has at least +1 stability

      The Mughals:

      • If have country modifier “Ibadat Khana”
        • Gain 400 administrative power
        • Remove the Stability cost modifier penalty from 'Ibadat Khana' modifier
      • Else:
        • Gain “Ibadat Khana” for the rest of the game, giving:
          • −5% Idea cost
      		 Indian Sufi Shrines

      Tolerant Society

      Repeal Jizya
      Jizya tax is oppressive and unfair for the Hindu population. Let us lighten the tax or outright remove it to earn the favor of the native population. We can cover the economic impact in other ways.
      		 The Mughals:
      • Have at least 4 government reforms
      • Own at least 10 Hindu provinces which:
        • Has any Tax or Courthouse building
      • If the Dhimmi estate exists:
        • At least one of:
