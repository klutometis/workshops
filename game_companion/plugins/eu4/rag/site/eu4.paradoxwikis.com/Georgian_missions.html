Vakhtang's Succession
The fate of Georgia is closely tied to King Vakhtang and his succession. Until we have a legitimate heir, the nobility and members of the Bagrationi dynasty will continue to vie for the throne.
   Georgia:
  • At least one of:
    • Has at least   90 Legitimacy

   Georgia:

The central authority in Georgia is experiencing a prolonged decline. In order to prevent the approaching crisis, it is crucial that all territories under our governance are fully under the control of the royal seat in Tbilisi.

  Georgia:

Preventing the Collapse
We have triumphed in diverting the crisis. While the kingdom may need more time to completely rebound from the upheaval, we are hopeful that this period of recuperation will bring about a renewal of our strength, a resurgence in our economy and commerce, and usher in a new era of prosperity.
   Georgia:
  • At least one of:

      • Has no ongoing disaster

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Recover Strength
The preceding century has been a tumultuous period for Georgia, characterized by immense suffering and a gradual decline. We find ourselves transformed into a feeble nation, isolated politically, and besieged by adversaries from every direction. If we are to endure and thrive in this ever-changing world, we should embark on a journey to reclaim our strength and forge alliances. By revitalizing our influence and fostering strategic partnerships, we can navigate the challenges ahead and secure a prosperous future for our beloved homeland.
   Georgia:
  • At least one of:
    • Has at least   2 allies
    • Has at least   90% of its maximum Manpower reserves
    • Has an army of at least   90% of its force limit
   Georgia:
  • Gains “Resurgent Georgia” for   15 years, giving:
    •   −10% Core-creation cost
  • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
    • Armenia
    • Shirvan
    • Dagestan
    • Circassia
 
Queen Tamar's Ambition
Under the rule of Queen Tamar, our dominion expanded to encompass vast territories, including the lands of Armenia. However, it is disheartening to witness the subjugation of our fellow Christian brothers and sisters at the hands of the Turkmen forces who currently hold control. It is incumbent upon us to prioritize their liberation and reclaim these territories, reinstating Tbilisi's authority over them.
   Georgia or its non-tributary subjects owns the Armenia area   Georgia:
  • Gains   'Armenian' as an accepted culture
  • Gains “Armenian Union” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +1 Max promoted culture
    •   +10% Manpower in accepted culture provinces
  • Gets a 75% reduced cost skill 2   Commandant, who has Armenian Culture and Coptic Religion
  • Gains a permanent claim on the Azerbaijan area
   Recover Strength
 
Heirs of the Seljuks
While the powerful Seljuks have become a mere chapter in the annals of history, their descendants persist in governing extensive territories and retaining control over the Near East. It is high time to break the legacy of Seljuk and reclaim these lands, reinstating Christian authority over their lands. The cross shall triumph over the crescent!
   Georgia or its non-tributary subjects owns the Azerbaijan area   Georgia:
  • Ruler gains   1 Military skill[2]
  • Gains “Breaking the Legacy of Seljuk” for   20 years, giving:
    •   +10% Morale of armies
    •   +5% Recover army morale speed
  • Gains a permanent claim on all core provinces of   Qara Qoyunlu
  • If completes this mission while no country with the capital in the Persia region has more   total development than us:
    • Gains   'Subjugation' casus belli against all countries with the capital in Persia for   25 years
   Queen Tamar's Ambition
 
Reclaim Shirvan
In the past, the Shirvan region was under the sovereignty of Georgia, granting us valuable access to the wealth of the Caspian Sea. It is of utmost importance for us to regain control over this strategically significant province and restore it to our dominion.
   Georgia or its non-tributary subjects owns the Shirvan area Every province in the Shirvan area
  • 33.3% chance of: Gains   1 base tax
  • 33.3% chance of: Gains   1 base production
  • 33.3% chance of: Gains   1 base manpower
  • Gains “Rein in the Azeris” for   20 years, giving:
    •   +3% Local missionary strength
    •   −0.1 Monthly autonomy change

  Georgia gains a permanent claim on the following areas:

  • Tabarestan
  • Zanjan
   Recover Strength
 
The Gates of Alexander
We have successfully consolidated Georgia, but now it is time for us to expand our power to the territories that once belonged to us. Let us, therefore, cross the so-called Gates of Alexander and subjugate the tribes living north of the Caucasus.
   Georgia or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 5 provinces in the following areas:
  • Dagestan
  • Circassia
   Georgia:
  • Gains   100 administrative power
  • Gains “Caucasian Campaign” for   20 years, giving:
    •   +10% Manpower recovery speed
    •   −10% Land maintenance modifier
  • If has at least   2 subjects with their capitals in the Caucasia region:
    • Gainq “Consolidate Caucasia” for   15 years, giving:
      •   −20% Diplomatic annexation cost
  • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
    • Astrakhan
    • Azov
    • Crimea
   Recover Strength
 
Seize Crimea
The Crimean Peninsula serves as the final sanctuary for Tatar invaders who pose a danger to our kingdom. It is crucial that we confront them and assert our authority over the peninsula, thereby reinforcing our dominion over the Black Sea and creating fresh avenues for trade with Rus'.
   Georgia or its non-tributary subjects owns the Crimea area   Georgia:
  • Builds   5 light ships for free, taking 10% of normal time in a random owned port province
  • Gains “Expand the Trading Towns” for   10 years, giving:
    •   −20% Center of trade upgrade cost
    • Upgrading Centers of Trade grants   1 base production
   The Gates of Alexander
 
Monopolize the Black Sea
Our successful campaigns against the Tatars have resulted in the majority of the Black Sea coastline falling under our dominion. Our next step is to solidify our governance over this maritime territory and effectively leverage it for trade with the Greeks, Turks, and the Balkans.
   Georgia:
  • Has at least   10 light ships
  • Controls at least   50% of the trade in the Crimea trade node
  • Owns at least 4 provinces   center of trade of at least level 2
   Georgia:
  • Gains   1 year's worth of   trade income
  • Gets a 50% reduced cost skill 3   Trader, who has our Culture and Religion
  • Gains “Black Sea Hegemony” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +10% Domestic trade power
   Seize Crimea
 
Bane of the Khagan
The steppes north of the Caucasus are home to nomadic tribes, successors of Genghis Khan, who have become a scourge for the Orthodox world. It is time to put an end to their rule and permanently eliminate this threat.
   Georgia controls province Sarai (466)   Georgia:
  • Gets the   “Fall of Sarai” event
  • Gains “Bane of the Khagan” until ruler's death, giving:
    •   +10% Shock damage
    • Winning battles against 'Steppe Nomads' countries grants   +0.5 Army Tradition
   The Gates of Alexander
 
An Envoy to the Tsar
The Tsars of Muscovy have undoubtedly risen as the dominant power of all Rus'. It would be wise to send a diplomatic mission to establish some kind of relations with them. In Muscovy, we might find a valuable ally; or a formidable foe.
   Georgia:
  • If   Muscovy, else   Russia, exists:
    • At least one of:
      • Is   allied to   Muscovy /   Russia
      • All of:
        • Rivals   Muscovy /   Russia
        • Has a larger army than   Muscovy /   Russia
  • Else:
    • Owns or non-tributary subjects owns at least 10 provinces in the 'East Slavic' culture group
   Georgia:
  • If completes this mission by forging an alliance with   Muscovy /   Russia:
    • Creates   20 streltsy infantry regiment in our capital
    • Gains “Alliance with the Tsar” for the rest of the game, giving:
      •   +1 Diplomatic relation
  • If completes this mission by forging rivaling   Muscovy /   Russia:
    • Gains   'Restoration of Union' casus belli against   Muscovy /   Russia for   25 years
  • Else:
    • Gains “Georgian Embassies” for   25 years, giving:
      •   +30% Spy network construction
      • Can claim entire states
   Bane of the Khagan

Deal with the Komnenids
The Trapezuntine Empire has been a longstanding friend throughout our history, a natural ally in our quest for survival. It is crucial for us to strengthen our bond with them, fostering a closer relationship and potentially considering a vassal arrangement. We must not forget that the Empire owes its establishment, in part, to the assistance provided by our Queen Tamar. It may be worth reminding them of this shared history and the significance of our past collaboration.
 At least one of:
  •   Trebizond:
    • Is our   ally
    • Has at least   +190 opinion towards us
    • Owes us at least 20 favors
    • Has at least 80 trust with us
  • All of:
    •   Georgia owns province Trebizond (330)
    •   Trebizond does not exist
   Georgia:
  • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
    • Kurdistan
    • Erzurum
  • If completes this mission by an alliance with   Trebizond:

  • If completes this mission by conquering Trebizond:
    • Province Trebizond (330):
      • Loses   100 years of separatism
      • Loses   100% local autonomy
 
Slay the White Sheep
The Turkomans have long posed a persistent threat to the safety and sovereignty of our kingdom. The looming specter of their victory and subsequent subjugation of our land has left us with no choice but to act swiftly and decisively. The time has come to take preventive measures, launching a proactive offensive and neutralizing the imminent danger they pose. The white sheep shall be slain!
   Georgia or its non-tributary subjects owns the following areas:
  • Kurdistan
  • Erzurum
   Georgia:
  • Gains a   general with:
    • If has less than 50 army tradition:   50 army tradition
    • If has between 51 and 74 army tradition:   75 army tradition
    • Else:   100 army tradition
  • Gains “Counter the Turkoman Tactics” for   20 years, giving:
    •   −10% Shock damage received
  • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
    • Al Jazira
    • Shahrizor
    • Iraq Arabi
    • Dulkadir
    • Çukurova
    • Rum
    • Karaman
    • Karadeniz
    • Aleppo

Encroach on the Mamluks
The weakness of the Catholic forces led to the loss of the Holy Land to the Saracens. However, we are determined to learn from their mistakes and emerge triumphant in our quest to restore the Levant under the righteous rule of the once proud religious icon. With unwavering determination, we will rectify the past failures, reclaiming the holy sites and reinstating the dominance of our faith in the region. Our resolute efforts shall serve as a beacon of hope and inspiration, ensuring that the light of the Holy Cross shines brightly once again in the lands of the Levant.
   Georgia or its non-tributary subjects owns the following areas:
  • Al Jazira
  • Aleppo
   Georgia:
  • Gains   100 military power
  • If is the   Defender of the Faith:
    • Gains   2 years' worth of manpower
  • Gains “Wrath of Christendom” for   20 years, giving:
    •   −10% War score cost vs other religions
    •   “Holy War” and “Purging of Heresy” Casus Belli against heathens and heretics respectively.
  • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
    • Province Cyprus (321)
    • Syria
    • Palestine
    • Syrian Desert
    • Transjordan
   Slay the White Sheep
 
Conquer Cyprus
Cyprus holds a paramount position as a strategic stronghold in the Eastern Mediterranean. Recognizing the immense importance of this island, it is crucial for us to forge alliances with the local Crusaders, forming a united front to solidify and expand our influence across the region. By doing so, we aim to consolidate our dominion and ensure our supremacy in this critical part of the world.
   Georgia owns province Cyprus (321)   Georgia:
  • Gains   1 000 sailors
  • Gains “Control East Mediterranean” for   20 years, giving:
    •   +20% Ship trade power
  • Gains a permanent claim on the following provinces:
    • Rhodes (320)
    • Crete (163)
   Encroach on the Mamluks
 
Cradle of Civilization
Since time immemorial, Mesopotamia has served as the focal point for numerous influential civilizations. It is time to liberate it from Muslim rule and harness its abundance for our own advantage.
   Georgia or its non-tributary subjects owns the Iraq Arabi area Every province in the Iraq Arabi area:

For as long as Jerusalem is dominated by the Saracen, a dark cloud shall loom over the name of Christendom. To reclaim the heartlands of Christ is the ultimate salvation - and our rise to make this reclamation shall prove an everlasting testimony of tireless faith. The Holy City must belong to us.

Centuries have passed since the fall of the Eastern patriarchates into the hands of Muslims, and since then, Christians have lived under their cruel domination. Our holy legions will ensure the liberation of these cities and their restoration. Let us remind the world that the light of the East has not yet been extinguished!

The Battle of Manzikert stands as a turning point in the history of Anatolia. Following the Byzantine Empire's defeat, the Turkic forces swiftly expanded their presence, firmly establishing their control over the region. It is up to us to succeed where they faltered, seek retribution for Manzikert, and emerge as the new hegemon of the region.

The Empire's Downfall
The Ottoman Empire has emerged as the dominant force in the Balkans and the Middle East, posing a significant threat to the entire Christian world. It is imperative to overthrow this empire of non-believers and consign it to the pages of history.
   Georgia or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 12 provinces in the following areas:
  • Karadeniz

   Georgia:

  • Gains a permanent claim on the Thrace area
   Anatolian Incursion
 
Throne of the Romans
The great city of Constantinople has been captured by heathen forces, an event that has brought shame and disgrace upon the illustrious legacy of the Romans. It is our sacred duty to embark on a holy mission to free this precious jewel of the Christian world from its oppressors and, under our governance, to revive its past splendor. With a Georgian Emperor on the Roman Throne, Constantinople shall be restored to its rightful place of glory and magnificence.
   Georgia owns core province Constantinople (151)   Georgia:

We have succeeded in our holy mission to restore Christianity's might. Standing as the shield of the true faith, it is time to prove our piety and God-given righteousness to the world - to make our realm a united paradise of Christendom!

Rebuild Tbilisi
For many years, our capital city has endured hardship and deterioration caused by enemy invasions within our country. What once was a magnificent city, a jewel of the Caucasus, has now become a mere impoverished urban area. The responsibility lies with us to revive its former grandeur.
 Province Tbilisi (423):
  • Is owned by   Georgia
  • Has less than   1 devastation
  • At least one of:
    • Has been developed at least 4 times
    • Has no remaining building slots available
 Province Tbilisi (423):
  • Gains   100 progress towards prosperity
  • If   Georgia has not embraced the   Renaissance institution:
    • Gains 25% progress towards the   Renaissance institution
  • Gains “Restore Tbilisi's Glory” for   50 years, giving:
    •   −35% Local development cost
 
Georgian Vineyards
The wine tradition in Georgia has deep roots in history, spanning centuries, if not millennia. With wine being one of our most vital commodities, it is important for us to strive towards establishing its global recognition and appreciation.
   Georgia owns at least 5 provinces which:
  • Produces   Wine
  • Has at least   4 Base production
  • Has any manufactory
 Every province which fulfills the requirements:
  • Gains   1 base production
  • Gains “Georgian Vineyards” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   −1 Local unrest

Revive Native Literature
Throughout the ages, our civilization has developed three distinct scripts - Asomtavruli, Nuskhuri, and Mkhedruli - each representing our rich heritage. Among them, Mkhedruli stands as the script of our land, distinct from its association with the sacredness of the church. It is this script that we shall proudly promote, enhance, and disseminate among our educated and esteemed elite. We strive for the widespread adoption of our language and writing across the entire region of Caucasia.
   Georgia:
  • Has embraced the   Renaissance institution
  • At least one of:
    • Has at least   +50 Prestige
    • Is employing any advisor of at least level 2
   Georgia:
  • Gains   100 splendor
  • Gains “The Second Golden Age” for   20 years, giving:
    •   −20% Cost of advisors with ruler's culture
    •   +20% Reform progress growth
   Rebuild Tbilisi
 
Legacy of the Builder
Under the rule of King David, who was renowned as the Builder, Georgia flourished in a golden age. Let us take inspiration from his remarkable legacy and work towards bestowing our cities with fame, wealth, and significance.
   Georgia owns at least 10 provinces with no remaining building slots available   Georgia:
  • Triples the chances of our rulers and heirs having the   Architectural Visionary personality trait (  −10% Construction Cost)

Every province in the Samtskhe, Imereti and Kartli-Kakheti areas:

  • Gains “Legacy of the Builder” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +1 Possible number of buildings
   Revive Native Literature
 
Svetitskhoveli Cathedral
The Svetitskhoveli Cathedral, renowned as the Cathedral of the Living Pillar, holds immense reverence as one of the holiest sites in the Caucasus and stands as the focal point of the Georgian Apostolic Church. Regrettably, the passage of time has left its mark on this sacred edifice, closely intertwined with the decline of our nation. Now is the opportune moment to embark on a comprehensive reconstruction project, breathing new life into the cathedral so that it may radiate the renewed might, resilience, and prestige of Georgia.
   Georgia:
  • Owns province Tbilisi (423) with any Tax building
  • If the   Clergy estate exists:

    • At least one of:

  • Gets a 50% reduced cost skill 3   Inquisitor, whose has our Culture and Religion

  • Else:
    • Gains “Religious Mission” for the rest of the game, giving:
      •   +0.25 Prestige per development from missionary
   Revive Native Literature

Our religion and the church stand as foundational pillars of our identity. The Georgian Church holds a position of great antiquity and reverence worldwide.
