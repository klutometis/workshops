      The Rajputs are an ancient caste of honorable warriors and proud nobles. While the rugged land of Rajasthan is the heart of their power, Rajput nobility can be found throughout northern India and many great Rajas claim Rajput descent.

      Availability[edit | edit source]

      Cultures in blue provinces qualify for the Rajput estate while those in red provinces qualify for the Marathas estate.

      General availability:

      • Available to all countries in the Indian technology group if the primary culture is Rajasthani/ Malvi or part of the Hindustani, Central Indian or Eastern Aryan group (except Sinhalese). (see Map).

      Specific conditions:

      • Does not use other mechanics
      • Does not have Celestial Empire government reform
      • Is not nomad
      • The Mughals may replace their Rajputs estate with the Marathas estate via the "The Maratha Caste" event, triggered by the "Control the Desh" mission

      For nations that are not Muslim, Sikh, Theravada, Vajrayana or Mahayana, this estate replaces the Nobility Estate. (this mainly concerns Hindu nations)

      Influence[edit | edit source]

      Influence – The estate has a base influence of 10%, modified by:

      • +10% Has Rajput as the primary culture or as an accepted culture
      • +5% Has the Feudal Nobility Tier 1 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • +5% Has the Noble Elite Tier 1 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • +10% Has the Strengthen Noble Privileges Tier 2 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • +5% Has the Grant Noble Castle Rights Tier 2 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • +10% Has the Nobles' Electorate Tier 2 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • +5% Has the Noble Officer Corps Tier 2 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • +5% Is Muslim and has the Meritocratic Rule Tier 2 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • +5% Has the Virtues of Politeia Tier 2 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • +5% Has enacted the Expanded Royal Court Tier 3 government reform
      • +10% Has the Governmental Purbias Register Tier 5 government reform
      • +10% Is Muslim and has the Meritocratic Recruitment Tier 5 government reform
      • +10% Has the Divine Nobility Tier 5 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • +5% Has enacted the General Estates tier 6 government reform
      • −5% Is not Hindu and has at least one Hindu or Sikh province
      • -10% Has the Curtail Noble Privileges Tier 2 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • -15% Has the Puppeteer the Nobility Tier 2 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • −5% Has enacted the Royal Favoritism tier 3 government reform
      • -15% Has enacted the Abolish the Castes tier 4 government reform
      • −10% Has enacted the Dynastic Administration tier 5 government reform
      • −5% Has enacted the Subservient Bureaucrats tier 5 government reform
      • −5% Has enacted the Les Six livres de la République tier 8 government reform
      • −40% If another estate has seized control of the nation (via disaster)

      Loyalty[edit | edit source]

      Loyalty – The estate's loyalty equilibrium is modified by:

      • +10% For Corruption at 100% (scaled)
      • +10% For completing the mission Unite West India available to Hindustan/Bharat
      • +5% Has Rajput as the primary culture or as an accepted culture
      • +10% Has the Nobles' Electorate Tier 2 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the Decentralized Bureaucracy tier 3 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the Maintain Balance of Power tier 4 government reform
      • +5% Has the Governmental Purbias Register Tier 5 government reform
      • +5% Has the Divine Nobility Tier 5 government reform and does not have the Nobility estate
      • +10% Has enacted the General Estates tier 6 government reform
      • +10% Has enacted the Supremacy of the Diet tier 6 government reform
      • +5% For Army Tradition at 100 (scaled)
      • −5% to +5% with current Prestige
      • -5% Has the Puppeteer the Nobility Tier 2 government reform
      • -5% Has enacted the Centralized Bureaucracy tier 3 government reform
      • −20% If another estate has seized control of the nation (via disaster)

      Rebels: If the Rajput are disloyal after land has been seized, Noble rebels may rise in revolt in a few provinces.

      Disaster: If the Rajputs estate reaches 100 influence, their estate disaster will begin ticking and - unless lowered - will eventually trigger the Rajput Independence disaster.
