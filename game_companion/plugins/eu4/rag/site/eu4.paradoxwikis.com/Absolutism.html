      Absolutism

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
      At least some were last verified for version 1.28.

      Absolutism means unrestricted and unlimited rule and is represented in EU4 by how ruthless and efficient a government is. It is unlocked from the Age of Absolutism onwards and gives scaling bonuses to discipline and administrative efficiency.

      At 100 Absolutism, the following benefits come into effect:

      +5% Discipline
      +30% Administrative efficiency
      −50% Foreign core duration
      −50% Monthly decadence

      Changing absolutism[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.31.5.2.
      Increasing modifiers
      +2 Strengthen government action
      +1
      • Increasing stability
      • Decreasing autonomy (per 20 development)
      • Harsh treatment action
      Decreasing modifiers
      −1
      • Decreasing war exhaustion
      • Debasing currency
      −2 Increasing autonomy (per 20 development)
      −10 Accepting rebel demands
      −0.1 Assigning a seat in parliament (per province development)

      Making use of the Harsh treatment action will produce absolutism (once the mechanic is unlocked). This is a powerful method to quickly gain absolutism during that age while reducing headaches from rebels. The cost of using Harsh Treatment can be significantly reduced through the Age of Absolutism ability "Harsher Treatment" (with Mandate of Heaven) granting a −50% cost reduction, and a few mission trees have a Harsh Treatment discount −25% that can be "saved" for use during the Age of Absolutism. Combining these two elements can gain +1 absolutism from as little as 8 military points if the rebel faction it is used on is small.

      An expert player trick to gain a large once-off increase in absolutism at the beginning of the Age of Absolutism is to accept Particularist rebel demands (affecting all accepted culture provinces) a few months before the previous age ends (the Age of Reformation). Accepting particularist rebel demands will increase autonomy on all accepted culture provinces by +30% at the cost of −20 prestige, increased estate influence and crown land. The moment the Age of Absolutism starts, be sure to be at peace and reduce the autonomy on all these affected provinces. High development provinces will yield the biggest gain in absolutism and the player will gain some reprieve from the extra unrest caused by the reduction in autonomy since each province will have the modifier "Recent uprising" giving −100 unrest for ten years. This exercise can comfortably gain 20 to 30 absolutism on a decent size empire with a large number of accepted cultures.

      Being a horde using this method and accepting the tribal rebel demands is even better as nearly all provinces are affected (except a few trade company provinces), whereas particularist rebels only affect accepted culture full core provinces.

      The remainder of the absolutism needed to trigger the Court And Country disaster can be gained from the harsh treatment or strengthen government method.

      Timing the integration of a large vassal or personal union subject at the beginning of the age can offer an additional opportunity to reduce autonomy on a large number of newly integrated provinces.

      Absolutism can also be changed through event choices, especially during the Court And Country disaster.

      Changing maximum absolutism[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.30.

      The base value for maximum absolutism is +65. While there is no limit to the maximum absolutism, the effects of absolutism are capped at 100 absolutism. The excess absolutism over this limit is only useful as a "buffer".

      However, it should be noted that some government reforms and missions rewards allow the cap of maximum effect of absolutism to be higher than 100 (see below).

      Absolutism modifiers[edit | edit source]

      • +65 Base value
      • +15 by ending the Janissary Coup disaster by killing the Janissaries
      • +10 from the Political Absolutism T11 reform for Monarchies
      • +5 from Empire government rank
      • +5 from Great power status
      • +5 during a Golden era
      • +5 for tributaries after enacting the Celestial Reform "Reign in the Estates"
      • 0 to +5 scaling linearly with Religious unity
      • 0 to +50, if Absolute Power is positive during the Parliamentary-Monarchy Struggle
      • −10 to +10 scaling linearly with Legitimacy. Note that with Mandate of Heaven the Celestial Empire does not use Legitimacy and cannot get modifiers from it.
      • −20 during the Court And Country disaster
      • Permanent +20, permanent +10, or temporary −10, depending on the resolution of the Court And Country disaster where absolutism at the time of resolution is respectively 65 or higher, 30-64, or 0-29.
      • −40 from being a republic.
      • −50 to +15 depending on crown land, and numerous −5/−10/−15/−20 modifiers from privileges granted to the estates
        • +1/+2/+3/+4 any privilege if the country has the Court Idea 5 "Respected Authority" (lessens the −5/−10/−15/−20 by 20%, no effect on privileges that don't reduce max Absolutism)
        • +10 "Mansabdar - Royal Rule" / "Legitimization of the Dynasty" Privilege
        • +5 "Outwards Perfection" Privilege

      Maximum effect of absolutism[edit | edit source]

      While the effects of absolutism reach their cap at 100 absolutism, a nation can profit from absolutism over 100 with an increase in maximum effect of absolutism.

      Sources of maximum effect of absolutism:

      • +10% from the Holy Imperial Monarchy T1 reform for the Holy Roman Empire
      • +20% from the Imperial mission Among the Great Powers
      • +20% from the Strengthened Bakuhan System T10 reform for Japan after completing the "Strengthened Bakuhan" mission
      • +30% from the "French Absolutism" Age Ability for France during the Age of Absolutism
      • +10% from the Russian mission Russian Revolution
      • +10% from the T3 Monument Brandenburg Gate, if the Prussian mission The Balance of Power was completed
      • +10% from the Emperor of China mission Heaven's Will
      • +10% as a possible reward from the Chinese mission Path of Expansion
      • +15% from the Great Moravia final idea ambition
      • +10% as a possible reward from the Netherlands mission Fate of the Confederation
      • +10% available to the Mughals after completing the Emperor of Hindustan mission and enacting the Commission the Peacock Throne decision
      • +10% from the Bohemia mission Peace in Europe
      • +10% from the Hungary mission The Imperial Ambition if HRE not exist
      • +5% from the Mughals mission Warehouse of the East
