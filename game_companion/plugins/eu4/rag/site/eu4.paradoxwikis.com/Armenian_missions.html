      Conquest missions[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.36.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Breaking the Chains
      For far too long, our great nation has been under the yoke of the Turkmen Qara Qoyunlu. Our people have borne the brunt of their rule, the chains of subservience weighing heavy on our national spirit. But no more. The time has come to rise up, to fight for our independence, to shatter the chains that have bound us.
      		 The country:
      • At least one of:
        • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
        • Is a subject nation whose overlord:
          • Is not Qara Qoyunlu
          • Has at least +190 opinion towards us
      		 The country:
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Armenia
        • Shirvan
        • Azerbaijan
      • If completes this mission by becoming independent:
        • If has a stability of less than +3,
          • then it gains 1 stability,
          • else it gains 50 administrative power.
        • Gains 100 administrative power
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
        • Gains 100 military power
        • Gains “Armenian Resurgence” for 20 years, giving:
          • −10% Aggressive expansion impact
      • If we complete this mission while a vassal:
        • Loses 50% liberty desire
      Caspian Riches
      The Caspian Sea has been a nexus of trade and cultural exchange for millennia, a link between east and west, north and south. Yet, we have been too long denied access to its wealth and opportunities. Now is the time for us to secure our rightful place on its shores and tap into the abundant riches it offers.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns the Shirvan area Province Shirvan (421):
      • Creates a level 1 center of trade

      The country:

      • Gains “Harness the Caspian Riches” for 15 years, giving:
        • +10% Trade efficiency
        • If completes this mission while controlling at least 15% of trade in the Persia trade node:
          • The duration of this modifier is doubled
      		 Breaking the Chains
      Overthrow the Turkmen
      The Turkmen of Qara Qoyunlu have been a thorn in our side for far too long. Their oppressive rule, their blatant disregard for our faith and heritage, their constant threats to our sovereignty, all this has caused immense suffering. It is time for us to end their reign of tyranny. By overthrowing the Turkmen, we will be safeguarding our future and making a strong statement to the world: that we will never bow to tyranny and that the Armenian spirit is indomitable.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns the Azerbaijan area The country:
      • Discovers all provinces in the Khorasan region
      • Gains −15 Aggressive Expansion with all neighbouring countries
      • Gains “Dethrone the Turkomans” for 15 years, giving:
        • +15% Shock damage
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Shahrizor
        • Iraq Arabi
        • Luristan
        • Zanjan
      		 Breaking the Chains
      Heritage of the Artaxiads
      The Artaxiads, one of the greatest dynasties in Armenian history, ruled over a vast empire that stretched beyond our current borders, deep into Persia. They epitomized the might and splendor of our nation in its prime. It's our duty to honor their legacy and reclaim the territories that were once under Armenian control. Persia, a land rich in culture and resources, has been a historical battleground of great empires. It is time for us to make our mark on this land. We must prepare for an incursion into Persia, driven by the ambition to expand our borders, assert our influence, and reclaim the glory of the Artaxiad era.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 10 provinces in Persia region The country:
      • Gains 25 convertible[2] prestige
      • Gains “Legacy of the Artaxiads” for 20 years, giving:
        • −20% Core-creation cost
      • Gains a permanent claim on the Persia region
      		 Overthrow the Turkmen
      The Church of the East
      The cradle of civilizations, Mesopotamia, now languishes under foreign rule, its Christian population yearning for liberation. The land of the Tigris and Euphrates is crying out for deliverance, and it is our duty to heed this call. We must march eastward, to free our brethren and restore the splendor of Christendom in these ancient lands.
      		 All provinces in the Iraq Arabi area:
      • Are owned by the country or its non-tributary subjects
      • Follow our religion
      		 The country:
      • If is Coptic:
        • Gets “The Church of the East” event
      • Else:
        • Gains “The Church of the East” for 30 years, giving:
          • +1 Missionary
          • +0.3 Prestige per development from missionary
      		 Overthrow the Turkmen
      Liberation of the Homeland
      The Turkmen tribe of Qara Qoyunlu, often referred to as the Black Sheep Turkomans, have long encroached upon and now control large swathes of our ancestral Armenian lands. It is a matter of national pride and duty that we liberate these lands and free our brethren from their reign. The liberation of our homeland is the key to fully realize the dream of a united and Great Armenia.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns all provinces in the Armenia area The ruler:
      • Gains 2 Military skill.[3]
      • Gains “Liberator of the Homeland” until ruler's death, giving :
        • +10% Morale of armies
        • +5% Recover army morale speed

      The country:

      • Gains cores on all provinces of Armenian culture
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Kartli-Kakheti
        • Samtskhe
        • Imereti
      		 Breaking the Chains
      Bagrationi Union
      Georgia, our northern neighbor and long-time ally, is teetering on the brink of civil war. Factions vie for power, threatening the unity and stability of this once-prosperous nation. We must not stand idle while our brethren in faith are descending into chaos.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 6 provinces in the following areas:
      • Kartli-Kakheti
      • Samtskhe
      • Imereti
      		 The country:
      • If 'Georgian' is not accepted culture:
        • Gains 'Georgian' as an accepted culture
      • Else:
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
      • Gains “Georgian Union” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +1 Max promoted culture
        • +10% Manpower in accepted culture provinces
      • Gains a permanent claim on the Caucasia region

      Every owned province of 'Georgian' culture:

      • Loses 50% local autonomy
      		 Liberation of the Homeland
      Secure Caucasia
      Caucasian lands have experienced their fair share of strife and division, and it is time to bring unity and stability under our just rule. This is not an act of domination, but a call for unity and cooperation amongst brethren. With our forces safeguarding these lands and our policies ensuring their prosperity, we can create a secure and flourishing Caucasia, a beacon of unity in a fractured world.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns all provinces in the Caucasia region The country gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
      • Astrakhan
      • Azov
      • Crimea

      All provinces in the Caucasia region gains “Caucasian Union” for 20 years, giving:

      • +20% Local manpower modifier
      • −2 Local unrest

      Ten randomly owned 'Caucasian' culture provinces:

      • Gains 1 base manpower
      		 Bagrationi Union
      Proclaim Great Armenia
      Our great nation has come a long way, standing tall against formidable foes, reclaiming lands of religious importance, and restoring our faith to prominence. Yet, there is still a mission that beckons us, one that will bring us closer to our ancestral roots and promise a new era of prosperity - the reconquest of our ancestral lands. The provinces lost in previous centuries are not just territories; they are the heart and soul of our Armenian identity. Only by uniting these lands under our rule can we truly say that Armenia has been restored to its ancient glory. The journey will be fraught with challenges, but once completed, we can stand proudly and proclaim the advent of Great Armenia, marking the beginning of a new Golden Age.
      		 The country:
      • Is Armenia
      • Owns all provinces of Armenian culture
      		 The country:
      • Gets the “Rebirth of Great Armenia” event
      • If is a great power:
        • Gains 200 splendor
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Dulkadir
        • Erzurum
        • Rum
        • Karadeniz
        • Karaman
        • Aleppo
        • Syria
        • Syrian Desert
        • Al Jazira
        • Kurdistan
      		 Liberation of the Homeland

      Bagrationi Union

      Shatter Osman's Legacy
      The rise of the Ottoman Empire has dramatically shifted the balance of power in Anatolia. They have spread their dominion far and wide, establishing a hegemony that has stifled the growth and independence of many nations, including our own. However, every empire has its twilight, and the time has come to challenge the Ottomans and shatter Osman's legacy.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 12 provinces in the following areas:
      • Dulkadir
      • Rum
      • Karadeniz
      • Karaman
      		 The country:
      • Gains “New Anatolian Hegemon” for 20 years, giving:
        • +0.25 Military tactics
        • +20% Manpower recovery speed
      • Gains a permanent claim on the Anatolia region
      • If completes this mission while the Ottomans are our Rivals or Enemies:
        • Gains +25 power projection

      Every owned province of 'Turkish' culture:

      • Loses 100% local autonomy
      • Loses 20 years of separatism
      		 Proclaim Great Armenia
      Cilician Rebirth
      In the southeastern corner of Anatolia, where the towering Taurus mountains meet the azure Mediterranean, lies the ancient land of Cilicia. This historic land was once home to a flourishing Armenian kingdom, the Kingdom of Cilicia, a beacon of Armenian culture and might. The time has come to rekindle the spirit of the Cilician Kingdom and repopulate this region with our people.
      		 Provinces Adana (327), Marash (332) and Ayntab (4298):
      • Are owned by the country or its non-tributary subjects
      • Follow our religion
      • Has less than 1 year of separatism
      		 The country gains “Reunite the Diaspora” for the rest of the game, giving:
      • −10% Culture conversion cost

      Provinces Adana (327), Marash (332) and Ayntab (4298):

      • Adopts our culture
      • If has completed the "Resettle Ancestral Lands" mission:
        • Gains 1 base tax
        • Gains 1 base manpower
        • Gains 1 base production
      		 Shatter Osman's Legacy
      Levantine Incursion
      The Mamluks, who currently reign over the Levant, are a formidable power in the region. They stand as a strong barrier to our holy cause of expanding the Christian faith. Their rule has not only hindered our religious mission but also threatens the balance of power in the region. A crusade against them, while daunting, is the only path forward.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 12 provinces in the following areas:
      • Aleppo
      • Syria
      • Syrian Desert
      • Al Jazira
      		 The country:
      • Gains a general with:
        • If has less than 50 army tradition: 50 army tradition
        • If has at least 50 and less than 75 army tradition: 75 army tradition
        • Else: 100 army tradition
      • Gains “A New Crusade” for 20 years, giving:
        • +3% Missionary strength vs heathens
        • −50% Missionary maintenance cost
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Palestine
        • Transjordan
      		 Proclaim Great Armenia
      Control the Holy Land
      The Holy Land has always been a focal point of the Christian world. Yet, it is currently under the control of forces that do not share our faith and reverence for these sacred lands. The city of Jerusalem, the symbolic heart of Christianity, must be returned to Christian hands.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns the Palestine area The country:
      • If DLC 'Leviathan' is active:
        • Adds 1 tier to the great project "Holy City of Jerusalem" in Jerusalem (379)
      • Else:
        • Empowers the "Conquest of Jerusalem" triggered province modifier by:
          • +0.5 Yearly Prestige
          • +1% Missionary strength vs Heretics
      • Gains a permanent claim on the Egypt region
      • If is Coptic and the Defender of the Faith title is vacant:
        • Becomes the Defender of the Faith
      		 Levantine Incursion
      Coptic Restoration
      In the times of ancient power and glory, the Coptic Church was the bedrock of faith and culture in Egypt. Yet, centuries of foreign domination have led to the marginalization of the Coptic community, with the once prominent church's influence substantially diminished. Our Armenian crusade is not only for liberation but also for the restoration of the spiritual heart of the Coptic community. Let us restore the glory of the Coptic Church and ensure the return of the Copts to prominence in Egypt.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 12 provinces in Egypt following our religion
      • Owns at least 12 provinces in Egypt with less than 1 year of separatism
      • Owns at least 12 provinces in Egypt with less than 10% Local autonomy
      		 The country:
      • If is Coptic and DLC 'Origins' is active:
        • Gets “The Coptic Restoration” event
      • Else:
        • Gains 100 administrative power
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
        • Gains 100 military power
        • Every province in the Egypt region gains “Coptic Restoration” for the rest of the game, giving:
          • −2 Local unrest
          • −10% Local development cost
          • +10% Local goods produced modifier
      		 Control the Holy Land

      Cultural missions[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.36.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      National Awakening
      The dawn of our independence heralds a period of national awakening - a time for cultural revival and a rediscovery of our identity as a proud, resilient nation. This is the moment to ignite the creative and intellectual spirit of Armenia, nurturing our arts, literature, sciences, and faith, all elements that define our cultural heritage. We must foster an environment that encourages cultural expression, intellectual curiosity, and spiritual growth.
      		 The country has at least 80 development in owned provinces which:
      • Have the culture of our country
      		 The country:
      • Gains 50 government reform progress
      • Gains “National Awakening” for 20 years, giving:
        • −20% Culture conversion cost
        • −20% Cost of advisors with ruler's culture
      • If completes this mission while our capital has at least 20 development:
        • Gains or upgrades local Center of Trade. If there is already a Level 3 Center of Trade:
          • Gains 3 base production
      Develop the Wool Industry
      Nestled between our mountains, our fertile valleys are home to large flocks of sheep, their wool a potential goldmine. It's high time we invested in the development of a flourishing wool industry, turning these flocks into a source of prosperity for our nation.
      		 The country owns at least 8 Wool producing provinces which:
      • Has at least 3 development
      • Has any production building
      		 The country:
      • Gets a 75% reduced cost skill 3 Natural Scientist, who has our Culture and Religion

      Every owned province fulfilling the trigger:

      • Gains 1 base production
      • Gains “Booming Wool Industry” for 20 years, giving:
        • +20% Local goods produced modifier
      		 National Awakening
      Rebuild Tigranakert
      Tigranakert, named after our renowned King Tigranes the Great, was once a jewel of Armenian culture and might, an embodiment of our golden age. However, the ravages of time and conflict have left this once-thriving city in ruins. Our ancestral legacy calls out to us from these stones, imploring us to restore Tigranakert to its former glory.
      		 The country owns province Diyarbakir (418) which:
      • Follows our religion
      • Has our culture
      • Has at least 15 development
      • Has any Tax building
      • Has any Trade building
      • Has any Production building
      		 Province Diyarbakir (418):
      • Is renamed "Tigranakert"
      • Has its provincial capital renamed "Tigranakert"
      • Changes its goods produced to Cloth
      • Gains “The Great City of Tigranes” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −35% Local development cost
        • +10 Trade value

      The country:

      • Unlocks a decision allowing us to move our capital to Tigranakert
      Resettle Ancestral Lands
      Throughout centuries, Armenians have been scattered across the globe, forming a rich and vibrant diaspora. Yet, the call of the homeland resonates in every Armenian heart, no matter how distant. It is time for our diaspora to unite and resettle our ancestral lands. Reclaiming and resettling these regions is more than a geopolitical strategy; it is a journey towards cultural renaissance, a beacon of hope for all Armenians, a testament to our unity and resilience.
      		 The country owns at least 15 provinces with our culture The country:
      • Converting provinces to our culture now gives 1 base Tax, Production and Manpower development
      • Gains “Resettle the Ancestral Lands” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −20% Culture conversion time
      		 Develop the Wool Industry

      Rebuild Tigranakert

      Religious missions[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.36.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Ecclesiastical Revival
      For centuries, the Apostolic Armenian Church has been a symbol of our nation's enduring faith and spirit, despite being subjugated under foreign rulers. Now, with our nation on the path of resurgence, it is time for an ecclesiastical revival, for the Church to once again become a pillar of strength and guidance for our people.
      		 The country:
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects owns at least 12 provinces following our faith
      • If the Clergy estate exists:
        • Has at least 60 clergy loyalty
        • Has completed at least 3 Clergy agendas
      		 The country:
      • Gains “Ecclesiastical Revival” for 20 years, giving:
        • +20% Manpower in true faith provinces

      Five randomly owned provinces following our faith:

      • Gains 1 base tax
      Etchmiadzin Cathedral
      The Etchmiadzin Cathedral, a beacon of our faith and our history, has stood tall amidst trials and tribulations, bearing witness to the resilience of the Armenian people. But time and conflict have left their marks on this sacred edifice. The cathedral, like our nation, yearns for restoration and revival.
      		 The country:
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects owns at least 5 provinces with any Tax building
      • Great project "Etchmiadzin Cathedral" in Yerevan (419) is at least noteworthy
      		 The country:
      • Gets a 75% reduced cost skill 3 Inquisitor, who has our Culture and Religion
      • Gains “Restored the Etchmiadzin Cathedral” for 10 years, giving:
        • +3 Tolerance of the true faith

      Three randomly owned provinces with a Temple:

      • Upgrades into a Cathedral
      		 Ecclesiastical Revival
      Control the Holy Sites
      Eastern Christianity has borne the brunt of conflict and subjugation for far too long. Our sacred sites, from Alexandria, the center of Coptic Orthodoxy, to Aleppo, a city of profound Christian heritage, remain under foreign dominance. It is our duty to reconquer and protect these hallowed grounds.
      		 The country:
      • If is Coptic:
        • At least 3 of the following provinces are owned by a Coptic country:
          • Alexandria (358)
          • Aksum (1227)
          • Antioch (2313)
          • Qasr Ibrim (1234)
          • Yerevan (419)
      • Else:
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects owns at least 10 provinces which:
          • Has any Tax building
          • Has at least 10 Base tax
      		 The country:
      • If is Coptic:
        • Every Coptic nation gains “Protectors of the Holy Sites” for 25 years, giving:
          • −10% Core-creation cost
          • +0.5 Yearly legitimacy
          • +10% Manpower recovery speed
          • +1.5% Missionary strength
          • +2.5% Discipline
          • If completes this mission while directly own all Coptic holy sites, this modifier is permanent for us only.
      • Else:
        • Gains “Sword of the True Faith” for 25 years, giving:
          • −10% War score cost vs other religions
          • Gain “Holy War” and “Purging of Heresy” Casus Belli against heathens and heretics respectively.
      		 Etchmiadzin Cathedral

      Government missions[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.36.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Refine the Nakharar
      The Nakharar system, a time-honored institution that forms the backbone of our society, needs refinement to meet the evolving needs of our growing nation. Our Nakharars, the noble lords, must be more than mere landlords; they need to become the pillars of a strong, centralized state that supports the common good alongside their personal interests.
      		 The country:
      • If the Nobility estate exists:
        • Has completed at least 3 Nobility agendas
        • At least one of:
          • Has at least 60 Nobility loyalty
          • The Nobility estate has at least 4 privileges
      • Else:
        • At least one of:
          • Ruler's military skill is at least 5
          • Gains at least 10 military power per month
      		 The country:
      • Unlocks the “The Nakharar Title” government reform
      • Gains 25 military power per privilege given to the Nobles, up to a maximum of 100
      The Office of Sparapet
      The Office of the Sparapet, the commander-in-chief of our armed forces, is a symbol of our martial tradition and resilience. As we stand at the brink of a new era, we must restore this illustrious office to guide our forces, and our nation, to new heights of glory.
      		 The country:
      • Gains at least 10 military power per month
      • Is employing any Military advisor of at least level 3
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least 60 Army tradition
        • Has at least 30% Army professionalism
      		 The country:
      • Unlocks the “The Sparapet Office” government reform
      		 Refine the Nakharar
