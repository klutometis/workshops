      The Sikh Faith

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      [Root.GetName] is situated on the border between adherents of Hinduism and Islam. In this area marked by religious tension and differences, a man named Nanak has started to preach that there is no Hindu or Muslim, but that the truth is with God, who is neither.

      Although his rejection of ancient customs has outraged devoted [Root.Religion.GetName] believers, Nanak's disregard for caste and creed has allowed his teachings to take root among the population. His disciples are known as Sikhs and are spreading this new faith rapidly throughout [Root.GetName].

       
      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Is Hindu and a neighbouring province's religion is in Muslim group, or vice versa

      The Sikh religion is not enabled
      The year is at least 1480.

      Mean time to happen:

      260 months
      Modifiers

      • Province is Doaba: ×0.4
      • Culture is Panjabi: ×0.6
      We'll need to talk to this Nanak about the [Root.Owner.Religion.GetName] faith.

      The Sikh religion is enabled
      The province:

      • Change religion to Sikh
      • Gain permanent province modifier “Religious Center”, giving:
        • −5% Local missionary strength
      • Gain province modifier “Religious Zeal” for 4500 days, giving:
        • −100% Missionary strength
      • Two random neighbouring non-Sikh provinces other than the country's capital:
        • Change religion to Sikh
        • Gain province modifier “Religious Zeal” for 4500 days, giving:
          • −100% Missionary strength

      Guru Angad

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      'Death would not be called bad, O people, if one knew how to truly die.'

      The founder of our faith has passed away. Guru Nanak's body is dead, but the spirit of the Guru lives on in his successor, Guru Angad. Angad was one of Nanak's most loyal followers and carries on his legacy in establishing the Sikh community, spreading the use of a new alphabet to promote education of the young.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • Religion is Sikh
      • Month is September or later
      • Current guru is “Guru Nanak”

      The year is at least 1539, but before 1552.

      		Mean time to happen

      7 days

      The light of the Guru shines on.

      Give access to “Guru Angad”

      id

      Guru Amar Das

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      Guru Angad has passed on and left the guruship in the care of his loyal servant Amar Das. A man of great humility, Guru Amar Das nevertheless has ambitions for the teachings of Sikhism, strengthening the doctrine of equality that has gained popularity among the common people and establishing religious representatives called the Masand, spreading the word of our faith further than the Guru alone ever could.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • Religion is Sikh
      • Month is March or later
      • Current guru is “Guru Angad”

      The year is at least 1552, but before 1574.

      		Mean time to happen

      26 days

      Our Guru is the strength of the weak and the emancipation of the slave!

      Give access to “Guru Amar Das”

      id

      Guru Ram Das

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      Guru Amar Das has left this world and passed the guruship to his disciple and son-in-law Jetha, now known as Ram Das. He will oversee the continued construction of our holy city and encourages followers to attain enlightenment not only through solitary meditation, but from actively engaging with the community and their fellow Sikhs.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • Religion is Sikh
      • Month is September or later
      • Current guru is “Guru Amar Das”

      The year is at least 1574, but before 1581.

      		Mean time to happen

      1 day

      Our Guru is a servant of God and we serve our Guru.

      Give access to “Guru Ram Das”

      id

      Guru Arjan

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      After the death of Amar Das, his younger son Arjan has been chosen as the next Guru, despite contention from his older brother. To preserve the knowledge and hymns of past gurus, Arjan has begun to compile a volume known as the Adi Granth. The spiritual writing collected within come from many great teachers and the Guru has instructed that anyone may partake of it. He has also founded a great temple, the Harmandir Sahib, giving all Sikhs a place to meet and worship.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • Religion is Sikh
      • Month is September or later
      • Current guru is “Guru Ram Das”

      The year is at least 1581, but before 1606.

      		Mean time to happen

      1 day

      We shall keep the knowledge safe and build upon it.
      • Give access to “Guru Arjan”
      • If an owned province (preferably Doaba) has a free building slot and no Temple, then build one
      • Otherwise, gain 50 administrative power

      Guru Hargobind

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      'As I crush these onions you have brought to me, so shall your son crush the heads of his enemies, and be at once a great warrior and exalted Guru.'

      So spoke seer Baba Buddha of Arjan's successor, Guru Hargobind. The world has changed and the Sikhs can no longer trust their potential enemies to be merciful, but have taken up arms against those who seek to make martyrs out of the Guru and his disciples.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • Religion is Sikh
      • Month is May or later
      • Current guru is “Guru Arjan”

      The year is at least 1606, but before 1644.

      		Mean time to happen

      25 days

      Swords shall replace the crown of the Guru.

      Give access to “Guru Hargobind”

      Guru Har Rai

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      Guru Hargobind's grandson, Har Rai, has been trained by his grandfather in preparation for taking over the guruship, and now that time has come. Although instructed in the ways of combat and maintaining the army built by his predecessor, Har Rai is of a more sensitive disposition and his love for all living things compels him to seek diplomatic solutions.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • Religion is Sikh
      • Month is March or later
      • Current guru is “Guru Hargobind”

      The year is at least 1644, but before 1661.

      		Mean time to happen

      3 days

      Enough of battle; now is the time for healing.

      Give access to “Guru Har Rai”

      Guru Har Krishan

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      The body of Guru Har Rai passed on, leaving a very young successor to assume the mantle. Har Krishan was deemed a better candidate than his older brother, who put diplomatic pragmatism before adherence to the faith. While our new Guru does not lack piety or kindness, his young age of only 4 years limits our diplomatic options.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • Religion is Sikh
      • Month is October or later
      • Current guru is “Guru Har Rai”

      The year is at least 1661, but before 1665.

      		Mean time to happen

      6 days

      Our Guru is still our blessing.

      Give access to “Guru Har Krishan”

      id

      Guru Tegh Bahadur

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      The young Guru Har Krishan has tragically passed away. After some confusion about who his successor might be, Tegh Bahadur has been chosen as the new Guru. Initially a reclusive man, he has risen to the occasion and proven to be a determined leader. Despite his own rejection of rituals and ceremonial practices, he has taken on the task of safeguarding not only the religion of his own followers, but of Hindus who flee to our lands from persecution.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • Religion is Sikh
      • Month is March or later
      • Current guru is “Guru Har Krishan”

      The year is at least 1665, but before 1675.

      		Mean time to happen

      20 days

      Our Guru is the True King and the shield of the oppressed.
      • Give access to “Guru Tegh Bahadur”
      • Every neighbouring Hindu country gains opinion modifier “Offered Friendliness” ( +50 opinion) towards us
      id

      Guru Gobind Singh

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      With the death of Tegh Bahadur, Sikhs have once again had to face hostility from their political and religious enemies. His son, Gobind Rai, is determined to unite the Sikhs and gather those who are willing to give their lives for their Guru. The coming decades will build up to the creation of the Khalsa, those initiated by the Guru's ceremonies into our faith, and Gobind Rai will be known to history as Guru Gobind Singh.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • Religion is Sikh
      • Month is November or later
      • Current guru is “Guru Tegh Bahadur”

      The year is at least 1675, but before 1708.

      		Mean time to happen

      11 days

      The Khalsa belong to God, Victory belongs to God!

      Give access to “Guru Gobind Singh”

      id

      Guru Granth Sahib

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      Guru Gobind Singh is gone, but has passed on the guruship to one who shall never die. The collection of sacred texts known as the Adi Granth, once created by Guru Arjan and built upon by gurus after him, was designated by Gobind Singh as the final successor who will guide the Sikhs: The Guru Granth Sahib. Henceforth, whatever questions Sikhs may have for the Guru shall be answered by the knowledge contained within it.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • Religion is Sikh
      • Month is October or later
      • Current guru is “Guru Gobind Singh”

      The year is at least 1708.

      		Mean time to happen

      7 days

      Those who want to meet God can find Him in its hymns.

      Give access to “Guru Granth Sahib”

      A man who some call a guru has arrived in [Root.GetName]. He claims to have received a revelation, being called by Heaven to travel from his home to distant lands, spreading the message of the one true God.

      His local disciples insist that this God is revealed through all religions, including [Root.Owner.Religion.GetName], and defends Nanak as simply revealing the truths in our own faith. However, there is no question that these teachings reject our rituals and traditions, making people stray from the true path of [Root.Owner.Religion.GetName].

      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Is not a religious center
      • Does not have the modifier “Religious Center”
      • Is not Sikh
      • At least one of:
        • Year is before 1515 and province is in Hindustan, Bengal or Western India region
        • Year is at least 1514, but before 1525 and province is in Kashmir or Himalayan Hills area or Tibet region
        • Year is at least 1519, but before 1521 and province is in Mashriq or Arabia region
        • Year is at least 1525 and province is in Lahore, Multan, or the Sirhind area

      The Sikh religion is enabled
      The year is before 1550.

      800 months

      • Religion is Muslim and piety is at least 25 towards Mysticism: ×0.8
      • Has province modifier “Sufi Shrine”: ×0.9
      • Local unrest is at least 5: ×0.7
      • Culture is Panjabi: ×0.75
      • Any neighbouring province is Sikh: ×0.75
      • Religion is Hindu and any neighbouring province is Muslim, or vice versa: ×0.5
      As long as they pay taxes, they can believe what they want.
