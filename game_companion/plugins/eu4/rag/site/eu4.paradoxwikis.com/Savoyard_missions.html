      National missions[edit | edit source]

      Italian conquest[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Unify Piedmont
      We have considerable holdings in Piedmont; however, the Counts of Saluzzo and Montferrat still maintain a degree of independence from us. We should consolidate our power by bringing them under our direct rule.
      		 Owns core provinces Saluzzo (4727) and Montferrat (2981) Turin (103)::
      • Gain 1 base tax
      • Gain 1 base production
      • Gain 1 base manpower

      This country gains permanent claims on the following area:

      • Liguria.
      Conquer Genoa
      Our access to the coast is dependent on the narrow channel we control around Nizza, which can be cut off easily. If we can add Genoa and the rest of Liguria to our territories, this threat will diminish, and we will be poised to become a strong regional naval and trading power.
      		 This country or its non-tributary subjects own all provinces in the Liguria area. This country gains permanent claims on the following areas:
      • Lombardy
      • Po Valley

      If this country and Corsica are independent Christian monarchies:

      • Gain a Restoration of Union casus belli on Corsica for 25 years

      If this country and Sardinia are independent Christian monarchies:

      • Gain a Restoration of Union casus belli on Sardinia for 25 years

      If this country is not an independent Christian monarchy or Corsica and Sardinia do not exist:

      • Gain permanent claims on the following area:
        • Tyrrhenian Isles

      Otherwise:

      • Gain permanent claims on all provinces in the Tyrrhenian Isles area which are not owned by a country that fulfills all of the following conditions:
        • is Corsica or Sardinia
        • is independent
        • is a monarchy
      		 Unify Piedmont
      Unite with Sardinia
      Sardinia might not be the wealthiest of territories, but the Kingdom of Sardinia is a well-established title. If we can claim the island - and its regnal title - then we will finally be able to legitimately count ourselves among the foremost realms of Europe.
      		 At least 3 provinces in the following area are owned by this country or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Tyrrhenian Isles
      		 This country gains 30 prestige

      If this country and Sicily are independent Christian monarchies:

      • Gain a Restoration of Union casus belli on Sicily for 25 years

      Otherwise:

      • Gain permanent claims on the following areas:
        • Western Sicily
        • Eastern Sicily
      		 Conquer Genoa
      Take Sicily
      Now that we control Sardinia, we should set our sights on Sicily, an island with an illustrious history and considerable riches. By taking it, we would control a commanding position in the seas west of Italy and be in a strong position to funnel riches from the Mediterranean trade routes into our capital.
      		 At least 5 provinces in the following areas are owned by this country or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Western Sicily
      • Eastern Sicily
      		 This country gains “Conquest of the Islands” for 20 years, giving the following effects:
      • −20% light ship cost
      • +25% ship trade power

      If this country and Two Sicilies are independent Christian monarchies:

      • Gain a Restoration of Union casus belli on Two Sicilies for 25 years

      Otherwise if this country and Naples are independent Christian monarchies:

      • Gain a Restoration of Union casus belli on Naples for 25 years

      Otherwise:

      • Gain permanent claims on the following areas:
        • Campania
        • Apulia
        • Calabria
      		 Unite with Sardinia
      Union with Naples
      The Neapolitans have large territorial holdings in the south of Italy. They have ever been the target for hostile takeovers by foreign powers.
      		 Province Napoli (121) is owned by this country or any of its non-tributary subjects

      At least 8 provinces are owned by this country or any of its non-tributary subjects in the following areas:

      • Campania
      • Apulia
      • Calabria
      		 This country gets "Unity of Italy" until the end of the game, giving the following effects:
      • +1 Yearly Legitimacy
      • -10% Diplomatic Annexation Cost
      		 Take Sicily
      Seize Lombardy
      The Milanese represent a powerful and aggressive force on our eastern border. However, their wealthy subject cities are fickle and could easily be brought to throw off their rule - and perhaps accept our own leadership.
      		 Province Milan (104) is owned by this country or any of its non-tributary subjects:

      At least 5 provinces are owned by this country or any of its non-tributary subjects in the following areas:

      • Lombardy
      • Po Valley
      		 This country gets "Greater Piedmont" for 20 years, giving the following effects:
      • -10% Construction cost
      • -10% Development cost
      • +10% Institution spread

      This country gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Venetia
      • Emilia-Romagna
      • Tuscany
      		 Conquer Genoa
      Push on Tuscany
      The city-states of Tuscany and Emilia-Romagna lack strong leadership. We should... persuade them to let us provide it.
      		 At least 8 provinces are owned by this country or its non-tributary subjects in the following areas:
      • Tuscany
      • Emilia-Romagna
      		 This country gets "A Rising Force" for 30 years, giving the following effects:
      • +1 diplomatic reputation
      • +30 vassalization acceptance
      		 Seize Lombardy
      Capture Venice
      Venice is the crown jewel of Italy. Our control of the north will never be secure until she is brought under our rule.
      		 Owns all provinces in the Venetia area This country:
      • Gain 20 Prestige
      • Gain 50 Diplomatic Power
      • Gain "The House of Savoy in Venice" ( 50 Trade Power) in Venice trade node for 20 years
      		 Seize Lombardy

      French ambition[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Integrate Geneva
      The city of Geneva enjoys considerable autonomy from our rule. Indeed, it has a strong connection to the growing Swiss Confederation and would surely leap at the chance of joining it. We must tighten our control over them, lest they seek to free themselves of our overlordship.
      		 Owns core province Geneva (4720) This country gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
      • Romandie
      • Switzerland
      Monter l'Escalade
      The Swiss Confederation on our northern border is a natural enemy of [Root.GetName]. Fiercely independent, their world view differs from ours in many aspects, and they are often a source of heretical beliefs. We would do well to snuff them out.
      		 At least 8 provinces are owned by this country or its non-tributary subjects in the following areas:
      • Romandie
      • Switzerland
      		 This country gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
      • Western Burgundy
      • Eastern Burgundy
      • Lorraine
      		 Integrate Geneva
      Conquer Burgundy
      The upstart Dukes of Burgundy threaten us from the north. Facing them and France united would be a fearsome prospect, but if we can take over the Burgundian territories in central France, perhaps we can defeat the French once and for all.
      		 At least 6 provinces are owned by this country or its non-tributary subjects in the following areas:
      • Western Burgundy
      • Eastern Burgundy
      • Lorraine
      		 This country :
      • Gain 50 Administrative Power
      • Gets "Duke of Burgundy" for 20 years, giving the following effects:
        • +10% Cavalry Combat Ability
        • +1 Yearly Prestige
      		 Monter l'Escalade
      Take the Provence
      The Duke of Provence has great pretensions, harboring claims to extensive territories. However, in truth, he rules but a puny outcrop on the Mediterranean Sea, with a few isolated exclaves elsewhere in France. We should make use of his weakness to unite Provencal holdings in southern France with our own, allowing us to dominate the lands around the Alps.
      		 At least 6 provinces are owned by this country or its non-tributary subjects in the following areas:
      • Provence
      • Savoy
      		 This country gains permanent claims on all European provinces with Occitan culture
      Savoyard Occitania
      In the past, we were but a small enclave in France, surrounded by stronger nations. We could change this, though. Our conquest of Provence has opened the way for us to dominate southern France. We must seize our destiny!
      		 At least 14 provinces are owned by this country or its non-tributary subjects where one of the following is true:
      • Province is Rouergue (198)
      • Province is Auvergne (199)
      • Province is Forez (4777)
      • Province is in area Provence
      • Province is in area Savoy
      • Province is in area Languedoc
      		 Every owned province of Occitan culture gains the province modifier “Savoyard Occitania” for 30 years, giving the following effects:
      • −0.05 Monthly autonomy change
      • −2 Local unrest

      This country gains permanent claims on:

      • All European provinces with Gascon culture
      • All provinces in the Île-de-France area
      		 Take the Provence
      Claim France
      The Kings of France have often turned their greedy eyes towards Savoy. Now that our positions are reversed, it is time for us to claim our rightful place on their throne.
      		 Is not part of the Holy Roman Empire

      One of the following must be true:

      • Owns core province Paris (183)
      • Have claim the throne of France
      		 This country:
      • Gets "King of France" for 20 years, giving the following effects:
        • -2% Prestige Decay
        • +3 Monthly Splendor
        • +15% Improve Relations
      • If is a duchy, becomes a Kingdom.
      • If this country and France are independent Christian monarchies:
        • Gain a Restoration of Union casus belli on France for 25 years
      • Else, gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Loire
        • Orléans
        • Poitou
        • Champagne
      		 Conquer Burgundy

      Savoyard Occitania

      Military defensiveness[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Strength in Numbers
      Savoy is a tempting prize for would-be conquerors. It is crucial that we befriend one of the great powers of Europe so that we have some recourse against foreign depredations.
      		 This country:
      • Has an army of at least 10 regiments
      • Has any ally which:
        • Has an army which is at least 50% stronger[2] than our army
        • Has at least +150 Opinion towards us
      		 This country gets “Astute Diplomacy” for 20 years, giving the following effects:
      • +1 Diplomatic relation
      • +1 Diplomatic reputation
      All Bases Covered
      The history of our nation has demonstrated that putting all our fruit in one basket can easily prove disastrous. We must see to it that our network of alliances is sufficient to ensure that we will have strong backers, no matter what may happen.
      		 Any ally:
      • Has at least +120 Opinion towards us
      • If our army has less than 60 regiments
        • Has an army at least as large as our army
      • Else:
        • Has an army of at least 60 regiments
      • Has any rival which:
        • Is our ally
        • Has at least +120 Opinion towards us

      At least 5 countries are either our ally or subject

      • Ruler gains 1 diplomatic skill
      • Heir gains 1 diplomatic skill
      • Gain country modifier “Savoyard Diplomatic Prowess” for 30 years, giving the following effects:
        • +2 Monarch diplomatic skill
        • −5% Aggressive expansion impact
      		 Strength in Numbers
      Alpine Fortifications
      The natural terrain of Savoy and Piedmont, the highlands on either side of the Alps, offers many positions of strength. By heavily fortifying our lands, we can make sure that we will not be an easy conquest for any aggressor.
      		 At least 4 owned provinces have the latest available fort in the following areas:
      • Piedmont
      • Savoy
      • Provence
      • Switzerland
      • Romandie area
      		 Every owned province with a fort in mission's areas:
      • If can be upgraded, upgrade fort to next level
      • Else, gain province modifier “Expanded Fortifications” for 100 years, giving the following effect:
        • +25% Local defensiveness
      		 All Bases Covered
      Military Self-Reliance
      We can only truly guarantee our survival through the establishment of a large standing army capable of taking on any of our neighbors who might seek to take our lands.
      		 This country:
      • Is not a subject nation
      • No neighbouring country has an army at least as large as our army
      • Has no mercenary regiment
      		 This country:
      • Gain 100 military power
      • Gets “The Savoyard Standing Army” for 30 years, giving the following effects:
        • +10% Morale of armies
        • +0.5 Yearly army tradition
      		 Alpine Fortifications

      Savoyard prosperity[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Prosper in Piedmont
      Our lands east of the Alps occupy an important spot on the crossroads between Genoa, Venice and France. Investing in their prosperity would not just mean more money flowing into our coffers but would also help us establish a base from which we can expand our holdings in Italy.
      		 This country:
      • Has embraced Renaissance institution
      • Has at least 120 Total Development

      Province Turin (103):

      • Is owned by this country
      • Has at least 20 Development
      • If is not our capital
        • Is not at war
      		 If Turin (103) is not our capital:
      • The event "The City of Torino" happens

      Else, Turin (103):

      • Gain 2 base tax
      • Gain 1 base production
      • Gain 1 base manpower
      Glory to Ciamberì!
      Other European rulers have been expanding their capitals, erecting monuments to their grandeur and constructing beautiful palaces. We must make sure we do not fall behind in this competition for splendor!
      		 Country's capital:
      • Has at least 30 Development
      • Has at least 5 buildings
      		 This country:
      • Gain 20 prestige

      Country's capital:

      • If is not a Center of Trade:
        • Add a Center of Trade
      • Else, if is not a level 3 Center of Trade
        • Increase Center of Trade level by 1
      • Otherwise:
        • Add 2 Base Production in country's capital
      • All owned provinces in the area gets “Prosperous Homelands” for 20 years, giving the following effects:
        • -10% Local development cost
        • +10% Local production efficiency
      		 Prosper in Piedmont
      A Center of Belief
      In these days, when vile heresies spread like wildfire, we must make our nation into a bastion of doctrinal purity and correct religious practice.
      		 This country:
      • Does not have a Regency Council
      • If is Catholic
        • If Papal States exists:
          • Is the Curia controller
          • Papal States have at least 150 opinion towards us
        • Else
          • The Reformation began at least 20 years ago
          • Has at least 100% religious unity
      • If is Protestant
        • Has activated at least 3 Aspects of Faith
        • Has at least 100 church power
      • If is Reformed
        • If DLC 'Wealth of Nations' is active
          • Has at least 100 fervor
        • Else
          • has at least 100% religious unity
      • Otherwise, has at least 100% religious unity
      		 The event "The Order of Saints Maurice and Lazarus" happens

      This country gets a permanent claim on Cyprus (321)

      		Glory to Ciamberì!
      Kingdom of Cyprus
      Through Duke Ludovico I's marriage to Anne de Lusignan, the House of Savoy has a claim to the Kingdom of Cyprus, the last bastion of the Crusaders in the Middle East. We should take up our rightful throne and establish a base from which to begin to win back lost territory for Christianity.
      		 Owns core province Cyprus (321) This country gets "King of Cyprus" for 30 years, giving the following effect:
      • +10% Morale of Armies

      This country gets permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Palestine
      • Syria
      • Aleppo
      		 A Center of Belief
      Powerhouse of the North
      By introducing modern economic practices and investing in the development of our cities, we can forge our state into a powerhouse capable of dominating France. Political leadership will surely swiftly follow economic supremacy.
      		 This country:
      • Has at least 100 development in country's capital area
      • Owns at least 12 provinces with any manufactory
      • Has an income of at least 10 times the starting income
      		 This country gets "Alpine Powerhouse" until the end of the game, giving the following effects:
      • +10% Goods produced modifier
      • +5% Administrative efficiency
      		 Glory to Ciamberì!
