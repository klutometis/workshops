      Flavour events[edit | edit source]

      id

      The Great Pyramid of Tenochtitlan

      It has now been some years since our ancestors shook off the yoke of the Tepanecs of Azcapotzalco and instead joined together in the Aztec triple alliance of Tenochtitlan, Texcoco, and Tlacopan. Since then the power of our people has grown significantly and by now our state controls the entire valley of Mexico. The city states under our rule still remember that we were all equals not so long ago and it is uncertain how far we can trust their loyalties.
      As it is every ruler's task to build a new stage of the Great Pyramid of Tenochtitlan we have an excellent opportunity to try the loyalty of our subjects. Any subject city that contributes to the project will be too preoccupied to revolt and any that refuses will have shown its worth and can be punished.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Is Aztec
      • Owns the province Mexico (852)
      • Owns at least 3 provinces

      The year is before 1450

      		Mean time to happen

      12 months

      All our subjects must contribute to the Pyramid!

      Every owned province (with the exception of the capital) receives one of the two following effects:

      • 80% chance: Gains “Contributing to the Great Temple” province modifier until the event Reaction to Pyramid Project:
        • −0.02 Monthly autonomy change
      • 20% chance: Noble rebels (size 1) rise up in revolt and occupy the province

      Aztec:

      • Gets the "Reaction to Pyramid Project" event in 120 days
      Let us use our own resources for this project.

      Aztec:

      • Loses 75 administrative power
      • Loses 75 diplomatic power
      • Great project 'Tenochtitlan' gains one level
      id

      Much as we suspected not all of the cities under our control have welcomed the chance to put all of their resources at our disposal, and it seems clear that we will have to carry a larger part of the costs than we had hoped.

      Every Aztec province with the modifier “Contributing to the Great Temple” loses it.

      Tlacaelel

      Tlacaelel, the foremost architect of the Aztec Triple Alliance and the first person to be named its Cihuacoatl, prime minister, is an indispensable force within our state. Having served a number of Tlatoanis, Tlacaelel is by now involved in the running of almost every part of our state. He is the one who organizes our military campaigns, he handles our finances and when our Tlatoani is in the field he acts in his stead.
      Above all however Tlacaelel is a great champion for the worship of Huitzilopochtli and has taken a number of measures to ensure that our deity and the martial traditions that surround him is given the proper respect.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Is Aztec
      • Is not at war

      The year is before 1487

      		Mean time to happen

      90 months

      Great!

      Aztec:

      • Gains 10 prestige
      • Gains 75 administrative power
      • Gains 75 military power
      id

      Sumptuary Laws & Warrior Culture

      Every male in our society is expected to take up arms in times of war and the success of our state has always depended on our brave warriors. In order to further encourage the warrior culture of our people the Cihuacoatl is now working on a set of laws restricting everything from how our subjects dress, what they may buy in the markets and what ornaments they can use or what offices may be held in government.
      While these laws will protect the rights of our nobility they are above all a way to reward valorous warriors. For the commoners their skill in battle will be what determines how they dress and what they may own. Even among the nobles positions in government will only be open to those who fight without fear.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Is Aztec

      The year is before 1460

      		Mean time to happen

      48 months

      Implement the Sumptuary Laws.

      Aztec:

      • Loses 100 administrative power
      • Gains 2% army professionalism
      • Gains “Sumptuary Laws” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +2.5% Discipline
        • −0.05 Monthly war exhaustion
      We do not need such laws, tradition will remain our guideline.

      Aztec:

      • Gains 15 legitimacy
      • If has a stability of less than +3,
        • then it gains 1 stability,
        • else it gains 50 administrative power.
      id

      The Famine Year of the One Rabbit

      Time is a cycle and what has been will always come again. The year of the one rabbit is upon us and so are the snows, the frost and later on the droughts associated with it.
      The weather will not be kind to us this year and a great famine is tearing up our society. As desperate men sell their children into slavery to feed their remaining families some demand that our $MONARCHTITLE$ should release them of servitude to him and allow them to seek new lives elsewhere.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Is Aztec

      The year is at least 1450, but before 1460

      		Mean time to happen

      30 months

      Allow People to Resettle.

      Aztec:

      • Loses 1 stability
      • Reduces the capital's base tax by 1
      • Gains “Year of One Rabbit” for 1 year, giving:
        • +15% Stability cost modifier
        • −0.25 Goods produced
        • +1 National unrest
      Hold them against their will.

      Aztec:

      • Loses 1 stability
      • Increases local unrest in the capital by 10
      • Gains “Year of One Rabbit” for 1 year, giving:
        • +15% Stability cost modifier
        • −0.25 Goods produced
        • +1 National unrest
      id

      Beginning of a New Cycle

      As we leave the disastrous year of One Rabbit behind us we enter a new cycle and the signs tell us this cycle is one that will see the rise of our people to greatness. A great happiness fills the hearts of our people as we perform the traditional New Fire Ceremony, they know the harsh times are behind them and that the coming years is going to be the beginning of the Aztec era.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Is Aztec
      • Has had the event The Famine Year of the One Rabbit
      • Does not have the “Year of One Rabbit” country modifier
      		 Mean time to happen

      1 month

      For thus it is ended; thus sickness and famine have left us

      Aztec:

      • If has a stability of less than +3,
        • then it gains 1 stability,
        • else it gains 50 administrative power.
      • Loses 5 doom
      • Gains “Recovering after Famine” for 1 year, giving:
        • −15% Stability cost modifier
        • −1 National unrest
      id

      The Power Balance of the Triple Alliance

      While the Aztec Empire can be seen as a unified state in many ways it is still technically an alliance of the three cities of Tenochtitlan, Texcoco and Tlacopan. When the alliance was created the first two could be said to have been of equal strength with Tenochtitlan being the center of a stronger military culture while Texcoco was the center of law, engineering and the arts. These roles have only become more pronounced as our empire has grown. Tenochtitlan, as the dominant city in all military matters has therefore come to rise to a position above the others and many of our subservient chiefs now send their tribute directly to Tenochtitlan.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Is Aztec
      • Has at least 10 cities

      The year is at least 1470, but before 1500

      		Mean time to happen

      90 months

      Continue to centralize power in Tenochtitlan.

      Aztec:

      • Gains 5 prestige
      • If has a stability of less than +3,
        • then it gains 1 stability,
        • else it gains 50 administrative power.
      Attempt to empower Texcoco to a greater degree.

      Aztec:

      • Loses 1 stability
      • Gains “Influence of Texcoco” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −5% Administrative technology cost
        • +0.02 Monthly autonomy change
      id

      Legal Reform of Nezahualcoyotl

      Traditionally legal matters in Mesoamerican states are left to the respective judges of the subject cities. As the Aztec state has grown however this has lead to considerable differences within its borders. Nezahualcoyotl, the leader of Texcoco, has therefore created a new collection of laws with strict rules and specified punishments for every crime. The plan is for these new laws to be implemented all over the Aztec domain resulting in a more unified Aztec legal system.
      To be sure that the local judges actually do make use of these new laws a number of them also deal with the punishment of judges that don't perform their duties in accordance with the laws.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Is Aztec
      • Has at least 10 cities

      The year is at least 1470, but before 1500

      		Mean time to happen

      90 months

      Let us implement these laws immediately!

      Aztec:

      • Loses 100 administrative power
      • Gains “Laws of Nezahualcoyotl” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −0.03 monthly autonomy change
      Why meddle with that which has worked for generations?

      Aztec:

      • Gains 15 legitimacy
      • If has a stability of less than +3,
        • then it gains 1 stability,
        • else it gains 50 administrative power.
      id

      Aztec Merchants Harassed

      The Tenochtitlan Pochteca Guild is complaining that the Totonacs to our north have been harassing their members. The Totonac claim that the traders are nothing but Aztec spies. Apart from the general slight against our country these long distance merchants are the only source of some of the luxury items that our society uses to distinguish between the social classes..
       
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Is Aztec
      • Is neigbhouring Totonac
      • Is not alliy, subject or overlord of Totonac
      • Does not have 'Trade war' casus belli against Totonac

      The year is between 1450 and 1470

      		Mean time to happen

      60 months

      They will regret this.

      Aztec:

      • Gains 'Trade war' casus belli against Totonac for 5 years

      Altepetls revolts[edit | edit source]

      id

      Altepetl Revolt

      Many of the city states absorbed into the Aztec Empire retain a high degree of autonomy and in many places the former local rulers still hold considerable power. One such city state has recently refused to provide the tribute we are due and the customary headdress of warning that was sent to its leader does not seem to have had much effect. A single city is hardly a considerable threat but should others see us as weak they might well follow into open rebellion.
       
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Is Aztec
      • Has at least 8 provinces
      • Has Administrative technology lower than 5
      • At least one of:
        • Has less than 1 Stability
        • Has at least 5 war exhaustion
        • Has less than 50 Legitimacy
      • Any owned province can spawn the Separatists rebel type
      		 Mean time to happen

      120 months

      Attempt to conspire with the elders of the city to stop this.

      One of the following events will happen:

      • 50% chance of: Gets the event "Rebellion Averted"
      • 50% chance of: Gets the event "Rebellion Grows"
      Prepare a military expedition to depose the leader of the city.

      Separatists (size 1) rise up in revolt and occupy a random non-capital eligible controlled province.

      id

      Rebellion Averted

      The emissary we sent to the elders of the city has managed to convince them to take care of the problem of the obstinate chief.
       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      selecting Attempt to conspire with the elders of the city to stop this. in Altepetl Revolt event

      Ok.

      Gain 5 prestige

      Rebellion Grows

      Our actions to avert rebellion have so far had little effect. The slowness of our reaction has resulted in more cities joining and an open revolt is now afoot.
       
      Trigger conditions
      • None

      selecting Attempt to conspire with the elders of the city to stop this. in Altepetl Revolt event

      Ok.

      Disloyal Altepetls

      The arrival of the hostile European forces in our lands has not gone unnoticed among the more dissatisfied elements among our subjects. While many remain hesitant about what to make of these newcomers others have shown little hesitation and have already pledged to aid the Europeans to overthrow our rule.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Is Aztec
      • One of the following is true:
        • Aztec follow Nahuatl religion, without fully reforming it
        • Does not have the Feudalism institution
      • Aztec is at war with any European country, which either:
        • has at least 50% war score against Aztec
        • controls Aztecan capital
        • is saved as targeted country

      Is not playing with a Random New World

      		Mean time to happen

      2 months

      Ok.

      Targeted country gets the event "Disloyal Altepetls"

      The Temple City of Cholula[edit | edit source]

      This event allows pagan nations converting to Nahuatl religion. It doesn't work correctly for countries outside of Americas (most notably Ryukyu) and has nothing to do with Aztec.

      id

      The Temple City of Cholula

      We have conquered the city of Cholula, an ancient Temple city dedicated to the worship of Quetzalcoatl and a major Mesoamerican pilgrim site. Throughout the city there are hundreds of temples to the pagan gods and a steady stream of natives travel here every year.
       
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Is not Nahuatl
      • Owns province Cholula (2644) which:
        • Has “Cholula Temples” modifier
      		 Mean time to happen

      12 months

      Option conditions

       Always enabled:

      Destroy the Temples.

      The country:

      • Gains 1% of yearly income

      Cholula (2644):

      • Loses “Cholula Temples” modifier
      • Gains 5 local unrest

      AI will select this option with 50% probability if the second one is enabled and always otherwise.

       Enabled if:

      • Is Pagan, but not Inti nor Mayan
      • Is not a colonial nation
      This is a fascinating religion, let us embrace it.

      The country:

      • Converts to Nahuatl
      • If is a native council:
        • Changes to tribal despotism

      If this option is enabled, AI will select it with 50% probability.

       Enabled if: has completed humanist idea group

      Let them continue to worship their gods here.

      The country:

      • Loses 1 stability

      Cholula (2644):

      • Loses “Cholula Temples” modifier
      • Gains 1 base tax

      Never selected by AI.

      Nahuatl Deities[edit | edit source]

      id

      Deities of the Nahua

      Whilst our nation already has a patron Deity, it is time we choose a new one to celebrate as our primary.
       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      Using the interaction “Nahuatl Deities” in the government mechanic “Tonalli Gathering”

      Option conditions

       Enabled if: has "Tezcatlipoca", "Quetzacoatl" or "Tlaloc" as current Patron Deity

      The God of War will lead us to victory!

      Make "Huitzilopochtli" the main Patron Deity of our nation, which will make Tonalli Gathering have the following effects at maximum power:

      • +10% Morale of armies
      • +20% Manpower in same culture group provinces

       Enabled if: does not have "Tezcatlipoca" as current Patron Deity

      The Black Jaguar will create a conflict that we'll emerge victorious!

      Make "Tezcatlipoca" the main Patron Deity of our nation, which will make Tonalli Gathering have the following effects at maximum power:

      • −15% Core creation cost
      • −10% Aggressive expansion impact

       Enabled if: does not have "Tlaloc" as current Patron Deity

      The God of Rain will bless us with fertile soil.

      Make "Tlaloc" the main Patron Deity of our nation, which will make Tonalli Gathering have the following effects at maximum power:

      • −20% Construction cost
      • −10% Development cost in primary culture

       Enabled if: does not have "Quetzalcoatl" as current Patron Deity

      The Feathered Serpent will create a society for us to thrive in.

      Make "Quetzalcoatl" the main Patron Deity of our nation, which will make Tonalli Gathering have the following effects at maximum power:

      • −3 National unrest
      • +3% Missionary strength

      Rulers of the Lake

      In Lake Texcoco, home to our Empire, one man on his throne had a dream. He saw something outside of this cage that we live in. He saw a land, a limitless land that he sought to conquer. The man's name is Moctezuma I, the second Emperor of the Aztec Empire. Although in the past the title of Emperor was only ceremonial, he transformed it into a proper title through the subjugation of his neighbors, as well as military crackdown of the other members of the alliance.

      With this recent shift in power, he can now start chasing his dream. There are many threats around him, such as the Purepecha Empire, the Tlaxcalan Senate, the remnants of the Zapotec, and even the disunited Mayan lands. There are many foes that threaten to stop this dream, but we shall conquer even Mictlan for him.
       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      Completing the “Rising Amidst the Lake” mission

      The reign of Moctezuma has just started.

      Ruler "Moctezuma I":

      • As long as he is alive, the ruler modifier "Rise of Moctezuma" will be upgraded through the mission tree
      • Gains “Rise of Moctezuma” until his death, giving:
        • +50% Vassal force limit contribution
        • +1 Monarch power tribute from tributary states


      id

      Conflict Ender

      The fires of the Flowers Wars have burned brightly, leaving many soldiers and citizens dead. Many dead means much blood spilled for the Gods, yet costly victories may lead to setbacks for our dream of Aztlan. With the fall of Tlaxcala, our greatest rival in Mexico has fallen. [Root.Monarch.GetName] is now the sole ruler of the cities around Lake Texcoco.

      With this newfound peace in our lands, the banners are slowly being raised again. In the past, we ended the conflict in Central Mexico. Now, we are preparing to divide and conquer the lands of the Zapotec.
       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      Completing the “Crush Tlaxcala” mission

      Our conflict with Tlaxcala is now over.

      Ruler "Moctezuma I":

      • Upgrades “Rise of Moctezuma” until his death, giving:
        • +50% Vassal force limit contribution
        • +1 Monarch power tribute from tributary states
        • −10% Liberty desire in subjects
        • +0.25 Yearly doom reduction
        • +50% Average monarch lifespan

      Cloud Splitter

      Conquest is never enough to subdue a populace. The military crackdown will only temporarily keep the spirit of the defeated low. In order to properly govern, to properly rule, you must first divide them. Make them feel alone, and isolated, split them apart, then rule them.

      [Root.Monarch.GetName] has followed this strategy against the Zapotec, the cloud people. As they were already divided, all that was left was to deal the final blow, and the remnants of their civilization are now under our rule.
       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      Completing the “Divide and Conquer” mission

      We have divided them. Now it is time we rule them.

      Ruler "Moctezuma I":

      • Gains 1 ruler military skill[2]
      • Upgrades “Rise of Moctezuma” until his death, giving:
        • +100% Vassal force limit contribution
        • +20% Income from vassals
        • +1 Monarch power tribute from tributary states
        • −10% Liberty desire in subjects
        • +0.25 Yearly doom reduction
        • +50% Average monarch lifespan


      id

      Blood-Spiller

      Blood is the nectar of the Gods. The Mayan people know it as much as we do, it is why they adopted our ways years ago. Yet they do not know the full extent that you must go in order to collect blood. Mere sacrifices will not do. Battles must be costly, tactics must be brutal, all in the name of victory. Conflict, as the God Tezcatlipoca promotes, is the source of blood.

      All of this is not enough. [Root.Monarch.GetName] knows that even in victory, we must celebrate with more blood. For that reason, we will hold a great sacrifice in the land of the Mayans.
       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      Completing the “Unify Yucatan Peninsula” mission

      Let the blood of our enemies grant the Gods more power.

      Ruler "Moctezuma I":

      • Gains 1 ruler administrative skill[3]
      • Gains 1 ruler diplomatic skill[4]


      id

      Wings of Light

      Since leaving the cage that was Lake Texcoco, the Aztec people could only dream of flying, of mastering the land around them and making everyone bend the knee to them. [Root.Monarch.GetName] had a dream though, to become the sole ruler of Mesoamerica, and recreate Aztlan.

      He set out to conquer the cities around Tenochtitlan, and establish himself as the first true Emperor of the Aztecs. He led a plethora of new reforms as he ended his conflicts with the Tlaxcala alliance. As he split the clouds in half, he created an administration to rule over the numerous city-states in Mexico. With his conquests of the Mayan people, he strengthened the religious character all around the realm, increasing the amount of blood offered to the Gods. Now, all that is left is to once again sit upon his throne, as a legend that brought light and unity in this land plagued by conflict and darkness.
       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      Completing the “Huehuetlatoania of Mexico” mission

      We are now the sole rulers of Mexico.

      Ruler "Moctezuma I":

      • Gains 1 ruler administrative skill[3]
      • Gains 1 ruler diplomatic skill[4]
      • Upgrades “Rise of Moctezuma” until his death, giving:
        • +100% Vassal force limit contribution
        • +30% Income from vassals
        • +2 Monarch power tribute from tributary states
        • −25% Liberty desire in subjects
        • +0.25 Yearly doom reduction
        • +50% Average monarch lifespan

      Fate of the Alliance

      Over the years, and after many attempts of centralization, the city alliance that had once found the Aztec Empire is now an empty shell, with its past influence amounting to nothing more but a title. Perhaps it is time we officially disband the alliance?
       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      Completing the “Fate of the Alliance” mission

      Tenochtitlan shall reign supreme!

      Aztec:

      • Loses “The Triple Alliance” nobility privilege
      • The Nobility estate loses 10% share of the land
      Maintaining this alliance will be to our benefit.

      Aztec:

      • The “The Triple Alliance” nobility privilege no longer gives a governing capacity malus, and the governing capacity modifier malus will be reduced from −50% to −25%
      • Gains 1 random development per subject in the capital
