      The Fall of Majapahit

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

      		Available only with the Leviathan DLC enabled.
      The Fall of Majapahit
      Nearly all of the once-glorious Majapahit Empire has been lost, and what remains is dangerously close to total collapse.

      The The Fall of Majapahit[1] is a disaster in Majapahit.

      Prerequisites[edit | edit source]

      The country:

      • Is Majapahit
      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • Does not use doom
      • Did not have this disaster before

      If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

      Monthly progress starts if Monthly progress halts if

      Does not have an ongoing disaster

      Monthly progress
      +100% Is Majapahit
      		Has an ongoing disaster

      Start[edit | edit source]

      If the progress reaches 100%, the disaster will start with the following event:

      id

      The Fall of Majapahit

      Majapahit is an empire in its death throes. Once the center of a vast network of vassal kingdoms and tributary states, Majapahit now has little power beyond [Root.Capital.GetCapitalName]. Chinese support for our rivals in Malacca as well as the rising power of Brunei have led to the collapse of our hegemony. Worse, internal struggles over the succession threaten to tear the remnants of the empire apart from the inside.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      Start of “The Fall of Majapahit” disaster

      These are dark times.

      Majapahit:

      • Loses 5 stability
      • Loses 50 legitimacy
      • Loses 50 heir claim strength

      Modifiers[edit | edit source]

      While the disaster is ongoing, the country gets:

      • +15% All power costs
      • −50% Average monarch lifespan
      • +15% Liberty desire in subjects

      Also the following events are triggered by a monthly pulse.

      Monthly events[edit | edit source]

      id

      The Rise of Demak

      The increasingly autonomous Muslim community in Demak have found a champion in the renegade Majapahit prince [Root.demak_sultan.GetName]. [Root.demak_sultan.GetName] has not only declared the independence of the Demak Sultanate, but also seeks to seize the throne of Majapahit for himself. Bound by our many struggles, there is little that we can do while Demak gathers its strength.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions

      Majapahit:

      • Has not completed the “Restore Order” mission
      • Has less than 75 legitimacy
      • Owns province Demak, which is Sunni
      • Owns at least 4 Sunni provinces in the Indonesia region

      Demak does not exist

      		Is triggered only by

      Monthly pulse of “The Fall of Majapahit” disaster

      Immediate effects

      Majapahit:

      • Releases Demak as a vassal who:
        • Gains cores on Demak, Kendal, Pajang, and Pekalongan provinces, if owned by us
        • Converts to Sunni
        • Becomes a monarchy
        • Enacts the "Mandala System" government reform
        • Gets a new ruler with our dynasty and 95 claim strength
      • Creates a truce with Demak
      Traitors!

      Demak:

      • Gains a claim on every province in the Indonesia region owned by us
      • Builds land units to force limit in the capital: 60% infantry, 30% cavalry, 10% artillery
      • Builds naval units to force limit in a random owned port: 20% light ships, 50% galleys, 20% transports


      id

      The Rise of Mataram

      Majapahit has proven itself unable to govern even its holdings on Java. A rival state, calling itself the Sultanate of Mataram, has emerged on the southern part of the island and has laid claim to the legacy of the empire. The [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] can do little but watch as [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] empire collapses around [Root.Monarch.GetHerHim].
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions

      Majapahit:

      • Has not completed the “Restore Order” mission
      • Has less than 75 legitimacy
      • Owns province Kotagede, which is Sunni
      • Owns at least 4 Sunni provinces in the Indonesia region

      Mataram does not exist

      		Is triggered only by

      Monthly pulse of “The Fall of Majapahit” disaster

      Immediate effects

      Majapahit:

      • Releases Mataram as a vassal who:
        • Gains cores on Kotagede, Karta, Kediri, and Pajang provinces, if owned by us
        • Converts to Sunni
        • Becomes a monarchy
        • Enacts the "Mandala System" government reform
        • Gets a new ruler with our dynasty and 95 claim strength
      • Creates a truce with Mataram
      Traitors!

      Mataram:

      • Gains a claim on every province in the Indonesia region owned by us
      • Builds land units to force limit in the capital: 60% infantry, 30% cavalry, 10% artillery
      • Builds naval units to force limit in a random owned port: 20% light ships, 50% galleys, 20% transports


      id

      Disloyalty to Majapahit

      The rulers of [maj_tributary_country.GetName] have been increasingly ignoring [Root.GetAdjective] rule. They have now crossed the line into outright defiance, withholding tribute and disrespecting our emissaries.
       
      Trigger conditions

      Majapahit:

      • Has not completed the “Demand Loyalty” mission
      • Has any subject who:
        • Has at least 50% liberty desire
        • Is AI-controlled
        • Has no truce with us and vice versa
      		 Is triggered only by

      Monthly pulse of “The Fall of Majapahit” disaster (Base weight: 10/380)

      Option conditions

       Always enabled:

      They will regret this.

      A random subject meeting the requirements:

      • If has at least 75% liberty desire:
        • Becomes independent
      • Else:
        • Gains 15% liberty desire

       Enabled if: A random subject meeting the requirements has at least 80% liberty desire

      Make concessions to restore their loyalty.

      Majapahit:

      • Loses 10 legitimacy

      A random subject meeting the requirements:

      • Loses 5% liberty desire


      id

      An Offer of Aid

      The situation in [Root.GetName] is dire, but it seems that all is not lost yet. A group of wealthy Muslim merchants have approached the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] with an offer. They will pay us handsomely for trading privileges in our ports and the right to practice their religion freely with [Root.GetName]. The Bhattara Saptaprabhu warn that the instability of the empire may attract our people to the merchant’s foreign faith, discarding the founding traditions of the Majapahit empire. Silver, however, makes for its own counter-argument.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions

      The disaster has been ongoing for at least 5 years

      		Is triggered only by

      Monthly pulse of “The Fall of Majapahit” disaster

      Their help is most welcome.

      Majapahit:

      • Gains 500 ducats
      • Loses 1% mercantilism

      Province Demak:

      • Becomes Sunni, if owned
      We do not need aid from heathens. Banish them.

      Majapahit:

      • Gains 1% mercantilism
      • Gains “Harmed Trade Relations” for 20 years, giving:
        • −25% Global trade power
        • −1 Diplomatic reputation


      id

      Spread of Islam in [maj_sunni_province.GetName]

      Muslim communities have existed in Majapahit since at least the time of Zheng He. Through trade, through the movement of peoples, and through proselytization these communities have grown ever larger. In these dark times, Islam offers solace and stability to communities that can no longer rely on the state to protect them. In [maj_sunni_province.GetName] the people have widely accepted Sunni Islam to the detriment of traditional Hindu-Buddhism.
       
      Trigger conditions

      Majapahit:

      • At least one of:
        • Is Sunni
        • Has not completed the “Restore Order” mission
      • Has chosen the option “Their help is most welcome.” during “An Offer of Aid” event
      • Owns any province in the Indonesia region which:
        • Is not Sunni
        • Neighbours a Sunni province or is a port
      		 Is triggered only by

      Monthly pulse of “The Fall of Majapahit” disaster (Base weight: 10/380)

      They dare abandon the gods?

      A random province meeting the requirements:

      • Converts to Sunni

      Finishing conditions[edit | edit source]

      The disaster can only be ended by completing the "Prevent Collapse" mission.
      When the disaster ends the country loses the above-mentioned effects and the following event is triggered:

      id

      A New Dawn for Majapahit

      Against all odds, Majapahit has emerged from decades of turmoil and decline as a newly resurgent power. A stable [Root.GovernmentName], loyal subjects, and a common faith bolster our resolve as we remember Gajah Mada’s oath: that one day Majapahit will rule all of Nusantara.
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      End of “The Fall of Majapahit” disaster

      We have returned from the brink.

      Majapahit:

      • Gains 1 stability

      Further events[edit | edit source]

      id

      [Root.maj_pretender.GetName] Challenges the Throne

      Struggles over the succession have become commonplace in [Root.GetName]. On this occasion a Prince named [Root.maj_pretender.GetName] has gathered an army and begun his march to the capital to overthrow the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle].
       
      Trigger conditions

      Majapahit:

      • Has not completed the “Protect the Faith” mission
      • Owns and controls any province in the Indonesia region

      “The Fall of Majapahit” disaster is ongoing

      		Is triggered only by

      Current ruler dying

      Immediate effects

      Pretender rebels (size 2) rise in revolt in a random owned province in the Indonesia region

      We will crush this upstart.
