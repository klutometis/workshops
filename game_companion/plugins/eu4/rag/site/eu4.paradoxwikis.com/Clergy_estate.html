      The Clergy estate represents the educated priests of your state religion. They have a long tradition of serving in the administration of your country and their traditional responsibility is to care for the spiritual well-being of it and your people. Over the centuries they have acquired both land properties and privileges from former rulers as well as pious individuals.

      Availability[edit | edit source]

      General availability:

      • This estate belongs to the base estates group which are available to all nations unless supplemented/replaced by special estate groups added via expansions.

      Specific conditions:

      • Does not use other mechanics
      • Does not have the Brahmins estate for nations with Sikh or Hindu religion
      • Is not nomad, unless the "Unity of Faith" mission was completed
      • Is not the United States unless government is a theocracy
      • Has not enacted "Seperate the Clergy from State" Reform
      • Has enacted the "Establish Religious Institutions" Reform if country is piratical
      • Has enacted the "Restoration of the Clergy" Reform if country is Revolutionary Empire
