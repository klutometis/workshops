      Bohemian missions

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

      		Available only with the Winds of Change DLC enabled.

      This is a list of all missions of Bohemia[1]. The branching missions can be previewed at the start of the game, but they can't be selected. Completing the mission End the Regency while being catholic will unlock the catholic branch while completing it as any other religion will unlock the hussite branch.

      For the missions from the emperor DLC, see Bohemian missions without winds of change

      Top half missions[edit | edit source]

      Left: To the South[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Hungarian Succession
      Until very recently, the Holy Crown of Hungary had been under our control. The devastating loss at the Battle of Varna has left the throne in Buda vacant, giving us the perfect opportunity to press our claims and retake it once again.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Has had the "Return of the Hussites" event
      • Has an army at least as large as that of Hungary who:
        • Has had the "Matthias Corvinus" event or the year is at least 1457
      		 Bohemia:
      • If both we and Hungary are independent Christian monarchies:
        • Gains "Restoration of Union" casus belli against Hungary for 25 years
      • Else:
        • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
          • Slovakia
          • Transdanubia
          • Alföld
      Claim the Holy Crown
      Hungary and Bohemia have previously been united under a single King, being separated only in 1439 with the death of Albert the Magnanimous. We should restore this rightful state of affairs.
      		 Bohemia:
      • At least one of:
        • Has Hungary as a subject with less than 50% liberty desire
        • Owns at least 12 provinces in Carpathia (region)
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Slavonia
        • Croatia
        • Carpathia (region)
      • If completes this mission while Hungary is our subject:
        • Unlocks the "Transfer the Ownership of Nitra" decision
        • Hungary loses 25% liberty desire
      • Else, if completes this mission by direct conquest:
        • Gains “Carpathian Intervention” for 20 years, giving:
          • +10% Morale damage
          • +10% Manpower recovery speed
      		 The Hungarian Succession
      Protect Against the Turk
      The Ottoman Turks are engaged in a dangerous spree of territorial conquest that threatens to engulf Christendom. We must ensure that the borders are fortified against the inevitable Turkish onslaught.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Is not a subject nation
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 5 provinces in Carpathia (region) with the highest level fort available
      • If the Ottomans exist:
        • Has an army at least 0.5 times stronger that of the Ottomans
      • Else:
        • Has an army of at least 50 regiments
      		 Bohemia:
      • Unlocks the "Order of the Dragon" local organization
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Bulgaria
        • Silistri
        • All provinces in the Carpathia and Balkans region owned by the Ottomans
      		 Claim the Holy Crown
      Revenge for Nicopolis
      In 1396 the Bohemian crown embarked on a righteous crusade against the Turks in Bulgaria, only to be defeated by the heathens. We must rally our forces once again, retake Bulgaria, and drive the Turks back across the sea!
      		 Bohemia:
      • Is not a subject nation
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own province Nikopol (150)
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 6 provinces in the following areas
        • Bulgaria
        • Silistri

      The Ottomans:

      • Do not own any province in the Balkans (region)
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains "Subjugation" casus belli on every independent nation with its capital in the Balkan (region) for 25 years
      		 Protect Against the Turk
      Liberate the Balkans
      Foreign oppressors have burdened the Balkans for too long. A strong Bohemian presence will be their liberation. Under our just rule, the people of the Balkans will enjoy freedom and prosperity.
      		 Bohemia or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 15 provinces in the Balkans (region)
      		 Bohemia:
      • Enables access to "Unite the Balkans" casus belli
      • Gains “Liberation of the Balkans” for 10 years, giving:
        • −20% Overextension impact modifier
      • If completes this mission while having any subject nation with a capital in the Balkan region:
        • Every subject country with a capital in the Balkan region:
          • Loses 10% liberty desire
      		 Revenge for Nicopolis

      Middle top: Restore Great Moravia[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Humiliate Austria
      Austria abuses the imperial throne and makes no secret of its plans to subjugate our nation. We must show the Princes of Germany, and the world, that Austria is not the mighty behemoth it pretends to be.

      • If completes this mission while having humiliated Austria:
        • Gains 25 convertible[2] prestige
      Recover Nitrava
      The realm of Great Moravia, the predecessor to Bohemia, once extended far beyond the lands of the Czechs. We should seek to expand our borders once again, beginning with the annexation of the old Principality of Nitra.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Owns the Slovakia area
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains “Slovak” as an accepted culture.
      • Gains +1 Max promoted culture until “Slovak” is demoted.

      Every province in Slovakia area:

      • Gains 1 random development
      		 The Hungarian Succession
      Otakar's Legacy
      Having humiliated the Habsburgs, we must now seek to expand our influence over their lands to secure our status in the Empire, just as Premysl Otakar did centuries ago.
      		 Bohemia or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 6 provinces in the following areas:
        • Inner Austria
        • Austria Proper
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains 25 power projection
      • Gains “Otakar's Legacy” for 20 years, giving:
        • −10% Core-creation cost
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Carniola
        • Tirol
        • Lower Bavaria
        • East Bavaria
        • Upper Bavaria
      • If completes this mission while “Humiliated the Habsburgs” modifier is still active:
        • “Humiliated the Habsburgs” modifier becomes permanent
      		 Humiliate Austria
      Secure the Vistula Land
      The strategic location and trade potential of the Vistula land are too significant to overlook. Securing it would boost our nation's influence, and would curb Polish influence and their capability to pose a threat to us.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Controls at least 20% of the trade in the Krakow trade node
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own province Nowy Sacz (2960)
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own province Krakow (262) which:
        • Has been developed at least 3 times
        • Has any Trade building
      		 Province Krakow (262):
      • Gets its Trade building upgraded[3]
      • Increases its Center of Trade level by 1

      Bohemia:

      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Sandomierz
        • Ruthenia
      • If completes this mission while having at least 30% or more trade power in the Krakow trade node:
        • Gets a −75% cost level 3 Trader advisor with our culture and religion
      		 Consolidate Silesia
      Reforge Great Moravia
      The beacon of Slavic unity, Great Moravia, once shone brightly. It's about time we reignite its flame and unite all Slavs, standing as the only and true successors to the legacy of Rostislav and Svatopluk.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Has +3 stability
      • Has at least +90 prestige
      • Has at least 90 legitimacy
      • Has at least 500 total development
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gets the “Legacy of Great Moravia” event

      Otakar's Legacy
      Recover Nitrava
      Secure the Vistula Land

      Right: To the North[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Consolidate Silesia
      Now that our own house is in order, we should look to our Crown Lands. Silesia has always been a part of our realm, and it is about time they enjoyed full status within our society.
      		 At least one of:
      • All of:
        • Silesia does not exist
        • Glogow and Opole:
          • Have at least +200 opinion of Bohemia
          • Have less than 1% liberty desire
      • All of:
        • Glogow and Opole do not exist
        • Bohemia or non-tributary subjects owns the Silesia area which:
          • Has less than 10% local autonomy
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Wielkopolska
        • Central Poland
        • Malopolska
      • If completes this mission by improving relations:
        • Opole[4]:
          • Inherits Glogow
          • Becomes Silesia
          • Becomes a free (cost no governing capacity penalty) incorporated vassal while keeping the previous liberty desire
      • Else:
        • Every provinces in the Silesia area:
          • Gains 1 base tax
          • Loses 10 years of separatism
      Polish Diplomacy
      The nature of the elective monarchy in Poland is an opportunity for our diplomats to advocate for a Bohemian heir to the Polish throne. We should ensure that a Bohemian dynasty rules in Poland.
      		 Bohemia:
      • If Poland (else the Commonwealth) exists:
        • At least one of:
          • Has an army at least as large as that of Poland (else the Commonwealth)
          • Has at least as much prestige as Poland
          • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 10 provinces in Poland (region)
      • Else:
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 12 provinces in Poland (region)
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains “Premyslid Heritage” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +50 Governing capacity
        • −5% Diplomatic annexation cost
      • If both we and Poland (else the Commonwealth) are independent Christian monarchies:
        • Gains "Restoration of Union" casus belli against Poland (else the Commonwealth) for 10 years
      • Else:
        • Gains a permanent claim on Poland (region)
      		 Consolidate Silesia
      Premyslid Heritage
      Bohemia reigns supreme once again. However, a Margrave in our court has slyly pointed out that we possess a legitimate claim to the lands of our forebears, the Premyslids. Times have moved on and borders shifted, but we believe some of these claims still carry weight.
      		 Bohemia or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 20 provinces in Poland (region)
      		 Bohemia:
      • Upgrades “Premyslid Heritage” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +100 Governing capacity
        • −10% Diplomatic annexation cost
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • East Prussia
        • West Prussia
      • If completes this mission while Poland or the Commonwealth are our subjects:
        • Becomes mutual historical friends with them
      		 Polish Diplomacy
      Pomerelian Reconquest
      The success of our conquest still rings in our ears. The Pomerelian lands to the North were also under the control of Wenceslaus II, and must therefore be ours.
      		 Bohemia or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 6 provinces in the following areas:
        • East Prussia
        • West Prussia
      		 Bohemia:
      • Unlocks a decision allowing us to release Prussia as a vassal
      • Gains 20 convertible[5] army tradition
      		 Premyslid Heritage
      Restore Královec
      The City of Königsberg was founded in honor of our king Otakar II. Under the rule of the Teutons, however, the city became but a shade of its former glory and importance. It's high time Královec returned to its rightful place under the Bohemian crown, reviving its past splendor.
      		 Bohemia or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns province Königsberg (41) which:
        • Has at least 25 development
        • Has our religion
        • Has no remaining building slot
        • Has more development than every coastal province bordering the Baltic Sea
      		 Province Königsberg (41):
      • Gains 3 base production
      • Gains “Pearl of the Baltic” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +10 Local trade power

      Bohemia:

      • Gains “Restore the Amber Road” ( +50 Trade power) in the Baltic Sea trade node for the rest of the game
      		 Pomerelian Reconquest

      Middle bottom: Into Germany[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Lion of the Empire
      As the only truly centralized realm and a kingdom within the Holy Roman Empire, we have the perfect position to assert our claims and exploit the weak structures of the Empire for our own benefit. We are the lion of the Empire, and we shall claim our destiny!
      		 Bohemia:
      • Has an army of at least 25 regiments
      • Has at least +40 prestige

      No country in the Holy Roman Empire:

      • Has an army larger than Bohemia
      • Has more development than Bohemia
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains “Lion of the Empire” for 15 years, giving:
        • −10% Aggressive expansion impact
      • If completes this mission while Saxony exists:
        • Gain "Subjugation" casus belli against Saxony for 30 years
      • Else:
        • Gains a permanent claim on the Saxony area
      • If completes this mission while Brandenburg exists:
        • Gain "Subjugation" casus belli against Brandenburg for 30 years
      • Else:
        • Gains a permanent claim on the Mittelmark area
      Electorate of Saxony
      We require the support of additional electors. We should set our sights on the Elector of Saxony next, as we have long sought an opportunity to control Dresden.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Is not a subject nation
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own Saxony area
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains 100 administrative power
      • If Saxony is our subject and is an Elector of the Holy Roman Empire:
        • Gets “The Fate of Saxony” event
      		 Lion of the Empire
      Boží Bojovníci
      During the Hussite Wars, the Hussites revolutionized the use of armored wagons during battles, which allowed them to claim victories against very unfavorable odds. No matter what our stance towards the Hussites is, it would be unwise to leave this successful tactic a thing of the past.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Has at least 60 army tradition is at least 60
      • Is employing any Military advisor of at least level 2
      • Has won at least 20 battles
      		 Bohemia:
      • Unlocks the “Wagenburg Tactics” Tier 5 government reform
      		 Lion of the Empire
      Electorate of Brandenburg
      If we wish to secure the throne of the Holy Roman Empire, we will need the support of the electors. And what better way to secure a vote than to make the upstart nation of Brandenburg subservient to our political whims?
      		 Bohemia:
      • Is not a subject nation
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own the Mittelmark area
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains −10 aggressive expansion with all nations in the Holy Roman Empire
      • If Brandenburg is our subject and is an Elector of the Holy Roman Empire:
        • Gets “The Fate of Brandenburg” event
      		 Lion of the Empire
      Bohemian Commonwealth
      We must look beyond what has traditionally been considered Bohemia to establish a greater commonwealth. Our empire shall span Central Europe, and we shall take our seat among the great powers of the world.
      		 Bohemia or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns the following provinces:
        • Prague (266)
        • Krakow (262)
        • Berlin (50)
        • Dresden (61)
        • Pest (153)
        • Wien (134)

      No province in the Balkans region:

      • Is owned by a Muslim country
      		 Bohemia:
      • Is renamed “Bohemian Commonwealth”
      • Gains 50 power projection
      • Remove modifier “Premyslid Heritage”
      • Gain “Bohemian Commonwealth” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +200 Governing capacity
        • −15% Diplomatic annexation cost
        • +1 Possible policies

      Electorate of Saxony
      Revenge for Nicopolis
      Electorate of Brandenburg
      Pomerelian Reconquest

      Bottom half missions[edit | edit source]

      Left: Religious matter[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      End the Regency
      The conflict between the various Hussite factions and those loyal to the Church of Rome has reached an uneasy settlement. Uncertainty in the line of succession is once again creating tensions between the religious factions. We must resolve the matter of succession and take a firm stance on the issue of religious orthodoxy.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Has had the "Return of the Hussites" event or the year is at least 1500
      • Is not in a regency
      • If is a monarchy:
        • Has at least 75 legitimacy
      		 Bohemia:
      • If completes this mission while being Catholic:
        • Unlocks the "Catholic branch" mission
        • Gains “Purge the Hussites” for 20 years, giving:
          • +1% Missionary strength vs heretics
      • Else:
        • Unlocks the "Hussite branch" mission
        • Gains “Return of the Hussites” for 20 years, giving:
          • −20% Missionary maintenance cost

      Catholic branch missions[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Purge the Hussite Heresy
      Although the radical Hussites have been destroyed during the Hussite Wars, many pockets of their resistance still plague the lands of the Bohemian crown. We must take a harsh stance against them and get rid of their heretical teachings, or else we risk another bloodshed in the future.
      		 Every province in the Bohemia, Moravia and Erzgebirge areas:
      • Is owned by Bohemia
      • Follows our religion
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gets a −75% cost level 2 Theologian advisor with our culture and religion
      • If completes this mission while being Catholic:
        • Catholicism loses 2% reform desire
        • Gains 20 papal influence
      • Else:
        • Gains 50 diplomatic power
      		 End the Regency
      Shield of the True Faith
      We have already proven our capability to fight against the heresies that threaten Christendom. Let us officially assume the title of our faith's protectors, so that we shall stand proudly as the champions of the cross.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Is the Defender of the Faith
      • Has won at least 20 battles against heretics or heathens
      • Has converted at least 10 provinces
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains “Shield of the True Faith” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +0.25 Prestige per development from missionary
        • +10% Religious unity
      • If completes this mission while being Catholic:
        • Upgrades the "Religious Diplomats" clergy privilege, granting an additional:
          • +10 Opinion of same religion
          • +10 Opinion from others of the same religion
      		 Purge the Hussite Heresy
      Catholic Empire
      As the shield of our faith, it is our sacred duty to protect the true teaching from those who seek to corrupt it. We must take a harsh stance against the heresies spreading throughout the Empire and make the Holy Roman Empire holy again!
      		 Bohemia:
      • Is the Holy Roman Emperor

      At least one of:

      • The religion of the Holy Roman Empire is fixed
      • All of:
        • At least 50 years have passed since the beginning of the Protestant reformation
        • All countries in the Holy Roman Empire follow our religion
      		 Bohemia:
      • If completes this mission while being Catholic:
        • Diplomat and Statesman advisors now give +5 reasons to elect per level
        • Gains “Triumph of the Cross” for 20 years, giving:
          • +20% Manpower in true faith provinces
      • Else:
        • Gains 50 convertible[2] prestige
      		 Shield of the True Faith

      Hussite branch missions[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Hussite Resurgence
      Despite, or perhaps due to the death of Jan Hus in 1415, proponents of the Hussite movement have harbored bitter resentment towards us for many years. Conflicts have come to a head once again, and it is only a matter of time before we must make a choice on behalf of our people.
      		 Every province in the Bohemia, Moravia and Erzgebirge areas:
      • Is owned by Bohemia
      • Is Hussite
      		 Bohemia:
      • If completes this mission while being Hussite:
        • Creates a Hussite Center of Reformation for 100 years in Prague (266)
        • Gains “Spread the Word of Hus” for 20 years, giving:
          • −33% Cost of enforcing religion through war
      • Else:
        • Gains “Utraquist Resurgence” for 20 years, giving:
          • −1 National unrest
          • −25% Stability cost modifier
      		 End the Regency
      Defenestrate the Heretics
      A Reformation is sweeping across Europe, and our history of unorthodoxy places us at the forefront of the theological and political conflict. We must take a stand for the true faith and introduce our heretical enemies to a window!
      		 At least one of:
      • Bohemia or non-tributary subjects owns at least 30 provinces following our religion
      • At least 10 countries follow our religion
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains “Defenestrations of Prague” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +1 Diplomatic reputation
        • +1% Missionary strength vs heretics
      		 Hussite Resurgence
      Hussite Empire
      The Catholic attempts to stop us from revealing the true teachings to the masses have been in vain. As Hussite faith reigns supreme, the time has come for us to officially claim the Imperial mantle and turn the Holy Roman Empire into a true Hussite Empire!
      		 Bohemia:
      • Is not a subject nation
      • If the Holy Roman Empire exists and is Catholic, Protestant or Hussite:
        • At least one of:
          • Was on the winning side in the Religious League War
          • All of:
            • Is the Holy Roman Emperor
            • At least one of:
              • The religion of the Holy Roman Empire is fixed
              • The Peace of Westphalia has been signed
      • Else:
        • The Reformed religion is enabled
        • Has at least 100% religious unity
      		 Bohemia:
      • If completes this mission while being Hussite:
        • Applying Hussite Aspects of Faith now has a 20% chance of refunding 25 church power
        • Gains “Pravda Vítezí” for 20 years, giving:
          • −5% All power costs
      • Else:
        • Gains 50 convertible[2] prestige
      		 Defenestrate the Heretics

      Middle: Internal development[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Bohemian Crystal
      The production of an especially beautiful style of glasswork has become popular in our realm. We should promote this industry and ensure that our works are prized throughout the courts of Europe.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Owns provinces Erz (1771) and Liegnitz (4238) which:
        • Has been developed at least 5 times
        • Has any Production building
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gets the “Bohemian Crystal” event
      • If completes this mission while owning at least 3 provinces which produce Glass:
        • Province Erz (1771) gets its Production building upgraded[6]
      Recover from Hussite Wars
      The Hussite Wars have devastated the lands of the Bohemian crown. Before we once again concentrate on external affairs, we must see to the restoration of internal order.
      		 Bohemia:
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least +1 stability
        • Has at least 90% of its maximum manpower reserves
      • Owns the following areas with less than 10 devastation:
        • Bohemia
        • Moravia
        • Erzgebirge
      		 Bohemia:
      • Removes all devastation from every owned province in Bohemia, Moravia and Erzgebirge areas
      • Gains “Recovered from the Hussite Wars” for 25 years, giving:
        • −1 National unrest
        • +12 Tax income
      Bohemian Industry
      The Kingdom of Bohemia should avoid being behind our neighbors in terms of industrialization. It is worth the investment to raise our profile in the area, and as such, to promote greater industry.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 10 provinces with any Production building
      • At least one of:
        • Gains at least 20 monthly income
        • Has at least 25% of the income is from production
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains 2 years of production income
      • Gains “Early Industrialization” for 15 years, giving:
        • +10% Production efficiency
        • −5% Development cost modifier
        • 20% chance of gaining 1 base production every time increasing the base production of provinces with a Production building
      The Golden City
      Prague, sometimes called the Golden City, is a pride of our kingdom and one of the most important cities in the entire Europe. It should be in our best interest to make the city deserve its nickname, and make it overshadow all capital cities of our rivals.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Owns province Prague (266) which:
        • Has at least 30 development
        • Has at least 3 buildings
      		 Province Prague (266):
      • Gains 1 base tax, 1 base production and 1 base manpower the next 3 times we expand infrastructure in Prague
      • Gains “The Golden City” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +50% Local tax modifier
        • +25% Local production efficiency

      Bohemia:

      • If completes this mission while Prague is the most developed province in Europe:
        • Gains 200 splendor

      Bohemian Industry
      Bohemian Crystal
      Recover from Hussite Wars

      The Vladislav Hall
      The Vladislav Hall is the largest hall of the Prague Castle, used for various public events, receptions, coronations, and even knight tournaments. Maintaining the grandeur of the Vladislav Hall is a testament to our nation's glory.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Owns province Prague (266) which:
        • Has at least 45 development
        • Has any Courthouse building
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least 60% crown land ownership
        • Gains at least 10 diplomatic power per month
        • Has at least 5 ruler's diplomatic skill
      		 Province Prague (266):
      • Gets its Courthouse building upgraded[7]

      Every currently and future owned province with a Courthouse building:

      • Gains “Efficient Courthouse” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −1 Local unrest
      		 The Golden City
      Reform the Bergordnung
      New uses for coal are being discovered that could revolutionize the face of industry throughout the world. We should reform our mining laws so that we can start producing coal at peak efficiency.
      		 Bohemia:
      • Owns at least 20 provinces with any manufactory
      		 Bohemia:
      • Gains the Industrious ruler personality trait
      • Unlocks the “Bergordnung Reforms” Tier 8 government reform
      		 The Golden City
      Czech Heavy Industry
      The lands we control are abundant in resources that fuel the currently ongoing industrial revolution. Let us put them to good use, and revitalize our economy thanks to massive investments into production and manufacturing.
