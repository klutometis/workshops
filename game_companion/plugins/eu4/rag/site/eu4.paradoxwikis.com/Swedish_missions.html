      Top missions[edit | edit source]

      Swedish independence[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Engelbrekt Rebellion
      In 1434 and in 1436, brave Swedish miners and peasants, led by the nobleman Engelbrekt Engelbrektsson, raised their pickaxes and pitchforks in hope of freeing Sweden from the foul influence of the Danes. But of course, the rebellion broke apart when Engelbrekt was assassinated. While his rebellion might have failed, its spirit still lives on in our people. It is time to raise troops who are ready to fight for the independence of our country!
      		 Sweden:
      • Has an army of at least 100% of its force limit
      • At least one of:
        • Is not a subject nation
        • Has 100% liberty desire
        • Any hired the “Free Company” or “Free City Company” mercenary company
      		 Sweden:
      • If is a subject nation:
        • Gets “The Engelbrekt Rebellion” event
      • Else:
        • Gains 10 000 manpower
      Swedish Allies
      While our people yearn for independence, our forces alone might not be enough to enforce our liberty from the Danes. We need to find allies outside Scandinavia who seek the destruction of the Kalmar Union.
      		 Sweden:
      • If is a subject nation:
        • At least one of:
          • At least 2 rivals or enemies of our overlord have at least +100 opinion of us
          • Has 100% liberty desire
      • Else:
        • Is not a subject nation
      		 Sweden:
      • If is a subject nation:
        • Gets the “Supporters of Swedish Independence” event
      • Else:
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
      Military Advice
      The Engelbrekt Rebellion failed due to the assassination of Engelbrekt, as well as the support of Stockholm for the Danish crown. But even if Engelbrekt would have managed to gain the support of Stockholm, he had no military skills to lead the rebels to victory. We shall do better and ensure military advice when the moment of our independence war comes.
      		 Sweden:
      • Is employing any military advisor
      • At least one of:
        • Is not a subject nation
        • Has the “Vasa leads the Rebellion” modifier
        • Has 100% liberty desire
        • Has at least 30% nobility control of the land
      		 Sweden:
      • If is either independent or has the “Vasa leads the Rebellion” modifier:
        • Gains 100 administrative power
        • Gains 100 military power
      • Else:
        • Gets “A New Leader Arises” event
      Support from the Estates
      While our peasants and lower class dream of a future without Danish influence, the higher class is more reluctant to follow the call of liberty. After all, the Danish king granted many privileges to our estates, which make them fairly loyal to the Danish cause. Fortunately, the greed of the estates does not know loyalty. If we grant them more privileges than the Danish kings have in the past, then there is a chance that they support us.
      		 Sweden:
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least 3 privileges granted to at least 2 estates
        • Has 1 or less estate
        • All of:
          • Is a subject nation
          • Has 100% liberty desire
          • Has had the "Stockholm Bloodbath" event
        • All of:
          • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
          • Is not at war
      		 Sweden:
      • If is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
        • Gains 50 administrative power
        • Gains 50 diplomatic power
        • Gains 50 military power
      • Else:
        • If has had the "Stockholm Bloodbath" event:
          • Gets “The Consequences of the Bloodbath of Stockholm” event
        • Else:
          • Gets “The Support of the Estates” event
      Declare Independence!
      The time for our independence has finally come! Let us break this union of incompetence and establish a crown which is worthy to rule over Sweden!
      		 Sweden:
      • Is not a subject nation
      		 Sweden:
      • If the country has a stability of less than +3,
        • then it gains 1 stability,
        • else it gains 50 administrative power.
      • Gets the “Rise of the Swedish Kingdom” event once winning its Independence War
      • Gains a permanent claim on the Skåneland area

      The Crown of Norway
      The Kalmar Union has been crippled by our independence, but it still remains due to the close ties between Denmark and Norway. Although the Danish king wants to deny it, Norway is mostly a province in the eyes of the Danish administration rather than a real kingdom of its own. It is our duty as good neighbor to free the Norwegians from Danish oppression and let them be ruled by a caring monarch for once.
      		 Sweden:
      • At least one of:
        • Has Norway as our subject
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 8 provinces in the following areas:
          • Østlandet, including at least Akershus (17)
          • Vestlandet, including at least Bergenhus (23)
          • Trøndelag, including at least Trøndelag (20)
      		 Sweden:
      • If completes this mission while Norway is our subject and has at least +50 opinion of us:
        • Creates a mutual historical friendship between Norway and Sweden
      • Gains “The Subjugation of Norway” for 15 years, giving:
        • +10% Manpower recovery speed
        • +5% Nobility loyalty equilibrium
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • North Jutland
        • Denmark
        • Schleswig-Holstein
      		 Swedish Skåneland
      Fall of Denmark
      With Norway under our care, Denmark is all that is left of the once mighty Kalmar "Union". Still, our independence will always be threatened as long a Danish king exists. It is time to take Denmark for ourselves and do what the Kalmar Union never did: unite all of Scandinavia under one proper king.
      		 Sweden or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns the following provinces:
        • Sjælland (12)
        • Lund (6)
        • Østjylland (4142)

      Denmark:

      • At least one of:
        • Does not exist
        • Does not own any province in Scandinavia (region)
        • Is our subject
      		 Sweden:
      • Gains 200 military power

      Province Lund (6):

      • Gets a level 2 center of trade

      Provinces Sjælland (12) and Østjylland (4142):

      • Get their center of trade increased by 1, to a maximum of level 2
      		 The Crown of Norway
      The German Coastline
      In order to achieve true hegemony in the Baltics we have to ensure that no coastline can endanger our trade or our national security. Right now, however, the areas of Pomerania and Mecklenburg are in foreign hands, which might be against our interests. We should either find a trustworthy ally in North Germany who holds these coastlines, or conquer them for ourselves.
      		 At least one of:
      • Sweden or non-tributary subjects:
        • Owns at least 7 provinces in the Hinterpommern, Vorpommern or Mecklenburg areas
      • Any ally with at least +100 opinion and 70 trust with us:
        • Owns at least 7 provinces in the Hinterpommern, Vorpommern or Mecklenburg areas
      		 Sweden:
      • If completes this mission through conquest:
        • Gains “The Conquest of Pomerania” for 25 years, giving:
          • −10% Core-creation cost
        • Gains a claim on the following areas:
          • Lower Saxony
          • Weser
      • If completes this mission through diplomacy:
        • Gains 50 favors with the ally who completes the mission
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
        • Gains “Germanic Alliance” for 25 years, giving:
          • +25% Monthly favor growth modifier
          • +1 Diplomatic reputation
        • Loses claim on the following areas:
          • Mecklenburg, expect Lübeck (45)
          • Vorpommern
          • Hinterpommern
      		 Declare Independence!
      Bane of the Hansa
      The Hansa is a major player in the Baltics. It controls the trade flow from North Germany, and has a hard grip on the trade of the Baltic Sea as a whole. Additionally, it was the cause of many wars between Denmark and the Holy Roman Empire and caused indirect damage to our country. It is time to remind these merchants where their place in the Baltics really is.
      		 At least one of:
      • Lübeck does not exists
      • Sweden or non-tributary subject owns Lübeck (45)
      • Sweden has at least +50 warscore against Lübeck

      Province Lübeck (45):

      • Is controlled by Sweden or non-tributary subject
      		 Sweden:
      • Gains “Swedish Trade Dominance” ( +30 Trade power) in the Lübeck trade node for 50 years
      • If completes this mission through owning Lübeck:
        • Province Lübeck (45):
          • Gains 100 progress towards prosperity
          • Gains 4 base production
      • Else, if completes this mission through controlling Lübeck:
        • Province Lübeck (45) gains 80 devastation
        • Gains money and military power proportional to city size
        • Province Lübeck (45) owner gets “The Pillage of Lübeck” event

      Conquer the East[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Varangians' Legacy
      The City of Novgorod was founded by the ancient Varangians of Scandinavia when they still followed the trade paths to the Byzantine Empire in the 8th century. Eventually, Novgorod became part of the East Slavic people and the old signs of Varangians vanished. But the city still is part of our ancient heritage and as such it is our right to reclaim it.
      		 Sweden:
      • Owns core provinces Neva (33) and Novgorod (310) which:
        • Has less than 1 devastation
        • Is in a state
        • Has enacted the “Centralization Effort” state edict
      		 Sweden:
      • Gains “Novgorodian” as an accepted culture[2]
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Tver
        • Moscow
        • Pskov

      Province Novgorod (310):

      • Is renamed "Hólmgarðr"
      • Get its center of trade increased by 1, to a maximum of level 2
      • Converts to our religion

      Province Neva (33):

      • Get its center of trade increased by 1, to a maximum of level 2
      • Converts to our religion
      • If is not Finnish nor 'Swedish':
        • Adopts Novgorodian culture
      		 Declare Independence!
      The City of the Rus
      The city of Moscow is the home of the Muscovite people, who eventually adopted the Rus name. Like Novgorod, so was Moscow founded by our ancestors, though they were mostly known as the Kievan Rus. Over the centuries, the connection between our people and Kievan Rus has become loose at best. However, the Russians of Muscovy have proven to be a formidable threat. It is in our interest to conquer their center of power to protect Sweden from their influence. The historical connection to the city is just a neat bonus.
      		 Sweden:
      • Owns core province Moskva (295) which:
        • Is connected to our capital
        • Has less than 20% local autonomy
        • Has less than 1 local unrest
        • Has our primary culture or an accepted culture
      		 Sweden:
      • Gains 30 prestige
      • Gains “East European Integration” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +2 Max promoted cultures
      		 Varangians' Legacy
      Stabilize the [Root.GovernmentName]
      With our breakout from the Kalmar Union, our [Root.GovernmentName] is in a time of turmoil and instability. We should stabilize our realm, legitimize our ruler and pay off the estates before we can expand into any direction yet.
      		 Sweden:
      • Has at least +1 stability
      • Has at least 80 legitimacy (or equivalent)
      • Is not a subject nation
      • Is not at war
      • At least one of:
        • Is not in debt with any of the estates
        • Does not have any estate
      		 Sweden:
      • Gets “The Crown of Polands” event

      Declare Independence!

      The Kingdom of Poland
      The Kingdom of Poland to our south is the target of the desire of many realms: the Teutonic Knights try to expand their power into Polish territory, the Muscovites are more than interested in annexing the West Slavic people, and the Ottomans in the south pose a serious threat to everyone in this region.

      To achieve true hegemony in the North we have to find a policy of how we handle Poland. On the one hand it might be worth trying to get our dynasty onto their throne, while on the other hand the Kingdom of Poland might become a strategic and reliable ally against the Russians and the Holy Roman Empire.

      Alternatively, we could conquer them the old fashion way and be one of many others who claim the valuable Polish lands for themselves.
      		 Sweden:
      • If is Christian and chose to follow a policy of Dynastic Ties:
        • If the Commonwealth, else Poland exists:
          • Shares the same dynasty than the Commonwealth, else Poland
          • Has at least as much prestige than the Commonwealth, else Poland
          • Has at least as much legitimacy (or equivalent) than the Commonwealth, else Poland
        • Else:
          • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 20 provinces in Poland (region)
      • Else, if chose to follow a policy of Conquest or a policy of Dynastic Ties without being Christian:
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 15 provinces in Poland (region)
      • Else, if chose to follow a policy of Coexistence:
        • If the Commonwealth, else Poland exists:
          • The Commonwealth, else Poland:
            • Is allied to us
            • Has at least +150 opinion towards us
            • Has at least 80 trust with us
            • Has a royal marriage with us, both both countries are monarchies
            • Has at least 600 total and non-tributary subject development
        • Else:
          • Neither the Commonwealth nor Poland exists
      		 Sweden:
      • If is Christian and chose to follow a policy of Dynastic Ties:
        • If the Commonwealth, else Poland exists:
          • Gains "Restoration of Union" casus belli against the Commonwealth else Poland for 20 years
        • Else:
          • Gains 100 administrative power
          • Gains 100 diplomatic power
          • Gains 100 military power
      • Else, if chose to follow a policy of Conquest or a policy of Dynastic Ties without being Christian:
        • Gains 150 administrative power
        • Gains “Polish” as an accepted culture[2]
        • Every owned or non-tributary subjects owned province in Poland (region):
          • Loses 25% local autonomy
          • Loses 10 years of separatism
      • Else, if chose to follow a policy of Coexistence:
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
        • Gains 400 splendor
        • If the Commonwealth, else Poland exists:
          • Becomes mutual historical friends with the Commonwealth, else Poland
      		 Stabilize the [Root.GovernmentName]

      Lord of Eastern Europe
      The Varangians, our ancestors, were once the rulers of Eastern Europe. They established a trade route from Sweden all the way to Constantinople, the capital of the Eastern Romans, in the 10th century. Over time, the Varangians eventually became the Kievan Rus, and the connection between Sweden and Greece dissolved as more and larger groups of interests found their way into Eastern Europe. Although we do not have any historical claims on Eastern Europe anymore, the trade routes of the old times still exist. These routes are our legacy, and it is our duty to reclaim them once again - either through diplomacy or through conquest.

      The Peasants' Agenda[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Peasants' Agenda
      The peasantry of Sweden plays an influential role within our country. They are the backbone of our economy and feed our population. Naturally, we should make sure that the peasantry stays loyal to their home country. Constructing manufactories in their name might show to them our good intentions.
      		 Sweden:
      • Has at least +2 stability
      • Does not have an ongoing disaster
      • Does not have Particularist or Peasant rebels
      • Owns at least 10 provinces which:
        • Produces Grain, Livestock or Fish
        • Has any manufactory
        • At least one of:
          • Has had "The Dacke War" disaster
          • Has at least 10 development
      		 Sweden:
      • "The Dacke War" disaster can not trigger
      • Gains “Support of the Peasantry” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −1 National unrest

      Every currently and future owned province which produces Grain, Livestock or Fish:

      • Gains “Support of the Peasantry” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +33% Local goods produced modifier

      Internal development[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Integrate the Sami
      The Sami are people living in the dense forests of the North, where no other Scandinavian - be it Swede, Norwegian or Dane - lives. They do not trade with the world outside their territory and are completely isolated. They are, however, living in a territory which formally belongs to us. We should integrate them into our society so we can benefit from these forests.
      		 Sweden:
      • Owns the Laponia, Norrland and Ostrobothnia areas which:
        • Follows our religion
        • Has an accepted culture
      		 Sweden:
      • Unlocks the “Forest Expansion” burghers privilege

      Every province in the Laponia, Norrland and Ostrobothnia areas:

      • Loses 100% local autonomy
      • Gains 2 base manpower
      Forest Expansion
      The forests of Scandinavia are mostly uncharted territory. Apart from the crafty Sami living there, the land is mostly empty of inhabitants due to the harsh winters and the lack of resources. However, the forests are great hunting grounds for fur and some have goods like iron in the ground. It might be advisable to expand into the forests and settle even the harsher parts of Scandinavia.
      		 Sweden:
      • Owns every Woods and Forest provinces in Scandinavia (region) which:
        • Has at least 6 development
      		 Every owned province in Scandinavia (region):
      • Gains 10 progress towards prosperity
      • If has Arctic climate:
        • Gains “Arctic Expansion” for the rest of the game, giving:
          • −40% Local development cost
          • +1 Possible number of buildings
      		 Integrate the Sami

      Improve the economy[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Increase our Income
      Scandinavia is not one of the richest regions of Europe. While our iron and especially our copper production keeps us above the deficit, it is still not enough revenue to secure a safe future for our [Root.GovernmentName]. We should increase our state income by any means necessary.
      		 Sweden:
      • Has increased its monthly income by at least 200% of its starting monthly income
      • Has no loan
      • Has less than 1 corruption
      • Is not bankrupt
      • Is not running a deficit
      		 Sweden:
      • Gains “Economical Upswing” for 25 years, giving:
        • +0.5 Global prosperity growth
        • −20% Shipbuilding time
        • +10% Production efficiency

      Every owned province in the Västra Svealand and Östra Svealand areas:

      • Gains “Economical Upswing” for 25 years, giving:
        • −5% Local development cost
        • −10% Local construction time
        • −10% Local construction cost
      Establish a Trade Fleet
      The Norwegian and the Danes are experts of the Sea while we are a battle hardened people on the land. Still, it is undeniable that a trade fleet is more than needed if we want to ensure wealth and economical stability for our [Root.GovernmentName].
      		 Sweden:
      • Has at least 12 light ships
      • Has a navy of at least 100% of its force limit
      		 Sweden:
      • Gains 15 navy tradition
      • Gains “Baltic Trade Fleet” for 25 years, giving:
        • +50% Naval tradition from protecting trade
        • +25% Ship trade power

      The Holy Roman Empire is a patchwork of ambitious German states who are busy with petty infightings. Obviously the Emperor can not keep these princes in check.       Secure the Low Countries
      The Low Countries are home to the Dutch, a people well known for their maritime and mercantilistic achievements. Their trade in the English Channel occasionally caused a cut to our own profit in Lübeck, but usually we do not interact much with each other. However, now that we have started to expand our influence into Germany it is clear that the Dutch are within our scope too. Depending on our position with the Holy Roman Empire we should either ensure that they are part of said Empire, or part of our own realm.
      		 Scandinavia:

      • Else:

            • +25% Local goods produced modifier

          • Saxony

          • North Rhine
          • North Westphalia
          • South Westphalia
          • Braunschweig
      		 Handle the Empire
