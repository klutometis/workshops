National missionsEdit

JHC_GER_1Edit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Secure the Low Countries
Dutch ambitions of independence must be crushed. We must not only subjugate the Low Countries but also occupy them directly, placing them under our direct and firm governance.
 The country:
  • Owns province Lübeck (45)
  • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 15 provinces in the Low Countries (region)
 The country:
  • Gets the   “Choosing our Trade Ports” event
  • Gains “Crushed Dutch Independence” for   20 years, giving:
    •   −5 Years of separatism
    •   −33% Harsh treatment cost
  • Gains a permanent claim on France (region)
   Blood and Iron
 
Invade France
France has had expansionist ambitions in Germany for centuries. We cannot allow such a powerful nation to threaten the unity of our nation. Let us take the fight to them and launch an invasion of France!
 The country or non-tributary subjects:
  • Owns province Paris (183)
  • Owns at least 40 provinces in France (region)
 The country:
  • Gets the   “Parade Through Paris” event
  • If is not revolutionary, has less than   100 absolutism and it is at least the   Age of Absolutism:
    • Gains   20 absolutism
  • Else:
    • Gains   200 military power
   Secure the Low Countries
 
Burn the Wooden Wall
The Royal Navy of the British Isles allows the English to project power all over the world, and most importantly in Germany. We must put an end to their meddling and burn their wooden wall to ashes that will rest at the bottom of the ocean!
 At least one of:
  • No country in Britain (region) has at least   5 heavy ships
  • The country or non-tributary subjects owns at least 8 provinces in Britain (region)
 The country:
  • Gains “Burned the Wooden Wall” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +10% Morale of navies
  • Gain a permanent claim on Britain (region)
  • If we complete this mission by destroying Britain navy:
    • Gains an   admiral with   100 navy tradition
    • Gains   25 navy tradition
  • If we complete this mission via conquest:
    • Gains a   general with   80 army tradition
    • Gains   15 convertible[2] army tradition

We can earn both rewards of this mission by fulfilling both requirements

  Imperial Navy

  Invade France

JHC_GER_2Edit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Blood and Iron
The Princes that have thus far resisted our rule must be brought into the fold. It will take more than gentle persuasion to unite Germany; it will require a politics of power and an exertion of military force.
 The country:
  • Owns at least 50 provinces in North or South Germany (region)
 The country:
  • Gets a −50% cost level 3   Statesman with our culture and religion
  • Gains “Blut und Eisen” for   30 years, giving:
    •   +10% Manpower recovery speed
    •   +10% Land fire damage
  • Gains a permanent claim on the the following regions:
    • Low Countries
    • Poland
  • Gains a permanent claim on the the following areas:
    • West Prussia
    • East Prussia
    • Denmark
    • North Jutland
 
Break the Habsburgs
The Habsburgs have traditionally been the Holy Roman Emperors and have remained to be the greatest obstacle to the German Unification. Their objections must be nullified.
 The country:
  • At least one of:
    •   Austria does not exist
    • Has won a war against   Austria
  • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 8 provinces in the following areas:
    • Austria Proper
    • Inner Austria
    • Tirol
  • If is   Germany:
    • At least one of:
      • The   Holy Roman Empire does not exist
      • Is the Holy Roman Emperor
 The country:
  • Gains   15 convertible[2] army tradition
  • Gains a permanent claim on Italy (region)
  • If is not the   Holy Roman Empire, was not   Austria and does not have the “von Habsburg” dynasty:
    • Gains “Defeated the Habsburgs” for the rest of the game, giving:
      •   +0.15 Monthly cultural unity
      •   +0.5 Yearly prestige
  • Else:
    • If has a stability of less than +3,
      • then it gains   1 stability,
      • else it gains   50 administrative power.
   Blood and Iron
 
Italian Invasion
Since the time of the Karlings the fates of Germany and Italy have been bound together. It is time to reestablish German hegemony over Italy.
 The country or non-tributary subjects:
  • Owns at least 40 provinces in Italy (region)
 The country:
  • If is the   Holy Roman Empire:
    • Gets   “The Shadow Kingdom” event
  • Else:
    • Gains   100 administrative power
    • Gains “Italy is Safer Divided” for the rest of the game, giving:
      •   +150 Governing capacity
    • If   Italy has never been formed:
      • Gains   20 power projection
   Break the Habsburgs
 
Imperial Navy
No German nation has yet had much success fighting at sea. We should build a fleet of heavy warships to rectify this situation.
 The country:
  • Has at least   1 admiral
  • Has at least   30 heavy ships
  • At least one of:
    • Has a navy of at least 100 ships
    • Has a navy of at least   90% of its force limit
 The country:
  • Gains   25 navy tradition
  • Gains a permanent claim on Southern Africa (region)
  • If has   'Lower Saxon' primary culture:
    • If has the "Hanseatic Doctrine" naval doctrine:
      • Gains “Hanseatic Doctrine Upgrade” for the rest of the game, giving:
        •   +1 Fleet movement speed
        •   +1 Max admiral maneuver
  • Else:
    • Unlocks the   "Hanseatic Doctrine" naval doctrine, which gives:
      •   +15% Ship trade power
      •   +100% Navy tradition from protecting trade
 
Scramble for Africa
The European powers are scrambling for a foothold in Africa, largely as a means of extending their reach into the East. [Root.GetName] cannot be left without its own African possessions, so we must mount an expedition!
 The country:
  • Owns at least 10 provinces in Southern Africa (region)
 The country:
  • Gets the   “Scramble for Africa” event
  • Gains   5% mercantilism
  • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
    • Shandong
    • South Hebei
    • North Hebei
  • Gains a permanent claim on every province in the following trade nodes:
    • Malacca
    • The Moluccas
    • Coromandel
   Imperial Navy
 
Overseas Empire
We should use our trading posts in Africa as a staging ground for expanding our reach to the Far East. The wealth of China awaits us!
 The country:
  • Owns at least 20 provinces in India or East Indies (region)
  • Owns any coastal province in China (region)
 The country:
  • Gains   ducats equal to 1 year of income
  • Gains “Asian Trade Companies” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   −10% Trade company investment cost

Most developed owned coastal province in China (region):

  • Gains “Treaty Port” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +5% Goods produced modifier
    •   +1 Local goods produced
    • As long as is controlled by us and has a merchant in the trade node
   Scramble for Africa

JHC_GER_3Edit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Annex Poland
The fertile farmlands of Poland could yield great profits and feed many in our nation if they could be wrested away from the incompetent rule of our enemies. We should launch an invasion of Poland to secure as much of this fertile land as possible.
 The country or non-tributary subjects:
  • Owns province Marienburg (1841)
  • Owns at least 25 provinces in Poland (region), including:
    • At least 5 provinces with Farmlands terrain
 The country:
  • Gets the   “Polish Partitions” event
  • "Malbork Castle" great project becomes   significant, if not already
  • Gain a permanent claim on the following regions:
    • Baltic
    • Scandinavia
    • Carpathia
   Blood and Iron
 
Carpathian Conquest
The Kingdom of Hungary has become a battleground in the struggle between great powers. We must lead our armies to the land of the Danube and the Carpathian Mountains and stake our own claim to the region, lest it fall into the hands of our opportunistic rivals.
 The country or non-tributary subjects:
  • Owns province Hunyad (1951)
  • Owns province Királyföld (4128)
  • Owns at least 20 provinces in Carpathia (region)
 The country:
  • Gets the   “Diploma Andreanum” event
   Annex Poland

  Break the Habsburgs

 
Industrialized Silesia
On our eastern frontiers lies Silesia, a land already greatly influenced by the German settlers and one that holds many coal deposits, especially in the east. To turn it into our industrial powerbase is of utmost importance.
 The country:
  • Owns province Ratibor (263)
  • Has at least   110 development in owned provinces in Silesia area with any manufactory
 Province Ratibor (263):
  • Will be able to get   Coal with the   Manufactories institution and   21 administrative technology

The country:

  • Gains “Silesian Industrialization” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +5% Production efficiency
   Protected Markets
 
Found Wilhelmshaven
A rising naval power needs a strategically placed harbor to keep their ships safe. The place for this haven has been chosen and the cornerstone has been placed. Now we just need to build it up.
 The country:
  • Owns province Oldenburg (55) which:
    • Has any Shipyard building
    • Has any Dock building
    • Has been developed at least 10 times
 Province Oldenburg (55):
  • Is renamed "Wilhelmshaven"
  • Gets a new   heavy ship flagship
  • Gains “North Sea Navy Port” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   −25% Local ship cost
    •   +50% Local ship repair
    •   −50% Local shipbuilding time
    •   +10% Naval forcelimit modifier
   Imperial Navy

JHC_GER_4Edit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Secure the North
It is imperative to connect the two halves of our maritime potential together, and receiving guarantees from the Scandinavians is one such end to the means.
 The country:
  • Owns province Slesvig (13)

At least one of:

  • The country or non-tributary subjects owns at least 7 provinces in Scandinavia (region)
  • Any   ally or non-tributary subjects owns at least 7 provinces in Scandinavia (region)
 The country:
  • Gains   10 navy tradition
  • Gains “New Ports in the Baltic” (  +50 Trade power) in the Lübeck trade node for the rest of the game

Province Slesvig (13):

  • Adopts   'Lower Saxon' culture
  • Gains “Resolved the Border Dispute” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   −10 Years of separatism
    •   −0.05 Monthly autonomy change
 
Baltic Conquest
We must seize the rich commercial ports of the Baltic and establish a military presence in the region.
 The country:
  • Is the   strongest trade power in the Baltic Sea trade node
  • Owns or non-tributary subject owns at least 30 provinces in Baltic or Scandinavia (region)
 The country:
  • Gains   2 years worth of   trade income
  • Gains a   general with   100 army tradition
  • Gains “Control Over the Ostsee” (  +50 Trade power) in the Baltic Sea trade node for the rest of the game
   Annex Poland

  Secure the North

 
Protected Markets
The profit that comes from trading in German goods should benefit German people in German nations. We must take measures to ensure that German markets are protected against foreign involvement.
 The country:
  • Controls at least   75% of the trade in the following trade nodes:
    • Saxony
    • Lübeck
    • Wien
    • Rheinland
 The country:
  • Unlocks the "Promote Mercantilism" burghers privilege
  • Gains “Protected Markets” for the rest of the game, givings:
    •   −33% Cost to promote mercantilism
  • Gains “Economic Turn Inwards” for   20 years, giving:
    •   +10% Goods produced modifier
   Promote Urbanization
 
Industrialized Rhineland
The Rhineland has the potential to be a great center of industry. This potential must not be wasted. We should invest heavily in the manufacturing industries in the region.
 The country:
  • Owns province Aachen (1878)
  • Has at least   250 development in owned provinces with any manufactory in the following areas:
    • North Rhine
    • Lower Rhineland
    • Palatinate
 Province Aachen (1878):
  • Will be able to get   Coal with the   Manufactories institution and   21 administrative technology

The country:

  • Gains “Rhenish Industrialization” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +10% Production efficiency
   Protected Markets
 
Dominant Economy
The [Root.GetAdjective] economy shall be the greatest in all of Europe. It will be impossible for other nations to rival our wealth if we develop our nation to its fullest potential.
 The country:
  • Is not bankrupt
  • Gains at least   600 ducats per month
  • Has at least   10 000 ducats
  • Has less than 5 loans
  • Controls at least   90% of the trade in the Home trade node
  • At least one of:
    • Has a   greater monthly income than any other country in Europe
    • Is the Economic hegemon
 The country:
  • Gains   150 administrative power
  • Merges "Rhenish Industrialization" and "Silesian Industrialization" into “The German Industrialization” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +20% Production efficiency
    •   −5% Development cost
  • If the   Age of Absolutism is not revolute:
    • Gain “Persian Giant” until the end of   Age of Absolutism, giving:
      •   +2 Diplomatic reputation
      •   −10% Liberty desire in subjects
      •   +15 Vassalization acceptance
  • Else:
    • Gain   150 diplomatic power

JHC_GER_5Edit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
United Industry
We ought to seek to have our industrial centers interconnected and developed. Through such, we will become industrial behemoths and will dominate European commerce.
 The country:
  • Owns at least 30 provinces with any manufactory
 The country:
  • Is able to construct the Kiel Canal regardless of its   administrative technology
  • Gains   100 government reform progress
  • Gains “Rapid Industrialization” for   30 years, giving:
    •   −10% Construction cost
 
Promote Urbanization
The multitudes of small towns that dot the German landscape must be transformed into glorious cities, each a beacon of culture and commerce.
 The country:
  • Owns at least 10 provinces in North or South Germany (region) with at least   30 development
  • Has developed its provinces at least 50 times
 The country:
  • If the   Burghers estate exists:
    • Gains “German Urbanization” (  15 burghers loyalty) for the rest of the game
  • Else:
    • Gains   5% mercantilism
  • Strengthen the "City Expansion Privileges" burghers privilege, making it:
    • No longer cost   max absolutism
    • While active, completing burghers agendas will distribute   3 random development

Three most developed provinces:

  • Gains “Major City” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +33% Local goods produced modifier
    •   +0.4 Local goods produced
    •   −30% Local development cost
   United Industry
 
Zollverein
Taxes and tolls between brother states serve only to hamper collective growth. Let us form a customs union to integrate our brotherly nations into our state economically.
 The country:
  • Has at least 8 government reforms
  • Has less than   10% average autonomy above the minimum
  • Gains at least   200 ducats per month
  • Is employing any   Administrative advisor of at least level 3
  • Is employing any   Diplomatic advisor of at least level 3
 The country:
  • Unlocks the "Customs Union" burghers privilege
  • Gains   3 years of production income
  • If is employing a   Treasurer advisor:
    • Gains   1.5 years of production income
   Promote Urbanization
 
Construct the Kiel Canal
To rely on the Öresund for navigation between the Baltic and North seas is to make a mistake. We ought to construct a canal to connect the two together without having to take the long route around
 The country:
  • Owns province Holstein (1775) which:
    • Has at least   50 province trade power
    • Has built the Kiel Canal great project
 The country:
  • Kiel Canal now grants   +1 merchant
  •   Trader advisors now also provides   +5% domestic trade power per advisor level

Province Holstein (1775) and Dithmarschen (4141):

  • Gain   2 base tax
  • Gain   2 base production
  • Gain   1 base manpower
   Zollverein

JHC_GER_3_gerEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Trust Your Neighbor
The German people are divided by petty squabbles. We cannot let divisions take hold of the nation; we must unify it together so we can brave what challenges the future holds for us.
   Germany:
  • Has at least   +80 prestige
  • If has enacted the “Cultural Fragmentation” government reform:
    • Has accepted at least 7 cultures
  • At least one of:
    • Has at least   50 power projection
    • Gains at least   11 diplomatic power per month
   Germany:
  • Gains   10 convertible[3] prestige
  • Gains   100 government reform progress
  • Removes “Cultural Fragmentation” government reform if enacted
 
Diplomatic Revolution
Europe has a new actor at its heart. It's time other nations started to take us seriously in their plans, especially as we are no longer a minor power.
   Germany:
  • Is at least the   fifth great power
  • Has an army of at least 200 regiments
  • Has at least   25 power projection
  • Has taken at least 4 Age rewards
   Germany:
  • Gains   100 splendor at the beginning of each following Age
  • If has a stability of less than +3,
    • then it gains   1 stability,
    • else it gains   50 administrative power.
  • Gains “Diplomatic Revolution” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +1 Diplomatic free policies
   Trust Your Neighbor

  Establish the Chancellorship

 
Concert of Europe
European diplomacy has become dominated by concerns about the balance of power. An ever-dwindling number of great nations have established themselves as the determinators of European affairs. Germany must be counted among these powers and carve out a place on the world stage.
   Germany:
  • At least one of:
    • Has at least   75 power projection
    • Has at least   2 500 total development
  • Has any   ally with at least   500 development, non-tributary subject included
  • If has any   rival:
    • Has any   rival with at least   500 development, non-tributary subject included
  • Else:
    • Has more   total development than any other nation in the world
   Germany:
  • Gains   100 diplomatic power
  • "Intervene in War" Great Power action now gives   15 power projection, decaying 1 yearly, up to a maximum of 30
  • "Influence Nation" Great Power action now gives   5 favors with the target country
  • Gains “Concert of Europe” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +1 Diplomatic reputation
   Diplomatic Revolution
 
Crush the Revolution
Armies of political radicals swarm the streets of the cities of Europe. We cannot abide this disruption of the natural order, and so we must march into the very heart of this Revolution and crush it utterly.
   Germany:
  • At least one of:
    • Has enacted the “Revolutionary Empire” or “Revolutionary Republic” government reform
    • Controls the capital of the   revolution target
    • All Centers of Revolution are removed
   Germany:
  • If the mission has been completed by Revolution destruction:
    • Gains   10 permanent power projection
    • Gains “Reactionary Zeal” for the rest of the game, giving:
      •   −20% Stability cost modifier
      •   −1 National unrest
  • If the mission has been completed by Revolution:
    • Gains “Flames of Revolution” for the rest of the game, giving:
      •   −10% Harsh treatment cost
      •   +10% Morale of armies
    • Gains “The Fight for Revolution” for the rest of the game, giving:
      •   +5% Discipline
      • Only applies to revolutionary guard regiments
   Concert of Europe

  Control the Junkers

JHC_GER_4_gerEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Establish the Chancellorship
[Root.GetName] cannot rely entirely on the skill of the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]. Our nation needs a reliable cabinet of ministers so that the governance of the state can be properly delegated, and those ministers must have a strong leader to organize their efforts. We should establish the office of Chancellor to provide this leadership.
   Germany:
  • Gains at least   10 diplomatic power per month
  • Has any Courthouse building in its capital
  • Is not running a deficit
  • Is employing every type of advisor of at least level 3
   Germany:
  • Gets the   “Excellent Statesmanship” event
  • Strengthen the "Statesman of the Nobility" nobility privilege, making it:
    • Give   +5 maximum absolutism
    • Making the   decision also recruit a   Statesman
  • Gains “The Chancellorship” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +1 Possible advisor
 
Control the Junkers
The landed nobility, or Junkers, have a great deal of influence over the [Root.GetAdjective] state. We must rein in these nobles and force them to serve the nation rather than extracting privileges from it.
   Germany:
  • Has at least   60 nobility loyalty, if they exist
  • Has at least   70% crown land ownership
   Germany:
  • If the   Nobility estate exists:
    • Gains “Loyal Junkers” (  15 nobility loyalty) for the rest of the game
    • Gains   5 military power for every   5 nobility loyalty up to a maximum of 75 at 75 loyalty
    • Gains “Support of the Junkers” for the rest of the game, giving:
      •   +1 Nobility max privileges
    • If has enacted the “Prussian Monarchy”, “Prussian Monarchy” or “Stratocratic Administration” government reform:
      • Strengthen the "Junker Supremacy" nobility privilege, making it give double the modifiers
    • Else:
      • Strengthen the "Primacy of the Nobility", making it give   −10% military technology cost
  • Else:
    • Gains   100 administrative power
   Establish the Chancellorship

  Diplomatic Revolution

JHC_GER_5_gerEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Compulsory Education
Every child in our realm should be required to attend German schools. This has the benefit of not only ensuring an educated workforce, but also of instilling proper German values and language into the youth.
   Germany:
  • Owns at least 10 provinces with a   University
   Germany:
  • Gains   5 convertible[4] innovativeness
  • Gains “German Education” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   −10% Culture conversion cost
    •   +10% Institution spread

If we had the   "Radical Reforms" event, we will be able to get it again. If we haven't we will be able to get it twice.

  Establish the Chancellorship
 
Kaiserreich
The German Empire shall eclipse all others. The fate of the world will rest in our hands alone.
   Germany:
  • Is a   monarchy,   republic or   theocracy
  • Has more   total development than any other nation in the world
  • If is not revolutionary:
    • Has at least   100 absolutism
  • Else:
    • Has at least   100 revolutionary zeal
   Germany:
  • Gets   “The Kaiserreich” event
  • Gains   60 convertible[3] prestige
  • Gains   40 power projection
   Compulsory Education
