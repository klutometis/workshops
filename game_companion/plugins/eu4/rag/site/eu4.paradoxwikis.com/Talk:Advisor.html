Better relations over timeEdit

What does Better relations over time: +30% actually mean? Does this only apply to the Improve Relations diplomatic action? Does it make it go 30% faster, or does it increase the 'cap' from 100 to 130? Or something unrelated or broader (all relations get better?) Thanks. --66.31.40.33 07:29, 31 December 2013 (CET)

I believe it simply makes good relations increase faster/decay more slowly, and negative relations increase slower/decay more quickly. ~ Meneth (talk) 10:36, 31 December 2013 (CET)

Updating bonus tablesEdit

Unsure how to update the information in the "Enlarging the advisor pool" bonus table. If it's easier, I can provide what's missing and someone else can fix it- have the information saved somewhere. TheWinner (talk) 15:22, 27 February 2015 (CET)

Each row in each table at {{Bonus table}} is an instance of {{BTRow}}, where the "i" parameter are the ideas that provide the bonus, the "t" parameter are the traditions, "b" are the ambitions (not sure why it isn't "a"), and "p" are the policies. Hope that helps. Anyone autoconfirmed (1+ edit, registered for 10+ minutes) can edit it. ~ Meneth (talk) 17:46, 27 February 2015 (CET)
I have added the deleted idea again and updated the bonus table template for 1.10. – Lillebror (talk) 13:12, 28 February 2015 (CET)

Trigger of additional portraitsEdit

What exact triggers the additional advisor portraits? – Lillebror (talk) 11:08, 6 March 2016 (CET)

I haven't been able to figure it out from game files (I believe it's not moddable), but from experience it seems to be based on the culture of the advisor's home province. As Timurids/Mughals for example I have Western, Muslim and Cossack portraits (Indian provinces give Western portraits, I think). Notably the Cossacks estate is not needed to get Cossack portraits. Hairy Dude (talk) 15:47, 16 March 2016 (CET)
