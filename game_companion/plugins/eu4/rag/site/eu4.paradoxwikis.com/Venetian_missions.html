      Venetian missions

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

      		Available only with the Winds of Change DLC enabled.

      This is a list of all missions of Venice[1]. The branching missions can be previewed and selected with the buttons right of the mission window after completing the mission Army of the Republic (the AI chooses the branch randomly).

      For the missions from the emperor DLC, see Venetian missions without winds of change.

      Clicking on a mission icon leads to the appropriate table row.

      The Promissione Ducale is the oath of office all candidates must swear by to become the new Doge of Venice. It outlines the duties of the Doge, and states the limits on the Doge's powers. Over the years, however, some of the checks have... waned, a little. If we were to draft a new primissione ducale, one that more firmly restates our values and the roles envisioned for the Doge, not only would our government be strengthened, but more virtuous candidates for office might step forward as well.

      Standardize Relazioni
      The 'Relazioni' are the reports our ambassadors send to the government, detailing the political, financial, and social state of the countries they serve in. These documents are essential for both our own diplomatic efforts and for maintaining the competitive advantage of our merchants abroad, but it is an increasingly common practice for copies of relazioni to be published and made available to anybody willing to pay a high price for them. This is a threat to our own diplomacy and security. We must crack down on these publications, and establish a common, secure system for the writing and dissemination of relazioni.
      		 Venice:
      • Is employing a Diplomat or Spymaster advisor of at least level 3

These associations, with names inspired by virtues or guilds, distinguish themselves through stocking hues and embroidered initials. Grand events, including masked processions, boat races, and theatrical productions, unfold amidst the splendor of Venice. Notably, the Festa della Sensa celebrates the city's union with the sea, featuring intricate rituals in the presence of foreign ambassadors and dignitaries.
