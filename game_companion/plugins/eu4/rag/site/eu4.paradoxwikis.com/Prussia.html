      Prussia is a formable country in Europe that can be formed by Brandenburg, the Teutonic Order, or any country with Prussian, Saxon, or Pomeranian culture (besides end-game tags such as Germany and a unified, centralised Holy Roman Empire). It also exists in campaigns started in 1525 or later. Prussia is a powerful military state with strong morale, discipline and military tradition; enough to ensure strong generals in commanding large armies, well-trained troops in aforementioned armies and good military rulers.

      If played correctly, Prussia can dominate Central Europe and the rest of the HRE. Prussia is considered one of the more advanced nations due to the difficult requirements and challenges that must be overcome to form, therefore it is not recommended for beginners. However, once inner quarrels are settled and the homeland is secured it can easily become one of the most entertaining yet still challenging nations, that can play a decisive role in the League Wars.

      Formation[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      There are three possible decisions that form Prussia. One is for the Teutonic Order, and is based on the secularization of that order into the Duchy of Prussia in 1525. One is for Brandenburg, based on the Electors of Brandenburg, having inherited the Duchy of Prussia, crowning themselves King in Prussia in 1701. The last is for any other country with Prussian, Saxon or Pomeranian culture, representing another German country taking the path that Brandenburg did historically. Note that the latter two decisions will elevate the nation to kingdom rank and will consequently make it leave the Holy Roman Empire unless it is an elector.

      Prussian governments[edit | edit source]

      Prussia has access to three unique T1 government reforms: Prussian Monarchy for monarchies, Stratocratic Administration for republics, and Militaristic Divine State for theocracies.

      Prussian Monarchy[edit | edit source]

      There are two versions of the Prussian monarchy in the game: "Prussian Monarchy" and "Prussian Monarchy (base)".

      "Prussian Monarchy" will only be gained by nations that can get the Prussian missions (such as Brandenburg) and requires Domination to be enabled. Other nations (or if is disabled) will gain the Prussian Monarchy (base) reform instead.

      Both reforms grant the same effects, except that "Prussian Monarchy" grants access to the Prussian militarization ability, while "Prussian Monarchy (base)" enables the Militarization ability instead. Prussian Militarization is an initially weaker (Early militarization) but potentially stronger (Total militarization) version of the standard Militarization ability.

      Achievements[edit | edit source]

      A Fine Goosestep
      Form Prussia and have at least 125% discipline.
      A Kaiser not just in name
      Form the Holy Roman Empire.
      An early Reich
      Form Germany.
      Electable!
      Become an elector in the HRE as a country which does not start as elector.
      For the Emperor!
      Form Prussia or Germany, have the Empire government rank and 40.000 Marines raised at the same time.
      Mare Nostrum
      Restore the Roman Empire and own the entire Mediterranean and Black Sea coast lines.
      The Emperors new clothes
      Overthrow Austria and become the Emperor of the Holy Roman Empire.
