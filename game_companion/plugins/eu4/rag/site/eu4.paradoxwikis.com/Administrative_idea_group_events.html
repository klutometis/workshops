Administrative idea group events[1] are the part of the idea group events which require to have unlocked the administrative idea group.

Positive eventsEdit

id

 Centralization Yields Benefits

Thanks to our centralizing policies we are better able to control the administration of our state, yielding benefits for all.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has enabled the   administrative ideas.
  • Does not have the “Centralized State” modifier
 Is triggered only by

the five year pulse I.

Base weight: 100
 
This is good news

The country:

  • If has not enacted one of the highest tier government reforms and is not primitive:
    • Gains   15 government reform progress
  • Gains “Centralized State” for   10 years, giving:
    •   −0.10 Yearly corruption
    •   −0.05 Monthly autonomy change
id

 Generous Terms

The one thing we couldn't resent more are the ruinous rates of interest we are charged. However, our solid administration has convinced our creditors that perhaps the interest rate is too high and they have agreed that a small reduced is merited, at least for a while.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has enabled the   administrative ideas.
  • Has taken at least 1 loan
  • Does not have the “Generous Terms” modifier
 Is triggered only by

the five year pulse I.

Base weight: 100
 
We approve!

The country gains “Generous Terms” for   10 years, giving:

  •   −0.25 Interest per annum
id

 Good Advice

Good advice is sometimes hard to come by but these days the number of people coming forward to advise us is increasing nicely. This gives us more choice than ever before.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has enabled the   administrative ideas.
  • Does not have the “Good Advice” modifier
 Is triggered only by

the five year pulse I.

Base weight: 100
 
Let us review the candidates

The country:

  • If has not enacted one of the highest tier government reforms and is not primitive:
    • Gains   15 government reform progress
  • Gains “Good Advice” for   10 years, giving:
    •   +1 Possible advisor
id

 Show people our Resilience

Our state is under pressure but it is time to show our resilience in the face of adversity. It is time for boys to step up and become men, for men to show the world what they are made of. Together we can go forward.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has enabled the   administrative ideas.
  • Has less than   +3 stability
 Is triggered only by

the five year pulse I.

Base weight: 100
 
It is time!
If the country has a stability of less than +3,
  • then it gains   1 stability,
  • else it gains   50 administrative power.
id

 Support the War

Our people are weary of this war, but their spirit is strong. They are ready to give one more effort for victory. We will be victorious.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has enabled the   administrative ideas.
  • Is at   war
  • Has at least   4 war exhaustion
 Is triggered only by

the five year pulse I.

Base weight: 100
 
Onward to Victory

The country:

  • Loses   2 war exhaustion

Negative eventsEdit

id

 Provinces Frustrated by Lack of Autonomy

Our Centralizing policies have lead many in the provinces to feel that they have lost a lot of autonomy. There is talk of revolt in the air.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has enabled the   administrative ideas.
  • Owns a city that:
    • Is not a core
    • Is not overseas
 Is triggered only by

the five year pulse I.

Base weight: 100
 
Let them revolt

A random owned province connected to the capital but not the capital itself:

  • Gains   10 local unrest
 
Give concessions

The country:

  • If has not enacted one of the highest tier government reforms:
    • Loses   25 government reform progress
  • Else:
    • Loses   10 prestige
    • Loses   25 administrative power
id

 Provinces want even more autonomy

One of our provincial governors has figured out, that under our current decentralized policies he could go into business for himself. The fact that the army he has managed to raise is way too small to support such a claim doesn't seem to worry him much.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has enabled the   administrative ideas.
  • Owns a city that:
    • Is not a core
    • Is not overseas
 Is triggered only by

the five year pulse I.

Base weight: 100
 
Give him the autonomy he wants

A random owned province connected to the capital but not the capital itself:

  • Gains   25 local autonomy
 
Rein them in

The country:

  • Loses   50 military power
id

 Failed Administration

Our administration has failed our people. Instead of justice for all there is justice for the few. It is undermining the very support of our state.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has enabled the   administrative ideas.
  • Has at least   −2 stability
 Is triggered only by

the five year pulse I.

Base weight: 100
 
It is a sad state of affairs

The country:

  • Loses   1 stability
 
The state will be supported by gold instead

The country:

  • If has not enacted one of the highest tier government reforms:
    • Loses   75 government reform progress
  • Else:
    • Gains   1 corruption
