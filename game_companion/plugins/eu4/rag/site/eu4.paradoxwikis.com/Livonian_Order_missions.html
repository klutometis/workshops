      liv_teutonic_mission_slot[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Teutonic Knights
      In 1236, the Livonian Brothers of the Sword was defeated by the Samogitians and their Semigallian allies. The remnants were incorporated into the Teutonic Order in 1237, which then eventually led to the creation of the Livonian Order. De jure, the Hochmeister of the Teutonic Order is our overlord, but de facto both our orders are more like autonomous allies than anything. The wars between the Teutonic Order and Poland have shown that the Teutonic Knights are not as military competent as they used to be. It is time to help our brethren in Prussia through renewing our alliance with them - or by replacing their archaic administration with our own superior reign.
      		 The Teutonic Order:
      • At least one of:
        • Does not exists
        • Is our ally or subject that:
          • Has at least +150 opinion of us
        • Rivals with or is rivaled by us and:
          • Is targeted by a 'Conquest' or 'Reconquest' casus belli by us
      		 The country:
      • If has completed this mission via alliance or subjugation:
        • Gains 50 military power
        • Gains 15 trust towards the Teutonic Order and vice versa
        • Gains 20 favors with the Teutonic Order
      • Else, if has completed this mission via rivalry:
        • Gains 'Subjugation' casus belli against the Teutonic Order for 30 years
        • Gains “Honorable Wars” for 20 years, giving:
          • −10% Aggressive expansion impact
      • Else:
        • Gains 100 military power

      The Livonian Confederacy
      The Livonian Confederacy was a loosely organized confederacy under our rule which was created in 1435. It is a defensive alliance of the five bishoprics and the Livonian Order to protect the German rule over Curonia, Livonia and Estonia against the threat from Novgorod and its Orthodox Church. However, the different bishoprics within the confederacy are not our subjects but autonomous states, and as such they often have petty rivalries with each other. It is time to dissolve this confederacy, and integrate the lands of the bishoprics into our own.

      • Has at least 1 admiral

      		 The country:
      • Has at least 15% mercantilism
      • Owns core province Livland (37)
      • Has any merchant present in the Baltic Sea trade node
      • Owns at least 5 provinces in Curonia, Livonia or Estonia areas with any Trade building
      		 The country:
      • Gains 50 months worth of income from the Baltic Sea trade node
      • Gains “Markets of the Baltic” ( +25 Trade power) in the Lübeck and Baltic Sea trade nodes for 50 years
      		 Expand the Fleet

      The Hansa is the most powerful trade federation of North Germany. Gaining any kind of presence in the market of Lübeck will prove quite difficult. However, it is not impossible.       • Is employing a Trader of at least level 3
      • Has any merchant present in the Lübeck trade node
      • If Lübeck exists:
        • At least one of:
