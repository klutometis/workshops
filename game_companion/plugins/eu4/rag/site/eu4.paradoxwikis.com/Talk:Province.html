      General info[edit source]

      Okay, I filled in some general information regarding provinces. It probably overlaps with several specialised topics, but I figured a general overview would be useful. --Grifen88 (talk) 13:27, 4 September 2013 (CEST)

      There are more than 3137 provinces mentioned. I think 3205 land provinces and 4941 provinces total if you count the water provinces as well. Might be more I don't have all de DLC's— Preceding unsigned comment added by 2a02:a211:2040:c300:fc52:7993:9a67:4658 (talk) 12:18, 6 July 2023‎ (UTC)

      There are actually 3272 land provinces. 4941 also includes sea tiles and provinces which can appear in the random new world. But this is not a useful metric for the wiki, because a real game will never have all possible random new world provinces and all normal new world provinces at the same time. I transcluded the province count from the continent table, so that it is easier to keep it uptodate. --Grotaclas (talk) 11:58, 7 July 2023 (UTC)

      Overseas provinces in Africa[edit source]

      In the part about Overseas provinces it says that Tangiers isn't an overseas province for Spain. However, if you play Portugal (same continent as Spain), Ceuta (same continent as Tangiers) IS overseas. You see that by the horrible tax income from there and not the possibility to hire mercs. So is the article correct? Skylofter (talk) 14:41, 15 August 2014 (CEST)

      The article is correct. It just fails to mention that distance is calculated from the capital, not from the nearest core province as one might expect. --Grifen88 (talk) 14:04, 14 December 2014 (CET)

      Changes in special trade provinces[edit source]

      Some provinces, which before was Important Center of Trade are now Important Natural Harbor, only giving +15 local trade power and +1 naval forcelimit, instead of +20 local trade power and +2 naval forcelimit. As an example I can mention Rio de Janeiro.
      I do not know when this was changed, but I think it was in the 1.11 patch - though I cannot see it in the patch notes. There isn't even a subject on it on the province page, so it might be another indication that this was recently changed. I will go ahead and change it here on the province page soon, but I would just let you know and possibly get some confirmation from other members. --Chrras (talk) 23:10, 8 March 2015 (CET)

      The permanent province modifier “Important Natural Harbor” were introduced with patch 1.10. The lists were last updated for patch 1.9, so that “Important Natural Harbor” is missing in the lists. Feel free to add and update the other modifiers as well. – Lillebror (talk) 23:33, 8 March 2015 (CET)
      Ah, I wasn't aware of that. Will do, it is on my to do list. --Chrras (talk) 23:57, 8 March 2015 (CET)
