      National missions[edit | edit source]

      Andalusia restoration[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Plight of our Kin
      For many hundreds of years, the infidel reconquista has persecuted our brethren in Iberia. Where once the benevolent caliphate brought peace to the region, now our people flock to us in droves. It becomes us to prepare a home for them, that they might find the welcoming embrace of the faithful.
      		 If Andalusia has the Clergy estate:
      • has at least 60 clergy influence, but less than 100

      Otherwise:

      • has at least 90% religious unity

      Andalusia has at least 2 stability

      		Andalusia's capital:
      • gains 2 base tax
      • gains 4 base manpower
      Qalat al-Hamra
      Since the days of Emir Mohammed ben Al-Ahmar, this towering palace has been the pinnacle of artisanal construction. His successors added numerous wonders to the grand complex; the Lion Court, the Court of the Myrtles, and the exquisite Hall of the Ambassadors. Should we continue in this tradition, we will enshrine ourselves as a center of art and culture for years to come.
      		 Garnatah (223):
      • is owned by Andalusia
      • has at least 20 Development
      • has a Mosque
      		 Garnatah (223) gets "Shining Jewel of the West" until the end of the game, giving the following effects:
      • -2 Local Unrest
      • +50% Institution Spread
      		 The Plight of our Kin
      Restore Qurtuba
      The city of Qurtuba underwent huge decline due both to the decadence of its elite, and the oppression of foreign tyrants. Return it to a state befitting our restored nation.
      		 Qurtuba/Córdoba (225):
      • is owned by Andalusia
      • one of the following must be true:
        • has at least 15 Development
        • has a Temple/Mosque

      All provinces in the following areas are owned by this country and follow the state religion:

      • Lower Andalucia
      • Upper Andalucia
      		 Qurtuba (225) gets "Qurtuba Restored" until the end of the game, giving the following effects:
      • +0.50 Yearly Prestige
      • +1 Tolerance of Heretics
      		 Qalat Al-Hamra

      South Mediterranean ambition[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Rout the Pretenders!
      The throne of the Emirate of Granada has been under constant attack from those who would see themselves Emir. We must present a united front to the encroaching Christian threat, by dealing once and for all, with the bitter feuds within our state.
      		 One of the following must be true:
      • "Granadan War of Succession" disaster is over.
      • The year is at least 1455
      		 Andalusia gets "Situation Stabilized" for 20 years, giving the following effect:
      • +20% Manpower Recovery Speed
      Restore Faith in the Throne
      The bloody struggle for the throne of Granada is finally over. We must institute a campaign of resolution and improvement across our land, to show all our people that the only true threats to our realm come from our foreign foes.
      		 If Andalusia is a monarchy with heir mechanic:
      • has an heir with 100 claim

      Andalusia:

      • has 100 Legitimacy or equivalent
      • has less than 2 average unrest
      		 If Andalusia is a monarchy and has an heir, heir:
      • gains 1 Administrative skill.
      • gains 1 Diplomatic skill.
      • gains 1 Military skill.

      Else, Andalusia:

      • gains 50 Administrative Power.
      • gains 50 Diplomatic Power.
      • gains 50 Military Power.

      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Algiers
      • Hautes Plains
      • Barbary Coast
      • Kabylia
      • Tunisia
      • Djerba
      • Tripolitania
      • North Morocco
      • Central Morocco
      • Gharb
      		 Rout the Pretenders!
      Moroccan Conquest
      It is with sadness that sometimes old friends must part ways. Despite our long history of support from Morocco, our people clamor for the union of their land with ours. The Moroccan Sultan must bend the knee.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned by Andalusia or its non-tributary subjects:
      • North Morocco
      • Central Morocco
      • Gharb
      		 Andalusia gains a claim on the following areas:
      • Tafilalt
      • Sus
      • Southern Morocco
      		 Restore Faith in the Throne
      Almohad Lands
      The Almohad dynasty forged an Empire as great as that of the legendary Alexander, whom the Greeks praise with adoring sycophancy. The northern coast of Africa is ours by right. Liberate it from the petty successors who know not the heritage they are heir to.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned by Andalusia:
      • Algiers
      • Hautes Plains
      • Barbary Coast
      • Kabylia
      • Tunisia
      • Djerba
      • Tripolitania
      		 Andalusia gains a claim on the following areas:
      • North Sahara
      • Ouled Nail
      • Fezzan

      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Cyrenaica
      • Delta
      • Bahari
      • Vostani
      		 Restore Faith in the Throne
      Into Egypt
      Tussled over for generations, the fertile delta of Egypt holds some of the richest land in the world. Before the slaving despots, the Mamluks, Saladin, and less notable conquerors, Egypt was ruled in harmony by the Fatimid Caliphate, who claimed descent from the Prophet Muhammad - peace be upon him. Take this land, and place it under our benevolent overlordship.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned by Andalusia:
      • Cyrenaica
      • Delta
      • Bahari
      • Vostani
      		 The Event "Scholarly Sentiment" happens.

      Andalusia gains a claim on Said Area.
      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Palestine
      • Syria
      • Aleppo
      • Al Jazira
      • Red Sea Hills
      • Tabuk
      • Medina
      • Mecca
      		 Almohad Lands
      Holiest of Cities
      Mecca, the most holy city upon this earth, lies within our grasp. Taking control of this vital place will give us the respect and admiration of our brethren the world over, of this we are sure.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned and cored by Andalusia:
      • Red Sea Hills
      • Tabuk
      • Medina
      • Mecca
      		 Andalusia gets "Shahada, Salah, Zakat" for 20 years, giving the following effects:
      • +1 Tolerance of Heretics
      • -0.25 Monthly Piety
      		 Into Egypt
      Levantine Restoration
      Damascus was the site of the capital of the very origin of the Umayyad dynasty. Syria itself is an ancient and holy land - it must be treated with care and respect, however, it must also be ours.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned and cored by Andalusia:
      • Palestine
      • Syria
      • Aleppo
      • Al Jazira
      		 Dimashq(382):
      • gains 3 Base Tax
      • gains 3 Base Production
      • gains 3 Base Manpower
      • gets "Great Mosque of Damascus" until the end of the game, giving the following effects:
        • +100% Institution Spread
        • -0.10 Monthly Devastation

      Andalusia gains a claim on the following areas:

      • Syrian Desert
      • Transjordan

      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Iraq Arabi
      • Basra
      • Khuzestan
      • Luristan
      • Shahrizor
      • Tabriz
      • Azerbaijan
      • Armenia
      		 Into Egypt
      Follow the Tigris
      The fertile plains of Mesopotamia are the birthplace of civilizations. This land is integral to the functioning of all surrounding continents, and whomsoever controls it, controls the flow of wealth in all directions.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned by Andalusia:
      • Iraq Arabi
      • Basra
      • Khuzestan
      • Luristan
      		 Andalusia:
      • gains 300 Administrative Power.

      Basrah (408):

      • Gain a Level 3 Center of Trade. If it already had a Level 3 Center of Trade, gain 4 base production. If it had a Level 2 Center of Trade, gain 2 base production

      Andalusia gains a claim on the following areas:

      • Gulf Coast
      • Farsistan
      • Kerman
      • Mogostan
      		 Levantine Restoration
      The Heartland
      Persia has seen millenia of war, conquest, and empire. This land belonged to our ancestors, and by extension, belongs to us. Many holy cities lie in this land - claim them in our name.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned by Andalusia:
      • Shahrizor
      • Tabriz
      • Azerbaijan
      • Armenia
      		 Andalusia:
      • gains 300 Administrative Power.
      • gets "Imperial Administration" for 20 years, giving the following effect:
        • -20% Core creation cost

      Andalusia gains a claim on the following areas:

      • Iraq-e-Ajam
      • Isfahan
      • Shirvan
      • Tabarestan
      • Kartli-Kakheti
      		 Levantine Restoration

      Mediterranean islands ambition[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Portuguese Naval Rivalry
      The key to success at the mouth of the Mediterranean has always been in maintaining a strong naval presence. The Portuguese learned this with trial and error. If we are to profit from our advantageous position, we must rival this threat immediately.
      		 Andalusia has a fleet equal or larger than Portugal Andalusia gets "Merchants at Ease" for 20 years, giving the following effects:
      • +20% Embargo Efficiency
      • +20% Ship Trade Power
      Seize Trade Dominance
      The wily merchants of Portugal have established a clear advantage in the waters around Iberia. Not all battles must be fought with sword and shield - make sure that their traders return with paltry offerings, and attain the mercantile dominance we deserve.
      		 Andalusia is the strongest trade power in the Sevilla Trade node. Gain a discount (75% cheaper) skill 2 Trader with our culture and faith.

      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Tyrrhenian Isles
      • Baleares
      		 Portuguese Naval Rivalry
      Islands of the Mediterranean
      The various islands situated around the Mediterranean could, with the right overlord, be ideally situated to take advantage of the sheer wealth that flows into Europe. Claim them, and make sure that they play an integral part in our revenge upon the Christian kings of the continent.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned and cored by Andalusia:
      • Tyrrhenian Isles
      • Baleares
      		 Cagliari (2986):
      • gains 4 Base Tax
      • gains 4 Base Production
      • gets "Port of Interest" until the end of the game, giving the following effects:
        • +25% Local Trade Power
        • +25% Local Goods Produced Modifier

      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Eastern Sicily
      • Western Sicily
      		 Seize Trade Dominance
      Retake Sicily
      The Emirate of Sicily was wrested from the hands of the decadent and destructive Byzantine Empire in the 9th Century. It flourished under Muslim rule, remaining a cultural melting pot of diverse peoples, until it fell to Roger of Sicily in the late 11th Century. Many long for this wondrous island to be returned to our fast-growing empire - make it so.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned and cored by Andalusia:
      • Eastern Sicily
      • Western Sicily
      		 If Sicily does not exist:
      • the event "The Emirate of Sicily" happens.

      Else, Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Calabria
      • Campania
      • Apulia
      		 Islands of the Mediterranean

      Iberian ambition[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Moroccan Ties
      The Sultans in Morocco have long held us in high regard. If we are ever to emerge from our precarious position in Iberia, we should consider cementing our friendship in a more formal arrangement. It should be said, however, that the glory we would gain from showing the world that we have no need of the patronizing support of our would-be benefactors, could taste even greater indeed.
      		 If Morocco exists and is not our subject:
      • One of the following must be true:
        • Morocco is our ally with at least 150 opinion towards us
        • Morocco is our rival and has been Insulted or Scornfully Insulted

      Else:

      • Andalusia has at least 2 allies
      		 Andalusia gets "Victory Assured" for 20 years, giving the following effect:
      • +5% Morale of armies

      If you choose to complete this mission without the help of Morocco:

      • gains 25 prestige.
      Prepare for War
      The armies of Castile have encroached ever further into land once rightfully conquered by our predecessors, the Almohads. Decades of instability in our nation must be put aside, and grievances abandoned, in order to begin the process of reclaiming our diminishing heritage.
      		 If Castile/ Spain exists and is our neighbour:
      • Has been Insulted or Scornfully Insulted by Andalusia

      Total army size is at least 100% of the land Force Limit

      		A new general becomes available for duty with
      • 60 army tradition if Insulted
      • 100 army tradition if Scornfully Insulted

      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Valencia
      • Lower Andalucia

      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following provinces:

      • Murcia (221)
      • Albacete (4547)
      		 Moroccan Ties
      The Port of Sebtah
      The meddling Christian kings ensured that the strategic port of Sebtah remains out of our grasp. Despite numerous attempts to liberate our mistreated kin, it once again lies under foreign rule. Make landfall, and establish our beachhead once again.
      		 One of the following must be true:
      • Ceuta is owned and cored by Andalusia
      • Ceuta is owned by Morocco which is our ally
      		 If Ceuta is owned by Morocco:
      • adds Morocco as historical friend
      • Andalusia gets "Victory Assured" for 20 years, giving the following effects:
        • -15% Aggressive expansion impact
        • +20% Improve relation modifier

      Else, Ceuta:

      • creates a level 1 Center of Trade (With Dharma)
      • gets "Strategic Port" until the end of the game, giving the following effect:
        • +25% Local Trade Power
      		 Moroccan Ties
      Retake Balansiyah
      The ancient Andalusian citadel of Balansiyah fell to the Reconquista, nearly two centuries previously. The mistreatment of our brothers and sisters in faith, however, has not been forgotten. We must reclaim this great city, and the surrounding lands, and remind Europe that we are not as helpless as they once believed.
      		 Following provinces are owned and cored by Andalusia:
      • Murcia (221)
      • Albacete (4547)
      • All provinces in the Valencia area
      		 Murcia (221), Albacete (4547) and all provinces in the Valencia area get "Rebuilding Mosques" for 20 years, giving the following effects:
      • -2 Local Unrest
      • +3% Local Missionary Strength
      		 Prepare for War
      Liberate Qurtuba
      The grand cities of Isbiliyah and Qurtuba were once renowned throughout the known world, as bastions of Islamic thought. If we are to retake our rightful place on the world stage, we must evict the foreign oppressors, and restore them to their former glory.
      		 All provinces in the following area are owned by Andalusia:
      • Lower Andalucia
      		 Andalusia:
      • gains 100 Administrative Power.

      Sevilla gets "Great Mosque of Isbiliya" until the end of the game, giving the following effects:

      • -3 Local Unrest
      • -15% Local Development Cost
      • -0.1 Local Devastation

      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Alentejo
      • Beiras
      		 Prepare for War
      Restore al-Andalus
      For nearly eight centuries, southern Iberia was known to the Islamic world as al-Andalus, and was a shining beacon of prosperity throughout the Mediterranean. It may seem a lofty goal, but the restoration of this blessed state would ensure that our names go down in history for all time. Bring those in our lands to the true faith, and proclaim our majesty once more!
      		 All provinces in the following area are in the muslim religious group and owned by Andalusia:
      • Lower Andalucia
      		 Andalusia gets "Sword of Islam" for 20 years, giving the following effects:
      • -25% War Score Cost vs Other Religions

      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Toledo
      • South Castile
      • Aragon
      • Valencia
      • E
      		 Liberate Qurtuba
      Caliphate of Cordoba
      The territory of the Cordoban Caliphate almost entirely encompassed Iberia. Rout the three Spains, and take back what is rightfully ours!
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned by Andalusia:
      • Toledo
      • South Castile
      • Aragon

      Following provinces are owned by Andalusia:

      • Badajoz (218)
      • Caceres (1747)
      • Salamanca (216)
      		 Andalusia:
      • gains 200 Administrativa Power.

      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Galicia
      • North Castile
      • Leon
      • Extremadura
      • Vasconia
      • Catalonia
      		 Restore al-Andalus
      End Kingdom of Portugal
      The Kingdom of Portugal has controlled the rich western coast of Iberia for too long. Much of this territory once rightfully lay in the hands of our ancestors, and must be reclaimed. Word has it that they are seeking new conquests in far-flung lands - perhaps we could use this opportunity as pretext to investigate further.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned by Andalusia:
      • Alentejo
      • Beiras
      		 If Random New World is not active:
      • Andalusia discovers Ceará Area.

      Andalusia gets "The New World?" for 20 years, giving the following effects:

      • +25% Colonial Range
      • +25 Global Settler Increase
      		 Liberate Qurtuba
      Iberian Dawn
      History always held a special place for the land of the Kingdom of Leon. The last lands out of reach of the Caliphate of Cordoba. Rectify this mistake, and bring all of Iberia under our control. All of Europe must tremble at our might!
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned and cored by Andalusia:
      • Galicia
      • North Castile
      • Leon
      • Extremadura
      • Vasconia
      • Catalonia
      • Alentejo
      • Beiras
      		 Andalusia gets "The White Phoenix Rises" for 20 years, giving the following effect:
      • +10% Morale of Armies

      Andalusia gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Languedoc
      • Provence
      		 Caliphate of Cordoba

      End Kingdom of Portugal

      Crusader Kings
      The French Kings have been responsible for numerous atrocities committed upon our brethren in the west. Now that the tables are turned, claim their own land - which they once thought safe - and push further into Europe.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned and cored by Andalusia:
      • Languedoc
      • Provence
      		 Andalusia gains claims on the following areas:
      • Liguria
      • Piedmont
      		 Iberian Dawn

      Exploration[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Coast of Brazil
      The coast of this new world holds wonders beyond all imagination. Bring enlightenment to the peoples thereof, and seek out what riches lie within. To fully exploit this region, we must establish a sizable colony.
      		 Andalusia or its non-tributary subjects own at least 10 fully colonized province in Colonial Brazil region. Andalusia gets "Native Concord" for 50 years, giving the following effect:
      • -50% Native Uprising Chance
      		 End Kingdom of Portugal
      Continue Exploration
      The surprise discovery of the new world has gripped our people in a frenzy of exploration. Continue this voyage of discovery, that we may lay claim to this new land before our erstwhile oppressors can.
      		 If Random New World is not active, Andalusia has discovered any province in the Colonial Caribbean region.

      Andalusia has discovered any province in the West African Coast Area.

      		Conquistador "Mustafa Azemmouri" (5/5/6/1) becomes available for duty. End Kingdom of Portugal
      Monopolize Guinea
      The wealth that appears to exist in these newfound lands is truly astounding. We must ensure that it is kept flowing to our ports, else we find ourselves starved of income.
      		 Andalusia:
      • owns at least 90% trade share in the Ivory Coast trade node
      		 If owns Garnatah (223):
      • creates a level 1 Center of Trade in Garnatah

      Else:

      • gains 200 diplomatic power
      		 Continue Exploration
      African Opportunities
      The Gold Coast, as it is known, holds many opportunities for diversion of valuable trade, and for exploitation of resources. Now that we are on our feet once again, we must look to these foreign climes for resources to fuel our expansion.
      		 Owns at least one Trade Company province in the Guinea region Andalusia gets "Naval Investment Policy" for 20 years, giving the following effect:
      • +20% National Sailors Modifier

      Gain a discount (75% cheaper) skill 4 Naval Reformer with our culture and faith.

      		Continue Exploration
      The Caribbean
      The sheer riches of this new land dotted with islands and archipelagos, is beyond our wildest dreams. Establish an administrative authority to act as a base of operations in our new colonial ventures.
      		 Andalusia or its non-tributary subjects own at least 5 fully colonized province in Colonial Caribbean region. Andalusia:
      • gains 25 Prestige.

      Andalusia gets "Unknown Frontier" for 20 years, giving the following effect:

      • +20% Settler Chance
      		 Continue Exploration
