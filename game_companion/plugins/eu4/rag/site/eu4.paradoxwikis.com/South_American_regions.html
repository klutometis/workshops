      South American continent
      For a list of countries in this area, see Central and South America regions.

      South American regions are regions that are part of South America. The table content is out of the files in /Europa Universalis IV/history/provinces and refers to a game start on the 11th of November, 1444.

      Note: Continents, regions and areas of EU4 do not always match reality.

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.
