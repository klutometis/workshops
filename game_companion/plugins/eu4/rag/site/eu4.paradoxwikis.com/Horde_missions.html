This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

Available only with the Winds of Change DLC enabled.

This is a list of missions which are shared by Golden Horde, Ilkhanate, Mongol Empire and by Tatar, Mongol and Moghulistan nations[1]:

Tatar nation Mongol nation Moghulistan nation

  • Bashkiria
  • Chahar[2]

  • Nogai

  • Mongolia

For the full mission trees of these countries see Mongol missions, Moghulistan missions, Tatar missions. Yuan missions include some of these as well.

The branching missions can be previewed and selected with the buttons right of the mission window after completing the mission Matters of Religion (the AI chooses the branch randomly).

Shared horde missionsEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Demand Allegiance
We may be a collection of different tribes, but those tribes often do not obey our rules and seek out glory for only themselves. Showing them who is the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] will remind them what happens to rebellious subjects.
 The country:
  • Has at least   65 Tribes loyalty, if they exist
  • Has   100 horde unity (or equivalent)
 The country:
  • If has the   Tribes estate:
    • Gains   5% share of the land from the   Tribes
  • Else:
    • Gains   10 convertible[3] prestige
  • If has an army of less than   100% of its force limit:
    • Builds   cavalry regiments up to the maximum force limit in the capital
  • Else:
    • Gains   0.8 years' worth of manpower
 
Strengthen the Basqaqs
The Basqaqs were enforcers in the Mongol Empire, responsible for maintaining order and collecting taxes. They operated as a decentralized system of administrators and military officials, ensuring compliance with Mongol rule across the vast territories.
 The country:
  • At least one of:
    • Has at least   3 subjects
    • Has increased its   development by at least 150
 The country:
  • Unlocks the "Basqaq Autonomy" Nobility and Tribes privilege
  • Gains   50 administrative power
 
Promote Tribal Wargames
To keep our armies in shape, we must keep them in fighting spirit. When we are not at war, we must create war, either externally or internally. For that, we must further promote our society to participate in wargames we may initiate.
 The country:
  • Has won at least 50 battles
  • Has an army of at least 50 regiments
  • Is not at war
  • Is not in a regency
 The country:
  • If has the   Tribes estate:
    • Gets the   “War Games” event, which:
      • Now gives   5 horde unity
      • Now has an increased chance of triggering the   “Rising Star” and “Shining Star” events, and the   general created from these events will have   +1 shock and   +1 siege pips
  • Else:
    • Gains   100 military power
    • Gains   10 convertible[3] prestige
    • Gains a new   general with   40 army tradition,   +1 shock and   +1 siege
   Demand Allegiance
 
Noble Tribes
Listening to the voices of the tribal leaders has not benefited us much. Perhaps if we organize some influential people in our realm as nobles, as many other states do, we will be able to more efficiently meet the demands of the nation.
 The country:
  • Has at least   45% crown land ownership
  • If has the   Tribes estate:
    • Has at least   70 Tribes loyalty
  • Else:
    • Has at least   500 total development
 The country:
  • Unlocks the   Nobility estate
  • Gains   0.7 years' worth of manpower
  • Gains   5 convertible[4] army tradition
   Demand Allegiance

  Strengthen the Basqaqs

 
Gathering of Tribute
Instead of directly taxing the people, we should focus more on what we have always done to those who bent their knees to us: gathering tribute.
 The country:
  • Is employing any   Administrative advisor of at least level 3
  • If has the   Tribes estate:
    • Has completed at least   4 Tribes agendas
  • Else:
    • Gains at least   11 administrative power per month
 The country:
  • Gains   50 government reform progress
  • The estate privilege "Basqaq Autonomy" nows grant +1% Loyalty for the Estate it is assigned to every time a Tributary pays Tribute to us
   Strengthen the Basqaqs
 
Management of the Tribes
A [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] should rule in an absolute manner. No tribal leader should defy our authority, as the quest to become the greatest of khans requires becoming the strongest in the realm.
 The country:
  • Has at least   65% crown land ownership
  • If has the   Tribes estate:
    • Has less than   65 Tribes influence
    • Has at least   60 Tribes loyalty
  • Else:
    • The ruler's skills add up to at least 9
 The country:
  • If has the   Tribes estate:
    • Unlocks the "Tribal Absolutism" Tribes privilege
  • Else:
    • Gains   2 ruler administrative skill
  • Gains “Curtailing the Tribes” for   15 years, giving:
    •   +10 Maximum absolutism
    •   +5% Estate loyalty change on privilege revoked
   Promote Tribal Wargames
 
Bowed Heads; Bent Knees
Many states have either become our vassals or are paying a tribute so that we do not invade them. This is proof that the Mongols were destined to rule the world.
 At least one of:
  • All of:
    • Is an   empire
    • Is at least the   third great power
  • All of:
    • Has at least   5 subjects
    • Every subject has less than   35% liberty desire
 The country:
  • If has completed the mission with subjects:
    • All Tributaries lose   10% liberty desire
    • For every 100 development tributary of us:
      • Gains   1% manpower recovery speed
      • Gains   1% national tax modifier
  • Else:
    • Gains   1 ruler administrative skill[5]
    • Gains “A Modern Empire” for the rest of the game, giving:
      •   −5% States governing cost
      •   +10% Monthly reform progress modifier
   Gathering of Tribute
 
Any Child Can be Khan!
Why should the rule be handed to adults only? If a child proves to be a great warrior or a great leader, they should be allowed to declare themselves as Khan. Instead of allowing corrupt or incompetent warlords to become khans, we should strive to make any child be Khan.
 The country:
  • Gains at least   11 administrative power per month
  • Has at least   80 Tribes loyalty, if they exist
  • Has won at least 200 battles
  • At least one of:
    • Has at least   4 ruler's military skill
    • Is employing any   Military advisor of at least level 3
 The country:
  • If has the   Tribes estate:
    • The "Secured Succession" Tribes privilege completely prevents the   “Tribal Succession Crisis” event
    • Our children may no longer die from Hunting Accidents
  • Else:
    • Gains   15 loyalty with every estate
    • Gains   1 ruler administrative skill
    • Gains   1 ruler diplomatic skill
    • Gains   1 ruler military skill
  • If has no heir but can have one:
    • Gets the   “Lux Stella” event
   Bowed Heads; Bent Knees

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Unity of Faith
Whether we embrace a new faith or strengthen our ancient traditions, the Mongol people must achieve religious harmony.
 The country:
  • Has at least   100% religious unity
  • At least one of:
    • Owns at least 50 heathen or heretic provinces
    • Has converted at least 50 provinces
 The country:
  • Removes   Sinner ruler personality trait, if he has it
  • Gains   50 administrative power
  • If is a steppe horde:
    • Unlocks the   Clergy estate
  • Else:
    • Gains “Unity of Faith” for   20 years, giving:
      •   −10 Years of separatism
      •   +2 Tolerance of the true faith
   Matters of Religion
 
Raiding Parties
Our nation depends on raiding other more civilized nations in order to amass wealth and keep itself intact. Let us organize more of these raiding parties and bring more loot to the tribes.
 The country:
  • Owns at least   35 cavalry regiments
  • Has won at least 30 battles
 The country:
  • Gains a new   general with   25 army tradition and   +3 shock
  • Gains “Raised the War Host” for   15 years, giving:
    •   +10% Cavalry combat ability
    •   −10% Cavalry cost
 
Expansion of the Silk Road
With our rule in China and Persia, we can bring the Silk Road new prosperity. By creating more trade centers along the road, and protecting it from any looters who may wish to disturb it, we can make a profit from it.
 The country:
  • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 5 provinces which:
    • Is in Persia or Khorasan (region)
    • Has at least   5 base production
    • Has any Production building
  • Controls at least   50% of the trade in the Persia and Samarkand trade nodes
 The country:
  • Gets access to discounted (50% cheaper) skill 2   Trader advisor
  • Gets access to upgraded trade policies
   Establish New Trade Routes
 
Establish New Trade Routes
Whilst a horde is not known for trading, the Mongols have had a tradition of bringing prosperity to traders who were part of their empire. That, combined with the profit it may bring us, entices many minor tribal leaders to protect these caravans.
 The country or its non-tributary subjects own at least 5 provinces with a   center of trade of at least level 2 The country:
  • Gains   2% mercantilism[6]
  • Gains “Steppe Trade” for   20 years, giving:
    •   +15% Caravan power
 
Continental Trade
Instead of ships sailing from one continent to another, or many tolls being paid so that a caravan reaches its destination, we will instead promote a system where the caravans are free to move from one corner of the empire to the next.
 The country:
  • Is the   strongest trade power in our Home trade node which:
    • Has at least   35 trade value
  • At least one of:
    • Has a   Trader advisor of at least level 3
    • Gains at least   10 diplomatic power per month
 The country:
  • Gains   100 diplomatic power
  • If has the   Tribes estate:
    • Unlocks the "Nomadic Caravans" Tribes privilege
  • Else:
    • Gains   5% mercantilism[6]
    • Gains   ducats equal to 1.5 years of income
   Establish New Trade Routes
 
Mantle of the Great Khan
Although Temujin is long dead, his successors still live. It is up to us to claim the mantle of creating the greatest empire the world has ever seen.
 The country:
  • Is the   first great power
  • Has the largest army in the world
  • Has at least   +90 prestige
 The country:
  • From now on, if our rulers win 50 battles as   generals whilst having the   Conqueror trait, it will be replaced with the   Legendary Conqueror one
  • Gains “A New Genghis Khan” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +100% Army tradition from battles
    •   +100% Prestige from land battles

Religious branchEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Matters of Religion
We may be a horde, but even a horde has an official religion. If we are to not fall into doom, then we must attend to these religious matters.
 At least one of:
  • Owns at least 25 heathen or heretic provinces
  • Has converted at least 10 provinces
 The country:
  • Gains access to the Tolerance, Devotion and Confucian missions
  • Gets access to discounted (50% cheaper) skill 2   Theologian advisor

Tolerance branchEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Men of Merit
We do not judge a man based on his beliefs, but rather, we judge him based on his abilities to serve us. Our nation contains many examples of these men, so we should grant them positions based on those merits.
 The country or its non-tributary subjects own at least 10 provinces which:
  • Has heathen or heretic religion
  • Has been developed at least 2 times
 The country:
  • Gets access to discounted (50% cheaper) skill 3   Theologian advisor
  •   Theologians now grants  /  +0.25 Tolerance of Heretics and Heathens per level
   Matters of Religion
 
State of Faiths
Instead of ruling through religion, we should instead rule by common law. Our state is a melting pot of many different faiths, so instead of reining them in, we should shape these laws to fit all of their values.
 The country:
  • Has at least   100% religious unity
  • Has   Theologian advisor of at least level 3
  • At least one of:
    • Ruler has the personality trait   Tolerant
    • Has completed   Humanist Ideas
    • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 25 provinces which:
      • Has heathen or heretic religion
      • Has any Tax building
 The country:
  • Unlocks the "Religious Tolerance" privilege for the Tribes and Clergy estates
  • Distributes   5 production development among random owned provinces
  • Distributes   5 tax development among random owned provinces
   Men of Merit

Devout branchEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Faith of the Steppes
If we are to prove to our subjects that we are worthy of the title of Great Khan, then we must also decide the faith of which we'll follow. It does not matter if some of the tribes do not follow us at first, for we will rein them in and force them to.
 The country:
  • Has converted at least 30 provinces
  • Has an   Inquisitor advisor of at least level 3
  • If is a steppe horde:
    • Has razed at least 35 provinces
  • Else:
    • Owns at least   40 cavalry regiments
 The country:
  • Gains the   Zealot ruler personality trait
  • If is   Tengri:
    • Gains “Followers of Tengri” for   20 years, giving:
      •   +1 Yearly horde unity
      •   +15% Razing power gain
  • Else, if is Muslim:
    • Gains “Muslims of the Steppes” for   20 years, giving:
      •   +15% Cavalry to infantry ratio
      •   −10% Cavalry cost
  • Else:
    • Gains “Tribal Zealotry” for   20 years, giving:
      •   +3% Missionary strength
      •   +1 Tolerance of the true faith
   Matters of Religion
 
For the Horde!
As a Great Khan, we need to serve as an example for our people to follow, not only in battle but also in religion. [Root.Monarch.GetName] will spread the [Root.Religion.GetName] faith as far as he can.
 The country:
  • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 150 provinces following the True faith
  • At least one of:
    • Ruler has the personality trait   Zealot
    • Has completed   Religious Ideas
    • All of:
      • Has an   Inquisitor advisor of at least level 3
      • Gains at least   12 administrative power per month
  • If is   Tengri:
    • When we switch Secondary Religion, we will no longer lose 50 prestige
    • The Clergy is renamed to Shamans
    • Gains   15 convertible[3] prestige
  • Else, if is Muslim:
    • The   "Unify Islam" decision will require fewer provinces
    • Gains   15 convertible[3] prestige
  • Else:
    • Gains “Religion of the Steppes” for the rest of the game, giving:
      •   +2 Tolerance of the true faith
      •   −5% War score cost vs other religions
   Faith of the Steppes

Confucian branchEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Teachings of Confucius
Rather than reining in any religion, we should instead follow a more harmonious path. Co-existence, as Confucius's teachings say. Peace and harmony between many different religious values.
 The country:
  • Has harmonized with   Pagan
  • Has at least   90% religious unity
 The country:
  • If DLC   'Mandate of Heaven' is active:
    • Gains “Harmonization of the Tengri People” for   20 years, giving:
      •   +15% Religious harmonization speed
      •   +1 Yearly harmony increase
  • Else:
    • Gains   150 administrative power
    • Gains   1 ruler administrative skill

Every owned   Tengri province:

  • Gains “Tengri Support” for   15 years, giving:
    •   +10% Local manpower modifier
   Matters of Religion
 
Eternal Harmony
With many religions under our empire, we must further strengthen our efforts for internal peace. A peace that will bring eternal harmony in our realm.
 The country:
  • Has at least   70 Clergy loyalty, if they exist
  • If DLC   'Mandate of Heaven' is active
    • Has harmonized with at least 4 religions or religious groups
    • Has at least 75 harmony
  • Else:
    • Has a   Theologian advisor of at least level 3
    • Gains at least   10 administrative power per month
 The country:
  • If is the   Emperor of China:
    • Gains   10% mandate
  • Gains “Harmony Between Faiths” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +1 Tolerance of the true faith
    •   +0.5 Yearly harmony increase
  • If DLC   'Mandate of Heaven' is active:
    • The "Inwards Perfection" Clergy privilege grants additional bonus
  • Else:
    • Gains   1 ruler administrative skill
    • If has a stability of less than +3,
      • then it gains   1 stability,
      • else it gains   50 administrative power.
   Teachings of Confucius
