      Militarization

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      (Redirected from Militarisation)
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
      At least some were last verified for version 1.35.

      Militarization is a broad term summary for three different mechanics of similar name and function. Militarization is represented via a meter in the Government tab of the interface and has a value scaling from 0 to 100%. The meter shows the current percentage of militarization the country has. The bonuses from militarization scale linearly with its current percentage.

      Militarization[edit | edit source]

      The government reforms Stratocratic Administration, Militaristic Divine State, Crusading Empire, Livonian Militarist Monarchy, Zulu Tribe, and Prussian Monarchy (base) have access to militarization.

      The effects of Militarization are proportional to its value. Listed below are the main milestones:
      Militarization 0 50 100
      Effects No Effect
      • +5% Discipline
      • +10% Manpower recovery speed
      • −10% Land maintenance modifier
      • −0.25 Militarization of state
      • +10% Discipline
      • +20% Manpower recovery speed
      • −20% Land maintenance modifier
      • −0.5 Militarization of state
      If the "Army with a State" reform is enacted No Effect
      • +5% Infantry combat ability
      • +10% Infantry combat ability

      Prussian Militarization[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Domination DLC enabled.

      The government reform Prussian Monarchy has access to Prussian militarization. The nation starts with "Early Prussian Militarization", which can be upgraded to "Prussian Militarization" with the Prussian mission "Army Reforms" and finally be upgraded to "Total Prussian Militarization" with the "An Army with a State" mission. Early Prussian Militarization and Prussian Militarization only have access to the "Boost Militarization" interaction, which, at the cost of 50 military power, raises militarization by 10.

      The effects of Prussian Militarization are proportional to its value. Listed below are the main milestones:
      Prussian Militarization 0 50 100
      Effects No Effect

      Early:

      • +5% Manpower recovery speed
      • −10% Land maintenance modifier
      • +2.5% Discipline
      • −0.05 Monthly early Prussian militarization

      Normal/Total:

      • +10% Manpower recovery speed
      • −10% Land maintenance modifier
      • +5% Discipline
      • −0.25 Monthly Prussian militarization

      Early:

      • +10% Manpower recovery speed
      • −20% Land maintenance modifier
      • +5% Discipline
      • −0.1 Monthly early Prussian militarization

      Normal/Total:

      • +20% Manpower recovery speed
      • −20% Land maintenance modifier
      • +10% Discipline
      • −0.5 Monthly Prussian militarization
      If the "Army with a State" reform is enacted No Effect
      • +5% Infantry combat ability
      • +10% Infantry combat ability
      Total Prussian Militarization has the same modifiers as Prussian Militarization but grants access to the "Scharnhorst maneuvers" and "Reform the Military manual" government interactions, in addition to "Boost Militarization".
      Thus, the government potentially has access to the following interactions:
      Ability Cost Effects Total only
      Boost militarism 50 military power Gain 10 Prussian militarization
      Scharnhorst Maneuvers 20 Prussian militarization
      • Gain "Thunderous Advance" for 5 years, granting the following:
        • +15% Movement speed
      Reform the military Manual 20 Prussian militarization
      • Gain "New Military Manual" for 5 years, granting the following:
        • −0.05 Monthly war exhaustion
        • +25% Reinforce speed
        • −33% Regiment drill loss
      Prussian Militarization can be gained/lost by the following:

      +0.02 / +0.05 / +0.05 Has the "Noble Generals" modifier (from Teutonic missions)
      +0.05 / +0.1 / +0.125 Has enacted the T5 government reform General War Commissariat (for Saxon, Prussian, or Pomeranian culture)
      +0.1 Has enacted the T2 government reform Army with a State (from German missions)

      +0 / +0.02 / +0.03 Per 600 points of owned development
      +0.05 / +0.075 / +0.1 With the “Extend Militarization of the State” parliament issue

      Mercenary Militarization[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Domination DLC enabled.

      The government reforms "Elite Mercenaries", available to nations that have completed Mercenary ideas, "Swiss Mercenaries", available to all Swiss nations, and "The Swiss Landsknechte", available to a Swiss Germany, have access to Mercenary militarization.

      The effects of Mercenary Militarization are proportional to its value. Listed below are the main milestones:
      Mercenary Militarization 0 50 100
      Effects
      • −10% Mercenary maintenance
      • −5% Mercenary maintenance
      • +12.5% Mercenary manpower
      • +5% Mercenary discipline
      • −0.25 Monthly mercenary militarization
      • +25% Mercenary manpower
      • +10% Mercenary discipline
      • −0.5 Monthly mercenary militarization
      If the Venetian mission "Refine the Condottieri" has been completed
      • −25% Assault fort cost modifier
      • −50% Assault fort cost modifier
      Mercenary militarization can only be increased by the following government interaction:
      Ability Cost Effects
      Sweeten Mercenary Contracts 0.1 years worth of income Gain 5 Mercenary Militarization
      If the "The Swiss Landsknechte" reform is enacted +5 Mercenary Militarization
