Pirate missionsEdit

Trade & piracyEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.31.
Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Pirate Trade Practices
You can get more with a kind word and a cutlass than with just a kind word.
 Country's privateers have at least 25% of   trade power in home trade node Country gets "Successful Pirate Trade" for   20 years, giving the following effects:
  •   +20% light ship combat ability
  •   +20% ship trade power
 
Free Trading Ports
By controlling key trade ports ourselves we can circumvent the criminally high trade fees set by local traders.
 Country owns at least 3 different level 2   centers of trade Country gets "Pirate Monopoly" for   20 years, giving the following effects:
  •   +10% trade efficiency
  •   +10% trade steering
   Pirate Trade Practices
 
Trading in Gold
Trading with gold is easy, it's value is indisputable. The only thing worth it's weight in gold is gold.
 Country owns at least 3   gold producing provinces Country gets "Pirate Gold" for   20 years, giving the following effect:
  •   +20% global trade power
   Free Trading Ports
 
Exploiting the Triangle Trade
By raiding the transatlantic slave traders we shall bolster our own numbers and show all slaving tyrants that a pirate's life is free!
 Country's privateers have at least 25% of   trade power in any of the following nodes:
  • Ivory Coast
  • Coast of Pernambuco
  • Straits of Gibraltar
 Country gets "Liberated Slaves" for   20 years, giving the following effects:
  •   +10% production efficiency
  •   +10% national manpower modifier
  •   +10% national sailors modifier
   Trading in Gold

The CaribbeanEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.31.
Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
A Second Madagascar
Following the words of Thomas Barrow we shall develop our free haven and create a pirate republic worthy of being called 'a second Madagascar'.
 Country's capital has at least 20   total development Country gets "A Pirate Haven" for   20 years, giving the following effects:
  •   -10% construction cost
  •   +10% national tax modifier
 
An Old Fort
Before conquering all of the Caribbean we must secure our position and build a fort that shall stand long after we all rest in Davy Jones' locker.
 Country's capital has a fort level of at least 3 Country gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
  • West Cuba
  • East Cuba
   A Second Madagascar
 
Running Down to Cuba
Cuba belongs to us pirates, why else would there be a song about it?
 All of the following must be true:
  • All provinces in the West Cuba area:
    • Owned by the country or its non-Tributary Subjects
  • All provinces in the East Cuba area:
    • Owned by the country or its non-Tributary Subjects
 Country gains a permanent claim on the Caribbeans region   An Old Fort
 
Pirates of the Caribbean
He who controls the gold controls the globe.
 All provinces in the Caribbeans region owned by the country or its non-Tributary Subjects Country gains a permanent claim on all provinces producing   gold in the following regions:
  • Central America
  • Mexico
  • Rio Grande
  • Mississippi
  • Southeast America
   Running Down to Cuba
 
The Golden Age of Piracy
There are no problems in this world that cannot be solved with gold. And if all else fails it can be used to buy more rum!
 Country owns at least 10   gold producing provinces Country gets "The Gold Must Flow" for   20 years, giving the following effects:
  •   -2 national unrest
  •   +0.2 yearly inflation reduction
   Pirates of the Caribbean

Naval power and diplomacyEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.31.
Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
A Pirate Fleet
With a big enough fleet, no prize will be out of our grasp!
 All of the following must be true:
  • Total navy size at least 100% of force limit
  • Have a naval force limit of at least 20
  • Have at least 5 light ships
 Country gets "Sea Dogs" for   20 years, giving the following effects:
  •   -10% naval maintenance modifier
  •   +30% privateer efficiency
 
A Scourge on the Seven Seas
Any respectable confederacy of pirates must have a flagship, and a captain worthy of commanding it.
 Country has a flagship Country gains an admiral with 50   navy tradition

Country gets "An Inspiring Captain" for   20 years, giving the follow effects:

  •   +10% national sailors modifier
  •   +0.5 yearly navy tradition
   A Pirate Fleet
 
No Purchase, No Pay
We will rally the bravest fighters to our cause by ensuring that those who fight for us are fairly rewarded, as according to the Pirate Code.
 Total army size at least 100% of force limit Country gets "The Pirate Code" for   20 years, giving the following effect:
  •   +5% discipline
   A Scourge on the Seven Seas
 
Pirate Diplomacy
Remember; it has to rhyme!
 One of the following must be true for any of the country's rivals:
  • Has opinion modifier "Insulted"
  • Has opinion modifier "Scornfully Insulted"
 Country gets "A Reputation" for   20 years, giving the following effect:
  •   -10% aggressive expansion impact
   No Purchase, No Pay
 
The Pirate Threat
When our name is known on all the seven seas, free pirates will flock to our cause and we will give those who do not yet fear us cause to.
 At least one of the country's rivals is a great power Country gains 100   military power

Country gets "No Kings" for   20 years, giving the following effects:

  •   +10% national manpower modifier
  •   +0.5 yearly army tradition
   Pirate Diplomacy

MiscellaneousEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.31.
Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
A Pirate Hoard
That is the second biggest pirate hoard I've ever seen!
 Have at least 3000   ducats Country gets "Another Golden Hoard" for   20 years, giving the following effects:
  •   +10% national tax modifier
  •   -20% cavalry cost
  •   +1 yearly prestige
 
A Pirate Federation
It's quite a loose federation and the rules are more like 'guidelines', but it will surely benefit us both.
 Any ally of the country has enacted pirate republic Country gets "Mutual Exchange" for   20 years, giving the following effects:
  •   +10% manpower recovery speed
  •   +10% land force limit modifier
  •   +1 diplomatic reputation
 
Renowned Buccaneer
A true pirate leader will inspire our buccaneers to great deeds, and make sure that tribute is paid promptly.
  • Country has an admiral with at least 12 pips combined (   fire,   shock, and   maneuver )
  • One of the following must be true :
    • Country Ruler is a historical pirate
    • All of :
      • Countr Ruler has at least 4 in every skill
      • Country has at least 95   Republican tradition
      • Country has at least 50   Navy tradition
  • Country gets "A Mighty Pirate" for   20 years, giving the following effects:
    •   +10% national tax modifier
    •   +10% morale of armies
  • Winning wars against Nations with more Development than our country (separate peace does count) now has a 5% chance to grant our Ruler the trait  "Legendary Pirate" and 1 Monarch point
 
The Pirate Round
Raiding the rich Indian coast will bring us much booty. Some will have to be shared with the pirates of Madagascar however, if we wish to use their ports to resupply. Unless, unless...
 Country's privateers have at least 25%   trade power in any of the following trade nodes:
  • Ganges Delta
  • Comorin Cape
  • Thatta
 Country gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
  • Madagascar Highlands
  • Betsimasaraka
  • Sakalava
  • Southern Madagascar

Administrative missionsEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
High Income
We must increase state income by any means necessary. Money is a requirement for any endeavor of the state, from the daily expenses of the government to the maintenance of armies to invade our enemies.
 The country has increased its   starting monthly income by at least 150% The country:
  • Gains a 50% cheaper skill 1   treasurer advisor of our culture and religion
  • Gains “Growing Economy” for   25 years, giving:
    •   −10% Construction cost
    •   −25% Construction time
 
Build Buildings
Let us never sit idle, even in peaceful times. Instead we must use our resources with industry, in order to make sure that we have funds available in time if misfortune should strike upon us.
 The country:
  • Owns at least 5 owned provinces with any Tax building
  • Owns at least 5 owned provinces with any Production building
 The country:
  • Gains   50 administrative power
  • Gains “Building Spree” for   25 years, giving:
    •   +10 Yearly tax income
   High Income
 
Build a Treasury
A large treasury is an imperative buffer for all things that could befall us through. If this is seen as miserly, it is because some do not realize that this vice is the very essence of what it means to reign.
 Country has at least   2 000 ducats The country:
  • Loses   2 corruption
  • Gains “Strong Treasury Department” for   25 years, giving:
    •   +0.10 Yearly inflation reduction
   Build Buildings
 
Build Manufactories
Great advances are being made in the field of production, both technological and organizational. Let us invest greatly in new manufactories, a great industrious revolution might soon be upon us.
 Country has at least 20 manufactories The country:
  • Gains   1.5 years of income
  • Gains “Proto-Industrialization” for   25 years, giving:
    •   +10% Production efficiency
   Build a Treasury
 
Imperial Conquest
Securing wealth must always be the foremost priority of our state. Let us make use of the expensive armies and fleets that our economic prosperity has bought us to acquire even more resources and subjects for an aspiring empire.
 Country has at least   1 000 total development The country:
  • Gains   30 convertible[2] prestige
  • Gains “Imperial Ambition” for   25 years, giving:
    •   −5 Years of separatism
  • Gains a permanent claim on every neighboring area
   Build Manufactories
