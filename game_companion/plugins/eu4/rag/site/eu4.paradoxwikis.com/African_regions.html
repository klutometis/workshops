      For a list of countries in North, West and Northeast Africa, see Africa northern regions.
      For a list of countries in Southern, Central and Southeast Africa, see Africa southern regions.

      The African regions and areas are all locations that are part of Africa. The table content is out of the files in /Europa Universalis IV/history/provinces and refers to a game start on the 11th of November, 1444.

      Note: The continents, regions and areas of EU4 do not always match reality.

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.
