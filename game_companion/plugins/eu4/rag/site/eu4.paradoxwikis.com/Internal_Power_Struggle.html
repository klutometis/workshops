Internal Power Struggle
The Ottoman government is corrupted to its core. The Sultan bows to the power of the Janissaries. The Harem is in charge of governmental decisions. The Eyalets consider defecting from the empire. The Pashas demand more power and our very own subjects are poisoned by the thought of nationalism.

The Internal Power Struggle[1] refers to a series of disasters that can trigger for the Ottomans after reaching 100 Decadence. Managing to survive them will enable the nation to get rid of Decadence and reform into a much stronger State.

PrerequisitesEdit

 Available only with the Domination DLC enabled.

The country:

  • Is the Ottomans
  • Has the decadence mechanic
  • Has not had this disaster before.

Playing with normal or historical nations.

If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

Progress starts if Progress halts if

The Ottomans:

  • Have 100 decadence
  • At least one of:
    • Have less than +3 stability
    • Have a ruler with any skill lower than 5
    • Have an Eyalet or Core Eyalet subject with at least 50 liberty desire
Monthly progress Ottomans:
+1% Has an Eyalet or Core Eyalet subject with at least 50 liberty desire
+1% Has at least 25% overextension
+1% Has any rebel army in its lands
+1% Has any province occupied by rebels
+1% Has less than +3 stability
+1% Has a ruler with every skill lower than 5

The Ottomans:

  • Have less than 75 decadence
  • Have +3 stability
  • Have a ruler with every skill higher than 5
  • Have less than 25% overextension
  • Have no rebel armies in its lands
  • Have no owned provinces occupied by rebels
  • Have every Eyalet and Core Eyalet subjects with less than 50 liberty desire

ModifiersEdit

While the disaster is ongoing the country gets:

  • +1 National unrest
  • Decadence will be frozen at 100

Also the following events are triggered by a monthly pulse. Each event scales its effects according to the value of a hidden variable representing the intensity of the power struggle, which starts at 0. Each time an event fires, it has a chance to change the intensity by:

  • 10% chance of 2
  • 80% chance of 1
  • 4% chance of −1
  • 1% chance of −2
  • 5% chance of no change.
