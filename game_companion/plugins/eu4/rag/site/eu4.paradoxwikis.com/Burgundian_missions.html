Burgundian missions

  • Language
  • Watch
  • Edit

Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.

This is a list of all missions of Burgundy. It requires Emperor expansion for the mission tree to be full.[1]

Clicking on a mission icon leads to the appropriate table row.

Without Emperor, Burgundy has access to generic missions, and two branches of these missions[2], listed here:

  • Placate Subjects
  • A Kingdom Divided
  • Conquer Liege
  • Frisian Conquest


  • Integrate Nevers
  • Reclaim Transjurania
  • Kingdom of the Arles

National missionsEdit

Expansion to the SouthEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Integrate Nevers
Rumor has it that our puppet on the throne of Nevers is a practitioner of black magics. It wouldn't do to associate with such heresy; Nevers is better off brought into the fold.
   Burgundy owns the following provinces:
  • Nivernais (191)
  • Rethel (181)
   Burgundy gains a permanent claim on:
  • Romandie area
  • Chambery (205)
  • Annecy (4719)
 
Reclaim Transjurania
The Kingdom of Upper Burgundy has been under the control of the Empire for long enough. In ages past it constituted a full third of Burgundy - it must be returned to us.
   Burgundy owns the following provinces:
  • All provinces in the Romandie area
  • Chambery (205)
  • Annecy (4719)
   Burgundy gains a permanent claim on:
  • Provence area
  • Savoy area
  • Nice (102)
   Integrate Nevers
 
Kingdom of the Arles
The Duchy of Lower Burgundy, once ruled by Richard Justiciarius, who united all of Burgundy under his reign, should be returned to us. The Arelat must be restored!
   Burgundy or its non-tributary subjects own at least 8 provinces in:
  • Provence area
  • Savoy area
  • Nice (102)
 If   Burgundy is not a kingdom:
  •   Burgundy becomes a   Kingdom

Otherwise, gains   100 Administrative power
  Burgundy gains "Arelat Restoration" for   20 years, giving:

  •   +1 Yearly legitimacy
  •   +2 Yearly prestige
  •   -25% Promote culture cost

  Burgundy gains a claim on the following areas:

  • Piedmont
  • Lombardy
  • Po Valley
   Reclaim Transjurania
 
Invade Lombardy
The rich land of Lombardy lies just across the Alps. Its incumbent rulers are mostly usurpers with a poor claim to their lands - we would be able to provide the peoples of Lombardy with much stronger and decisive leadership, if we can seize control of them.
   Burgundy or its non-tributary subjects own:
  • Milan (104)
  • at least 10 provinces in the following areas:
    • Piedmont
    • Lombardy
    • Po Valley
   Burgundy gains   100 Administrative power

  Burgundy gains a permanent claim on the following areas:

  • Liguria
  • Venetia
  • Emilia-Romagna
  • Tuscany
   Kingdom of the Arles

Expansion to the NorthEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Placate Subjects
As the leader of so many fragile unions, we cannot be seen to have any disloyal subjects. While we consider our claims on Lotharingia, we must ensure that all our partners are bolstered and ready to act.
 All   subjects have less than   1% Liberty desire   Burgundy gains a permanent claim on the Lorraine area
 
Unite the Realm
Our holdings in the Low Countries are not directly under Burgundian control and chafe under our rule. We should integrate them into Burgundy and ensure that they obey the same laws as the rest of our domains.
 The following countries do not exist:
  •   Brabant
  •   Flanders
  •   Holland

  Burgundy owns at least 10 provinces in the following areas:

  • Flanders
  • South Brabant
  • North Brabant
  • Holland
   Burgundy gains   50 Diplomatic power

All owned provinces in the Low Countries region gain "Burgundian Netherlands" for   20 years, giving:

  •   +15% local tax modifier
  •   -0.05 monthly autonomy change
   Placate subjects
 
A Kingdom Divided
Our holdings in the North and South are extensive, yet the division of our birth right is difficult at best. The Duchies of Lorraine and Barrois are the missing link, and clearly belong under our ownership.
   Burgundy owns all provinces in the Lorraine area   Burgundy gains a permanent claim on the following provinces:
  • Liege (93)
  • Aachen (1878)
  • Loon (2974)
  • Upper Guelders (1742)
   Placate subjects
 
Control Liege
Historically, the Bishopric of Liège has been a strong voice in the Vatican, and in the Holy Roman Empire. By extension, if we were to control this voice, we could better represent our interests.
   Burgundy or its non-tributary subject own the following provinces:
  • Liege (93)
  • Aachen (1878)
  • Loon (2974)
  • Upper Guelders (1742)
   Burgundy:
  • Gains   15 Prestige
  • Gains "Voice of Reason" for   15 years, giving:
    •   +2 Diplomatic reputation
  • Gains a permanent claim on the Frisia area
   A Kingdom Divided
 
Secure the Lowlands
The peoples of the Netherlands harbor ambitions of independence. We must snuff these out and make sure our control over our territories remains complete and uninterrupted.
 The   "Dutch Revolt" has happened or year 1650 has been reached without triggering it

  Burgundy owns all provinces in the Low Countries region as core provinces

Low Countries region gets "The Low Countries Secured" for   20 years, giving:
  •   -2 Local unrest
  •   -0.10 Local autonomy change
   Unite the Realm
 
Frisian Conquest
The rest of the Low Countries region is in thrall to us. Only Frisia remains outside our control - we shall have it for ourselves.
   Burgundy or its non-tributary subjects own all provinces in the Frisia area   Burgundy:
  • Gains   20 prestige
  • Gains   100 Administrative power
  • Gains "Offshore assets" (  +20 trade power) for   20 years in the North Sea trade node
   Control Liege
 
Dominate Channel Trade
The port cities of the Low Countries are wealthy commercial centers, but they face stiff competition from the English across the Channel. We must make sure we have a monopoly over trade through these parts.
   Burgundy:
  • Is the   strongest trade power in the English Channel trade node
  • Owns at least 3 provinces in the Low Countries region with a   level 2 center of trade[3]

If DLC   'Dharma' is not active:

  •   Burgundy owns at least 3 provinces in the Low Countries region with a trade depot or stock exchange
   Burgundy gains "Channel Trade Domination" until the end of the game, giving:
  •   +5% Trade efficiency
  •   +10% Trade steering
   Secure the Lowlands

Imperial ambitionsEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Secure the Succession
The French King and the Holy Roman Emperor alike have their eyes on our lands and will take any pretext to claim our title. We must secure the succession and make sure our children's children will have a kingdom left to inherit.
 Resolve the "Burgundian Succession Crisis" or reach the year 1500 without triggering it.

  Burgundy:

  • Is not a subject nation
  • Is at peace

There is no active Imperial Incident.

  Burgundy gains "The Seed is Strong" until the end of the game, giving:
  •   +33% chance of a new heir

  Burgundy gains "Dynastic Authority Restored" for   20 years, giving:

  •   -15% diplomatic annexation cost

If   Burgundy has 'Marie of Burgundy' as a ruler:

  • the   event "The Laws of Queen Marie of Burgundy" happens

If all the following conditions are fulfilled:

  • Is   Burgundy
  • Is not the Emperor of the HRE
  • Is not a member of the HRE
  •   Holy Roman Empire exists
  • Does not have a female heir
  • "Burgundian Succession Crisis" is not ongoing
  • At least one of:
    • The "Burgundian Succession Crisis" has started at least   5 years ago[4]
    • All of:
      • Does not have “Philippe III” as ruler
      • Has an heir

Then the   event "Imperial Entrance" happens
Otherwise, if the HRE exists and   Burgundy is not a member:

  •   Burgundy and all owned provinces join the HRE
 
Join the Empire
We own territories that form part of the Holy Roman Empire, but as we were in the past technically subjects of France, we have never joined it. Membership could provide a much-needed bulwark against the French... and provide us with greater opportunities to expand our power in Germany.
   Burgundy is not a subject nation

One of the following must be true:

  • Is a member or the Emperor of the HRE
  • The HRE does not exist
  • The question of our membership in the HRE is resolved.[5]
 If   Burgundy is the Emperor of the HRE or the HRE does not exist:
  •   Burgundy gains   25 power projection

  Burgundy gains a permanent claim on the following areas:

  • Alsace
  • Lower Rhineland
  • North Rhine
   Secure the Succession
 
Influence the Princes
In the past, we have been outsiders to the Empire. This must change. We should make a concerted effort towards improving our standing among the Imperial Princes.
 If the HRE does not exist:
  • At least 5 countries with their capital is in the North or South Germany regions:
    • Have at least   +60 opinion towards   Burgundy

Otherwise,

  • At least 5 Electors:
    • Are backing   Burgundy to become Emperor
    • Have at least   +100 opinion towards   Burgundy
  • At least 15 HRE member countries (if the HRE has at least 20 members), else 10 HRE member countries:
    • Have at least   +60 opinion towards   Burgundy
 If   Burgundy is a   monarchy and has less than 90 legitimacy:
  •   Burgundy gains   10 legitimacy

Otherwise,   Burgundy gains   15 prestige
  Burgundy gains "Imperial Influencer" for   20 years, giving:

  •   +2 diplomatic reputation
   Join the Empire
 
The Crown of Lothair
The Frankish King Lothair II once carved out a mighty kingdom from the Low Countries to Savoy. We are the obvious successors of Lothair's kingdom, and yet we have to content ourselves with lesser titles. This will not do. We must reclaim the rest of Lothair's territories and insist on our coronation as Kings of Lotharingia.
   Burgundy:
  • Is at peace
  • Owns the following core provinces:
    • Aachen (1878)
    • Lotharingen (189)
    • At least 25 provinces in the following areas:
      • Alsace
      • Lower Rhineland
      • North Rhine
      • Lorraine
      • Wallonia
      • South Brabant
      • North Brabant
      • Holland
      • Frisia
 The   event "Lothair's Legacy" happens

  Burgundy gains a permanent claim on the following areas:

  • Upper Rhineland
  • Palatinate
  • Hesse
  • Westphalia
  • North Westphalia
  • Weser
  • Lower Saxony
  • Braunschweig
   Join the Empire
 
Lead the Empire
If we are to be part of the Empire, it is surely so that we can claim leadership over it. The throne is ours for the taking.
 If the HRE exists,   Burgundy:
  • Is the Emperor of the HRE
  • Has at least   25 imperial authority

Otherwise:

  •   Burgundy and its non-tributary subjects own at least 25 provinces in North or South Germany region
  • No country with their capital is in the North or South Germany region and their non-tributary subjects own at least 10 provinces
   Burgundy gains   20 prestige

If the HRE exists:

  •   Burgundy gains   25 imperial authority

Otherwise,   Burgundy gains   100 Diplomatic power

  Influence the Princes

French dominationEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
English Alliance
When the time comes to rid ourselves of the French menace to our west, the English would be our natural ally. We should consider securing their allegiance.
 If exists,   Great Britain, else   England, else   Scotland:
  • One of the following must be true:
    •   Burgundy is allied to this country
    • All of the following must be true
      •   Burgundy rivals this country or vice versa
      • This country has been (scornfully) insulted by   Burgundy

One of the following must be true:

  •   France does not exist
  •   France is a subject of   Burgundy
  • All of the following must be true
    •   Burgundy rivals   France or vice versa
    •   France has been (scornfully) insulted by   Burgundy
   Burgundy gains "Burgundian Diplomatic Prowess" for   20 years, giving:
  •   +25% improve relations
 
League of the Public Weal
We are technically subjects of the King of France, but owing to our strength, we have been able to secure considerable autonomy. The French resent this and seek to centralize their authority. We must counteract these efforts and - with the support of France's regional nobility - lead a general revolt against the King.
   Burgundy is not in a regency

One of the following must be true:

  •   France does not exist
  •   France is Burgundian subject
  • If at least 3 of   Brittany,   Provence,   Lorraine,   Auvergne,   Armagnac,   Bourbonnais,   Orleans,   Foix,   Alençon,   Nevers, or   Normandy are French subjects:
    • At least 3 French vassals have at least   +100 opinion towards   Burgundy
  • Otherwise, at least   2 Burgundian allies rivals   France or vice versa
   Burgundy gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
  • Île-de-France
  • Champagne
  • Orleans

If   France exists, is not Burgundian subject and at least 3 of Brittany, Provence, Lorraine, Auvergne, Armagnac, Bourbonnais, Orleans, Foix, Alençon, Nevers, or Normandy are French subjects:

  •   France:
    • Gets the   event "The League of the Public Weal"
    • Every subject with its capital in the France region gains   50 liberty desire
   English Alliance
 
King of the Franks
We must break the power of the French King and take his title and domains for ourselves.
   Burgundy:
  • Is not in a regency
  • Owns core province Paris (183)
  • Has an army equal to or larger than   France's

  Burgundy and its non-tributary subjects:

  • Own at least 18 provinces in the France region
  • Have greater   development than   France
   Burgundy gains "Rex Francorum" for   20 years, giving:
  •   +1 diplomatic relation
  •   +15% morale of armies

If   France exists:

  •   Burgundy gains a permanent claim on all provinces owned by   France in the France region

If   France has any subject with its capital in the France region:

  •   France gets the   event "[From.Monarch.GetName]: King of the Franks"
  • All French subjects with their capital in the France region become   vassals of   Burgundy
   League of the Public Weal

Internal affairsEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Papal Relations
The Pope has long sought to interfere in the affairs of Germany and France. However, he may have overreached - resentment towards the Papal establishment is growing in many quarters. It will not do to have an ambiguous stance. We must either commit ourselves to being good Catholics and supporters of the Pope, or seek a clean split from him and all he stands for.
 One of the following must be true:
  • All of the following must be true:
    •   Burgundy is   Catholic
    •   Burgundy has at least   50 papal influence
    •   Papal States has at least   +100 opinion towards   Burgundy
  • All of the following must be true:
    •   Burgundy is not Catholic
    • Either the   Papal States:
      • Do not exist
      • Rivals   Burgundy or vice versa
      • Have been insulted by   Burgundy
 If   Burgundy is   Catholic:
  •   Burgundy gains "Papal Favor" for   20 years, giving:
    •   +1 yearly papal influence

Otherwise,

  •   Burgundy gains "Enemy of the Pope" for   20 years, giving:
    •   +0.5 yearly prestige
    •   +3% missionary strength
 
Feast of Pheasants
The growth of Ottoman power is alarming. We must gather momentum for a fresh crusade to once again drive them from the shores of Europe.
   Burgundy is not in a regency

One of the following must be true:

  • The   Ottomans do not exist
  • The   Ottomans follow the same religion as   Burgundy
  • At least   2 Burgundian allies rival the   Ottomans or vice versa
  • Any   Burgundian ally is a   Great power and rivals the   Ottomans
 If the   Ottomans exist and do not follow the same religion as   Burgundy:
  • The   event "The Feast of the Pheasant - Burgundian version" happens
  • The   Ottomans get the   event "The Feast of the Pheasant - Ottomans version"
  • Every European country that:
    • follows the same religion as   Burgundy
    • has a positive opinion of   Burgundy
    • has less than   50 opinion towards the   Ottomans
  • gets the   event "The Feast of the Pheasant"

Otherwise,   Burgundy:

  • Gains   100 diplomatic power.
  • Gains   100 military power.
   Papal Relations
 
Appease the Estates
Keeping the estates contented has often been a difficult and tiresome balancing act for the Burgundian king. We must end the dissent and division that has plagued our lands and ensure that all work together as one to achieve the aim of greater glory and prosperity.
   Burgundy:
  • Has at least   30% crown land ownership
  • Has at least   60 nobility loyalty, if exists
  • Has at least   60 burghers loyalty, if exists
  • Has at least   60 clergy loyalty, if exists
 If   Burgundy does not have the   nobles,   burghers and   clergy estates:
  • If   Burgundy has a stability of less than +3,
    • then it gains   1 stability,
    • else it gains   50 administrative power.

Otherwise:

  •   Burgundy gains   5 crown land ownership, if has less than 60%
  •   Nobles,   Burghers and   Clergy gain "Appeased Estates" (  +10 loyalty) for   20 years, if exists
 
Chivalry is not Dead
The ideals of chivalry and hospitality are worth preserving. We must stick to these guiding pillars and see to it that our court is renowned across Europe for its chivalric splendor.
   Burgundy:
  • has at least   80 prestige
  • has at least   80 army tradition
  • is not in a regency
 The   event "The Court of [Root.Monarch.GetName]" happens   Appease the Estates
