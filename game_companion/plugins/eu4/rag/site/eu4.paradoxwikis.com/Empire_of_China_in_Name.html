      Empire of China in Name

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
      At least some were last verified for version 1.31.

      		Available only with the Mandate of Heaven DLC enabled.
      Empire of China in Name
      While we have succesfully secured the title of Emperor over the Chinese our Empire is one that scarcely includes any Chinese. If we are to be seen as anything but a foreign emperor we will need to integrate the Chinese as part of our government.

      Empire of China in Name [1] is a disaster in the Emperor of China.

      Prequisites[edit | edit source]

      This country:

      • is the Emperor of China.
      • did not have this disaster before.
      • primary culture is not in the Chinese culture group

      If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

      Monthly progress starts if Monthly progress halts if

      Emperor of China:

      • does not have an ongoing disaster.
      • accepts less than 2 cultures in the Chinese culture group.
      Monthly progress Emperor of China
      +1.0% accepts less than 2 cultures in the Chinese culture group.
      −0.5% has completed the Humanist idea group.
      −1.0% accepts at least 2 cultures in the Chinese culture group.
      		at least one of the following is true:
      • has an ongoing disaster.
      • accepts at least 2 cultures in the Chinese culture group.

      Start[edit | edit source]

      If the progress reaches 100%, the disaster will start with the following event

      id

      Emperor of China in Name

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      While we have achieved a general recognization as the new Empire of China we have yet to earn the respect of the Chinese people. Our great reliance on foreign, even uncivilized, people in our armies and administration is putting the Mandate of our dynasty in question.
      Unless we take measures to become a truly Chinese Empire we will remain an Empire in name only and will appear to have lost the Mandate of Heaven.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      the start of the ‘Empire of China in Name’ disaster.

      In time, they will learn.
      • Lose 1 stability
      • Lose 25 mandate
      • Separatist rebels (size 1) rise in revolt in two random provinces that:
        • is not the primary culture
        • is in the Chinese culture group

      Modifiers[edit | edit source]

      While the disaster is ongoing the country gets:

      • −0.3 Monthly mandate
      • +5 Years of separatism
      • +20% Technology cost

      Finishing conditions[edit | edit source]

      If any of the following conditions are true, the disaster will end:

      • is not the Emperor of China
      • accepts at least 2 cultures in the Chinese culture group.
      • primary culture is in the Chinese culture group.
      • disaster has been active for 10 years.

      then it loses the above-mentioned effects and the following event is triggered:

      id

      Acceptance of the Chinese

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      Our efforts to integrate the Chinese in the state that rules them have born fruit and our claim to the Mandate of Heaven is no longer in question due to lacking ties to the people themselves. Like many of the foreign conquerors China has seen over the centuries our Empire may never come to be seen as truly Chinese in origin, but it is truly an Empire of China.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      the end of the ‘Empire of China in Name’ disaster.

      For the Son of Heaven!
      • Gain 25 mandate
