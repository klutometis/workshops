      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
      At least some were last verified for version 1.22.

      Mechanics are aspects of the game which require detailed discussion. Herein is a brief about each mechanic and a link to a more detailed page.

      Economy and colonisation

      Mechanic Ver. DLC Notes
      Economy 1.24 The economy is one of the biggest features and revolves around income and expenses.
      Tax 1.30 Tax is the money collected directly from a province.
      Production 1.24 Production relates to the goods produced and traded locally.
      Trade 1.25 Trade is income for buying and selling goods competitively abroad.
      Privateer 1.30 Privateer (pirate) enemy trade and loot booty.
      Raid Coast 1.30 Loot coastal lands and capture slaves to use as sailors.
      Development 1.24 Development is the relative richness of a province. It is the sum of Tax, Production and Manpower.
      Exploration 1.27 Exploration of the terra incognita; find New Worlds to colonise and new trade routes.
      Colonization 1.25 Colonies in undeveloped lands are a way to expand without conquest.
      Trade goods 1.30 The goods traded across the world.

      War

      Mechanic Ver. DLC Notes
      Manpower 1.29 Manpower is a measure of the number of men available to the army.
      Warfare 1.24 Warfare is the final extent of diplomacy and allows openly hostile actions among nations.
      Professionalism 1.24 Increasing army professionalism unlocks abilities, such as building supply depots and refilling fort garrisons.
      Fort 1.24 A fort is a strategic defensive feature available to build in a province. It requires monthly upkeep.
      Army 1.23 Land-based warfare is conducted by the army. It requires monthly upkeep.
      Navy 1.24 Sea based warfare is conducted by the navy. It requires monthly upkeep.
      Alliance 1.30 A military agreement between two nations.
      Casus belli 1.30 A reason for war.
      Claim 1.27 This document entails that a province belongs rightfully to someone.
      Condottieri 1.30 Troops can be hired out to other nations.
      Discipline 1.26 How long will the army stand in face of enemy.
      Force limit 1.29 How large the army or navy can be.
      Land units 1.30 The army is made up of 3 types of units: infantry, cavalry and artillery. They advance throughout the game.
      Land warfare 1.24 How battles are conducted within the game.
      Military leader 1.22 Admirals, Generals, Explorers and Conquistadors.
      Military tradition 1.26 Tradition defines the quality of leaders.
      Naval units 1.29 The navy is made up of 4 types of vessels. They advance throughout the game.
      Naval warfare 1.25 How battles are conducted within the game.
      Sailors 1.23 Sailors are men available for the navy.
      War exhaustion 1.30 A prolonged war is draining on a nation.
      Power projection 1.30 Power projection is how powerful a nation is relative to its rivals.

      Technology

      Mechanic Ver. DLC Notes
      Technology 1.30 Technology is how a nation advances through the game and acquires new abilities or options.
      Innovativeness 1.30 Innovativeness gives scaling bonuses to monarch power spending and military tradition decay.
      National ideas 1.30 Each country in the game has access to seven national ideas. These ideas help define the strengths of each country.
      Idea groups 1.30 Ideas are a representation of the disposition of a nation and their abilities. Ideas can be nation specific or selected from a list.
      Institutions 1.24 Institutions impact technology cost and replace westernization and the old technology groups.

      Internal country management

      Mechanic Ver. DLC Notes
      Absolutism 1.28 Grow a ruler's absolutism so as to wield unlimited power.
      Governing capacity 1.30 Grow large a realm can grow while still being managable.
      Province 1.27 Provinces are the smallest unit of land which a nation can own.
      Core 1.29 Cores represent rightful and recognized ownership of a province.
      Ruler 1.25 The head of a nation.
      Ruler personalities 1.29 Rulers with individual personalities; brave or cowardly, embezzler or architectural visionary, no two rulers are ever quite the same.
      Monarch power 1.28 Monarch power is how a nation progresses and represents the skill of a ruler.
      Advisors 1.24 Advisors provide additional Monarch Points and benefits.
      Government 1.30 Different governments offer different mechanics.
      Monarchy 1.27
      Republic 1.30
      Theocracy 1.23
      Tribal government 1.24
      Buildings 1.28 Buildings are built in a province to improve it, by expanding on the manpower, tax or production.
      Religion 1.26 Religion is the beliefs of the people in a province.
      Culture 1.25 Culture shows the commonality between the people in a province and how it contrasts with another province.
      Estates 1.30 Estates are a method for devolving power to province in exchange for another advantage.
      Burghers 1.30
      Clergy 1.30
      Nobility 1.30
      Cossacks 1.30
      Dhimmi 1.30
      Tribes 1.30
      Brahmins 1.30
      Jains 1.30
      Marathas 1.30
      Rajputs 1.30
      Vaishyas 1.30
      Autonomy 1.30 Autonomy is the measure of how much self-determination a province has.
      Stability 1.25 Stability indicates how much internal cohesion a country has.
      Corruption 1.27 Embezzlement, treachery and loss of leader's authority.
      Rebellion 1.23 Rebellions are uprisings against the nation's rule, they can be for many different reasons.
      Disasters 1.30 Disasters are large scale problems which can befall a nation.
      Great project 1.32 Large constructions for nations to consider.
      Capital 1.25 The seat of Government.
      Factions 1.29 Factions represent the different interests of special groups within a nation.
      Overextension 1.29 Taking new land puts strain on a nation.
      Policies 1.30 Policies reflect on how a nation intends to act.
      States and territories 1.28 A way of focusing on more important provinces.
      Missions ~ Missions are goals set for a nation with a reward should they be completed
      Decisions ~ Decisions are active steps taken by a nation.
      Events ~ Events happen to nations, they can be based on historical events of the time or random.

      External relations

      Mechanic Ver. DLC Notes
      Relations 1.24 Direct relations between two nations.
      Diplomacy 1.24 Diplomacy is the mechanic for interaction between nation states.
      Defender of the Faith 1.30 Become the Defender of the Faith and protect all nations of your religion from heathens and heretics.
      Regions 1.30 Areas, regions and super-regions are how the provinces in the game are split up and categorized.
      Subject nation 1.25 Nations which are controlled by another.
      Vassal 1.23 A nation that pays taxes to its overlord and joins its wars.
      March 1.25 A military-focused subject that pays no taxes.
      Client state 1.25
      Personal union 1.30 Two or more countries under one ruler.
      Colonial nation 1.23 Subject in the Americas and Oceania which pays tariffs.
      Trade company 1.27 A group of overseas provinces optimized for trade.
      Tributary 1.27 A highly autonomous subject that pays tribute to its overlord.
      Holy Roman Empire 1.29 A loose federation of states in western Europe that plays a key part in the Reformation.
      Diplomatic feedback 1.30 Diplomacy feedback, how one nation responds to another.
      Emperor of China 1.30 The Emperor claims the Mandate of Heaven.
      Envoy 1.24 Envoys perform tasks for their nation in various places in the world.
      Espionage 1.27 Diplomats aren't always what they seem.
      Papacy 1.29 The Pope and the Curia of the Catholic church.
      Prestige 1.26 How a nation is viewed throughout the world.
      Council of Trent 1.30 A system for modelling tensions between Christian nations during the Reformation and how the Reformation unfolds.

      Meta

      Mechanic Ver. DLC Notes
      Achievements 1.30 Achievements using the Steam achievements mechanics.
      Ages 1.28 The wheel of time turns; complete age objectives to earn national splendour and unlock age abilities.
      Luck 1.30 Some nations are luckier than others and get bonuses to help them.
      Nation designer 1.28 Build a new country.
      Random New World 1.29 Build a new world.
      Score system 1.30 A way of competing across many games.
