      The Cossacks are the free people of the Steppes. Their background varies, some have been born on the grasslands while others are escaped serfs from the great latifundia that border the wild fields. Great raiders and hardy settlers, the Cossacks aspire to be recognized as an estate of the same importance as the Nobility and to strike down at anyone who would attempt to limit their freedom.

      Availability[edit | edit source]

      General availability:

      • Available to any country that is Christian, has the Eastern unit type and owns at least 1 province with steppe terrain. The estate remains available even after losing the last steppe province.

      Specific conditions:

      • Is not nomad
      • Does not have Celestial Empire government reform
      • Does not use other mechanics
      • Danzig, Teutonic Order and a Prussia formed by them may unlock this estate via "Annex Ruthenia" Mission, Russia unlocks it when switching to the tech group via the "Great Imperial Ambitions" Mission. A country which has unlocked the estate doesn't have to fulfill the "General availability" conditions.
      • A country with the Sich Rada government reform has access to the estate without having to fulfill any of the conditions(but it still needs the DLC)

      Influence[edit | edit source]

      Influence – The estate has a base influence of 0%, modified by:

      • +5% Has enacted the Livonian Elective Monarchy tier 1 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the Expand Royal Court tier 3 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the General Estates tier 6 government reform
      • +10% Has at least 1 province with Steppe terrain and 10 cities in total
      • −5% Has enacted the Royal Favoritism tier 3 government reform
      • -15% Has enacted the Abolish the Castes tier 4 government reform
      • −10% Has enacted the Dynastic Administration tier 5 government reform
      • −5% Has enacted the Subservient Bureaucrats tier 5 government reform
      • −5% Has enacted the Les Six livres de la République tier 8 government reform
      • −40% If another estate has seized control of the nation (via disaster)

      They gain twice as much influence from land share (1:1 ratio).

      Loyalty[edit | edit source]

      Loyalty – The estate's loyalty equilibrium is modified by:

      • +10% For Corruption at 100% (scaled)
      • +5% Has enacted the Decentralized Bureaucracy tier 3 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the Maintain Balance of power tier 4 government reform
      • +10% Has enacted the General Estates tier 6 government reform
      • +10% Has enacted the Supremacy of the Diet tier 6 government reform
      • -5% Has enacted the Centralized Bureaucracy tier 3 government reform
      • -5% to +5% with current Prestige
      • −20% If another estate has seized control of the nation (via disaster)

      Rebels: If the Cossacks are disloyal after land has been seized, Cossack rebels may rise in revolt in a few provinces.

      Disaster: If the Cossacks estate reaches 100 influence, their estate disaster will begin ticking and - unless lowered - will eventually trigger the Cossack Independence disaster.
