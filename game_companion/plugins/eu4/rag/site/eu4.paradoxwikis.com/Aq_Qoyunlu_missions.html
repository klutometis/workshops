Our mission is to construct a majestic mosque, the Blue Mosque, that will serve not only as a house of worship but also as a beacon of cultural and artistic achievement.
   Aq Qoyunlu:
  • Owns province Tabriz (416) which:
    • Has at least   10 Base tax
    • Has any tax building
   Aq Qoyunlu:
  • “Building Mosques now grants   −1 Local Unrest.”

Province Tabriz (416):

  • If has a   Cathedral:
    • Gains   2 base tax
  • Else, if has a   Temple:
    • Upgrades the local   Temple to a   Cathedral
  • Gains “Renovate Tabriz” for   20 years, giving:
    •   −35% Local development cost
   Patronize Arts and Literature
 
Pearl of the Empire
Tabriz, the glorious city, is the heart of our realm, throbbing with life, culture, and commerce. It's time to elevate it even further, transforming it into the 'Pearl of the Empire', a city of unrivaled prestige and wealth. Tabriz must become a city of wonder, the city of the world's desire, attracting scholars, merchants, and artisans from all corners of the earth.
   Aq Qoyunlu:
  • Owns province Tabriz (416) which:
    • Has at least   40 Development
    • Has at least 5 buildings
 Province Tabriz (416):
  • Gains   1 base tax
  • Gains   1 base production
  • Gains   1 base manpower
  • Gains “Pearl of the Empire” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +50% Local goods produced modifier
    •   +1 Possible number of manufactories

  Aq Qoyunlu:

  • If completes this mission while no Muslim province has more development than Tabriz (416):
    • Gains   300 splendor
   Build the Blue Mosque

  Imperial Fabrics

 
Splendid Court
A ruler's court is more than a symbol of sovereignty; it is a crucible of wisdom and decision-making that shapes the destiny of an empire. We must strive to build a court filled with the wisest men in the realm. Their sage advice, scholarly wisdom, and diplomatic acumen will guide our governance and ensure our decisions reflect the needs and aspirations of our people.
   Aq Qoyunlu:
  • Is employing any advisor of at least level 3
  • Owns at least 5 provinces with a Courthouse building
   Aq Qoyunlu:
  • Triples the chances of our rulers and heirs having the   Well-Connected personality trait (  −20% Advisor Cost)
  • Gains “Splendid Court” for   20 years, giving:
    •   −20% Cost of advisors with ruler's religion
    •   +1 Possible advisor
   Patronize Arts and Literature
 
Kitab-i Diyarbakriyya
Our story is the story of the Turks, the narrative of a people both indomitable and visionary. The chronicles of the Turkmen dynasties, from the days of the Oghuz tribes to the rise of the Aq Qoyunlu, are filled with tales of valor, wisdom, triumph, and occasionally, the harsh lessons of defeat. It is time these stories are etched in the annals of history, not just as fragmented tales but as a consolidated account that encapsulates our shared heritage.
   Aq Qoyunlu:
  • Has at least   +60 prestige
  • At least one of:
    • Ruler's   diplomatic skill is at least 5
    • Gains at least   10 diplomatic power per month
    • Ruler has the personality trait   Well Connected
   Aq Qoyunlu:
  • Gains   5 innovativeness
  • Gets the   “Abu Bakr Tihrani” event
   Splendid Court
