      The [empty_neighbor_target.GetAreaName] Company

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The description below is one of several available for this event.
      The following description is used if:

      • Is a colony
      The [This.GetAreaName] Company that was charged with the duty of claiming [This.GetName] in the name of [This.Owner.GetName] has decided instead to expand its borders to an adjacent region close to our colony. The promise of a new life in this uninhabited land is greatly appealing to the [Root.Owner.Culture.GetName] settlers, and should we permit the expansion, it will attract a far greater number of settlers than our existing colony in [This.GetName].

      The following description is used if:

      • Is not a colony
      Our nation is surrounded by territories as yet unclaimed by any of our rivals. Our ambition to expand the lands belonging to the crown has spread into nationalistic fervor within our people, and a new private company has established itself in [Root.GetName] with the ambition of bringing new settlers to the lands of [empty_neighbor_target.GetName] to claim the territory for Glory, Gold, [Root.Owner.Monarch.GetTitle] [Root.Owner.Monarch.GetName], and the [empty_neighbor_target.GetAreaName] Company. Should we permit the expansion, they will bring a great deal of settlers to the province and rapidly expand [Root.Owner.GetAdjective] influence in the area.''
       
      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Has empty adjacent province

      The country:

      • Has less than 2 unfinished colonies
      • Has activated Exploration or Expansion Ideas
      		 Is triggered only by

      the colonial pulse (Base weight: 100/16625)

      Option conditions

       Always enabled:

      This can only be good.

      One random neighbouring uncolonized province:

      • Create new colony with 200 Population
      • Gain "Colonial boom" for 20 years, giving:
        • +15 Local settler increase

       Enabled if: The country has at least 1 unfinished colony

      Direct them to an existing colony.

      One random owned colony:

      • Gain 100 Population
      • Gain "Colonial boom" for 5 years, giving:
        • +15 Local settler increase

      Founding Father in $PROVINCENAME$.

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      There is a gentleman in [Root.GetName] preaching to the people that their home would be better off free from the “clutches of [Root.Owner.GetAdjective] imperialism” and that the entirety of the region should embrace self-governance. While many regarded him at first as a harmless nuisance, the number of followers he’s gained is rapidly growing, and some members of our government worry that eventually, this “founding father” will incite a violent rebellion in [Root.GetName] in an attempt to break away from the [Root.Owner.GetAdjective] Empire.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Is overseas
      • Is not a colony
      • Is is the same religion group than its owner
      • Does not have province modifiers:
        • “Founding Father”
        • “Loyalist Stronghold”

      The country:

      • Does not have country modifiers:
        • “Increased Narrow-minded”
        • “Increased Serfdom”

      It is the Age of Revolutions.

      		Is triggered only by

      the colonial pulse (Base weight: 100/16625)

      He can't do any harm.

      The province gains “Founding Father” for the rest of the game, giving:

      • +3 Local unrest
      • +20% Local tax
      Execute him.

      The country:

      • Gain “Increased Narrow-minded” for 10 years, giving:
        • +5% Technology cost
        • +1% Missionary strength
      • Gain “Increased Serfdom” for 10 years, giving:
        • +15% Stability cost modifier
        • -10% Land morale
        • +0.1 Yearly corruption

      Minorities flock to $PROVINCENAME$

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The so-called pilgrims following the [heretics_from_here.Religion.GetName] faith are flocking to the colonies to escape religious persecution, and $PROVINCENAME$ in particular has attracted a sizeable [heretics_from_here.Religion.GetName] population. Their ways are savage and ungodly and they will most likely perish in the colonies.
      Still, they have managed to gain the support of many of our settlers and have made a new home for themselves in this faraway land.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Is overseas
      • Is a colony with at least 600 Population
      • Is is the same religion group than its owner
      • Does not have province modifier “Religious Migration”

      The country:

      • Does not expel minority to this province
      • Owns any province which:
        • Is not overseas
        • Has a religion which is in the same group as the target province, but not the same
        • Is not expelling minorities
        • Saved as heretics_from_here
      		 Is triggered only by

      the colonial pulse (Base weight: 100/16625)

      The natives will deal with them.

      The province:

      • Change religion to that of heretics_from_here
      • Change culture to owner
      • Gain 400 Population
      • Gain “Religious Migration” for 24 years, giving:
        • -25% Local missionary strength
      They are not part of our Colony or our nation!

      The province:

      • Lose 100 Population

      The heretics_from_here province:

      • Gain “Enforcing unity” for 10 years, giving:
        • +3% Local missionary strength
        • −50% Local missionary maintenance cost

      A Witch!

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The villagers in $PROVINCENAME$ have caught a witch red-handed as they claim she attempted to bake a group of children alive in her oven. The town is in outrage and it seems she is likely to be burnt at the stake. Friends of “evidence” and “logic” in our court claim that the proof for her acts of witchery are flimsy and weak at best, and that burning an innocent woman alive is an abomination, according to both the laws of our land and the holy texts. But since when have facts and logic played any part in the administration of our state?

       
      Trigger conditions

      The province is either:

      • Massachusett (968)
      • Manahahttan (965)
      • Nipmuc (966)
      • Narraganset (967)
      • Roanoke (2547)
      • Salem (4414)

      The country:

      • Is Christian

      It is the Age of Absolutism.

      		Is triggered only by

      the colonial pulse (Base weight: 100/16625)

      Burn the witch, burn to ash and bone!
      If the country has a stability of less than +3,
      • then it gains 1 stability,
      • else it gains 50 administrative power.
      Everyone knows there is no such thing as witches. Free the poor woman.

      The country:

      • Gain 50 Diplomatic power

      The Call of the Hunt

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The young men living in $PROVINCENAME$ are far wilder at heart than those back home in the capital. They often wander from the colony to experience life with the [hunter_province_target.Culture.GetName], learn their ways, and many yearn to cast aside their home to become wanderers and hunters in the great wilderness beyond the borders of [Root.GetName].

       
      Trigger conditions

      Any neighbouring province(saved as hunter_province_target):

      • Does not produce Fur
      • The owner:
        • Has either a tribal or native governement
        • Is in one of the following culture group:
          • Eskaleut
          • Central Algonquian
          • Eastern Algonquian
          • Iroquoian
          • Siouan
          • Muskogean
          • Sonoran
      		 Is triggered only by

      the colonial pulse (Base weight: 100/16625)

      Condemn this foolishness.

      The province:

      • Gain 2 Base manpower
      They might as well explore a bit.

      The country:

      • Gain Colonial Governor advisor

       Enabled if: Does not produce Fur

      As long as they bring back some furs...

      The province:

      • Change province's goods produced to Fur

      Fur Trade Dwindles

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      As civilization marches on, so does the expansion of our settlement. It seems that the rapid expansion of [Root.GetCapitalName] has driven many of the nearby beasts on to wilder lands and the woodlands in which we hunt have become scarce of creatures with pelts worth collecting. It would seem that fur hunting is no longer possible in $PROVINCENAME$.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Produces Fur
      • Did not have this event nor the event New World Coffee in the last 10 years
      • No neighbouring province is empty
      • No neighbouring province is owned and controlled by Steppe Nomads
      • No neighbouring province has a culture in one of the following culture groups:
        • Eskaleut
        • Central Algonquian
        • Eastern Algonquian
        • Iroquoian
        • Siouan
        • Muskogean
        • Sonoran
      		 Is triggered only by

      the colonial pulse (Base weight: 100/16625)

      If the forests are empty I guess we can cut down the trees...

      The province:

      • Change province's goods produced to Naval Supplies
      Maybe they'll finally settle down on their farms!

      The province:

      • Change province's goods produced to Grain

      Jesuit mission founded

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      A monastic group from the Society of Jesus has traveled to the New World to establish a mission in the [Root.GetName] colony, to Christianize the native people on our colonial borders. With any luck, the Jesuits may be able to civilize these people and teach them to embrace the true word of Christ.

       
      Trigger conditions

      • Has country modifier “The Societas Jesu”

      Modifiers: The country:

      • Has country modifier “Counter-Reformation” : ×1.5

      • +10 Local settler increase

      Our colony has been struck by Roman Fever!

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      Illness has struck our colony in $PROVINCENAME$. Just as we have brought many foreign diseases to the native people, it would seem there is a curse on the land and our people are falling ill and dying at an alarming rate. If we do not organize a response, we risk losing our colony in [Root.GetName] entirely.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Is a colony
      • Is in Asia, Africa, South America or Oceania

      Can happen only once every 15 years per country.

      		Is triggered only by

      the colonial pulse (Base weight: 100/16625)

      Send someone to organize a response.

      The province:

      • Gain “Organized Colony” for 5 years, giving:
        • −10 Local settler increase

      The country:

      • Lose 25 Administrative power
      Quarantine the province!

      The province:

      • Gain “Colony in Quarantine” for 5 years, giving:
        • −30 Local settler increase
      Pray for the best.

      The province and a random owned neighbouring colony:

      • Gain “Roman Fever” for 5 years, giving:
        • −15 Local settler increase

      Growth of the port of [Root.GetName]

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The growth of our colony in [Root.GetName] has always been closely associated with the Natural Harbor in the area and over time the province has come to act as something of a local trade hub.
      As the settlement has grown so has the importance of its port. The settlement has come to act as the entrepôt for the entire region and foreign as well as local traders all go there to conduct their trade.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Has a center of trade level 1
      • Has a port
      • Is in North America, South America or New World
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least 15 Development
        • Has a Market Place building
        • Has fully embraced Feudalism

      The country:

      • Has at least +1 Stability
      • Has embraced Feudalism
      		 Mean time to happen

      300 months
      Modifiers: The country:

      • Has completed Trade Ideas: ×0.9
      • Has +3 Stability: ×0.9
      • Has embraced Global trade: ×0.7

      The province:

      • Has at least 20 Development: ×0.8
      • Has a Trade depot building: ×0.7
      • Has a Dock building: ×0.9
      • Has at least 30 Development: ×0.5

      It is currently the:

      • Age of Reformation: ×0.95
      • Age of Absolutism: ×0.9
      • Age of Revolutions: ×0.85
      This must be the the result of our wise policies.

      The province:

      • If has a center of trade level 1
        • Increase center of trade by 1
      • Else,
        • Gain 2 Base production
