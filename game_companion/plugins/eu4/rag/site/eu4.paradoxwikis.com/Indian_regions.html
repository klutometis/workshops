      For a list of countries in this area, see India super-region.

      Indian regions are regions that are part of the Indian subcontinent, which belongs to Asia. The regions of the East Indies and other parts of Asia have their own pages. The table content is out of the files in /Europa Universalis IV/history/provinces and refers to a game start on the 11th of November, 1444.

      Note: Continents, regions and areas of Europa Universalis 4 do not always match reality.

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.
