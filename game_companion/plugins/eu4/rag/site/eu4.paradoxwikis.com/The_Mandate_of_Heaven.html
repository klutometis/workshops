      Triggered modifiers[1] and province triggered modifiers[2] apply automatically whenever their conditions are met. Unlike events, they are always active, and unlike missions and decisions, they do not explicitly give an option to a country.

      List of triggered modifiers[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
      At least some were last verified for version 1.28.

      Name Visibility Requirements Effects

      East Indian Trade Route[edit | edit source]
      • has at least 1 home port.
      • has its capital not in the continents Asia or Oceania.
      • has at least 4 home ports.
      • has discovered the Cape of Good Hope (1460).
      • has unlocked exploration ideas 1: Quest for the New World.
      • has trade power and a merchant in at least one of the following trade nodes:
        • Malacca, Bengal, Gujarat or Coromandel.
      • +5% Trade efficiency

      Custodian of the Holy Cities[edit | edit source]
      • is playing in historical setup
      • has discovered the provinces Mecca (385) and Medina (384).
      • is Muslim.
      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • owns or has a subject who owns Mecca (385) and Medina (384).
      • +0.5 Yearly legitimacy
      • +1 Missionary

      Subjugation of the Papacy[edit | edit source]
      • is playing in historical setup
      • has discovered the province Roma (118).
      • is Catholic.
      • is not the Papal State.
      • is the overlord of the Papal States
      • is not Italy
      • is not the Holy Roman Empire
      • if the country is the emperor of the HRE and the Papal states is a member, the reform “Revoke The Privilegia” has not been passed
      • does not have the country flag general_flag_for_FU_Rome_flag from the event Treaty of the Golden Bulls or from completing the french mission City of Lights as catholic
      • −2 Diplomatic reputation

      Rapid Collapse of Society[edit | edit source]
      • is playing in normal or historical setup
      • the capital is in North America, South America or in the New World.
      • does not have Feudalism institution.
      • European Diseases have struck for less than 25 years.
      • +33% All power costs
      • −10% Discipline
      • −20% Land morale

      Submission to the Emperor[edit | edit source]
      • "The Shadow Kingdom" was completed[3].
      • the country was not reined in by the emperor through war[4].
      • is in the Latin culture group.
      • is not the emperor of the Holy Roman Empire.
      • year is before 1550.
      • is not Italy.
      • has its capital in the HRE.
      • year is before 1550.
      • is not allied with the emperor of the Holy Roman Empire.
      • +3 National unrest
      • +10% Stability cost modifier
      • −1 Yearly prestige

      Counter Revolution[edit | edit source]
      • The revolution target exists.
      • is not revolutionary.
      • has its capital on the same continent as the capital of the revolution target or borders the revolution target.
      • The revolution target exists.
      • is not revolutionary.
      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not at war with the revolution target.
      • does not have a truce with the revolution target.
      • is a monarchy.
      • −5 Yearly prestige
      • +30% Stability cost modifier
      • +5 National unrest

      Reaction[edit | edit source]
      • The revolution target exists.
      • has its capital on the same continent as the capital of the revolution target.
      • The revolution target exists.
      • is not revolutionary.
      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is at war with the revolution target.
      • is a monarchy.
      • +10 Yearly prestige
      • −10% Stability cost modifier
      • −10 National unrest

      Crusade[edit | edit source]
      • A crusade target exists.
      • is Catholic.
      • is at war with the crusade target.
      • controls at least 1 province owned by the crusade target.
      • has land maintenance set to maximum.
      • +30% National manpower modifier
      • +10% National tax modifier
      • +10% Morale of armies
      • +1 Yearly prestige
      • +1 Yearly papal influence
      • +0.25 Yearly devotion

      Excommunication[edit | edit source]
      • is Catholic.
      • is excommunicated.
      • −2 Yearly prestige
      • −10 Yearly papal influence
      • −5 Yearly devotion
      • −3 Tolerance of the True Faith

      The Pentarchy[edit | edit source]
      • is playing in historical setup
      • is Orthodox.
      • has discovered at least 1 of the following provinces: Roma (118), Constantinople (151), Antioch (2313), Jerusalem (379) and Alexandria (358).
      • owns the following provinces: Roma (118), Constantinople (151), Antioch (2313), Jerusalem (379) and Alexandria (358).
      • These provinces are Orthodox.
      • +0.5 Yearly prestige
      • +1 Missionary

      The Mandate of Heaven[edit | edit source]
      • is a celestial empire.
      • The ‘Mandate of Heaven’ DLC is not enabled.
      • owns at least 20 cities.
      • has a stability of at least 0.
      • has a legitimacy of at least 60.
      • −10% Stability cost modifier
      • −5 National unrest

      The Mandate of Heaven Lost[edit | edit source]
      • is a celestial empire.
      • The ‘Mandate of Heaven’ DLC is not enabled.
      • any of the following is true:
        • owns fewer than 20 cities.
        • has a stability of less than 0.
        • has a legitimacy of less than 60.
      • −10% Discipline
      • +50% Stability cost modifier
      • +10 National unrest

      Emperor does not own and control Beijing[edit | edit source]
      • is a celestial empire.
      • The ‘Mandate of Heaven’ DLC is enabled.
      • does not own or control Beijing (1816)
      • −0.05 monthly Mandate

      Emperor does not own and control Nanjing[edit | edit source]
      • is a celestial empire.
      • The ‘Mandate of Heaven’ DLC is enabled.
      • does not own or control Nanjing (1821)
      • −0.05 monthly Mandate

      Emperor does not own and control Canton[edit | edit source]
      • is a celestial empire.
      • The ‘Mandate of Heaven’ DLC is enabled.
      • does not own or control Canton (667)
      • −0.05 monthly Mandate

      War for the Celestial Empire[edit | edit source]
      • is a celestial empire.
      • The ‘Mandate of Heaven’ DLC is enabled.
      • Is at war and
        • is the attacking leader
        • using the Unify China CB
      • +0.05 monthly Mandate

      Catholic Empire[edit | edit source]
      • has its capital in Europe.
      • is either Catholic , Protestant or Reformed.
      • is Catholic.
      • Catholic is the dominant faith of the Holy Roman Empire.
      • The Holy Roman Empire has an official, unchangeable faith.
      • +0.25 Yearly legitimacy
      • +1 Tolerance of the true faith
      • +1% Missionary strength
      • +25% Imperial authority growth modifier

      Protestant Empire[edit | edit source]
      • has its capital in Europe.
      • is either Catholic , Protestant or Reformed
      • is Protestant.
      • Protestant is the dominant faith of the Holy Roman Empire.
      • The Holy Roman Empire has an official, unchangeable faith.
      • +0.25 Yearly legitimacy
      • +1 Tolerance of the true faith
      • +1% Missionary strength
      • +25% Imperial authority growth modifier

      Reformed Empire[edit | edit source]
      • has its capital in Europe.
      • is either Catholic , Protestant or Reformed.
      • is Reformed.
      • Reformed is the dominant faith of the Holy Roman Empire.
      • The Holy Roman Empire has an official, unchangeable faith.
      • +0.25 Yearly legitimacy
      • +1 Tolerance of the true faith
      • +1% Missionary strength
      • +25% Imperial authority growth modifier

      Hussite Empire[edit | edit source]
      • has its capital in Europe.
      • is either Catholic, Protestant, Reformed, or Hussite.
      • is Hussite.
      • Hussite is the dominant faith of the Holy Roman Empire.
      • The Holy Roman Empire has an official, unchangeable faith.
      • +0.25 Yearly legitimacy
      • +1 Tolerance of the true faith
      • +1% Missionary strength
      • +25% Imperial authority growth modifier

      Robot Parts[edit | edit source]
      • Synthetics Defeated
      • Synthetics Defeated
      • −25% Technology cost

      The Golden Age of Piracy[edit | edit source]
      • The event The Golden Age of Piracy happened, but less than 50 years ago.
      • the country has discovered Jamaica Channel (1516).
      • The country's privateers have at least 1% trade power in the Caribbean trade node.
      • +20% Privateer efficiency

      Pope of the Empire[edit | edit source]
      • Papal State has joined the Holy Roman Empire
      • is catholic
      • is not part of the Holy Roman Empire
      • Papal State has joined the Holy Roman Empire
      • State Religion is Catholic
      • −0.5 Yearly prestige

      The Italian Wars
      • The event ‘The Italian Wars’ happened less than 50 years ago.
      • The country:
        • has its capital in Europe.
        • is Christian.
      • Either the country:
        • has its capital in Italy (region),
        • is at war with a country that:
          • is Christian,
          • has its capital in Europe,
          • has a total development of at least 150,
      • or the country:
        • does not have its capital in Italy (region),
        • has a total development of at least 150,
        • is at war with a country that:
          • has its capital in Italy (region).
      • −10% Mercenary cost
      • +5% Mercenary discipline

      Great Peasants War[edit | edit source]
      • has its capital in Europe.
      • The Great Peasants' War is active[5].
      • is part of the Holy Roman Empire.
      • is not a Peasants Republic[6].
      • +6 National unrest
