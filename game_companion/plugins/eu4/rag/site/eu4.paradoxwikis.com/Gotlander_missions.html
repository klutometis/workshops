Consolidate the KingdomEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
The Emperor's Treasury
The Kalmar Union is powerful and possess naval, military and economical power beyond what we can muster in Gotland.
Fortunately for us, the Emperor of the Holy Roman Empire has his own agenda against the Kalmar Union as the Duchy of Holstein is de jure part of the Empire, yet de facto a subject of the Union. We should make a deal with the Emperor for subsidies in our war efforts.
 At least one of:
  • The   Holy Roman Empire does not exist
  • Is the Holy Roman Emperor
  • The Holy Roman Emperor has at least   +100 opinion towards us
 If the   Holy Roman Empire exists:
  • If is not the Holy Roman Emperor:
    • Gets the   event “A Deal with the Emperor”
  • Else, if is the Holy Roman Emperor:
    • Gains   5 000 ducats

Else:

  • Gains   75 military power
 
The Enemy of My Enemy
The Kingdom of Denmark has made several enemies and rivals over the centuries. It is crucial for our survival to contact them and request an alliance with them.
 The country:
  • Is not at war
  • At least one of:
    •   Denmark does not exist
    • Any   rival or   enemy of   Denmark:
      • Has at least   +75 opinion towards us
 The country:
  • Gains   skill 1 Statesman advisor (75% cheaper to employ)

Every   rival or   enemy of   Denmark with at least   +75 opinion towards us:

  • Gets the   event “Plea for an Alliance from Gotland”
 
Dynastic Relations
The Kalmar Union is powerful and possess naval, military and economical power beyond what we can muster in Gotland.
We need to contact the relatives of [Root.Monarch.GetName] [Root.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName] of the main land and request military help.

Fortunately for us, there is little reason for them to deny such request. After all, the crown of Denmark is not only a victory for [Root.Monarch.GetName] but also for the entire [Root.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName] dynasty.
 At least one of:
  • No known country, which is not our subject:
    • Has our dynasty on their throne.
  • Any known country, which is not our subject:
    • Has our dynasty on their throne.
    • Has at least   +100 opinion towards us
 If we complete this mission while not sharing our dynasty:
  • Gains “Rise of a New Dynasty” for   25 years, giving:
    •   +100% Chance of new heir
    •   +2 Diplomatic reputation
    •   +1 Yearly legitimacy

Otherwise:

  • Gain   10 000 manpower per country sharing our dynasty and having at least   +100 opinion of us
 
Curtail the Estates
Eric Gryf, the previous king of the Kalmar Union, was deposed by treacherous nobles who lost their trust in Eric. We cannot allow our rulers to be deposed the same way. The estates' power must be limited!
 The country:
  • Has at least   40% crown land ownership

The   Nobility estate, if exists:

  • Has at least   60 loyalty
  • Has less than   50 influence

The   Burghers estate, if exists:

  • Has at least   60 loyalty
  • Has less than   50 influence

The   Clergy estate, if exists:

  • Has at least   60 loyalty
  • Has less than   50 influence
 The country gains “Curtailed Estates” for the rest of the game, giving:
  •   +10 Maximum absolutism
  •   −10% All estates influence modifier
 
Develop the City of Visby
The city of Visby is the main point of interest for anybody who visits Gotland. We should expand the city in any way possible in order to make it into the main port of the Baltic Sea.
 The country owns core province Gotland (25) which:
  • Has at least   15 development
 Province Gotland (25):
  • If it has at least a   Level 2 Center of Trade
    • Gain   2 base production.
  • Otherwise, gains a   Level 2 Center of Trade
  • Gains “Expanded Port of Visby” for   25 years, giving:
    •   +10 Trade value
    •   +50% Local ship repair
    •   −65% Local shipbuilding time

Restore the True KingEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
War for the Crown
The time has come to take what is rightfully ours!
The time has come to go to war with the Kalmar Union and end the false king's reign!
 At least one of:
  •   Denmark does not exist
  • Has any   casus belli against   Denmark
 The country:
  • Will not be able to switch missions type
  • Gains   15 army tradition
  • Gains   15 navy tradition
  • Gains “War against the Danish Power” for   25 years, giving:
    •   +10% Morale of armies
    •   +10% Morale of navies
  • If both our country and   Denmark are independent Christian monarchies:
    • Gains   'Restoration of Union' casus belli against   Denmark for   20 years
  • Else:
    • Gains a core on the following areas:
      • North Jutland
      • Denmark
      • Skåneland
 
Norwegian Trust
Following the the war against the Hansa in 1435, the Danish, Swedish and Norwegian nobility deposed Eric from the throne. But it is apparent that the current king of the Kalmar Union is lacking the competence of [Root.Monarch.GetName]. It is our duty to let the Norwegians know that they are led by a simpleton.
 At least one of:
  •   Norway does not exist
  •   Norway has at least   +75 opinion towards us
  • If   Norway is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.:
    • Has at least 25 spy network in   Norway
  • Else:
    • Has at least 25 spy network in   Norway's overlord
 If   Norway exists and is a subject other than ours:
  • Gets the   event “Restoration of Norwegian Loyalty”

Else:

  • Gains   100 administrative power
  • Gains   2 000 sailors
  • If   Norway exists:
    •   Norway and our coutry becomes mutual historical friends
 
Secure Swedish Loyalty
Sweden has never been an easy subject. It was the Swedish nobility which deposed Eric first when the war against the Hansa took a turn for the worst.
However, they are still part of the Kalmar Union, and as such it is in our best interest to regain their loyalty - either through diplomacy or through espionage.
 At least one of:
  •   Sweden does not exist
  •   Sweden has at least   +75 opinion towards us
  • If   Sweden is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.:
    • Has at least 50 spy network in   Norway
  • Else:
    • Has at least 50 spy network in   Norway's overlord
 If   Sweden exists:
  • Gets the   event “Restoration of Swedish Loyalty”

Else:

  • Gains   75 military power
 
Take over [DAN.Capital.GetName]
The city of [DAN.Capital.GetName] - the capital of the Kalmar Union; the capital our monarch used to rule from - is our most urgent war goal. If we want to retake the Kalmar Union for ourselves then we have to occupy [DAN.Capital.GetName].
 If   Denmark exists:
  •   Denmark's capital is controlled by our country or non-tributary subject

Else:

  • Has at least   +1 stability
 If   Denmark exists:
  • Gets the   event “The Occupation of Sjælland”

Else:

  • Loses   10 war exhaustion
  • Gains   2 stability
   War for the Crown
 
Execute the Wrong King
Eric Gryf, once great King of Denmark and sovereign ruler of Scandinavia... When the nobles of the Kalmar Union collectively decided to depose him, his nephew Christoph III von Wittelsbach took the throne of the Union. Ever since, a foreigner rules over the Scandinavian lands. Without finesse; without any sense of competence.

It is time to hang the false king in his own capital, and take over the throne of Denmark once and for all.
 At least one of:
  •   Denmark does not exist
  • All of:
    • Is at war against   Denmark for at least 3 years
    • Has at least   +50 warscore against   Denmark
    •   Denmark's capital is controlled by our country
  • All of:
    • Is not at war
    • At least one of:
      • Is the senior partner in a personal union with   Denmark
      • Owns core province Sjælland (12)
 If   Denmark exists:
  • If we complete this mission while having   Denmark as a junior partner or by being at war with   Denmark and controlling its capital:
    • Gets the   event “The Execution of [DAN.Monarch.GetName]”
  • Else, if we complete this mission while not being at war and Sjælland (12) being owned by the country or non-tributary subjects:
    • Becomes a   Kingdom, if is not an   Empire
    • Gains   100 prestige
    • If   Denmark is our subject:
      •   Denmark:
        • Loses   100% liberty desire
        • Gains “Danish Submission” for   15 years, giving:
          •   −100% Liberty desire
    • If both our country and targeted country are independent Christian monarchies:
      • Gains   'Restoration of Union' casus belli against   Denmark for   20 years
      • Gains   'Restoration of Union' casus belli against   Norway for   20 years
      • Gains   'Restoration of Union' casus belli against   Sweden for   20 years

Otherwise:

  • Gains   100 legitimacy
  • Gains   800 splendor
  • Gains   100 administrative power
  • Gains   100 diplomatic power
  • Gains   100 military power

  Norwegian Trust
  Take over [DAN.Capital.GetName]
  Secure Swedish Loyalty

 
Back in Control
It is time to drop the identity of Gotland as we are now the King of the Kalmar Union once again!
 The country:
  • Has at least   95 legitimacy
  • Has at least   +2 stability
  •   Denmark is our subject or does not exist
  •   Norway is our subject or does not exist
  •   Sweden is our subject or does not exist
 The country:
  • Gets the   event “Reformation of the Danish Crown”
   Execute the Wrong King

High Income
We must increase state income by any means necessary. Money is a requirement for any endeavor of the state, from the daily expenses of the government to the maintenance of armies to invade our enemies.
 The country has increased its   starting monthly income by at least 150% The country:
  • Gains a 50% cheaper skill 1   treasurer advisor of our culture and religion
  • Gains “Growing Economy” for   25 years, giving:

Build Buildings
Let us never sit idle, even in peaceful times. Instead we must use our resources with industry, in order to make sure that we have funds available in time if misfortune should strike upon us.

  • Owns at least 5 owned provinces with any Production building
 The country:
  • Gains   50 administrative power
  • Gains “Building Spree” for   25 years, giving:
    •   +10 Yearly tax income
   High Income
 
Build a Treasury
A large treasury is an imperative buffer for all things that could befall us through. If this is seen as miserly, it is because some do not realize that this vice is the very essence of what it means to reign.
 Country has at least   2 000 ducats The country:
  • Loses   2 corruption
  • Gains “Strong Treasury Department” for   25 years, giving:
    •   +0.10 Yearly inflation reduction
   Build Buildings
 
Build Manufactories
Great advances are being made in the field of production, both technological and organizational. Let us invest greatly in new manufactories, a great industrious revolution might soon be upon us.
 Country has at least 20 manufactories The country:
  • Gains   1.5 years of income
  • Gains “Proto-Industrialization” for   25 years, giving:
    •   +10% Production efficiency
   Build a Treasury
 
Imperial Conquest
Securing wealth must always be the foremost priority of our state. Let us make use of the expensive armies and fleets that our economic prosperity has bought us to acquire even more resources and subjects for an aspiring empire.
 Country has at least   1 000 total development The country:
  • Gains   30 convertible[2] prestige
  • Gains “Imperial Ambition” for   25 years, giving:
    •   −5 Years of separatism
  • Gains a permanent claim on every neighboring area
   Build Manufactories

Consolidate the tradeEdit

Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
 
Imperial Protection
The Kingdoms of Scandinavia seek our subjugation. They outnumber our defenses and possess more ships of war than we could ever muster with the resources we have. Our only hope of overcoming our precarious situation is to join the Holy Roman Empire at all costs.
 At least one of:
  • The   Holy Roman Empire does not exist
  • The Holy Roman Emperor has at least   +100 opinion towards us
 The country:
  • Gains a permanent claim on:
    • Hinterpommern area
    • Vorpommern area
    • Province Riga (38)
  • If the   Holy Roman Empire exists:
    • Joins the Holy Roman Empire
  • Else:
    • Gains   75 administrative power
    • Gains   75 diplomatic power
 
Great Trade Federation
The Baltic Sea is known for its commercial activities. For the sake of our economy it is more than important that we found our own trade enterprise in the Baltic Sea so we can benefit from the trade here.
 At least one of:
  • Is the leader of a trade league
  • Owns at least 3 provinces which either:
    • Has a   river estuary
    • Has a   center of trade
    • Has province modifier “Sound Toll”
 The country:
  • Gains   5% mercantilism
  • Gains trade modifier “Growth of the Gutnish Trade” (  +20 Trade Power) for   25 years in Baltic Sea and Lübeck trade nodes
   Imperial Protection
 
Lead the Baltic Trade
We have expanded our influence. Now it is time to put it to good use to develop Visby and establish a Baltic monopoly.
 The country:
  • Controls at least   20% of the trade in the Baltic Sea trade node
  • Owns core province Gotland (25) which:
    • Has at least   15 development
 Province Gotland (25) gains “Investment into Visby” for the rest of the game, giving:
  •   +5 Trade value
  •   −10% Local development cost

The country gains a permanent claim on the following areas:

  • Mecklenburg
  • Schleswig-Holstein
   Great Trade Federation
 
Compete with the Hansa
The Hansa is our greatest competitor in the Baltic Sea when it comes to the trade. We should end its dominance once and for all and become the new replacement of the Hansa.
 The country:
  • Owns or non-tributary subjects own province Lübeck (45)
  • Controls at least   15% of the trade in the Lübeck trade node
 The country:
  • Gains   5% mercantilism
  • Gains skill   3 Trader advisor (90% cheaper to employ)
  • Gains “Lübecker Trade Fleet” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +25% Ship trade power
   Lead the Baltic Trade
 
Channel's Trade
Although the wealth is plentiful in the Lübeck trade, our English naval neighbors make a fortune from their control of the English Channel. We should challenge their monopoly of the channel and earn our fair share of the Channel's wealth for ourselves.
 At least one of:
  • Controls at least   25% of the trade in the English Channel trade node
  • Has at least   25% trade power from privateering in the English Channel trade node
 The country:
  • Gains   10% mercantilism
  • Gains trade modifier “Gutnish Traders” (  +25 Trade Power) for the rest of the game in the English Channel trade node
   Compete with the Hansa

Gutnish Trade Fleet
We are an island nation which mostly lives from commercial success. It is imperative that we construct a fleet which serves as both our naval defense and as our merchants on the sea.

  • Has at least   10 light ships
 If the   Aristocrats faction is in power:

Strong Mercantilisim
To truly dominate the trade of the Baltic Sea we must ensure that our internal factions are pleased and that competent advisors hold positions which allow them to maximize our profits.
 The country:

  • If has the merchant republic factions:

 If the   Aristocrats faction is in power:

Else, if the   Traders faction is in power:

  • Gains “Trader Reign” for   25 years, giving:

  • Gains “Trader Reign” for   25 years, giving:

Gotland's Trade Empire
Our mighty commercial empire is poised to exert a monopoly over trade and power in the Baltic Sea. None who is not approved by our merchants shall have any presence in the Baltic Sea!
 The country:
  • Controls at least   70% of the trade in the Baltic Sea trade node
  • Has at least   4 merchants
  • Has a navay of at least   60 Naval forcelimit
  • If is the leader of a trade league:
    • Has at least 7 trade league members
  • Else:
    • Has at least   200 total development
 If the   Aristocrats faction is in power:

    •   +5% Discipline
    •   +10% Ship durability
    •   +0.1 Monthly aristocrats influence

Else, if the   Traders faction is in power:

  • Gains “Traders' Commercial Focus” for the rest of the game, giving:

    •   +0.1 Monthly traders influence

Else, if the   Guilds faction is in power:

  • Gains “Guilds' Bureaucratic Focus” for the rest of the game, giving:

    •   +0.1 Monthly guilds influence

Otherwise:

  • Gains “Traders' Commercial Focus” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +25% Global trade power

Hire the Mercenaries
Although we [Root.GetAdjective] people are proud of our state and defend it to the last man, we are still just an island state. We need professional soldiers to defend us from the Scandinavian threat. We need competent mercenaries!
 The country:
  • Has hired at least 2 mercenary companies
 The country:
  • Gains   5% army professionalism
  • Gains   10 army tradition
  • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
    • Denmark
    • North Jutland
    • Skåneland
 
Take the Sound Toll
No other Danish achievement is as vile as the Sound Toll. Thanks to it, it is virtually impossible to trade with the rest of Europe as long as they have to pay a fee to the Danes. We should do the world a favor and take the Sound Toll for ourselves. This way their Ducats will at least go into our own coffers instead of the Danish ones.
 The country:
  • Owns core province Sjælland (12)
 The country:
  • Gains   ducats equal to 2 years of income
  • Set country flag dan_completed_mission_dan_the_sound_toll

Province Sjælland (12):

  • Builds   Coastal Defence
  • Builds   Marketplace
  • Gains “Sound Toll Protection” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +3 Local trade power
    •   +3 Hostile fleet attrition
    •   +2 Possible number of buildings
    •   −5% Cost to promote mercantilism
    • This modifier is lost in the following conditions are not fulfil:
      • Has any coastal defence buildings
      • Has any trade buildings
      • Does not have country flag dan_completed_mission_dan_the_sound_toll

The Novgorodians intend to dominate the Baltic trade for themselves, letting us suffocate in poverty. We cannot allow that to happen!
The only reasonable way to stop that is through warfare. All we need is just any reason to wage war against them.
 Province Neva (33) is owned by either:
  • Our country or non-tributary subject
  • Any country which we have any   casus belli against
 The country gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
  • South Karelia
  • Novgorod
 
Kontor in Russia
Although Novgorod is a commercial rival of us, their capital of Novgorod is a vital center of trade for our merchants, which intend to make a living with the Russian trade. The city, however, is threatened by the ambitious Russian principalities. We must ensure that our dominance over the city's markets is unchallenged.
 At least one of:
  • Owns core province Novgorod (310) which:
    • Has province modifier “Trading Post”
  • Province Novgorod (310) is owned by a member of our trade league
 The country:
  • Gains   5% mercantilism
  • Gains trade modifier “Gutnish Control over Novgorod” (  +50 Trade Power) for   20 years in the Novgorod trade node
