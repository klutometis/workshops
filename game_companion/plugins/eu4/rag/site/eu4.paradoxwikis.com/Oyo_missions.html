      Cavalry Missions[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.32.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Trade with the Muslims
      Trade has always been prominent in our area, especially that led by the Muslims through Timbuktu and Gao. We must not fall behind them if we are to profit, we need to also be active participants in the trade flow and be able to control it.
      		 At least one of:
      • Oyo owns a Center of Trade in the Timbuktu Trade Node.
      • Any owner of a Center of Trade in the Timbuktu Trade Node has at least 90 opinion or is a subject of Oyo.

      Oyo has at least 50 ducats

      		Oyo:
      • If the mission is finished diplomatically:
        • Gets the event “Purchase of Horses”
      • Else:
        • Oyo gains country modifier “Access to Horses” for 25 years, giving the following effect:
          • −25% Cavalry cost
      Cavalry for [Root.GetName]
      Thanks to our trade with the Muslim nations, we have now access to horses, a resource until now uncommon in our country. We must also form cavalry regiments, in the likes of the ones the Muslim countries have.
      		 Oyo:
      • Has at least 4 cavalry regiments
      • Is employing any Military advisor
      		 Oyo enables the "Oyo Cavalry" Mercenary Company. Trade with the Muslims
      Chaaarge!!
      Now that we have cavalry regiments, it is time to put them to use. We can use their superior maneuverability to quickly snatch the enemy's capital and utterly crush them. We shall do precisely that.
      		 The capital of a Rival, an Enemy, a Great Power or a country with more total development than Oyo is under our control while a cavalry unit is located in said capital. Oyo:
      • Gain +40 power projection
      • Hidden effect: Does not lose army professionalism when recruiting the Mercenary company “Oyo Cavalry”.
      		 Cavalry for [Root.GetName]

      Imperial Missions[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.32.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Growth of Power
      We, the Oyo, are a great nation. But we can always become even greater. We need to expand our glorious nation so that it can be the envy of the world.
      		 Oyo:
      • Has increased Total develoment by at least 75
      • Owns at least 10 provinces
      		 Oyo:
      • Gains 100 administrative power
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Dendi
        • Upper Volta
        • Katsina
      		 Appease the Oyo Mesi

      Appease the Ogboni

      Develop the Capital
      Our capital is the shining jewel of our country, and its symbol. We must develop it into a true bustling city that clearly shows its importance.
      		 Country's capital
      • Is controlled by Oyo
      • Has at least 15 Development
      		 Oyo:
      • Gains ducats equal to 5 years of income from Production of the capital

      If country's capital does not have a Center of Trade

      • Create a level 2 center of trade

      Else, if country's capital has a level 1 center of trade

      • Upgrade Center of Trade level by 1

      Else:

      • Gains 3 base production
      		 Growth of Power
      Reform the Government.
      Our old tribal traditions have become too small for our great ambitions. We must reform our government into a glorious state if we are to stand among the giants of the world.
      		 Government is not tribal Oyo unlocks Nobility privilege “Grant Power to the Bashorun”
      Imperial Oyo
      Oyo has always been an Empire in our eyes. The time has come for it to become one in the eyes of everyone. We need to show true strength if we are to deserve to be called such.
      		 At least one of:
      • Oyo has at least 6 subjects
      • Oyo has at least 300 total development
      		 Oyo:
      • Becomes an Empire
      • Gains country modifier “Oyo Empire” for the rest of the game, giving the following effects:
        • −0.5% Prestige decay
        • +0.5 Yearly legitimacy
        • +0.2 Yearly republican tradition
        • +0.5 Yearly devotion
        • +0.5 Yearly meritocracy
        • +0.5 Yearly horde unity
      		 Reform the Government.

      Gather Cults Eradicate the Muslims

      Ogboni Missions[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.32.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Appease the Ogboni
      The Ogboni have always been an influential part of our government. Apart from their involvement in religious matters, they also have influence in the appointment of rulers. As such, we need to make sure they are on our side.
      		 Clergy estate has at least 60 loyalty or does not exist country's capital:
      • Build a Temple

      If has completed the mission “Appease the Oyo Mesi”

      • Oyo gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Benin
        • Lower Niger
        • Zazzau
        • Atacora Oueme
        • Lower Volta
      Unite the Fetishists
      Our beliefs have always been an integral part of our identity. We need to convince the people around us to share the same vision we have.
      		 At least 15 provinces:
      • Is in Niger region
      • Is owned by Oyo or its non-tributary subjects
      • Has our religion
      • Oyo gets the event “The Quest for a new Cult”
      		 Appease the Ogboni
      Gather Cults
      Now that a lot more people share our beliefs, we are more keenly aware that there are subtle differences among their shamans. We need to collect these differences to be able to have a unified true vision.
      • Oyo has unlocked at least 6 cults
      • Enables "Choose a new Cult" decision

      Oyo gains country modifier “Shaman Gathering” for 25 years, giving the following effects:

      • −2 National unrest
      • +1 Tolerance of the true faith
      • −10% Stability cost modifier
      		 Develop the Capital

      Unite the Fetishists

      Mesi Missions[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.32.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Appease the Oyo Mesi
      The Oyo Mesi, our council of nobles, have always been an integral part of our government. No ruler can be enthroned without their approval, and they can also remove him from the throne if they deem him too tyrant. We must keep them happy with our rule.
      		 Nobility has at least 60 loyalty or does not exist Oyo:
      • Gains a general with 40 army tradition

      If has completed the mission “Appease the Ogboni”

      • Oyo gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Benin
        • Lower Niger
        • Zazzau
        • Atacora Oueme
        • Lower Volta
      Push North
      Unlike the dense jungles to the South, the lush plains of the Sahel to the North are a great source of wealth. Therefore, the only sensible choice we have is to expand northwards.
      		 At least 8 provinces in the following region are owned by Oyo or its non-tributary subjects
      • Sahel
      		 Oyo gets permanent claims on every province in the following regions which are not of our faith:
      • Sahel
      • Niger
      • Guinea
      		 Appease the Oyo Mesi

      Growth of Power

      Eradicate the Muslims
      The Muslims around us have ceased to serve their purpose for us and have started to become a nuisance. We must get rid of them completely to make space for our own glorious expansion.
      		 Every province in the following regions, which is NOT uncolonized, is either owned by a subject of Oyo or follows our faith.
      • Sahel
      • Niger
      • Guinea
      		 Oyo gains +25 power projection Push North

      African missions[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Slave Trade
      Slavery is ubiquitous in Africa. We should either monopolize the slave trade or seek to end the barbaric practice entirely.
      		 One of the following must be true:
      • Is the production leader of Slaves and owns at least 4 provinces producing Slaves.
      • Has enacted the decision “Pass Abolition of Slavery Act” and does not have a neighboring country with a province producing Slaves
      		 If the country is the production leader of Slaves, then:
      • Gains 2 Mercantilism
      • Gets “Slave Trade Monopoly” for 20 years, giving:
        • +10% Trade efficiency

      Otherwise, gets “Abolitionism” for 20 years, giving:

      • −3 National unrest
      African Modernization
      European colonialism threatens to wipe out our nation if we do not adapt rapidly. We must have the technology and state apparatus to challenge them.
      		 The country:
      • Is not a Tribe nor an Iqta
      • Has any Western neighboring country which is less than 1 technology ahead
      		 The country:
      • Gains 20 Prestige
      • Gains 3 Innovativeness
      Wealth of Africa
      Africa is rich in gold, coffee, and ivory. We should ensure that we control at least our fair share of these valuable goods so that we might establish economic independence.
      		 One of the following must be true:
      • Owns at least 3 provinces producing Gold
      • Is the production leader of Ivory and owns at least 7 provinces producing Ivory
      • Is the production leader of Coffee
      		 The country gets “Wealth of Africa” for 20 years, giving:
      • +10% Production Efficiency
      • +0.10 Yearly Inflation Reduction
