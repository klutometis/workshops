      French Wars of Religion
      France is being torn apart by a string of religious civil wars.

      The French Wars of Religion[1] is a disaster in France during the Age of Reformation.

      Prerequisites[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      It is currently the Age of Discovery or Age of Reformation and the country:

      • is France
      • is Catholic, Protestant, Reformed, Anglican or Hussite.
      • has not been force-converted.
      • did not have the Religious Turmoil disaster.
      • did not have this disaster before.

      If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

      Monthly progress starts if Monthly progress halts if
      France:
      • does not have an ongoing disaster.
      • has less than 90% religious unity.
      • has not completed the Humanist idea group.
      • owns a European province that:
        • is Catholic, Protestant, Reformed, Anglican or Hussite
        • but not following the state religion.

      It is currently the Age of Reformation.

      Monthly progress France
      +2.0% has a hostile center of reformation.
      +2.0% has religious unity of less than 80%.
      +1.0% has religious unity of at least 75 % and less than 90%.
      +1.0% has Religious ideas.
      +1.0% has stability of less than 1.
      		at least one of the following is true: France:
      • has an ongoing disaster.
      • has at least 90% religious unity.
      • has completed the Humanist idea group.

      Wars of Religion in France

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The description below is one of several available for this event.
      The following description is used if the religion is Catholic

      With the [religion_province.Religion.GetName] faith spreading like wildfire among the population and their leaders growing increasingly bold, many Catholics are becoming uneasy at the situation in [Root.GetName]. Rising tensions between the two groups have come to a head with the formation of the [religion_province.Religion.GetName] League, a paramilitary organization that seeks to install a [religion_province.Religion.GetName] [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] on the throne of [Root.GetName]. Failing that, they demand significant rights and freedoms to safeguard their future. For their part, leaders of the Catholic community warn against making concessions, hinting that violence would follow should the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] be seen to turn [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] back on the Pope's teachings. Either way, turmoil awaits us.

      What should be our course of action?

      The following description is used if the religion is not Catholic

      There is widespread anger among French Catholics at the conversion of the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] to the [Root.Religion.GetName] faith. Resenting the sweeping changes in our nation and fearing for their future, they have formed a paramilitary organization they call the Catholic League in order to demand that the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] once again bows to the Pope - or failing that, that they are given significant rights and freedoms to safeguard their future. For their part, leaders of the [Root.Religion.GetName] community warn against making concessions, hinting that violence would follow should the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] be seen to favor the Catholics. Either way, turmoil awaits us.

      What should be our course of action?''
       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      the start of the ‘French Wars of Religion’ disaster.

      France is and will always be Catholic.

      France:

      • gets the modifier “French Catholics” for 20 years with the following effects:
        • −2 tolerance of heretics,
        • +1 tolerance of the true faith,
        • +1% global missionary strength,
        • +3 national unrest.
      • If France is Catholic:
        • loses 1 stability.
      • Else,
        • loses 3 stability.
        • changes religion to Catholic
      Catholicism has no place in France.

      If France is Protestant:

      • loses 1 stability.

      Else,

      • loses 3 stability.
      • if Reformed is the dominant faith or no owned province is Protestant:
        • changes religion to Reformed
        • gets the modifier “French Reformers” for 20 years with the following effects:
          • −2 tolerance of heretics,
          • +1 tolerance of the true faith,
          • +1% global missionary strength,
          • +3 national unrest.
      • else,
        • changes religion to Protestant
        • gets the modifier “French Protestants” for 20 years with the following effects:
          • −2 tolerance of heretics,
          • +1 tolerance of the true faith,
          • +1% global missionary strength,
          • +3 national unrest.
      We should seek to avoid bloodshed between religions.

      France:

      • loses 2 stability.
      • gets the modifier “Supporting French Moderates” for 20 years with the following effects:
        • +1 tolerance of heretics.

      Inspired by treasonous anti-royalist tracts, the religious fanatic [Root.fra_royal_assassin.GetName] has murdered the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]! The assassin entered the royal camp as a humble friar, claiming that he wished to bless the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle], but instead drew a knife and thrust it into the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]’s spleen, fatally wounding [Root.Monarch.GetHerHim]. [Root.fra_royal_assassin.GetName] was immediately executed, but his crime will change the course of French history forever.

      [Root.Monarch.GetName] had no legitimate heir, and so the throne passes to the distantly related House of Bourbon. Who among the Bourbons is to succeed is a controversial matter: the moderates and Huguenots support the Protestant Henri, while zealous Catholics support his uncle Charles, a respected Cardinal of the Catholic Church.
