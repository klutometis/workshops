Heir needed in $COUNTRY$

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.36.

The description below is one of several available for this event.
As we currently have no designated heir for $MONARCHTITLE$ $MONARCH$, we have several options to pick from.

 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Does not have the "Papacy" government
  • Can have heir
  • Did not had the Scandinavia's event "The Educational Focus of our Ruler"
 Is triggered only by

A theocracy needing an heir

Option conditions

 Enabled if: Does not have The Cologne Secundogeniture

A Local Noble

The country:

  • Loses 5 devotion
  • Gains 10 prestige
  • Gain a new heir with the following attributes:
    • Aged 35 years
    • Of the same culture as province viable for Nobles
    • Is female if has 100% Female Advisor Chance
  • If has the "Theocrats vs. Monarchists" mechanic:
    • “The Monarchists will be in power when this heir becomes our ruler.”
    • Strengthen Monarchists by 15%
  • If the Nobility estate exists:
    • Gains 15 Nobility loyalty
  • Else, if is Hindu and the Marathas estate exists:
    • Gains 15 Marathas loyalty
  • Else, if is Hindu and the Rajputs estate exists:
    • Gains 15 Rajputs loyalty
  • If the Clergy estate exists:
    • Loses 15 Clergy loyalty
  • Else, if is Hindu and the Brahmins estate exists:
    • Loses 15 Brahmins loyalty

 Enabled if:

  • Does not have The Cologne Secundogeniture
  • Has any random neighbouring monarchy which is not in a regency and follows our religion
A Foreign Noble

  • Gains opinion modifier “Same Dynasty as Heir” ( +50 opinion / −1 yearly decay / 50 years) towards us
  • Provides an heir

Too Much Religious Freedom

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.36.

One of your advisors has asked you to consider the matter of religious freedom a bit more thoroughly. They are claiming that having a large group of people that doesn't adhere to our state religion is having a severe impact on our national stability.

 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Did not have this event before
  • Is a theocracy
  • Does not have the "Papacy" government
  • Owns any heretic or heathen province
  • Does not own any province with an active missionary
  • Does not have country modifiers “Religious Tolerance” or “Religious Intolerance”
  • Employs any advisor following our faith
 Mean time to happen

180 months
Modifiers

  • Is the Defender of the Faith: ×0.9
  • Has idea “Divine Supremacy”: ×0.9
  • Has Theologian advisor: ×0.9
  • Has a level 3 Theologian advisor: ×0.9
Take their advice under consideration

The country:

  • If the Clergy and Dhimmi estate exist:
    • Gains 10 clergy loyalty
  • If the Dhimmi estate exists:
    • Loses 10 dhimmi loyalty
  • Gains the “Religious Restrictions” modifier for 2 years, giving:
    • +1% Missionary strength
    • +15% Stability cost modifier
It's not of any consequence

The country:

  • If the Clergy and Dhimmi estate exist:
    • Loses 10 clergy loyalty
  • If the Dhimmi estate exists:
    • Gains 10 dhimmi loyalty

40% chance of: Rebels (size 1) rise in revolt in a random owned heretic or heathen province

Problematic Relations

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.36.

It has become a real issue for you that some of your neighbors refuse to accept the one true faith. Your relations are already far from ideal but this only makes matters worse.

 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Did not have this event before
  • Is a theocracy
  • Neighbours any country which:
    • Has less than −50 opinion towards us
    • Does not follow our religion
    • Saved as targeted country
 Mean time to happen

180 months
Modifiers

  • Is the Defender of the Faith: ×0.9
  • Has idea “Divine Supremacy”: ×0.9
  • Has Theologian advisor: ×0.9
  • Has a level 3 Theologian advisor: ×0.9
  • Ruler's military skill is at least 7: ×0.9[2]
  • Ruler's military skill is at least 8: ×0.9
  • Ruler's military skill is at least 9: ×0.9
  • Ruler's military skill is less than 4: ×1.2
Make your opinion clear to them

The country:

  • Loses 10 papal influence

Targeted country:

  • Gains opinion modifier “Forceful Conversion” ( −50 opinion / +2 yearly decay / 10 years) towards us
Make preparations for a future attack

The country:

  • Gains 10 army tradition
  • Loses 40 ducats
