      Qing missions

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      For pre Domination missions, see Qing missions without Domination

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

      		Available only with the Domination DLC enabled.

      This is a list of all missions for Qing[1]. Some of these are shared with the Manchu nations. By completing the mission Globalization of Trade with the Embrace Free Trade government reform, the country gets the open ports mission branch. Completing the mission without that reform unlocks the closed ports mission branch.

      Traditional Jurchen hunting parties have served us well in conflicts against our rivals, but if we ever wish to conquer an empire we must reorganize our forces into disciplined Banner regiments.
      		 The country:

      The basis of a people is their language. Many of our people do not possess the ability to write or speak the language of the Chinese Emperor. However, time and time again our kin has been raided by foreign conquerors. Torn from our elders, we were made to speak foreign languages. With each new dynasty and conqueror our masters changed along with the words they made us speak. With each change, we changed too. Our thoughts, personalities and how we viewed right from wrong. Thus, to cement our place and shield ourselves from foreign influences, our people need a common tongue.

      • Has embraced all the active institutions

      • Is not running a deficit

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Cornerstone of an Empire
      A nation whose borders encompass such vast swathes of land cannot but depend on few powerful figures within our courtyard. Thus, to ensure proper stability and operational integrity, we must discern the best way forward and distribute power accordingly.
      		 The country:
      • At least one of:
        • All of:
          • Has imposed restrictions on the Eunuchs in "The Eunuchs of the [Root.GetName] Empire" event
          • Is employing every type of advisor
          • Has at least 40% crown land ownership
        • All of:
          • Has at least 60 eunuchs loyalty
          • Has at least 5 eunuchs privileges
      		 The country:
      • If completes the mission by empowering the Eunuchs:
        • Gains “Increased Eunuch Influence” for 50 years, giving:
          • +10% Eunuchs influence
          • +15% Reform progress growth
          • +0.1 Yearly corruption
      • Else:
        • If is the Emperor of China:
          • Enacting the "Expand Palace Bureaucracy" decree will also grant −20% advisor cost
        • Else:
          • Gains 10% crown land ownership
          • If has a stability of less than +3,
            • then it gains 1 stability,
            • else it gains 50 administrative power.

      		 The country:

      • Has +3 stability
      • Has less than 5 average unrest in provinces
      • Owns at least 15 provinces with any Courthouse building

      • Gets "The Great [Root.GetName]'s Supervisory Agency" event

      Reform Civil Registration

        • Every “Population Census” decree will also grant +1 monthly administrative power

          • Loses 150 government reform progress
          • Gains the Reformist ruler's personality trait
        • Else:
          • Gains 1 ruler administrative skill
          • Loses 100 government reform progress

      Mutual Trade System
      Projecting our strength and exchanging gifts, favors and coin for protection is a relatively efficient and extensively profitable relationship between our great dynasty and the many satellite countries across our sphere of influence.       		 The country:
      • Has saved up at least 150 government reform progress
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least 5 tributaries that have less than 10% liberty desire and at least +120 opinion of us

        • Gets the “Evolution of the Tributary System” event
