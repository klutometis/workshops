      The Brahmins are the priestly caste of Hinduism. They are the keepers of ceremonies and scriptures, as few non-Brahmins are educated in Sanskrit literature. They command great respect from Hindus and non-Hindus alike, and sometimes take roles in government.

      Availability[edit | edit source]

      General Availability:

      • Available for Hindu or Sikh nations, replacing their Clergy estate
      • Available for Muslim nations with the Indian technology group, replacing their Dhimmi estate

      Specific Conditions:

      • Does not use other mechanics
      • Is not the United States unless government is a theocracy
      • Is not nomad
      • Has not enacted the decision Establish the Dhimmi Estate

      Without Dharma, countries that meet these conditions have an estate named "Brahmins", but it is the ordinary Clergy estate by a different name.

      Influence[edit | edit source]

      Influence – The estate has a base influence of 0%, modified by:

      • +20% If Hindu
      • +10% If non-Hindu with majority Hindu provinces
      • +5% If non-Hindu with at least one Hindu province (but not majority)
      • +10% If a Hindu Theocracy
      • +10% If Hindu and has enacted the Religious Values tier 2 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the Expanded Royal Court tier 3 government reform
      • +10% If has enacted the Expand Brahmin Privileges tier 4 government reform
      • +10% If Hindu and has enacted the Administrative Clergy tier 4 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the General Estates tier 6 government reform
      • −15% If Hindu and does not have a Hindu majority
      • −5% Has enacted the Royal Favoritism tier 3 government reform
      • −10% Has enacted the Dynastic Administration tier 5 government reform
      • −5% Has enacted the Subservient Bureaucrats tier 5 government reform
      • −5% Has enacted the Les Six livres de la République tier 8 government reform
      • −40% If another estate has seized control of the nation (via disaster)

      Loyalty[edit | edit source]

      Loyalty – The estate's loyalty equilibrium is modified by:

      • +10% Has completed the Divine idea group
      • +5% If Hindu and has enacted the Religious Values tier 2 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the Decentralized Bureaucracy tier 3 government reform
      • +10% If has enacted the Expand Brahmin Privileges tier 4 government reform
      • +5% If Hindu and has enacted the Administrative Clergy tier 4 government reform
      • +5% Has enacted the Maintain Balance of Power tier 4 government reform
      • +10% Has enacted the General Estates tier 6 government reform
      • +10% Has enacted the Supremacy of the Diet tier 6 government reform
      • +10% If Hindu and has enacted the Safeguard Holy Sites tier 8 government reform
      • +10% If Hindu and has completed the Religious idea group
      • +10% If Muslim and has completed the Humanist idea group
      • −10% to +10% with current piety (inversely proportional)
      • −2.5% to +2.5% If Hindu with current devotion
      • −5% to +5% with current prestige
      • -5% Has enacted the Centralized Bureaucracy tier 3 government reform
      • −20% If another estate has seized control of the nation (via disaster)

      Rebels: If the Brahmins are disloyal after land has been seized, Hindu Zealot rebels may rise in revolt in a few provinces.

      Disaster: If the Brahmins estate reaches 100 influence, their estate disaster will begin ticking and – unless lowered – will eventually trigger the Dominance of the Brahmins disaster.
