      National missions[edit | edit source]

      Develop Java[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.31.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Consider Islam
      The Islamic faith is spreading rapidly throughout Nusantara. We should learn more about this faith and consider whether it is right for us.
      		 The country:
      • Owns at least 4 Sunni provinces
      		 If the country is not Muslim:
      • Gets the event "The Sunni Faith"

      Else:

      • Gains 20 prestige
      Develop New Cities
      As times change, cities rise and fall. We must develop new centers of trade and population, so that they may become seats of power for our nation.
      		 The country:
      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • Is not at war

      Provinces Banten (624), Cirebon (4791) and Kalapa (630):

      • Are owned by our country
      • Have at least 20 Development
      • Follow our religion
      		 Province Kalapa (630):
      • Is renamed 'Jayakerta'
      • Povincial capital is renamed 'Jayakerta'

      Province Cirebon (4791):

      • Creates a level 1 center of trade

      If is not Banten nor Cirebon:

      • Gets the event "A New Capital, A New [Root.GovernmentName]"

      Else, if is Banten:

      • Province Banten (624):
        • Gains 2 base tax
        • Gains 2 base production
        • Gains 2 base manpower

      Else, if is Cirebon:

      • Province Cirebon (4791):
        • Gains 2 base tax
        • Gains 2 base production
        • Gains 2 base manpower
      		 Consider Islam
      Rebuild Candi Temples
      Java is littered with Candi temples, disgracefully allowed to fall into decay and disuse. We must set ourselves to the task of rebuilding and reclaiming these temples.
      		 The country:
      • Has at least 15 provinces with the latest available Tax building
      • Is allied to any country which:
        • Has at least 100 total development
        • Is Buddhist

      If Clergy estate exists:

      • Clergy has at least 65 loyalty
      		 If DLC 'Wealth of Nations' is active and is Hindu:
      • Unlocks the "Buddha" personal deity

      Else:

      • Gets “Religious Peace” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +10% Religious unity
      Restore Borobudur
      Majapahit has allowed the great temple of Borobudur to fall into ruin. We must restore it.
      		 If DLC 'Leviathan' is active:
      • Is Theravada
      • Province Kotagede's Great project "Borobudur Temple" is Magnificent

      Else:

      • Has at least 10 provinces with a Cathedral
      		 If DLC 'Leviathan' is active:
      • Gets “Borobudur Restored” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −25% Great project upgrade cost

      Else:

      • Gets “Candi Shrine Construction” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −10% Construction cost
      		 Rebuild Candi Temples
      Improve Rice Production
      An army marches on its stomach. We must improve our rice production to ensure a healthy populace and a well-supplied army.
      		 The country:
      • Has at least 5 provinces with a Farm Estate
      		 If Burghers estate exists
      • Grants the estate privilege “Advanced Irrigation Techniques”

      Else:

      • Gains 100 diplomatic power

      Indonesian conquest[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.33.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Legacy of Bubat
      Many years ago we were betrayed by Majapahit, leading to the terrible Battle of Bubat. Our relations have since soured, perhaps beyond repair. Perhaps the only way to ensure our safety is to subject Majapahit to our rule.
      		 At least one of:
      • Majapahit does not exist
      • Majapahit is our subject
      • All of:
        • Has guaranteed Majapahit
        • If DLC 'Leviathan' is active:
          • Majapahit:
            • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
            • Has “The Fall of Majapahit” disaster ongoing
      		 If Majapahit is our subject or is guaranteed by us:
      • Gets the event "A Momentous Wedding"

      Else:

      • Gains a permanent claim on:
        • East Java area
        • Central Java area
        • Surabaya area
      Rule Java
      All of Java must be incorporated into the Sunda Kingdom.
      		 The following areas are owned by our country:
      • East Java area
      • Central Java area
      • West Java area
      • Surabaya area
      • Banten area
      		 The country gets “Rulers of Java” for the rest of the game, giving:
      • +1 Administrative free policies
      		 Legacy of Bubat
      Conquer Lampung
      Southern Sumatra is rich in pepper production, and would make an excellent target for our expansion.
      		 At least 3 provinces in Lampung area are owned by our country If any known 'Sumatran' or 'Acehnese' country is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • Gains 'Subjugation' casus belli against every previously mentioned country for 10 years

      Else:

      • Gains a permanent claim on:
        • Aceh area
        • Riau area
        • Lampung area
        • Jambi area
      Subjugate Sumatra
      The petty Sultans of Sumatra are disunited and unready for a true war. We should strike before they manage to unify.
      		 At least 12 provinces in the following areas are owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Aceh
      • Riau
      • Lampung
      • Jambi
      		 The country:
      • Gains 100 administrative power
      • Gains 100 diplomatic power
      • Gains 100 military power
      		 Conquer Lampung


      Indonesian missions[edit | edit source]

      Control trade[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.32.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      [Root.GetAdjective] Navy
      It is all but impossible to lead campaigns of conquest on foot in this region. We must maintain a powerful fleet of galleys and enough transport ships to carry our soldiers.
      		 Country has at least 30 galley ships Country gets “Galley Warfare” for 20 years, giving the following effect:
      • +20% Galley combat ability
      Trade Dominance
      In Nusantara the success of an empire is measured in its ability to control trade.
      		 If DLC Dharma is active:
      • Owns any province with center of trade of at least level 3

      Our country controls at least 60% of the trade in the home trade node

      		Country gets “Indonesia Trade Dominance” for 20 years, giving the following effect:
      • +15% Trade efficiency
      The Spice Must Flow
      We must achieve complete dominance over the spice trade, both in terms of production and distribution.
      		 Country has the trading in bonus for Spices

      Country is the leading producer of Spices

      		Country gets “The Spice Must Flow” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +5% Goods produced modifier
      		 Trade Dominance
      Trade Supremacy
      The Straits of Malacca shall become far more than one stop along the maritime silk road. We shall make our homeland into the very center of global commerce.
      		 Country is Malaya

      Malacca trade node:

      • Is the highest valued trade node in the world
      • Our country is the strongest trade power in this node
      		 Country gains 10% mercantilism The Spice Must Flow

      Dominate the islands[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.32.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Vassal Kingdoms
      We should not expand only through direct conquest. We should also subjugate other kingdoms and collect tribute from them.
      		 Country has at least 3 vassals which:
      • Liberty desire is less than 50%
      		 Country gets “Extracting Tribute” for 20 years, giving the following effect:
      • +100% Income from vassals
      Religious Conversion
      The religious landscape of Nusantara is changing, and not necessarily in our interests. We must ensure that the people of our region follow the true faith.
      		 It is not the Age of Discovery

      Religion is not in Pagan group
      All provinces in the following areas have same religion as our country or are open for colonization:

      • If capital is in Kutai, Brunei, Kalimantan, Banjar or Sabah areas
        • All provinces in above areas
      • If capital is in East Java, Central Java, West Java, Lesser Sunda Islands, Surabaya or Banten areas
        • All provinces in above areas
      • If capital is in Aceh, Riau, Lampung, Jambi, Batak or Minangkabau areas
        • All provinces in above areas
      • If capital is in Central Tenasserim, North Tenasserim, Malaya, Johor, North Malaya or Malacca areas
        • All provinces in above areas
      • If capital is in Moluccas, North Sulawesi, South Sulawesi, Makassar or Spice Islands areas
        • All provinces in above areas
      • If capital is in Northern Luzon, Southern Luzon, East Mindanao, West Mindanao, Visayas or Palawan areas
        • All provinces in above areas
      • Else:
        • At least 30 provinces in any area
      		 Country gets “Devotees of the Faith” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • −20% Missionary maintenance cost
      Religious Dominance
      To spread the faith is our sacred duty. All of Nusantara must know the light of the divine.
      		 At least 60 provinces in the following regions have same religion as our country:
      • Moluccas
      • Indonesia
      • Malaya

      Country has converted at least 20 provinces.

      		Country gets “Religious Dominance” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +2 Tolerance of the true faith
      		 Religious Conversion
      Unite Nusantara
      All the peoples of the isles must be united into a single nation.
      		 Country is Malaya

      At least 70 provinces in the following regions are owned by our country:

      • Malaya
      • Indonesia
      		 Country gains permanent claims on the following region:
      • Indochina

      Country gets “United Nusantara” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:

      • +1 Diplomatic reputation

      Set government rank to Empire

      Invade the Mainland
      Now that we have gathered the full strength of Nusantara, we can set our sights to the mainland, where crumbling kingdoms are ripe for conquest.
      		 At least 50 provinces in the following region are owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Indochina
      		 Country gains permanent claims on the following region:
      • Burma

      Country gets “Military Domination” for 15 years, giving the following effect:

      • +10% Morale of armies
      		 Unite Nusantara
      Expel Europeans
      The European powers are unwanted invaders in Nusantara. We will not be forced into unequal trade deals or accept their deceitful promises of protection. We will drive them out.
      		 It is currently the Age of Revolutions or Age of Absolutism

      No province in the following regions is owned by a country with Western or Eastern technology group

      • Malaya
      • Indonesia
      		 Country gains:
      • 20 absolutism
      • 100 administrative power
      • 100 diplomatic power
      • 100 military power
      		 Unite Nusantara


      Exploit local resources[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.32.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Palopo Iron
      Sulawesi is named after its rich iron deposits. It would be foolish for us not to exploit these resources by building a weapons manufactory.
      		 Province Palopo:
      • Is owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects
      • Has Weapons Manufactory building
      		 Province Palopo gains permanent province modifier “Palopo Iron” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +2 Local goods produced
      Banjar Textiles
      Banjar's textile production is booming. We should encourage this industry by constructing a textile manufactory.
      		 Province Banjar:
      • Is owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects
      • Has Textile Manufactory building
      		 Province Banjar gains permanent province modifier “Banjar Textiles” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +25% Local production efficiency
      Lampung Peppers
      The southern region of Sumatra is famous for producing a huge portion of the world's peppers. We should ensure maximum yields by establishing factories in Lampung.
      		 Province Tulangbewang:
      • Is owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects
      • Has Trade Station building
      		 Province Tulangbewang gains permanent province modifier “Lampung Pepper” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +2 Local goods produced
      Moluccan Cloves
      Cloves are among the most highly prized goods in the world. If we could gain a monopoly on cloves, we could be rich beyond our dreams.
      		 Provinces Ternate and Tidore:
      • Are owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects
      • Have Trade Station building
      		 Provinces Ternate and Tidore gain permanent province modifier “Clove Boom” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +20% Local production efficiency
      Sea Cucumbers
      Traders from Sulawesi tell of an island to the south where people harvest sea cucumbers. We should discover this island and either trade for this valuable commodity or else take over its production by force.
      		 Province Tiwi:
      • At least one of:
        • Province is owned by our country or its non-tributary subjects
        • Province owner is allied to our country
      • If produces Gold
        • Province Development is at least 15
      • Else:
        • At least one of:
          • Has Naval Equipment Manufactory building
          • Has Trade Depot building
          • Has any manufactory
      		 Province Tiwi gains permanent province modifier “Sea Cucumbers” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
      • +3 Local goods produced
