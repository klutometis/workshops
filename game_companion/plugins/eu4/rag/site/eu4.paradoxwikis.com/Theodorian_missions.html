      Theodorian missions

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

      		Available only with the Winds of Change DLC enabled.

      This is a list of all missions of Theodoro[1]. Completing the mission A Russian Aristocracy by getting a Russian ally will unlock the Russian Alliance branch. That ally is required to complete the missions of that branch. Completing A Russian Aristocracy by army size (without having the ally) will unlock the Russian Conquest branch.

      Clicking on a mission icon leads to the appropriate table row.

      National missions[edit | edit source]

      feo_1[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Black Sea Fleet
      The Black Sea has been a hotspot of mercantile competition ever since the arrival of the Italians in the Crimean shores. Our Principality has seen a slow decline in maritime actions, being pushed around by the Ottomans, Italians, Hordes, as well as fellow Greeks. Creating a new fleet to combat these problems will be quite beneficial.
      		 Theodoro:
      • At least one of:
        • Has a navy of at least 15 ships
        • Has a navy of at least 100% of its force limit
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gains 10 navy tradition
      • Gains “Black Sea Fleet” when select a naval doctrine for 15 years, giving:
        • +10% Galley combat ability
        • −10% Naval maintenance modifier

      Province Theodoro (2410):

      • Creates a center of trade or increases its level by 1 if already has one
      Black Sea Domination
      Trade has always been the main source of revenue for the Greeks. Trade, which Gothia has seen a decline of since the grip of the Komnenos dynasty loosened, and the Italian Yoke emerged. Reclaiming our position in the Black Sea will put a stop to this decline.
      		 Theodoro:
      • At least one of:
        • Has a center of trade of at least level 2 in its capital
        • All of:
          • Is allied to Genoa
          • Gains at least 10 ducats per month
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least 20 province trade power in its capital
        • Controls at least 40% of the trade in its home trade node
      		 Theodoro:
      • Unlocks the "Livestock Regulations" burghers privilege
      • Gets a −75% cost level 3 Trader
      		 Black Sea Fleet

      Genoese Relations

      Ties With the Komnenos
      The Komnenos in Trebizond were our old allies decades ago. They were the rulers of Gothia, yet our merchant family was able to slowly emerge as a player in the Black Sea politics. Now that their realm is in decline, it is perhaps time the roles are reversed.
      		 At least one of:
      • Trebizond is allied to, has a royal marriage with and has at least 70 trust with Theodoro
      • Trebizond is a vassal of Theodoro
      • Theodoro owns province Trebizond (330)
      		 If completes this mission by allying Trebizond:
      • Trebizond:
        • Gets the “Offer From the Gavras Family” event

      Else, if completes this mission by vassallasing Trebizond:

      • Trebizond:
        • Loses 20% liberty desire
        • Gains 1 ruler diplomatic skill

      Else, if completed this mission owning by province Trebizond (330):

      • Province Trebizond (330):
        • Gets its Fort upgraded[2]
        • Gains “Defense of Trebizond” for the rest of the game, giving:
          • −75% Local fort maintenance
          • +25% Local defensiveness
      A Merchant's Family Home
      Constantinople, the city where many Emperors ruled before us. The old center of culture, wealth, and prestige for any Greek, and the city where many families like ours emerged from. It is time we return once more, and hold a triumph in the streets to let them know that we have vanquished our foes.
      		 Theodoro or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns province Constantinople (151)
      • Owns at least 10 provinces in the Anatolia region
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gets the “Successors of Rome?” event
      • Gains “One Goth to Rule Them All” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −25% Culture conversion cost
        • −20% Culture conversion time
      • Gains a permanent claim on the Balkans region

      Province Constantinople (151):

      • Adopts our culture
      		 Ties With the Komnenos

      Gothia Ascendant

      Basileus of the Balkans
      The Balkans were one of the more important regions of the old Byzantine Empire. Whilst it is a mountainous area, some of its wealth cannot be underestimated. Let us prepare a campaign to reclaim this lost Byzantine land.
      		 Theodoro or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns province Kosovo (1766)
      • Owns at least 20 provinces in the Balkans region
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gains 25 of each monarch power for every Balkan province that has a great project at least noteworthy

      Province Kosovo (1766):

      • Next time infrastructure are expanded, gains 2 years of its production income
      • Gains “Gavras Family Gold Deposits” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −10% Local development cost modifier
        • −100% Local gold depletion chance modifier
        • +2 Local goods produced

      Every owned province in the Balkans region

      • Loses 35% local autonomy
      • Loses 15 years of separatism
      		 A Merchant's Family Home

      feo_2[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Genoese Relations
      The arrival of the Genoese forever shook our position in Gothia. Many foreign merchants have been buying land off us, and have reduced our coastal influence to but a single port. Maybe it is time we try and combat this threat, either with the sword or with the pen.
      		 At least one of:
      • Genoa does not exist
      • Genoa has at least +75 opinion of Theodoro
      • Theodoro:
        • Has an army of at least 5 regiments
        • Rivals or has insulted Genoa
      		 Theodoro:
      • Builds 2 galleys for free, taking 30% of normal time, in a random owned port
      • If completes this mission by getting good relations with Genoa:
        • Adopts a peaceful Italian approach
        • Gains 15 favors with Genoa
        • Gains 5 mutual trust with Genoa
        • Becomes mutual historical friends with Genoa
      • Else, if completes this mission by rivalling with Genoa:
        • Adopts an hostile Italian approach
        • Gain a permanent claim on the following provinces:
          • Caffa (285)
          • Matrega (2447)
          • Azov (286)
      Dealing With the Italians
      Gothia's coastal influence has been in tatters for far too long. If we are to ever stand a chance at reclaiming our status in the Black Sea, the Italians must be pacified, or destroyed.
      		 Theodoro:
      • If has adopted a peaceful Italian approach and Genoa exists as a non-subject nation:
        • Genoa owes us at least 20 favors
      • Else:
        • Is employing a Trader advisor
        • Owns province Theodoro (2410) which:
          • At least one of:
            • Has at least 15 development
            • Has at least 2 buildings
      		 Theodoro:
      • If has adopted a peaceful Italian approach:
        • Gains 100% progress towards the Renaissance institution in the capital
        • “Italian Coastal Influence” burghers privilege now grants:
          • +5% Trade efficiency
          • +15% Institution spread
      • Else:
        • Removes the “Italian Coastal Influence” burghers privilege
        • Gains 5% mercantilism
        • Province Theodoro (2410):
          • If has a center of trade:
            • Increases center of trade level by 1
          • Else:
            • Gains 2 base production
      		 Genoese Relations
      Gothia Ascendant
      The Hordes and the Italians have been dealt with. Our principality which started from the city of Mangup is now more than just that. We can finally start the next step of our nation's ambitions: Restoring Gothia.
      		 Theodoro:
      • At least one of:
        • Has increased its total development by at least 110
        • Owns at least 15 non-colony provinces
      		 Theodoro:
      • Is renamed “Gothia”
      • Gains 10 power projection
      • Gets a −50% cost level 2 Commandant
      • Gains a permanent claim on Constantinople (151)
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following regions:
        • Anatolia
        • Ruthenia

      Every owned or non-tributary subjects owned province in the Crimea area:

      • Gains 1 base manpower

      Question of Azov
      Dealing With the Italians
      Black Sea Domination

      Aegean Supremacy
      What are merchants but selfish chancers? And what would selfish chancers in the Aegean want other than complete control? Securing the Aegean Islands, or fortifying its coast will allow us to reign supreme against our rivals and enemies that may reside there.
      		 Theodoro or non-tributary subjects:
      • At least one of:
        • Owns at least 5 of the Aegean coastal provinces with any Coastal Defense building and at least 15 development:
          • Gumulcine (4705)
          • Macedonia (148)
          • Thessaly (147)
          • Athens (146)
          • Euboea (3003)
          • Corinth (4701)
          • Morea (145)
        • Owns the following provinces:
          • Lesbos (4700)
          • Chios (2348)
          • Naxos (164)
          • Euboea (3003)
          • Rhodes (320)
          • Crete (163)
      		 Theodoro:
      • Renames the Aegean Sea to "Gothic Sea"

      If completes this mission by conquering the Aegean islands:

      • Every Aegean island:
        • Gains “Aegean Trading Outpost” for the rest of the game, giving:
          • −15% Local development cost
          • +20% Local trade power modifier
          • +10 Local trade power

      Else, if completes this mission by building coastal defenses:

      • Every owned Aegean island:
        • Gains “Aegean Coastal Outpost” for the rest of the game, giving:
          • Blocks slave raids
      		 A Merchant's Family Home

      feo_3[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Question of Azov
      The province of Azov in Crimea is one of the most vital ports in the Black Sea. Developed by the Italians as their main haven for conducting trade, it is a threat to our Black Sea domination plans. Perhaps then, it is time for some merchant-to-merchant conversation....
      		 Theodoro:
      • If has adopted a peaceful Italian approach:
        • Genoa:
          • Is allied to us
          • Has at least 70 trust with us
          • Has at least +150 opinion of us
      • Else:
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects owns the following provinces:
          • Caffa (285)
          • Matrega (2447)
          • Azov (286)
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Lower Don
        • Astrakhan
        • Saratov
      • If has adopted a peaceful Italian approach:
        • Genoa gets the “Our Trusty Allies in Crimea” event
      • Else:
        • Gets the “Remnants of the Genoese” event
      		 Genoese Relations

      Crimean Oppressors

      Plains of Ruthenia
      Our conquest of the Steppes has brought us many foreign riders, perhaps way too many for us to manage. By conquering Ruthenia, we may perhaps have the opportunity to settle some of them there.
      		 Theodoro:
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects owns at least 15 provinces in the Ruthenia region
      • Owns province Kiev (280)
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gets the “Grazing Grass in Ruthenia” event

      The “Kiev Pechersk Lavra” great project:

      • Now also grants the following at magnificent level:
        • −25% Missionary maintenance cost
        • +1 Missionary
      		 Gothia Ascendant

      Into the Steppes

      Gavras Triumphant
      We were but a humble merchant family years ago. Now, we sit atop a throne made of gold, triumphant over our Greek rivals. The rest of the world shall soon know of this accomplishment.
      • Byzantium is our subject or does not exist
      • Trebizond is our subject or does not exist
      • Theodoro is at least the fifth great power
      		 Theodoro:
      • The “Recruit Foreign General” diplomatic action will now grant us a commandant advisor as well
      • If has enacted the “Merchant Aristocracy” Tier 1 government reform:
        • Unlocks the “Merchant Basileus” ruler personality trait
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
        • Trader advisor now grants +2% burghers loyalty per level
      • Else, if has enacted the “Gothic Monarchy” Tier 1 government reform:
        • Recruiting mercenary companies now costs 50% less army professionalism
        • Unlocks the “Gavras House Guards” mercenary company, which does not cost army professionalism when hired
      		 $MONARCHTITLE$'s Vanguard

      Aegean Supremacy

      Gothic Invasion
      Gothia may have returned, but the Gothic ways have not. Germany has yet to be conquered. The kings and Queens there have yet to bend their knees to us. Then, we shall make them knee using the sword.
      		 Theodoro:
      • Is an Empire
      • If the Holy Roman Empire exists:
        • Has an army at least as strong that of the Holy Roman Emperor
        • At least one of:
          • Is rivaled by or rivals the Holy Roman Emperor
          • Has insulted the Holy Roman Emperor
      • Else:
        • Has an army of at least 200 regiments
      		 Theodoro:
      • Unlocks the “Gothic Invasion” casus belli

      Every current and future owned province in the North or South Germany regions:

      • Gains “Return to the Gothic Ways” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −25% Local culture conversion cost
        • −20% Local culture conversion time

      Owning directly or through non-tributary subjects the following provinces gives:

      • Vienna: −10% Diplomatic annexation cost
      • Prague: −15% Cost of advisors with ruler's culture
      • Cologne: +1 Missionary and +2% Missionary strength
      • Aachen: +5% Administrative efficiency
      • Lubeck: +20% Naval forcelimit modifier and +15% Provincial trade power modifier

      Settling the Goths
      Basileus of the Balkans
      Gavras Triumphant

      feo_4[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Crimean Oppressors
      The Crimean people have been a thorn in our side for many decades. Always raiding our border, and sometimes sponsoring invaders like Tamerlane to raze our land. Blood and ash shall be the only tribute they receive.
      		 Theodoro or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 4 of the following provinces:
        • Crimea (284)
        • Yedishkul (1974)
        • Kyzyl-Yar (2412)
        • Mansur (2411)
        • Tyn (2413)
        • Azaraba (2414)
        • Kuban (287)
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gains 20 cossacks loyalty
      • Gains a new general with:
        • 60 army tradition
        • +1 shock
      Into the Steppes
      Our Crimean campaign was a major success, as we have dealt with two birds using one stone. Now, it is time we push even deeper into Steppe territory, using our now seasoned warriors to bring us victory.
      		 Theodoro or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns Province Sarai (466)
      • Owns at least 7 provinces in the following areas:
        • Lower Don
        • Astrakhan
        • Saratov
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gains 10 convertible[3] prestige
      • The “Freedom of the Steppes” Cossacks privilege now grants an additional −2 unrest in steppe provinces
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following regions:
        • Caucasia
        • Ural
        • Central Asia

      Province Sarai (466):

      • Gains “Heart of the Steppes” as long as is not at war, giving:
        • +25% Caravan power
        • +1 Merchant
        • +10% Cossacks loyalty equilibrium
      		 Question of Azov
      Gateway to the Middle East
      Caucasia is a mountainous area, perfect for us to use against anyone that may invade us from the Middle East. We shall make sure that our soldiers secure these mountainous passes.
      		 Theodoro or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 15 provinces in the Caucasia region
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gains 5 years of production income from provinces producing Livestock in the Caucasia region
      • Gains 10 military power for every owned Mountain or Hill province in the Caucasia region
      		 Into the Steppes
      $MONARCHTITLE$'s Vanguard
      The Tatars may have been an enemy once, but one cannot deny their prowess in battle. Instead of eliminating them, perhaps we can use them in our society or in our ranks.
      		 Theodoro:
      • Has at least 150 development in owned Steppes provinces
      • Has at least 70 cossacks loyalty
      • Has at least 30 cossack regiments
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gets the “One With the Tatars” event
      		 Plains of Ruthenia

      Subdue the Hordes

      feo_5[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      A Russian Aristocracy
      We may be of Greek origin, but much of our aristocracy has major connections to the families in Russia. It may be best if we use these connections to secure new allies, but it may also be worthwhile to use them in order to claim the land for ourselves.
      		 Theodoro:
      • At least one of:
        • Has any ally in Russia (region) with at least 100 development
        • Has an army of at least 20 regiments
      		 Theodoro:
      • If completes this mission by allying any Russian nation with at least 100 development:
        • Unlocks the "Russian Alliance branch" missions
        • Gains 5% Patriarch authority
        • Russian ally gains 5% Patriarch authority
      • Else, if completes this mission by getting at least 20 regiments:
        • Unlocks the "Russian Conquest branch" missions
        • Gains the "Restoration of Union" casus belli on the highest developed Russian monarchy

      feo_5_1[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Subdue the Hordes
      Although the Pontic Steppe is free from the Tatar yoke, there are still some nomads in the Urals that pose a threat to us. Through subjugating them, we will remind any rider who wishes to plunder our land to think about their decision again.
      		 Theodoro or non-tributary subjects:
      • Owns at least 20 provinces in the Ural or Central Asia regions
      		 Every current and future Steppe province in the required regions
      • Gains “Steppe Construction Efforts” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −10% Local construction cost
        • −10% Local construction time

      The Steppe people will no longer be as aggressive towards us, making it so "Cossack Aggression" event doesn't fire against us

      		Into the Steppes
      Settling the Goths
      The Steppes are filled with foreigners, which are unreliable for bringing profit to our realm. Settling some of our people there to create cities would help fix that problem.
      		 Theodoro:
      • Owns at least 10 core Steppes provinces with at least 3 buildings
      • Owns at least 5 core Steppes provinces with at least 20 development
      		 Theodoro:
      • Distributes 10 manpower development among random owned Steppes provinces with at least 20 development
      • The “Prime Herding Rights” Cossacks privilege now:
        • Gives an extra +10% Local manpower in Steppe provinces
        • No longer gives a malus on absolutism

      One random owned province following any of the requirements:

      • Gains 3 base production
      		 Subdue the Hordes

      Russian alliance branch[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Russian Aid
      Our newfound allies in the North have been of great help so far. However, our local manpower has always been scarce and our officers unreliable. Perhaps by asking them to send some officers to help us, we can knock out one of these problems.
      		 Our Russian ally:
      • Has at least 65 trust with us
      • Is allied to us
      • Has +200 opinion of us
      		 Theodoro:
      • The “Nobility in Officer Corps” nobility privilege now grants −10% regiment cost
      • Gains 10 army tradition
      		 Crimean Oppressors

      A Russian Aristocracy

      Guards of the [Root.GovernmentName]
      The Gothic people are limited, and we have so far relied on foreign recruits, who are not as disciplined as those of our Russian brothers. Requesting them to send a small guard of elites would help with this problem.
      		 Theodoro:
      • Has an army of at least 80 regiments
      • Has at least 300 total development

      Our Russian ally:

      • Has at least 80 trust with us
      • Is allied to us
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gains 50 military power
      • Unlocks the “Russian Mercenary Guard” Tier 5 government reform, which will allow us to recruit Streltsy
      		 Russian Aid

      Russian conquest branch[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Our Russian Claim
      With our connections in Russia solidified, we are now able to press our claim to the throne. Although our aristocracy may help us with that, we still need to placate the people...
      		 Theodoro:
      • At least one of:
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 15 provinces in the Russia region
        • Has any Russian junior partner who:
          • Has less than 50% liberty desire
          • Has at least 150 total development
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gains “Connections to the Russian Aristocracy” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +15% Improve relations
      • If owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 15 provinces in the Russia region:
        • Accepts the entire 'East Slavic' culture group without taking a slot
        • Every owned province in the Russia region:
          • Loses 20% local autonomy
          • Loses 20 years of separatism
      		 Crimean Oppressors

      A Russian Aristocracy

      Palace of the Tsars
      The Gavras dynasty's connections to the Russian lands have proven to be beneficial for us. Now, our rulers sit atop the throne at the Kremlin, masters of the land of the Rus. All that is left is to crown ourselves Tsars...
      		 Theodoro:
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects own province Moskva (295)
      • At least one of:
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 40 provinces in the Russia region
        • Has any Russian junior partner who:
          • Has less than 5% liberty desire
          • Has at least 400 total development
      		 Theodoro:
      • Gets the “Atop the Kremlin” event
      		 Our Russian Claim
