 The Retrial of Joan of Arc

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

The mother of Joan of Arc, with the support of [Root.GetName], has petitioned His Holiness to order an inquiry on her daughter's trial. An inquisition meticulously examined the 1431 trial and after numerous hearings cleared her of all charges. Joan of Arc is now considered a martyr of the Catholic Church and her judge has been declared a heretic.

 
Trigger conditions
  • None
 Is triggered only by

The Fate of Joan of Arc decision

 
[Root.Monarch.GetName] really is the Church's firstborn
  • Gain   10 Prestige
  • Gain   10 Legitimacy
  • If   Orleans exists
    •   Orleans gets country flag "had_maid_of_orleans"
  • Set global flag "maid_of_orleans"

 Protestants Destroy Joan of Arc's Statue

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

A crowd of Protestants have torn down a prominent statue of Joan of Arc in Orléans. It would seem that her symbol is becoming associated with the fight against 'heresy' that Catholicism is bringing against the Reformation. Maybe we should do something to ease the tensions?

 
This event happens only once during a campaign.
Trigger conditions
  • Global flag "maid_of_orleans" is set
  • Owns Orleans (184)
  • Has French culture group
  • Is Christian
  • Current age is   Age of Reformation
  •   Protestantism is enabled
 Mean time to happen

360 months
Modifiers
×0.75: Our religion is either:

  •   hussite
  •   protestant
  •   reformed
  •   anglican

×0.75: Orleans province's religion is either:

  •   hussite
  •   protestant
  •   reformed
  •   anglican

  Enabled if: Is   Catholic

 
How dare they? Rebuild that statue!
  • Lose   ducats equal to 0.05 years of income
  • Gain country modifier “Rebuilt Statue of Joan of Arc” for   10 years, giving:
    •   +1 Tolerance of the true faith
    •   −1 Tolerance of heretics

  Enabled if: Is not   Catholic

 
Show tolerance, rebuild the statue
  • Lose   ducats equal to 0.05 years of income
  • Gain country modifier “Rebuilt Statue of Joan of Arc” for   10 years, giving:
    •   −1 Tolerance of the true faith
    •   +1 Tolerance of heretics

  Always enabled:

 
It's time for that symbol to go
  • If is   Catholic
    • Gain country modifier “Destroyed Statue of Joan of Arc” for   10 years, giving:
      •   −1 Tolerance of the true faith
  • Else
    • Gain country modifier “Destroyed Statue of Joan of Arc” for   10 years, giving:
      •   +1 Tolerance of heretics
  • Clear global flag "maid_of_orleans"

 The Catholic League welcomes our support

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

The Catholic League has been using Joan of Arc as a rallying cry lately, which made [Emperor.Monarch.GetTitle] [Emperor.Monarch.GetName] send us a delegation to personally welcome us to their faction. [Emperor.Monarch.GetSheHeCap] had no doubt that the country of Joan of Arc would pick God's side in the conflict against the Reformation. We have of course shared the good news with our soldiers to boost their morale.

 
This event happens only once during a campaign.
Trigger conditions
  • Global flag "maid_of_orleans" is set
  • Owns Orleans (184)
  • Has French culture group
  • Is Christian
  • Religious leagues are enabled
  • HRE has no official religion
  • HRE has no religious treaty
  • Is part of the Catholic league
 Mean time to happen

1 month

 
We are merely doing God's work
  • Gain country modifier “Catholic League Symbol” for   5 years, giving:
    •   +5% Land morale

 The Canonization of Joan of Arc

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

After years of petitioning the Holy See, the Cardinal of Orléans has finally convinced His Holiness [PAP.Monarch.GetName] to declare Joan of Arc a saint of the Catholic Church. The [Root.GetAdjective] people are now forever assured that God will stand by them in their darkest hours.

 
This event happens only once during a campaign.
Trigger conditions
  • Global flag "maid_of_orleans" is set
  • It is currently the   Age of Absolutism or the   Age of Revolutions
  • Owns Orleans (184)
  • Has French culture group
  • Is   Catholic
  •   The Papal State exists
  • Province Orleans has a   cardinal
 Mean time to happen

1200 months
Modifiers

  • Is the Curia controller: ×0.75
  • Country flag had_maid_of_orleans is set: ×0.1
 
Saint Joan of Arc, I like the sound of it
  • Gain country modifier “Joan of Arc Beatified” for the rest of the campaign, giving:
    •   +20% Fort defense
    •   +1 Tolerance of the true faith
  • If it has the country flag "had_maid_of_orleans"
    • Gets country event "The Militant Order of Saint Joan" in 30 days

 The Militant Order of Saint Joan

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

A local order of Catholic Nuns dedicated to Saint Joan of Arc have come to us with the most peculiar news. They assert that since it took a pious maid to save [Root.GetName] from the English, then who could be more efficient at fighting [Root.GetName]'s enemies that a militant order of nuns? They call themselves the 'Order of Our Martyred Lady' and stand ready to fight for us.

 
Trigger conditions
  • None
 Is triggered only by

the   event The Canonization of Joan of Arc

 
We could use them.

A sisterhood's life for me!

    •   May recruit female generals
    •   +20% National manpower modifier
    •   +100% Female advisor chance
    •   +1 Clergy max privileges
    •   +5% Clergy loyalty equilibrium
    •   +1 Tolerance of the true faith
    •   −3 Tolerance of heretics
  • If government type is not a   Theocracy
    • Change government type to   Theocracy
  • If our ruler is not a female
    • Gain a new female ruler named "Jeanne" with at least   3 Military skill

  • Enact government reform "The Sisterhood of Jeanne d'Arc"
