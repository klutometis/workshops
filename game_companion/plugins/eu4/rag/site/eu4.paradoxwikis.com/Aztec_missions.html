      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

      		Available only with the Winds of Change DLC enabled.

      This is a list of all missions of the Aztec. The lower part are sunset invasion missions which are shared with Inca. Other countries can gain access to these missions by enacting the "Adopt Aztec Traditions" decision.[1]

      Nourishment for the Gods
      In Aztec mythology, the Gods are powered by the blood of the sacrificed. They use that power to prevent this world from reaching Doomsday. We must keep offering them blood, the nourishment that keeps the sun from being extinguished.
      		 The country:
      • If has the “Tonalli Gathering” mechanic:
        • Has at least 15 “Tonalli”
      • Else:
        • Has won at least 15 battles
      • If is primitive and Nahuatl:
        • Has passed at least 1 Nahuatl reform

      • Gains 10 “Tonalli”

      The Fifth Cradle
      Before our world, the Five Suns were fighting against each other as to who gets to be the Sun deity of the world. First was Tezcatlipoca, whose humanity was devoured by his jaguars when his sibling Quetzalcoatl dethroned him. Second was Quetzalcoatl, whose humanity grew corrupt, leading to their banishment. Third was Tlaloc, whose grief, sorrow and anger destroyed the earth on which the humans lived in. The Fourth was Chalchiuhtlicue, whose humanity was tricked by Tezcatlipoca to hate their Sun. The Fifth was Huitzilopochtli, God of War, and the current sun of humanity. In his constant battle to keep humanity alive, we must offer humans sacrifices to nourish him, as well as keep Tezcatlipoca at bay, and offer blood for the great Quetzalcoatl.
      		 The country:
      • Is not at war
      • If is Nahuatl:
        • Is not primitive
        • At least one of:
          • Gains at least 9 administrative power per month
          • Is employing a Theologian advisor of at least level 3
      • Else:
        • Owns or non-tributary subjects own at least 20 provinces which:
          • Has any Tax building
          • Has at least 3 base tax
      		 The country:
      • Gains 10 clergy loyalty
      • Gains 100 administrative power
      • If has the “Tonalli Gathering” mechanic:
        • Regardless of our Patron Deity, Tonalli will now give the following scaling modifier:
          • +15% Clergy loyalty equilibrium
          • −5% Technology cost
      • Else:
        • Gains “The Fifth Sun” for the rest of the game, giving:
          • +5% Clergy loyalty equilibrium
          • −2.5% Technology cost
      		 Nourishment for the Gods
