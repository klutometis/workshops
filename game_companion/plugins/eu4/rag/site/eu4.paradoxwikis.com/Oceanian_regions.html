      Oceanian regions are regions that are part of Oceania. The table content is out of the files in /Europa Universalis IV/history/provinces and refers to a game start on the 11th of November, 1444.

      Note: Continents, regions and areas of EU4 do not always match reality.

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.
