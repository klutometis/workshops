      Norwegian missions[edit | edit source]

      Control the North Sea[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Please the Burghers
      The estates are the backbones of our country. The Clergy handles the religious questions and the Nobility manages our diplomacy with our neighbors. But the most important is the estate of the Burghers as they are responsible for the development and monetary flow of our [Root.GovernmentName]. We should appeal to them so we can use their expertise in the future.
      		 Norway:
      • At least one of:
        • Does not have the burghers estate
        • Has completed at least 2 burghers agendas
      		 Norway:
      • Unlocks the “Mountain Expansion” burghers privilege
      • If has the burghers estate:
        • Gains 15 burghers loyalty
      • Else:
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
      North Sea Markets
      The North Sea has always played a crucial role in the Norwegian history. Its fishes nourish our people, and its waves lead us to foreign treasures. But it also plays another important role as it is our sea of commerce and trade. We should establish markets in key provinces of our country so we can control the trade.
      		 Norway:
      • Owns core provinces Akershus (17), Trøndelag (20) and Bergenhus (23) which:
        • Have any Trade building
        • Have a center of trade of at least level 2
      		 Norway:
      • Gains 5% mercantilism

      Provinces Akershus (17), Trøndelag (20) and Bergenhus (23):

      • Gain “North Sea Trade Post” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −5% Local development cost
        • −0.05 Monthly autonomy change
        • +50% Local trade power modifier
      		 Please the Burghers
      Monopolize the North Sea
      The North Sea is inevitably linked to the trade of Lübeck and the English Channel, and whoever dominates the North Sea will also have a say in these two other rich markets. We should monopolize the North Sea and expand our influence in the neighbor markets.
      		 Norway:
      • Controls at least 75% of the trade in the North Sea trade node
      • Controls at least 35% of the trade or is the strongest trade power in the Lübeck trade node
      • Controls at least 15% of the trade or is the strongest trade power in the Baltic Sea trade node
      		 Norway:
      • Gains 4 years worth of trade income
      • Gains “North Sea Monopoly” ( +20% Burghers loyalty equilibrium) for the rest of the game
      		 North Sea Markets

      The Fleet of Norway
      The Norwegian people have always been a people of the sea. Centuries ago, our ancestors used longboats to loot and plunder the European coasts. It is time for us to expand our fleet so they can serve once again for the glory of Norway.

      This mission is not available when playing with a Random New World

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Navigation Mastery
      Some believe the best navigators are from Iberia, and they are the reason why the New World has been discovered in the first place. Of course this is absolute nonsense! After all, it was a Scandinavian who discovered the New World while the Iberians were still busy with their Reconquista. We shall show the world that the true masters of navigation are living in the cold north!
      		 Norway:
      • Has discovered a direct sea path around the world[2]
      • Has done the First or Second circumnavigation
      		 Norway:
      • Gains “Great Navigators of the North” for 100 years, giving:
        • +33% Colonial range
        • +33% Trade range

        • Gains 100 administrative power
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
        • Gains 100 military power

      Development mission[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Forts of the North
      Our country consists mostly of defendable territory. The Scandes itself is a great wall between us and our overambitious Swedish neighbor. However, mountains alone cannot block trespassers. We should construct additional forts within our realm to secure our borders and protect our people.
      		 Norway:
      • Owns at least 2 provinces in Scandinavia (region) with the highest level fort available
      		 Norway:
      • Gets a −50% cost level 1 military engineer
      • Gains “Scandes Fortresses” for 25 years, giving:
        • +15% Fort defense
        • −75% Fort maintenance
      Restore the Leidang
      Leidang is the name of a special form of conscription which helps us organize a coastal fleet for offensive and defensive wars. The system works by conscripting the sailors from our peasantry and contracting them for a service for two or three summer months during peace time. During war, everyone of the free men have to serve in our fleet.

      However, during the years between the 13th and the 15th century the system has been replaced by the classic feudal system led by the nobility.

      While feudalism has its own appeal, it cannot compete with the Leidang conscription when it comes to recruiting competent sailors. We should restore the Leidang system so we have access to a competent sailors.
      		 Norway:
      • Has at least 20 land force limit
      • Has at least 20% army professionalism
      • Has at least 10 army tradition
      • Owns at least 6 costal provinces which:
        • Has any Manpower building
        • Has any Dock building
      		 Norway:
      • Gains 2 years' worth of sailors
      • Gains 1 year's worth of manpower
      • Unlocks the “Leidang Conscription” nobility privilege
      		 Forts of the North
      Expand the Royal Navy
      Some countries use diplomacy to enforce their will. Others prefer to use brute force in form of a strong military. We, on the other hand, shall use the sea and our navy to achieve our goals. But we need a navy which can project our power in the first place.
      		 Norway:
      • Has at least 40 naval force limit
      • Has at least 20 marine regiments
      • Has a navy of at least 90% of its force limit
      • Has at least 20 000 maximum sailors
      • Has at least 40 navy tradition
      		 Norway:
      • Gains 150 military power
      • Unlocks a more powerful version of the "Norwegian Sailors" naval doctrine
      • Gains “Marines of Norway” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −10% Shock damage received (on marine regiments only)
      		 Restore the Leidang
      Restore the Infrastructure
      The Black Death of 1349 was an event of heavy impact for our country. Half of Norway died to the plague, and our fate has ever since been in the hands of somebody else. Not only did our population suffer but so did our infrastructure too. If we want our country to return to former glory it is vital to restore the infrastructure.
      		 Norway:
      • Has embraced the Renaissance institution
      • Owns the Trøndelag, Vestlandet and Østlandet areas which:
        • Have at least 8 development
      		 Norway:
      • Gets the “In Search for Iron and Copper” event
      		 Forts of the North
      Industrialize Norway
      Scandinavia is rich in iron and copper - both essential materials for a strong army and highly sought after in the European market. If we expand our mining and smelting of iron and copper we can not only equip our troops with higher quality weapons, but also make a large profit in the European trade.
      		 Norway:
      • Owns at least 5 core provinces which:
        • Produces copper or iron
        • Has a weapons manufactory
        • Has any Production building
      		 Norway:
      • Gets “The Iron and Copper of Norway” event
      		 Restore the Infrastructure
      Norwegian Great Power
      The Kingdoms of the North have rarely been taken seriously ever since Denmark's humiliating defeat against the Hanseatic League. It is time to show the world that Scandinavia is not a region which should be underestimated. It is time to show the world that we are one of the Great Powers of Europe!
      		 Norway:
      • Has at least 600 own or non-tributary subject development
      • Is not running a deficit
      • Has no loan
      • Has less than 1 corruption
      • Has +3 stability
      • Has at least +50 prestige
      • Is at least third great power
      		 Norway:
      • Becomes an empire
      • Gains “Empire of the North” as long as its primary culture is Nordic, giving:
        • −25% Stability cost modifier

      Every owned province of the Nordic culture group:

      • Gains “One United People” as long as the primary culture of Norway is Nordic, giving:
        • −2 Local unrest

      Industrialize Norway
      Expand the Royal Navy

      Catholic branch[edit | edit source]

      Norway has chosen to stay loyal to the Holy See.

      The Reformation is based on misinformation and sinful thoughts. Clearly, these reformers are lost by God himself. But worst of all, they and their venomous thoughts are a threat to the Catholic world. We need to establish ourselves as the protector of the Holy See so these heretics know what will await them if they attack any of our brothers and sisters in faith.
      		 Norway:
      • Is Catholic
      • Has an army of at least 30 regiments
      • At least one of:
        • Is the papal controller
        • All of:
          • Is Defender of the Faith
          • Has at least 4 Catholic allies
      		 Norway:
      • Gains 25 papal influence
      • Gains “Defender of Catholicism” for 25 years, giving:
        • +1 Yearly papal influence
        • −20% Curia powers cost
        • These effects are lost if the state religion changes
      		 Theological Questions
      Restore the Stave Church
      The Heddal Stave Church in Bratsberg is the largest stave church in all of Scandinavia. Constructed at the beginning of the 13th century, the church has seen decades of use. However, time was not very fond of the church, and eventually it got into a state where a restoration is needed. Let us renovate the church in the name of the Pope, and decorate the interiors with the finest gold the Catholic church can afford!
      		 Norway:
      • Is Catholic
      • Has country modifier “Papal Sanction for Increased Taxes” (Papal action: Levy Church Tax)
      • Owns core province Bratsberg (4144) which:
        • Is Catholic
        • Has at least 10 base tax
        • Has "Heddal Stave Church" great project at least significant
      		 Province Bratsberg (4144):
      • Gains “Renovated Heddal Stave Church” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +75% Local tax modifier
        • +100% Resistance to reformation
        • +0.25 Yearly papal influence
        • These effects are lost if the state or province religion changes or remove the tax building
      		 Protect the Catholic Faith

      Protestant branch[edit | edit source]

      Norway thinks that the Reformation is our future.

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Spread the Reformation
      These Catholic fools cling to their deceitful ways, demanding money from every sinner and giving them a "free pass to Heaven". We end this abuse of power within the Christian world, and spread the Reformation. The corrupt Papacy needs to lose its grip on our misled brothers and sisters!
      		 Norway:
      • Is Christian, but not Catholic
