      Military traditions location

      Army tradition and navy tradition (collectively military tradition) are long term, floating measures of the pool of experience of a nation's military forces on land and sea. They give bonuses depending on their strength, are gained through actions of the appropriate forces and decay over time if not increased. Army tradition provides higher morale and quicker manpower recovery, and naval tradition provides higher morale and better trade steering. High tradition significantly improves the skill of recruited military leaders.

      Army tradition[edit | edit source]

      Apart from increasing the chances of getting better generals (Military Leader), army tradition gives the following bonuses, scaling up to 100% maximum as below:[1]

      +10% Recover army morale speed
      +25% Morale of armies
      +10% Manpower recovery speed
      +5% Siege ability
      +0.10 Monthly Commanderies influence
      +0.10 Monthly Aristocrats influence
      +0.10 Monthly Girondist influence
      +0.10 Monthly militarization
      +5% Nobility loyalty equilibrium
      +5% Marathas loyalty equilibrium
      +5% Rajputs loyalty equilibrium
      +5% Janissaries loyalty equilibrium
      +5% Qizilbash loyalty equilibrium
      +5% Ghilman loyalty equilibrium
      +10% Tercio force limit fraction
      +10% Samurai force limit fraction

      Gaining army tradition[edit | edit source]

      • Battles are the primary source of army tradition for small and medium-sized nations. One can view how much one has gained in any particular battle on the battle result screen.
      1. One earns more tradition the more army losses one suffers relative to overall force limit, hence losing a battle will generally cause one to gain more tradition than winning.
      2. More specifically, for any particular battle Battle Army Tradition Gain = 20 ⋅ Army Losses ⋅ Number of Battle Participants Total Force Limit of All War Allies {\displaystyle {\text{Battle Army Tradition Gain}}={\frac {20\cdot {\text{Army Losses}}\cdot {\text{Number of Battle Participants}}}{\text{Total Force Limit of All War Allies}}}} all of which is shared equally with all the participants in that particular battle regardless of their number of units actually participating. In the formula force limits used include fractions to one decimal place and the net result is rounded to the nearest one decimal place.
      3. Worded differently, army tradition from battles equals total army losses in that battle divided by total war ally force limits multiplied by 20 and multiplied by the number of countries participating in that specific battle. The result is shared equally with all the participants in that battle regardless of the number of units each actually had participating. This equal sharing between the number of nations involved in the battle regardless of actual troop numbers opens room for some exploitation.
      4. Therefore, one can see from the formula that if say three small war ally countries with a 5k force limit each get wiped in the same battle, losing their entire 15k troops force limit, they will each gain +20 army tradition from that single battle.
      5. Since only army losses affect tradition no army tradition is gained from mercenary stacks in battle.
      6. However, Condottiere can help one gain army tradition: simply by being part of someone else's battles as a troop (even if just 1k) attached to the renting country's stacks one earns an equally split share of the tradition gained (mostly from their losses). Note that the country providing the Condottiere is added to the numerator of the formula above, as an additional participant.
      7. If there are no army battle losses a stack wipe gains a flat +0.2, e.g. when a zero morale army retreats after a battle and gets wiped instantly with no battle losses. Hence in such a case even a 1k stack loss will be the same as losing a mega stack.
      8. Certain ideas increase the amount of military tradition gained from battles:

      • Leading (not merely participating in) a successful siege grants +2 army tradition per siege - regardless of the number of units participating, based on the fort's level versus the max fort level for the sieging army.
      1. For example, if the max fort level is 4 and one sieges a level 4 fort, one will earn +2 army tradition. If the max fort level is 4 and one sieges down a level 2 fort, one will only earn +1 tradition. Successfully sieging forts is the primary source of army tradition for larger nations.
      2. This implies that beelining to be the first on an enemy fort is key to earning tradition from sieges, even if it means only having a 1k stack on the target fort with war allies attaching to complete the siege.

      Gaining navy tradition[edit | edit source]

      • Some events directly affect navy tradition.
      • Battles are the primary source of navy tradition. You can view how much you gained in any particular battle on the battle result screen. The amount of tradition gained in battle is directly proportional to the casualties inflicted on the enemy, as well as the relative strength of the nations involved. Small weak nations will gain considerably more tradition when fighting large strong nations. The number of ships that you send into battle, the losses that you suffer, and the quality of your leader have no observable effect on your naval tradition gain.

      Losing navy tradition[edit | edit source]

      The base tradition decays are 5% of the current tradition annually. (This means –5% of the current tradition, not a flat –5; so if you have 20 tradition then decay will change it by -1 annually.)

      Ideas and policies that reduce yearly navy tradition decay are:

      Traditions Ideas Bonuses Policies
      −1.0%
      • Mindanao traditions
      • Aristocratic idea 4: Noble Officers
      • Luzon idea 5: Luzon Sailors
      • Mahri idea 6: Book of Navigation
      • So idea 2: Wakou Tradition
      • Sulawesi idea 2: The Bajau
      • Utsunomiya idea 4: Cadet Branches in Western Japan
      • Candarid ambition
      • Sulu ambition
      		 —
      −0.5% — — —
      • Aristocratic-Maritime: Noble Naval Traditions

      Note: Battles lost will not reduce tradition. It is gained regardless of victory or defeat.

      In addition Innovativeness reduces navy tradition decay up to -1% at 100 innovativeness.

      Furthermore, each level 3 Coastal Center of Trade will provide a -0.2% reduction to the decay rate. For example, having 10 level 3 Coastal Centers of Trade (regardless of location) changes decay rate by -2%.
