      Castilian Civil War
      Our kingdom is being torn apart by our meddling nobility which has now thrown us into a civil war.

      The Castilian Civil War[1] is a disaster in Castile.

      Prerequisites[edit | edit source]

      The country:

      • Is Castile
      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • Is a monarchy
      • Does not have doom mechanic
      • Did not have this disaster before

      Portugal and Aragon exist and are not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.

      If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

      Monthly progress starts if Monthly progress halts if

      Castile:

      • Does not have an ongoing disaster
      • Is not at war
      • Has an heir
      • Is not the lesser partner in a union
      • At least one of
        • Is in a regency
        • Heir's claim strength is less than 80
        • has a female heir

      It is currently the Age of Discovery

      Monthly progress Castile:
      +3% • Has the current ruler for less than 5 years
      +1% • Has less than 90 legitimacy
      +1% • Does not have an heir
      +1% • Has less than +1 stability
      +0.5% • Has less than 2 ruler administrative skill
      +0.5% • Has less than 2 ruler diplomatic skill
      +0.5% • Has less than 2 ruler military skill
      −1% • Has +3 stability

      At least one of:

      • Has an ongoing disaster
      • Is the lesser partner in a union
      • Is not a monarchy

      Start[edit | edit source]

      If the progress reaches 100%, the disaster will start with the following event:

      id

      Castilian Civil War

      During the past century the Castilian nobility have time and time again actively tried to interfere with the succession of the Castilian crown and have not flinched from allying outside powers. A new such battle seems to be brewing and the succession is again in question. The future legitimacy of the Castilian throne is being questioned by some leading nobles who would prefer a relative of the King aligned with the Aragonese Crown. Meanwhile, the supporters of the current establishment are making overtures to the Portuguese crown in order to secure the position of their candidate.
      Things have now spiraled into open conflict and it seems increasingly likely that the faction without royal support will attempt to put their candidate directly on the throne rather than in queue for it. Some parts of the nobility remain undecided yet, and some have chosen to make use of this opportunity to protest against perceived infringement of their old rights in general.
       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      The start of "Castilian Civil War" disaster

      Immediate effects

      Castile:

      • Loses 2 stability

      Noble rebels (size 1) rise in revolt and occupy a random owned province with either a castle or a bastion, else a province with at least 10 development

      Get support from Portugal

      Castile:

      • Will bring us closer to Portugal![2]
      • Gets a new heir of our current dynasty
      • Loses 20 legitimacy

      Pretender rebels (size 1), friendly to Aragon, rise in revolt and occupy:

      • 1 random owned province with a castle
      • 2 other random owned provinces

      Portugal gets the “Castilian Civil War - Portugal” event in 10 days.
      Aragon gets the “Castilian Civil War - Aragon” event in 20 days.

      Support the Aragonese Candidate

      Castile:

      • Will bring us closer to Aragon![3]
      • If can have heirs and did not support the infantes during the "Infantes of Aragon" disaster[4] and did not choose Isabella as an heir in the "Isabella of Castile" event:
        • Gets the "Isabella of Castile" event in 10 days
      • Else::
        • Gets a new heir of our current dynasty

      Pretender rebels (size 1), friendly to Portugal, rise in revolt and occupy:

      • 1 random owned province with a castle
      • 2 other random owned provinces

      Portugal gets the “Castilian Civil War - Portugal” event in 10 days.
      Aragon gets the “Castilian Civil War - Aragon” event in 20 days.

      Modifiers[edit | edit source]

      While the disaster is ongoing, Castile gets:

      • +7 National unrest

      Also the following events are triggered by a monthly pulse.

      Monthly event[edit | edit source]

      The weight that no event is triggered is 120 and for each monthly event it is 1.

      id

      Spread of Civil War

      Prominent nobles in one of our provinces have joined the revolt and taken up arms against us.
       
      Trigger conditions

      Castile owns a province which:

      • Is not the capital
      • Is controlled by its owner
      • Is in Europe

      This event fires only once per area

      		Is triggered only by

      The monthly pulse of the "Castilian Civil War" disaster

      Where is the loyalty...

      Pretender rebels rise in revolt in this province:

      • 15% chance: size 2
      • 85% chance: size 1

      Finishing conditions[edit | edit source]

      The disaster ends if:

      • At least one of:
        • Pretender rebels have enforced their demands
        • All of:
          • Has at least +1 stability
          • Does not have rebel controlled provinces
          • Does not have pretender rebels

      then it loses the above-mentioned effects and the following event is triggered:

      id

      End of the Castilian Civil War

      With the conclusion of hostilities the Castilian Kingdom is once again at peace internally. In the wake of the conflict the balance of power between Royal power and that of the nobles has shifted, as has our relationship to our neighboring countries.

      		 Is triggered only by
