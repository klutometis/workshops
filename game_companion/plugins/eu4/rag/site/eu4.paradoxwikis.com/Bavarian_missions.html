      Bavarian missions

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
      At least some were last verified for version 1.32.

      This is a list of all missions of Bavarian nations[1], which are:

      • Bavaria
      • Ingolstadt
      • Landshut
      • Munich

      It requires Emperor expansion for the mission tree to be full.

      Clicking on a mission icon leads to the appropriate table row.

      Without Emperor, Bavaria has access to generic diplomatic and administrative missions, the regional missions, and one branch of these missions[2], listed here:

      • United and Strong Bavaria
      • Expand Bavaria
      • Conquer Franconia
      • Unite the Wittelsbachs
      • Conquer Swabia

      National missions[edit | edit source]

      Bavarian conquests[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      United and Strong Bavaria
      The Duchy of Bavaria has been divided since the middle of the 14th century, when the sons of Ludwig IV, and later those of Stephan II, decided to formally split their inheritance. However, a divided Bavaria is weak and vulnerable to depredations of its stronger neighbors. We must unify our ancestral lands if we are to stand the test of time!
      		 The country:
      • Is Bavaria
      • Has an army at least 100% of its total force limit

      All owned provinces have less than 1 local unrest

      		Bavaria gains permanent claims on the following provinces:
      • Oberpfalz
      • Salzburg
      • Regensburg

      Bavaria gets "Bavaria Reunited" for 20 years, giving the following effects:

      • +100% Prestige from land battles
      • -15% Core-creation cost
      Expand Bavaria
      Even a united Bavaria cannot stand against Austria or Bohemia on its own. We must expand our core territories and bring our weaker neighbors under our rule.
      		 Bavaria owns at least 10 provinces

      At least one the following provinces is owned by Bavaria or its non-tributary subjects:

      • Oberpfalz
      • Salzburg
      • Regensburg
      		 If Bavaria and The Palatinate are independent Christian monarchies:
      • Bavaria gains "Restoration of Union" Casus Belli on The Palatinate for 25 years.

      Else, Bavaria gains permanent claims on the following:

      • Palatinate area
      • all further provinces owned by The Palatinate

      Bavaria gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Lower Franconia
      • Upper Franconia
      		 United and Strong Bavaria
      Conquer Franconia
      The House of Wittelsbach was granted various Franconian lands by the Emperor Henry V. It is time that Bavaria took them.
      		 At least 6 provinces in the following areas are owned by Bavaria or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Lower Franconia
      • Upper Franconia
      		 Bavaria gains 20 prestige

      Bavaria gets "Bavarian Ambition" for 20 years, giving the following effects:

      • +1 Yearly prestige
      • +30% Improve relations

      Bavaria gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • West Swabia
      • East Swabia
      		 Expand Bavaria
      Conquer Swabia
      We cannot have any rivals in our own little corner of Germany. The lands of Swabia are rich but divided. To ensure that no other power can unite them against us, we must have them for ourselves.
      		 At least 6 provinces in the following areas are owned by Bavaria or its non-tributary subjects:
      • West Swabia
      • East Swabia
      		 Bavaria:
      • Gains 100 administrative power
      • Gains permanent claim on the following area:
        • Baden
      		 Conquer Franconia
      Unite the Wittelsbachs
      The divisions of the House of Wittelsbach reach far beyond the borders of Bavaria. A branch of the Wittelsbach family has long been established in the Rhenish and Upper Palatinates, acquiring for itself the status of Elector, a position which the Bavarian Wittelsbachs are clearly more worthy of. It would only be right to reunite the families and - with a bit of persuasion to secure the Emperor's agreement - claim the Electorate for ourselves.
      		 One of the following muse be true:
      • The Palatinate is the lesser partner in a personal union with Bavaria
      • The following provinces are owned by Bavaria or its non-tributary subjects:
        • Heidelberg (77)
        • Oberpfalz (1757)
        • Pfalz (1761)
        • Zweibrücken (4767)
      		 If:
      • Limited to:
        • Bavaria is part of the Holy Roman Empire
        • Bavaria is not an elector
        • The Palatinate is an elector
        • The Palatinate is the lesser partner in a personal union with Bavaria
        • The Holy Roman Empire religion is either not locked or is our religion
      • The Emperor of the Holy Roman Empire gets the "Transfer the Palatine Electorate to Bavaria?" event

      Else, Bavaria:

      • Gains 10 prestige
      • Gains 10 legitimacy
      • Gains 10 devotion
      • Gains 5 republican tradition
      • All owned provinces in the Palatinate area get "Efficient Integration" for 20 years, giving the following effects:
        • -0.05 monthly autonomy change
      		 Expand Bavaria
      Sway Cologne
      By binding the independent-minded Colognian Electorate to our power - one way or another - we will have removed one more obstacle to our rise to power in Germany.
      		 Province Köln (85):
      • Is owned by Bavaria or its non-tributary subjects
      • Is owned by a country in our religion group
      		 If Bavaria and Brandenburg are independent Christian monarchies:
      • Bavaria gains "Restoration of Union" Casus Belli on Brandenburg for 25 years.

      Else, Bavaria gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Mittlemark
      • Neumark

      If:

      • Limited to:
        • Cologne is a theocracy
        • Cologne is an elector
        • Cologne is in the same religion group than us
        • Cologne is our subject
        • Bavaria is a monarchy
        • Bavaria can have heir
      • Bavaria gets "The Cologne Secundogeniture" event

      Else, Bavaria:

      • Gains 10 prestige
      • Gains 10 legitimacy
      • Gains 10 devotion
      • Gains 5 republican tradition
      		 Unite the Wittelsbachs
      Tame Brandenburg
      Brandenburg is another of the territories once held by the Wittelsbach family. However, the upstart Hohenzollerns have ambitions of establishing a powerful state of their own in northern Germany. We should make sure that these ambitions remain nothing more than a dream.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned by Bavaria or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Mittlemark
      • Neumark
      		 Bavaria gets "Brandenburg Tamed" for 20 years, giving the following effects:
      • +0.5 Yearly Army Tradition
      • +2.5% Discipline
      		 Sway Cologne
      Claim Tirol
      The County of Tirol once belonged to the Duchy of Bavaria, a claim which we only dropped in 1369. We should revive this claim and end the Habsburg occupation of the County.
      		 At least 2 provinces in the following area are owned by Bavaria or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Tirol

      One of the following must be true:

      • No province in the Tirol area is owned by Austria
      • Austria is our subject
      		 All owned provinces in Tirol area:
      • Lose -15% Local autonomy
      • Lose 10 years of separatism

      If Bavaria and Austria are independent Christian monarchies:

      • Bavaria gains "Restoration of Union" Casus Belli on Austria for 25 years.

      Else, Bavaria gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Austria Proper
      • Inner Austria
      		 Unite the Wittelsbachs
      Union with Austria
      The upstart Habsburgs have built themselves a large territorial empire in Austria, a region which once belonged to the Dukes of Bavaria. Even though this was many centuries ago, many Bavarians have never fully contented themselves with living in Austria's shadow, and would gladly take the chance to turn the tables on them, should the opportunity arise.
      		 All provinces in the following areas are owned by Bavaria or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Austria Proper
      • Inner Austria
      		 Bavaria gains 20 prestige

      Bavaria gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Frisia
      • Holland
      • South Brabant
      • North Brabant
      • Venetia
      • Cariniola
      		 Claim Tirol
      Bavarian Netherlands
      Bavaria has an admittedly distant claim to various territories in the Netherlands. It seems only right, now that we have restored Bavarian power to much of our former glory, to revive these claims and reestablish our control over this prosperous region.
      		 At least 12 provinces in the following areas are owned by Bavaria or its non-tributary subjects:
      • Frisia
      • Holland
      • South Brabant
      • North Brabant
      		 Bavaria gets "Bavarian Netherlands" for 20 years, giving the following effects:
      • +15% Trade efficiency
      • +15% Trade steering
      		 Union with Austria

      Sway Cologne

      Expand into Italy
      Over the generations, the people of Bavaria have been filled with a great yearning for the fresh sea breeze of the Adriatic coastline. Now, our nation is in a position to acquire such territories for our [Root.GovernmentName]. We must seize Venetia and Carniola for ourselves!
      		 At least 8 provinces in the following areas are owned by Bavaria:
      • Venetia
      • Carniola
      		 Bavaria gains 100 diplomatic power

      If maximum sailors is lower than 1000

      • Gain 1000 sailors

      Else,

      • Gain sailors equal to 10 years of monthly sailor recovery

      Bavaria gets "Seafaring Bavaria" for 20 years, giving the following effects:

      • +25% National sailors modifier
      • +100% Sailor recovery speed
      • -20% Ship cost
      		 Union with Austria

      Bavarian prestige[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Develop München
      Our peers in the great courts of Europe will never take us seriously until we have a capital equal in splendor to theirs.
      		 Country's capital:
      • Has at least 25 Development
      • Has at least 5 buildings built
      		 If country's capital does not have a Center of Trade level 3
      • Add or upgrade Center of Trade by 1 in country's capital

      Else, country's capital:

      • Gain 1 base tax
      • Gain 1 base production
      • Gain 1 base manpower
      Establish a Standing Army
      The increasing demands on our military to defend and increase our territorial holdings necessitate the establishment of a strong standing army, a Bavarian national force that can stand alone without needing to be supplemented by unreliable mercenaries.
      		 The country:
      • Owns at least 6 provinces with any Army building
      • Has at least 75% of its maximum Manpower reserves
      • Has an army of at least 100% of its force limit
      • Does not have any mercenary regiment
      		 Country's capital:
      • Gain 2 base manpower

      If DLC 'Cradle of Civilization' is active

      • Gain “Bavaria's Professional Army” for 20 years, giving the following effects:
        • +5% Discipline
        • +15% Army drill gain modifier
        • −15% Regiment drill loss

      Else:

      • Gain “Bavaria's Professional Army” for 20 years, giving the following effect:
        • +7.5% Discipline
      		 Develop München
      Regal Splendor
      The more we embrace pomp and luxury, the more the other rulers of Europe do the same. We cannot let others outpace us when it comes to the magnificence of our rulers and the glory of our nation.
      		 The country:
      • Is not a subject nation
      • Has at least 95 Legitimacy (or equivalent)
      • Has Kingdom or Empire Government rank
      • If DLC 'Mandate of Heaven' is active
        • Is or was in a Golden age
      • Else:
        • Has at least 80 Prestige
      		 The country:
      • Gain 20 prestige
      • Gain “Splendorous Bavaria” for 20 years, giving the following effects:
        • −2% Prestige decay
        • +15% Improve relations
        • +3 Monthly splendor
      		 Develop München
      Establish Oktoberfest
      Our prestige and fame would be truly second to none if we would establish an annual festival to the glory of our nation, celebrating the magnificence of its rulers, the fairness of its people, the swiftness of its horses and the taste of its beers.
      		 The country:
      • Owns at least 5 provinces with a Farm Estate building
      • Produces at least 25 Grain goods
      		 Country's capital:
      • Gain “Oktoberfest” for the rest of the game, giving the following effects:
        • +0.5 Yearly prestige
        • +20% Local production efficiency
        • −10% Local development cost
      • If:
        • Limited to:
          • devastation is less than 1
          • Prosperity is less than 100
          • DLC 'Mandate of Heaven' is active
        • Gain 50 progress towards prosperity
      		 Regal Splendor
      Show Piety
      The religious dissentions that plague other states must not be allowed to establish themselves in Bavaria. We must see to it that piety and faithfulness remain cornerstones of our nation.
      		 If is Catholic and The Papal State exists
      • Has at least 100 Papal influence
      • The Papal State's opinion of our country is at least +150
      • Has at least 1 cardinal

      Else, if is Catholic

      • Has at least 100 Papal influence
      • Has at least 100% Religious unity

      Else, if religion is Protestant

      • Has activated at least 3 Aspects of Faith
      • Has at least 100 Church power

      Else, if religion is Reformed and DLC 'Wealth of Nations' is active

      • Has at least 100 Fervor

      Else:

      • The Reformation began at least 20 years ago
      • Has at least 100% Religious unity
      		 The country gains “Religious Piety” for 20 years, giving the following effects:
      • +1 Tolerance of the true faith
      • +2% Missionary strength
      Spread Piety
      We should establish a center of [Root.Religion.GetName] learning to act as a base from which we can exert our influence and ensure that our neighbors view matters of religion in much the way that we do.
      • The Reformation began at least 50 years ago
      • Owns any province in Upper, Lower or East Bavaria area with a University building
      • No neighboring country follows a heretic religion.
      		 The country
      • Gain “A Proselytizing Nation” for 20 years, giving the following effects:
        • +2 Diplomatic reputation
        • +5% Morale of armies

      Every neighbouring country:

      • Gain opinion modifier “Show the Way of the True Faith” ( +50 opinion / −1 yearly decay) towards us
      		 Show Piety
      Boarisch als Leitkultur
      Bavarian culture must take pride of place among the realms of the German peoples, and our beautiful dialect must become the lingua franca of all Germany.
      		 The country:
      • Has 'Bavarian' as Primary culture
      • Has at least 500 development in owned Bavarian provinces
      • Is the strongest trade power in our home trade node

      All provinces in the North Germany and South Germany regions:

      • One of the following must be true:
        • Is owned by us
        • Does not have higher Development than our country's capital.

      Country's capital is Bavarian

      		The country gains “Bavarian Cultural Dominance” for the rest of the game, giving the following effects:
      • +1 Yearly prestige
      • +1 Diplomatic reputation
      		 Establish Oktoberfest

      Spread Piety

      Deal with the Illuminati
      Our glorious [Root.GovernmentName] has seen the rise of a secret organization that calls itself "the Enlightened". While some of their aims are worthy, others are potentially dangerous, relying on revolutionary ideas. We should take a firm stance on the matter and either adopt their program or stamp the organization out.
      		 The country has embraced the 'Enlightenment' institution

      If:

      • Limited to:
        • Clergy, Nobility and Burghers estates do not exist
        • Has not enacted the “Revolutionary Republic” or “Revolutionary State” government reform
      • Has at least 100% crown land ownership

      Else, if Nobility estate does not exist

      • At least one of:
        • Has enacted the “Revolutionary Republic” or “Revolutionary Empire” government reform
        • All of:
          • Has less than 50 Absolutism
          • Clergy has at least 60 loyalty
          • Burghers control less than 5% of the land
        • All of:
          • Has at least 85 Absolutism
          • Burghers has at least 60 loyalty

      Else:

      • At least one of:
        • Has enacted the “Revolutionary Republic” or “Revolutionary Empire” government reform
        • All of:
          • Has less than 50 Absolutism
          • Clergy has at least 60 loyalty
          • Nobility has at least 60 loyalty
          • Burghers control less than 5% of the land
        • All of:
          • Has at least 85 Absolutism
          • Burghers has at least 60 loyalty
      		 If Clergy, Nobility and Burghers estates do not exist
      • Gain “Enlightened Despotism” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
        • +5 Maximum absolutism

      If:

      • Limited to:
        • Has less than 50 Absolutism
        • Clergy has at least 60 loyalty
        • Nobility has at least 60 loyalty
        • Burghers control less than 5% of the land
      • Nobility estate gains “Dealt with the Illuminati” for the rest of the game, giving 10 loyalty equilibrium
      • Clergy estate gains “Dealt with the Illuminati” for the rest of the game, giving 10 loyalty equilibrium

      Else, if:

      • Limited to:
        • Has at least 85 Absolutism
        • Burghers has at least 60 loyalty
      • Gain “Enlightened Despotism” for the rest of the game, giving the following effect:
        • +5 Maximum absolutism

      Else, if has enacted the “Revolutionary Republic” or “Revolutionary Empire” government reform

      • Gain “Enlightened Idealism” for the rest of the game, giving the following effects:
        • +0.5 Yearly revolutionary zeal
        • +0.25 Yearly republican tradition
      		 Spread Piety

      Regional missions[edit | edit source]

      European missions[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Global Dominance
      The acquisition of wealth is one thing; but being wealthier than all others, is a sign of true mastery. Grow our home trade node, and own a controlling stake in it.
      		 The country:
      • Is the strongest trade power in its home node which:
        • Is the highest valued trade node in the world
      		 Country:
      • Gains 30 convertible[3] prestige
      • Gains “World Renowned” for 20 years, giving:
        • +15% Trade efficiency
      Faith's Bastion
      Heresy must not be tolerated. The [Root.Religion.GetName] Church needs a pious role-model to light the way.
      		 The country:
      • Is the Defender of the Faith
      • Has at least 100% religious unity

      It is NOT the Age of Discovery

      		The country:
      • Gains a 50% cheaper skill 3 theologian advisor of our culture and religion
      • Gains “Armor of Righteousness” for 15 years, giving:
        • +5% Morale of armies
        • +1% Missionary strength vs heretics

      Holy Roman Empire missions[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Imperial Ambition
      Like all Princes of the Holy Roman Empire, we have our eye on the throne. We should stir up some support from the electorate.
      		 If the Holy Roman Empire exists:
      • Has at least 2 Electors backing us to become Emperor

      Else:

      • The country has at least 3 3 allies
      		 The country:
      • Gains 10 convertible[3] prestige
      • Gains “Imperial Graces” for 15 years, giving:
        • +1 Diplomat
        • +25% Improve relations
      Charlemagne's Legacy
      In times immemorial, the Carolingian Kings ruled over the great and Holy Roman Empire. Their legacy has not been forgotten, and neither has their ambition. We must be crowned Emperor.
      		 The country:
      • If the Holy Roman Empire exists:
        • Is the Holy Roman Emperor
      • Else:
        • Is an Empire
      		 The country:
      • Gains a 50% cheaper skill 2 statesman advisor of our culture and religion
      • Gains “Imperator Romanorum” for 15 years, giving:
        • +3 Diplomatic reputation
      		 Imperial Ambition
      Defend the Empire
      Over the course of time, many of our Princes have foolishly lost territory to the uncivilized nations beyond the Imperial borders. Unfortunately, that makes us look weak. Recover them.
      		 If the Holy Roman Empire exists:
      • The country is the Holy Roman Emperor
      • The "Call for Reichsreform" Imperial reform is enacted
      • Ever provinces in the Empire is under the control of a member state or its subject

      Else:

      • The country owns every core province
      		 The country:
      • Gains 10 prestige
      • If the Holy Roman Empire exists:
        • Gains 10 Imperial authority
        • Gains “Imperial Unity” for 15 years, giving:
          • +0.10 Monthly imperial authority
      		 Charlemagne's Legacy
