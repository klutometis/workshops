Government events[1] are events that change government or remove modifiers if the requirements are no longer fulfilled.

id

 Constitutional Restoration

The transformation of this nation's government is unconstitutional and unlawful. We demand the restoration of the Constitutional Monarchy.'
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Is a   monarchy
  • Does not have a parliament
  • Has the “Separation of Power” modifier
  • Has reached any Tier 5 government reform
  • Has not enacted:
    • “Parliamentarism”
    • “Constitutional Monarchy”
    • “Celestial Empire”
 Mean time to happen

12 months

 
Why don't you forget about that.

The country:

  • Loses the “Separation of Power” modifier
  • Loses   2 stability

  Noble rebels (size 3) rise up in revolt in a random owned core province

 
Well, when you put it that way...

The country:

  • Enacts the “Parliamentarism” Tier 6 government reform
  • If has a stability of less than +3,
    • then it gains   1 stability,
    • else it gains   50 administrative power.

 The worst excesses stop

Things are slowly returning to normal in $COUNTRY$. These days, it is almost safe to profess religious belief, or even support for a non-decimal calendar. The sleep of reason gives birth to demons, but the cult to father most demons may very well have been that whose expressed purpose was the worship of reason.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • has not enacted “Revolutionary Republic” or “Revolutionary Republic” government reform.
  • Has the “Cult of Reason” modifier
 Mean time to happen

12 months

 
Maybe it is for the best

The country:

  • Loses the “Cult of Reason” modifier
  • If has a stability of less than +3,
    • then it gains   1 stability,
    • else it gains   50 administrative power.

 [Root.GetName] - No longer a Free City of the Empire

[Root.GetName] is no longer a Free City of the Holy Roman Empire. The City leadership has been condemned for Machiavellian misuse of power and disrespect for the Imperial institutions and have lost all privileges associated with the title it was once granted.
 
Trigger conditions

  Never [2]
The country:

  • Owns at least 2 cities
  • Has enacted the “Free City” government reform
 Mean time to happen

12 months

 
Unfortunate

The country:

  • Abolishes the “Free City” government reform[3]

 Change of Administration

Having abandoned the religion of our ancestors we are also changing some of our administrative customs. Rather than relying on the Islamic establishment for legitimacy all power now comes from the Monarch and the circle around [Root.Monarch.GetHerHim] instead.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Is not Muslim
  • Has enacted the “Iqta”, “Mamluk Government” or “Indian Sultanate” government reform
 Mean time to happen

12 months

 
Ok.

The country:

  • Abolishes the “Iqta”, “Mamluk Government” or “Indian Sultanate” government reform

As befits a ruler of [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] caliber and stature, the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] has several capable children and many who show great promise as future rulers. Our advisors are adamant that it is now high time for [Root.Monarch.GetName] to name a favorite among the possible successors.

 Election of a New Ruler

With the death of our previous monarch the circle of the [Root.GovernmentName]'s advisors have come together in order to elect the next ruler of [Root.GetUsableName].
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Has an election whenever the current ruler dies mechanic
  • Is not a junior partner
  • Does not have or is electing "Jirí z Podebrad" ruler from the   "Return of the Hussites!" event
 Is triggered only by

The death of current ruler

Immediate effects
Hidden effect:
  • Save any neighbor country as "selected country" which:
    • Has the   highest development
    • Is not in a regency
    • Has the   Nobility,   Marathas or   Rajputs estate
    • Is a   monarchy
    • Is not at war with us
    • If we are christian, is christian
Option conditions

  Enabled if: Is not in a regency

 
Elect [Root.Monarch.GetName].

The country:

  • Gains   5 legitimacy

  Enabled if: Is in a regency with an heir

 
Elect [Root.Heir.GetName].

The heir:

  • Gains   10 claim strength

  Enabled if: Selected country has been saved

 
Choose one from the House of [selected_biggest_country.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName].

The country:

  • Heir dies
  • Gets a new ruler with the following attributes:
    • Of the same dynasty, culture and religion as the selected country
    • If has "Improved Rule Election" mechanic:
      • With skills hidden (reduced by −1)
      •   75 Claim strength
    • Else:
      • With skills hidden (reduced by −2)
      •   50 Claim strength

  Enabled if: At least one of:

  • Has the   Nobility,   Marathas or   Rajputs estate
  • Is in a regency without an heir
 
Crown one from our Nobility.

The country:

  • Heir dies
  • Gains   20 nobility loyalty, if they exist
  • Else, gains   20 marathas loyalty, if they exist
  • Else, gains   20 rajputs loyalty, if they exist
  • Gets a new ruler with the following attributes:
    • If has "Improved Rule Election" mechanic:
      •   75 Claim strength
    • Else:
      • Reduced skills by −1
      •   50 Claim strength

  Enabled if: Has an army of at least 30 regiments

 
Our general would make a fine ruler.

The country:

  • Heir dies
  • Gains   10 army tradition
  • Gets a new ruler with the following attributes:
    • Is turned into a   general with   50 army tradition
    • Increased   military skill by +2
    • If has "Improved Rule Election" mechanic:
      •   75 Claim strength
    • Else:
      • Reduced   administrative and   diplomatic skills by −1
      •   50 Claim strength

  Enabled if: Has an navy of at least 30 ships

 
Only an admiral can lead the [Root.GovernmentName].

The country:

  • Heir dies
  • Gains   10 navy tradition
  • Gets a new ruler with the following attributes:
    • Is turned into an   admiral with   50 navy tradition
    • Increased   diplomatic skill by +2
    • If has "Improved Rule Election" mechanic:
      •   75 Claim strength
    • Else:
      • Reduced   administrative and   military skills by −1
      •   50 Claim strength

  Enabled if: Has at least   80 Burghers or   Vaishyas influence

 
Pick an influential trader.

The country:

  • Heir dies
  • Gains   ducats equal to 0.1 years of income
  • Gets a new ruler with the following attributes:
    • Increased   administrative skill by +2
    • If has "Improved Rule Election" mechanic:
      •   75 Claim strength
    • Else:
      • Reduced   diplomatic and   military skills by −1
      •   50 Claim strength

  Enabled if: Can elect a ruler from the ruling dynasty

 
Somebody from the [Root.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName] shall take over.

The country:

  • Heir dies
  • Gains   10 prestige
  • Gets a new ruler with the following attributes:
    • Of the same dynasty as Our country
    • If has "Improved Rule Election" mechanic:
      • Increased   administrative skill by +1
      •   100 Claim strength
    • Else:
      • Reduced skills by −1
      •   75 Claim strength
Effects after selecting any option

If is the Holy Roman Emperor:

  • Gets   “The Emperor is Dead...” event
