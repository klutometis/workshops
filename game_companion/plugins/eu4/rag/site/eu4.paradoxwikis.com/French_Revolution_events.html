      French Revolution
      The French people have risen to overthrow the monarchs that once ruled their country.

      The French Revolution[1] is a disaster in France during the ‘Age of Revolutions’. The counterpart of the revolution disaster of all other countries.

      Prerequisites[edit | edit source]

      Playing with normal or historical nations.

      This country:

      • did not have this disaster nor the Revolution disaster before nor did it embrace the revolution in some other way.
      • is France.
      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • does not have doom.
      • is a monarchy. (Non-monarchy France gets regular Revolution instead.)
      • does not have the celestial empire reform.
      • is not revolutionary.
      • if Emperor is active:
        • is a great power.
        • the capital is in Europe or the revolution is present in the capital.
      • otherwise:
        • has at least 30 cities.
        • has its capital in Europe.
        • The revolution target does not exist.

      If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

      Monthly progress starts if Monthly progress halts if

      It is currently the ‘Age of Revolutions’ and France:

      • does not have an ongoing disaster.
      • at least one of the following is true:
        • has stability of less than 2.
        • rebels control at least 8 provinces including the capital and revolutionary rebels are active in the country.
      • at least one of the following is true:
        • has stability of less than 0.
        • has the revolution in at least 50% of its development.
        • has at least 50 loans.
        • has at least 10% inflation.
        • has at least 10 war exhaustion
        • has negative prestige.
        • is the nation with the biggest army and highest development which has their capital in Europe.
        • has rebels controlling at least 8 provinces including the capital and active revolutionary rebels.
      Monthly progress France
      +0.75% 50%~65% of the development has the revolution.

      +3.0% has its capital occupied by rebels.
      +0.5% is the nation with the biggest army and highest development which has their capital in Europe.
      +1.0% has the revolution in the capital.

      The Storming of the Bastille

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.36.

      Following months of stalled debates in the Estates General, in which the very composition of the assembly could barely be agreed upon, let alone a course of action for social, economic and constitutional reform, the revolutionary masses have had enough. Today, on [GetDateText] - a date that will go down in [Root.GetAdjective] history - fed up with the lack of progress, the people took matters into their own hands: a mob gathered and stormed the Bastille, a prison fortress towering over Paris as a symbol of royal oppression. Although [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] [Root.Monarch.GetName] called in the troops, [Root.Monarch.GetSheHe] was left powerless as the army refused to fire on the people and instead joined the revolt.

      Now, with a surge of revolutionary zeal coursing through the masses, France has been declared a Republic. The Revolution has arrived...

       
      This event happens only once during a campaign.
      Trigger conditions 
      • revolution_event_can_happen_trigger = yes
      		 Is triggered only by
      • Monthly pulse of the “French Revolution” disaster
      • The event “The Estates General” option “Perhaps they are right...”
      Vive la Révolution!
      • If the Ruler is Female or is a Regency for a Female heir :
        • Set country flag french_revolution_female_ruler
      • Else:
        • Set country flag french_revolution_male_ruler

      • If all of the following are true:
        • Is France
        • Is the revolution target or is Revolutionary
        • DLC Domination is active
      • Trigger country event “New Traditions & Ambitions”

      Modifiers[edit | edit source]

      While the disaster is ongoing, France gets:

      • +10 national unrest,
      • +10% stability cost modifier.

      Also the following events are triggered by a monthly pulse.

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 25% of cases.
