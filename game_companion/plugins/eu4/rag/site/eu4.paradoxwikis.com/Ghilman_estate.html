QizilbashEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.36.
 
The Qizilbash are Shia warriors of Turkoman origins from Azerbaijani-speaking tribes. Founded by the Safaviyya Sufi order, they are the state's elite infantry and cavalry who have grown in political power to be a direct rival to the Nobility. As a Shia militant force, the Qizilbash adhere to the Shi'i doctrines and as such oppose any anti-Shia sentiment from the state.

Conditions:

  • One of the following must be true:
    • Is Persia
    • Is Ardabil
    • Has unlocked the Qizilbash estate[1]
  • Is not a nomad
  • Does not use other mechanics

  Influence – The estate has a base influence of 10%, modified by:

  Traditions Ideas Bonuses Policies

  • +5% If the religion is neither   Shia nor   Zoroastrian but the nations   tolerance of Heretics (if religion is in the muslim group) or   Heathens (if religion is not in the muslim group) is at least +0.01

  • -10% If the religion is neither   Shia nor   Zoroastrian but the nations   tolerance of Heretics (if religion is in the muslim group) or   Heathens (if religion is not in the muslim group) is 0 or less.
  • −20% If the religion isnt   Zoroastrian and the nation does not own any   Shia provinces

GhilmanEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.36.
 
The Ghilman are a unit of military slaves with Caucasian origins. Usually, former Christians, the Ghilman undergo strict military training and are well-practiced in the usage of firearms, making them an essential part of the state's army. They serve as the counterbalance to the influential Qizilbash.

Conditions:
