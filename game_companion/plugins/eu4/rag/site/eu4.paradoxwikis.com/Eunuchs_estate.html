      Janissaries[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.35.
      The Janissaries are elite corps only loyal to the Sultan himself. Their ranks are made up through the devshirme system of child levy and go through a strict military training. As such, the Janissaries are the most disciplined forces of the Empire. As time goes on the influence of the Janissaries grew up to a point where the Janissaries are a class of their own.

      Conditions:

      • Has enacted one of the following Government Reforms :
        • Ottoman Government
        • Modernized Ottoman Government
        • Reorganized Ottoman Government
        • Reformed Janissaries
      • Has not enacted the "Mansure Army" Reform

      Influence – The estate has a base influence of 10%, modified by:

      Traditions Ideas Bonuses Policies
      In patch 1.37 no ideas and policies have the modifier “janissary influence”
      • +1% to +100% Depending on how much (in equivalent percent) of the army is made up of Janissary regiments
      • -5% If the nation is not employing any Janissary regiments
      • -40% If another estate has seized control of the nation (via disaster)


      Loyalty – The estate's loyalty equilibrium is modified by:

      Traditions Ideas Bonuses Policies
      +10% — —
      • Full Quality
      		 —
      • +5% times the Rulers Military Skill
      • -1% For every percent below 40% of the army that is made up of Janissary regiments (so -40 if 0% of the army is Janissaries)
      • -20%If another estate has seized control of the nation (via disaster)


      Rebels: If the Janissaries are disloyal after land has been seized, Janissary rebels may rise in revolt in a few provinces.

      Disaster: If the Janissaries estate reaches 100 influence, their estate disaster will begin ticking and - unless lowered - will eventually trigger the Janissary Independence disaster.

      Eunuchs[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.35.
      The Eunuchs are a powerful estate within the Empire of China. At times their influence is curbed by powerful heads of state who rein them in with an iron fist. However, should the reigning monarch be deemed weak or incapable, they are quick to turn even the Emperor himself into an elaborate puppet.

      Conditions:

      • Is the Emperor of China
