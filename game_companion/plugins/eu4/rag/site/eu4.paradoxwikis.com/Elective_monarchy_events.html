Available only with the Res Publica DLC enabled.

This is a list of all Elective monarchy / Polish Elective Monarchy events. One event may occur every four years.

The following events are triggered by a four year pulse:

  • Total weight of all events (and no event) in this pulse is 17 300
  • Each event in this page has a Base weight between 25 (~0.15%) and 100 (~0.58%)

Sejm Supports $HEIR$ $HEIR_DYNASTY$

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.30.

The Sejm stood united today in their support for $HEIR$ $HEIR_DYNASTY$, who despite $HEIR_ADJ$ foreign roots is viewed as a worthy successor to the throne of $COUNTRY$. This will strengthen his chance to assume power, should our current $MONARCHTITLE$ pass away.

Available only with the Res Publica DLC enabled.
 
Trigger conditions
  • Has government type where: Has Polish Election
  • Has a foreign heir
 Is triggered only by

a four year pulse.

Base weight: 150

$HEIR$ is truly worthy.
  • Gain 10 support for our heir

Bribing the Sejm

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.30.

Winning an election is often a matter of knowing what palms to grease. A member of our dynasty is currently the leading candidate to succeed to the throne in a foreign nation, and several powerful nobles from that nation have made it clear that they are willing to further shore up his position if our nation proves to be 'as generous as it is great'.

Available only with the Res Publica DLC enabled.
 
Trigger conditions
  • Government is monarchy
  • If Poland exists :
    • Knows about Poland
    • Poland:
      • Has government type where: Has Polish Election
      • Heir's culture is the same as Our country
  • Else, if Commonwealth exists :
    • Knows about Commonwealth
    • Commonwealth:
      • Has government type where: Has Polish Election
      • Heir's culture is the same as Our country
  • Else:
    • Any known country:
      • Has government type where: Has Polish Election
      • Heir's culture is the same as Our country
  • Treasury contains at least 0.5 years' worth of income
 Is triggered only by

a four year pulse.

Base weight: 25

Reward their support generously.
  • Lose ducats equal to 0.5 years of income
  • Gain 3 prestige
  • Targeted “Polish Election” country:
    • Gain 25 support for our heir
Send them home empty-handed.
  • Lose 1 prestige
  • Targeted “Polish Election” country:
    • Lose 10 support for our heir
id

Plot Against $HEIR$ $HEIR_DYNASTY$

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.30.

A certain faction within the Sejm have made no secret of their dislike for $HEIR$ $HEIR_DYNASTY$, and now your spymaster has uncovered that they are plotting the assassination of $HEIR_ADJ$. If they are allowed to make the attempt, there is a considerable risk that we will be blamed, but arresting them would upset their friends in the Sejm. How should we proceed?

Available only with the Res Publica DLC enabled.
 
Trigger conditions

  • Has a foreign heir
 Is triggered only by

Let the plot unfold unhindered.

Arrest these plotters!

Religious Differences

[Root.Monarch.GetName] Inherits Title

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.30.

Although [Root.Monarch.GetSheHe] is our [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] first and foremost, [Root.Monarch.GetName] still has the right to some titles in the land from which [Root.Monarch.GetSheHe] hails. While foreigners might have supported [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] ascent to the throne with hopes of ruling over us, having a rightful claim on a title in their lands may well give us a chance to do the reverse...

Available only with the Res Publica DLC enabled.
 
Trigger conditions
  • Has government type where: Has Polish Election
  • Ruler is foreign
  • Any known country:
    • Ruler is of our dynasty
    • Is not a subject nation
    • Their capital is on the same continent as our capital
    • Any owned province:
      • Is not a claim nor a core of our country
      • Is in the Capital area of its owner
 Is triggered only by

a four year pulse.

Base weight: 150

It is [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] birthright!
  • Province described in the trigger conditions:
    • Gains a claim on this province
We have no ulterior motives with our choice.
  • Lose 5 prestige

Sejm Forces Abdication

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.30.

[Root.Monarch.GetName] knows that [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] time may soon come. Wishing to secure the title for [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] dynasty, [Root.Monarch.GetSheHe] has begun to seek support for selecting one of [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] progeny as next in line, questioning the choice of the Sejm. The nobles have not taken kindly to this and are considering forcing the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] to abdicate in favor of the heir they have already chosen for the throne.

Available only with the Res Publica DLC enabled.

    • Ruler is of our dynasty

    • Our country:
      • Does not have their heir
 Is triggered only by

a four year pulse.

Base weight: 150

Absolutely. Absolutism must be avoided.

Our [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] can't bow so easily to nobles!
  • Lose 1 stability
  • Lose 10 support for our heir

The [Root.Religion.GetName] Convert

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.30.

The world is seeing turbulent religious change. A monarch shifting religious allegiance can be seen as a powerful trend, and when [Root.Monarch.GetName] turned to the [Root.Religion.GetName] faith on ascending to the throne, many took note. If the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] could convert, what is stopping the other unenlightened from seeing the truth? Nothing, its supporters argue. The pressure on everyone, noble or commoner, who has not yet fallen into line is now higher than ever.

Available only with the Res Publica DLC enabled.
 
Trigger conditions
  • Has government type where: Has Polish Election
  • Ruler is foreign
  • Any known country:
    • Ruler is of our dynasty
    • Is not a lesser partner in a personal union
    • Their religion is not our religion
    • Ruler flag elective_monarchy_heir_refused_converted is not set
 Is triggered only by

a four year pulse.

Base weight: 150

Let us hope they see reason, or at least faith.
  • Gain ruler modifier “Converted Monarch”, giving the following effects:
    • +2% Missionary strength
    • −1 Tolerance of heretics

Loyalties of [Root.Monarch.GetName] and [Root.Heir.GetName]

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.30.

Electing monarchs from different nations allows us, to some extent, to avoid a dynasty taking control over [Root.GetName] entirely, but also causes foreign nobles to sometimes doubt our loyalty. The [Root.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName] family to which our current [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] belongs has noticed that the increased support in the Sejm for [Root.Heir.GetName] of the [Root.Heir.Dynasty.GetName] dynasty. Although they hopefully understand that they can't rule our nation forever, they are beginning to ask questions about how this will affect future cooperation between us.

Available only with the Res Publica DLC enabled.

  • Ruler is foreign

  • Any known country (tagged as "Ruler's dynsty"):
    • Ruler is of our dynasty
    • Is not a lesser partner in a personal union
    • Our country:
      • Does not have their heir
 Is triggered only by

a four year pulse.

Base weight: 150

Assure them that the Sejm stays loyal to the [Root.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName] line.

Admit that a shift is necessary.

Stay neutral. The Sejm owes allegiance only to [Root.GetName].

Golden Liberty

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.30.

Ruling in [Root.GetName] is not like ruling in other nations. Dictating the nation's goals is difficult without approval from nobles, sometimes impossible. To a foreign monarch, this may seem disappointing and frustrating, but this cornerstone of [Root.GetAdjective] politics is highly valued in its homeland and is, in fact, what [Root.Monarch.GetName] has to thank for coming into power in the first place. Embracing these ideas might make the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] seem weak internationally, but would aid in integrating better and gaining supporters in the Sejm.

Available only with the Res Publica DLC enabled.
 
Trigger conditions
  • Has government type where: Has Polish Election
  • Ruler is foreign
  • Can only happen once per ruler
 Is triggered only by

a four year pulse.

Base weight: 150

They were wise to elect us. Let us listen to them.

    • +20% Stability cost modifier
    • −20% Advisor costs
  • Lose 10 prestige
  • Gain 10 legitimacy

[Root.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName] Family Gains Claim on [Root.GetAdjective] Throne

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.30.

Recent power shifts among the foreign relatives of [Root.Monarch.GetName] and clashing inheritance laws have led to an unfortunate situation with the house of [Root.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName]. A member of the dynasty has now been suggested to be the rightful ruler of not only [Root.Monarch.GetName]'s homeland, but ours as well.

Whether or not this claim will be pushed through war or is merely used to bolster the foreign monarch's status for a domestic power struggle remains to be seen. Regardless, the possibility of being forced into a union in this manner is ominous.

Available only with the Res Publica DLC enabled.
 
Trigger conditions
  • Has government type where: Has Polish Election
  • Ruler is foreign
  • Any known country:
    • Ruler is of our dynasty
    • Government is monarchy
    • Is not a subject nation
    • Is not at war
    • Is not in a regency
    • Is an independent Christian monarchy
 Is triggered only by

a four year pulse.

Base weight: 25

We elected one of theirs as a show of good faith!

[Root.Monarch.GetName] Gains Claim on [From.GetAdjective] Throne

Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.30.

Our support for [From.Monarch.GetName], our candidate for the [From.GetAdjective] throne, has yielded us many diplomatic advantages since [From.Monarch.GetSheHe] was elected. Recently, however, it has been suggested that the [Root.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName] family's claims on [From.GetName] reaches beyond this single individual and that [Root.Monarch.GetName] should in fact be considered the ruler of both [Root.GetName] and [From.GetName].

[From.Monarch.GetName] considers [From.Monarch.GetHerselfHimself] sovereign, of course, but [Root.Monarch.GetName] outranks [From.Monarch.GetHerHim] in the dynasty. Surely a [From.Monarch.GetTitle]'s first loyalty must be to family, must it not?

 
Trigger conditions
  • None
 Is triggered only by

the event "[Root.Monarch.Dynasty.GetName] Family Gains Claim on [Root.GetAdjective] Throne"
