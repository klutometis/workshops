      National missions[edit | edit source]

      Uniting the Upper Saxons[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Claim the Borderlands
      The powerful kingdom of Bohemia is a threatening presence on our borders. However, the borderlands of Lusatia and the Erzgebirge have attracted many settlers from Saxony and other parts of Germany. If we can defeat the Bohemians, they will surely be happy to accept their new saxon overlords.
      		 The country owns at least 3 provinces in the following areas:
      • Erzgebirge
      • Lusatia
      		 Every owned provinces in the Erzgebirge and Lusatia areas:
      • Loses 10 years of separatism
      • Gets "Saxon Settlements" for 30 years, giving:
        • −33% local culture conversion cost

      The country gains a permanent claim on the Silesia area

      Take Silesia
      The rich lands of Silesia, which like Lusatia are an attractive target for Saxon settlers, stand between us and further eastern expansion. We should seek to conquer this strategically important area.
      		 The country owns at least 3 provinces in the following area:
      • Silesia
      		 Every owned provinces in the Silesia area:
      • Loses 10 years of separatism
      • Gets "Saxon Settlements" for 30 years, giving:
        • −33% local culture conversion cost

      The country gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Neumark
      • Mittelmark
      		 Claim the Borderlands
      Conquer Brandenburg
      The Hohenzollerns of Brandenburg have ambitions of establishing a powerful state in northern Germany. We must make sure this dream of theirs does not become reality, lest our lands receive unwanted visits by their armies.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 6 provinces in the following area:
      • Neumark
      • Mittelmark, including at least Berlin (50)
      		 The country gets "Conqueror of Brandenburg" for 20 years, giving:
      • +5% Morale of armies
      • +2.5% Discipline
      		 Take Silesia

      Restoring the Stem Duchy[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Expand Westwards
      Our neighbors, the Welfs of Brunswick, Askaniers of Anhalt and Bishops of Magdeburg, would pounce at the slightest sign of weakness on our part. We should bring them into our monarchy.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 2 provinces in each of the following areas:
      • Saxony-Anhalt
      • Braunschweig
      		 The country gains permanent claims on the following areas:
      • Lower Saxony
      • Weser
      • South Westphalia
      • North Westphalia
      Avoid Fragmentation
      The House of Wettin is frequently rent by divisions and fragmentation as a result of succession disputes. If we are to remain a powerful force in the Holy Roman Empire, we must make sure that we avoid this and keep the Saxon lands united.
      		 The country:
      • is not a subject nation
      • owns all provinces in the following areas as core:
        • Saxony
        • Thuringia
      • if is Saxony:
        • Thuringia does not exist
      • otherwise, Saxony does not exist
      		 The country gains permanent claims on the following areas:
      • Upper Franconia
      • Lower Franconia
      • Saxony-Anhalt
      • Braunschweig
      Reclaim our Ancestral Lands
      The core of the old Duchy of Saxony was in Westphalia and Lower Saxony. Reclaiming our ancestral lands would be a worthy goal for an ambitious saxon duke.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 10 provinces in the following areas:
      • Lower Saxony
      • Weser
      • South Westphalia
      • North Westphalia
      		 The country gets "Saxon Ambitions" for 20 years, giving:
      • −10% core-creation cost
      • −10% aggressive expansion impact
      		 Expand Westwards

      Avoid Fragmentation

      Conquer Franconia
      We have long considered Coburg to be our base in Franconia. Perhaps it is time for us to use it as a springboard from which to expand our holdings there.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 6 provinces in the following areas:
      • Upper Franconia
      • Lower Franconia
      		 The country:
      • gains 50 administrative power
      • gets "Ruler of Duchies" for 20 years, giving:
        • +1 Yearly prestige
      		 Avoid Fragmentation

      Saxon production[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Saxon Manufacturing
      Saxony has many natural resources. By converting these raw materials into finished goods, we will be able to greatly increase our land's prosperity.
      		 The countrys:
      • owns core province Leipzig (62)
      • owns at least 3 provinces with any manufactory.
      		 Leipzig (62) gets the event "Leipzig Propsers"
      Porcelain from Meissen
      Europe has traditionally imported its porcelain from the Far East. By learning the secrets of porcelain manufacturing and establishing the craft in our lands, we could secure significant riches for ourselves.
      		 One of the following must be true:
      • The event "European Traders in China" has happened
      • The year is at least 1700

      The country:

      • is the strongest trade power in the Saxony trade node
      • has increased its monthly income by at least 300%
      • owns province Dresden (61) which:
        • has a workshop or a counting house
        • has at least 20 development
      		 If no country has had the event before:
      • gets the event "Porcelain in Meissen"

      Otherwise, Dresden (61):

      • gains 4 base production
      • gets "Investment in Meissen" for 20 years, giving:
        • +20% Goods produced modifier
      		 Saxon Manufacturing
      Industrialize Saxony
      We must ensure that our manufacturing strength continues to grow, so that Saxony may become one of the industrial centers of Germany.
      		 This country owns at least 5 provinces, including at least Leipzig (62) and Niederlausitz (1763), if owned:
      • have any manufactory
      • have at least 20 development
      		 The country gets "Prospering Production" for the rest of the game, giving:
      • +10% Production efficiency
      		 Porcelain from Meissen

      Arts and culture[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Wittenberg University
      We must establish a center of learning where we can train the theologians, jurists and medics of the future, and attract the leading minds of Germany to serve the monarchy.
      		 The country:
      • has embraced the Renaissance
      • owns province Wittenberg (59) which:
        • has at least 15 development
        • has a temple or a cathedral
        • has a courthouse or a town hall
      		 The country gets "Wittenberg University" for 20 years, giving:
      • +2% Missionary strength
      • +1 Yearly legitimacy

      Province Wittenberg (59):

      • gains a university
      Weimar Classicism
      Our cities must become renowned centers of high culture in Germany.
      		 The country:
      • has embraced the Enlightenment
      • is employing an Administrative advisor with at least skill 3
      • is employing a Diplomatic advisor with at least skill 3
      • is employing a Military advisor with at least skill 3
      • is not running a deficit
      		 The country:
      • gains 25 prestige
      • gets "Weimar Classicism" for 20 years, giving:
        • +1 Advisor pool
        • −15% Advisor cost
      		 Wittenberg University
      Spread the von Wettins
      The House of von Wettins has a long and distinguished history. To safeguard the prestige and influence we have accumulated into the future, we should seek further titles for our House and make sure that von Wettins' occupy as many thrones as possible.
      		 If is a monarchy and uses heir mechanics, one of the following must be true:
      • Countries ruled by members of the current dynasty (including colonial nations) are present on at least 5 continents.
      • At least 4 countries who are ruled by members of the current dynasty have at least 120 opinion towards the country

      Otherwise, at least 5 countries:

      • are allied to or subjects of the country
      • have at least 120 opinion towards the country
      • have the same government type as the country
      		 If this county is a monarchy and uses heir mechanics:
      • the country gets "Dynastic Network" for 20 years, giving:
        • +1 Diplomatic reputation
        • −15% Diplomatic annexation cost
        • +15% Improve relations

      Otherwise, the country gets "Saxon Influence" for 20 years, giving:

      • +1 Diplomatic reputation
      • +15% Improve relations

      If the country and the Commonwealth (else Poland) are an independent Christian monarchies:

      • the country gains a "Restoration of Union" casus belli on the Commonwealth/ Poland

      Otherwise, the country gains permanent claims on the following areas:

      • Wielkopolska
      • Central PolandWeser
      • Malopolska
      • Sandomierz
      		 Wittenberg University
      Victor for the True Faith
      For the good of Europe, we must make sure that we win the Wars of the Reformation for our [Root.Religion.GetName] faith.
      		 If the country is a member of the HRE:
      • One of the following must be true:
        • This country's religion is the official faith of the Holy Roman Empire.
        • The Wars of the Religious Leagues are over and no neighboring country follows a heretic religion.

      Otherwise,

      • The Reformation began at least 50 years ago.
      • No neighboring country follows a heretic religion.
      		 The country gets "A Victory of Faith" for the rest of the game, giving:
      • +1 Tolerance of the true faith
      		 Wittenberg University
      Become King
      Saxony will never be seen as anything more than a second-rate power until we can claim for ourselves the title of King.
      		 One of the following must be true:
      • The country is a kingdom
      • The country has any subject country who:
        • Is the junior partner in a personal union
        • Is a kingdom
        • Has less than 50% liberty desire
      		 The country:
      • gains 50 Administrative power
      • gains 50 Diplomatic power
      • gains 50 Military power
      • If is a duchy:
        • Becomes a kingdom
      • Otherwise,
        • gains 33 prestige
      		 Spread the von Wettins
      King of Poland
      We should cement our rising power by binding one of our biggest potential threats to us as a possession of the saxon duke.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 10 provinces in the following areas:
      • Wielkopolska
      • Central Poland
      • Malopolska
      • Sandomierz
      		 The country:
      • gains 25 prestige
      • gains 50 Diplomatic power
      • gets "Lord of Poland" for 20 years, giving:
        • +2 Diplomatic reputation
      		 Spread the von Wettins

      Regional missions[edit | edit source]

      European missions[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Global Dominance
      The acquisition of wealth is one thing; but being wealthier than all others, is a sign of true mastery. Grow our home trade node, and own a controlling stake in it.
      		 The country:
      • Is the strongest trade power in its home node which:
        • Is the highest valued trade node in the world
      		 Country:
      • Gains 30 convertible[2] prestige
      • Gains “World Renowned” for 20 years, giving:
        • +15% Trade efficiency
      Faith's Bastion
      Heresy must not be tolerated. The [Root.Religion.GetName] Church needs a pious role-model to light the way.
      		 The country:
      • Is the Defender of the Faith
      • Has at least 100% religious unity

      It is NOT the Age of Discovery

      		The country:
      • Gains a 50% cheaper skill 3 theologian advisor of our culture and religion
      • Gains “Armor of Righteousness” for 15 years, giving:
        • +5% Morale of armies
        • +1% Missionary strength vs heretics

      Holy Roman Empire missions[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Imperial Ambition
      Like all Princes of the Holy Roman Empire, we have our eye on the throne. We should stir up some support from the electorate.
      		 If the Holy Roman Empire exists:
      • Has at least 2 Electors backing us to become Emperor

      Else:

      • The country has at least 3 3 allies
      		 The country:
      • Gains 10 convertible[2] prestige
      • Gains “Imperial Graces” for 15 years, giving:
        • +1 Diplomat
        • +25% Improve relations
      Charlemagne's Legacy
      In times immemorial, the Carolingian Kings ruled over the great and Holy Roman Empire. Their legacy has not been forgotten, and neither has their ambition. We must be crowned Emperor.
      		 The country:
      • If the Holy Roman Empire exists:
        • Is the Holy Roman Emperor
      • Else:
        • Is an Empire
      		 The country:
      • Gains a 50% cheaper skill 2 statesman advisor of our culture and religion
      • Gains “Imperator Romanorum” for 15 years, giving:
        • +3 Diplomatic reputation
      		 Imperial Ambition
      Defend the Empire
      Over the course of time, many of our Princes have foolishly lost territory to the uncivilized nations beyond the Imperial borders. Unfortunately, that makes us look weak. Recover them.
      		 If the Holy Roman Empire exists:
      • The country is the Holy Roman Emperor
      • The "Call for Reichsreform" Imperial reform is enacted
      • Ever provinces in the Empire is under the control of a member state or its subject

      Else:

      • The country owns every core province
      		 The country:
      • Gains 10 prestige
      • If the Holy Roman Empire exists:
        • Gains 10 Imperial authority
        • Gains “Imperial Unity” for 15 years, giving:
          • +0.10 Monthly imperial authority
      		 Charlemagne's Legacy
