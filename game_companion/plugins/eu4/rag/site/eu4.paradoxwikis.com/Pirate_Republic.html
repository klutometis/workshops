Pirate republic

  • Language

(Redirected from Pirate Republic)
Available only with the Golden Century DLC enabled.

A pirate republic is a form of republic where power is held by a loose collection of pirate crews.

Instead of estates, pirate republics have access to unique factions representing the shifting balance of power dictating their government: the Buccaneers, the Smugglers and the Captains. They can reenable their estates through unique government reforms.

Through eventEdit

Main article: Piratical_events#Formation_events

Historical and formable pirate republics are tied to their regions and have different conditions to be created. See their pages to find out the specifics.

Releasing a former pirate republic tag as a vassal or through peace deal creates a regular non-pirate country.

Through decisionEdit

Players can convert their nation to a Pirate Republic if they meet certain criteria. Most importantly, they need to be located on an island or in the Mahgreb.

Pirate ElectionEdit

Pirate elections work mostly the same as the elections of normal republics, besides the fact that electing a candidate will increase the influence of that candidates faction by 20. However they have one important additional feature: legendary pirates. A ruler with the   Legendary Pirate trait can be reelected at no cost to   republican tradition. Additionally, depending on the location of the republic's capital region and the date of the game, special historical legendary pirates will be available in the election event. Electing a historical pirate for the first time will also usually gift the nation a   heavy ship, named after that pirate's flagship (this ship will be a flagship if the nation does not already have one).

Coastal raidingEdit

Main article: Naval_warfare#Coastal_Raiding

Pirate republics get an extra source of income by raiding coasts of other non-allied nations (or nations with relations over 100) for   ducats and   sailors. Use 3-4 light ships or 4-5 galleys.

War Against the WorldEdit

Pirate republic can enact tier 6 reform War Against the World. This gives the country a casus belli against all other countries and an ability to demand provinces at   0 diplomatic cost.

It also disallows   slaves. When a country passes this reform, all its provinces producing   slaves are immediately set to produce   “unknown”. This will also remove any Trade Stations in the province if present, as well as the province modifier “Slave Entrepot”. A new trade good will be randomly reassigned at the beginning of the next month based on the new weights for that province.

StrategiesEdit

GeneralEdit

Pirate republics is a very powerful government type that receives huge income during early game from coastal raiding, and an access to early imperialism-like casus belli. Being in Asia provides an extra benefit of being able to cause early collapse of   Ming through   devastation caused by coastal raiding. See Emperor_of_China#Mandate

However, it's impossible to get good outcome during Court and Country disaster, arguably making it optimal to switch away from this government type during   Age of Absolutism.

PrivateeringEdit

Sending a fleet to privateer will create trade power =   ship trade power × (1 +   privateer efficiency) × 1,5 .

Therefore, to increase the trade power of privateers, increasing ship trade power is an equally important factor in privateering and should not be neglected.

Base ship trade power:

Barque 2.0
Caravel 2.5
Early Frigate 3.0
Frigate 3.5
Heavy Frigate 4.0
Great Frigate 5.0

Having a flagship with the "Trade Route Map" or "Portuguese Trade Route Map" modification in a fleet will give every ship +2/+4 trade power.

  trade power from light ships: +254%

  • +33% - Doctrine: Merchant navy
  • +30% - Maneuver skill 6 of the fleet's admiral
  • +33% - Policy Mercenary-Trade: Merchants for Hire Act
  • +33% - Policy Naval-Trade: Fortified Trading Posts
  • +20% - Policy Maritime-Plutocratic: The Protected Shipping Lanes Act
  • +30% - Cartagena de Indias Fort System great project  in Cartagena, Columbia
  • +30% - Bara Katra great project   in Dhaka, Bengal
  • +30% - Kilwa City great project  in Kilwa, East Africa
  • +15% - "Extortioner" Admiral Trait


Privateer efficiency: +406%

  • up to +25% - scaling with naval tradition
  • +33% - Reform tier 2: Council of Captains
  • +25% - Maritime idea 7: Naval Fighting Instruction
  • +25% - Enlist Privateers decision (after completing Maritime ideas)
  • +33% - Espionage idea 6: Privateers
  • +50% - Policy Offensive-Exploration: Letters of Marque
  • +50% - Policy Mercenary-Exploration: Transportation Act
  • +15% - parliament debate "Issue Letters of Marque"
  • +25% - Flagship with "Spare Jolly Rogers" modification in fleet
  • +50% - Visby great project  in Gotland
  • +50% - Tortuga island great project   in Tortuga
  • +25% - "Legendary Pirate" ruler trait

This list is not exhaustive, many modifiers are only available as certain nations (i.e:   Netherlands Powerful VOC Indiaman ships or   Gotland Mission rewards), are only temporary, don't fit in a pirate playstyle (i.e: being in a trade league gives +20%  ), or are mutually exclusive with a better bonus (the leader traits "Extortioner" and "Buccaneer" give both +15% their bonus). All mentioned policies are diplomatic, and can therefore not be used at the same time without increasing diplomatic possible policies by 2.

Example with 1 policy removed: 10 Caravels with ship trade power +234% = 83,5 trade power.

Privateering efficiency +406%

Privateer power = 422,51, times 1,5 for any privateer mission: 633,7

In comparison, sending 10 Caravels without any modifiers to protect trade would add 25. In 1444, the trade node of Sevilla has around 270 trade power from its provinces.
