      what causes a country to be primitive?[edit source]

      Technology#Primitive says ""Primitives" refers to nations in the following technology groups:", but I don't think that this is the case. I tested this with a bunch of custom nations and it seems that three conditions have to be met to make a country primitive at the start of the game:

      • North American, Mesoamerican, Andean or South American tech group
      • inti, maya or nahuatl religion or native council government reform
      • the DLC is enabled which enables the reform mechanic for the chosen religion/government form (El Dorado or Conquest of Paradise)

      Without the El Dorado DLC, all the normal Inti, Maya and Nahuatl countries start as non-primitive and without Conquest of Paradise all the native council countries start as non-primitive. Furthermore any country can become primitive if they change their religion to Inti, Maya or Nahuatl while the El Dorado DLC is active. The first condition seems to only apply to custom nations, because Castile started as primitive in a test that I did when I changed their religion to nahuatl or their government to native council with a mod(which changed nothing else). --Grotaclas (talk) 19:49, 8 February 2021 (UTC)
