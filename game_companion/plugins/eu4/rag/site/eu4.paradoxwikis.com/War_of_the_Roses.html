      War of the Roses
      Following the death of our monarch our kingdom is being torn apart by supporters of two junior branches of the royal dynasty.

      The War of the Roses[1] is a disaster in England during the ‘Age of Discovery’.

      Prerequisites[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The country:

      • is England.
      • is a monarchy.
      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • does not use doom mechanic.
      • did not have this disaster before.

      It is currently the ‘Age of Discovery’.

      If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

      Monthly progress starts if Monthly progress halts if

      England:

      • does not have an ongoing disaster.
      • has a ruler from the "York", "Lancaster" or "Plantagenet" dynasty.
      • one of the following:
        • does not have an heir.
        • Has an heir of other than "York", "Lancaster" or "Plantagenet" dynasty.
      • does not have a regency.
      • is not the lesser partner in a personal union.

      It is currently the ‘Age of Discovery’.

      Monthly progress England:
      +3.0%

      Has a ruler with the modifier “Low Fertility”[2] or the ‘Infertile’ personality
      The year is at least 1455.

      +1.0%

      Legitimacy is less than 90%.

      +0.5%

      Is not ruled by the 'York' dynasty.

      +0.5%

      Is not ruled by the 'Lancaster' dynasty.

      +0.5%

      stability is less than 1.

      +0.5%

      Ruler's administrative skill is less than 2.

      +0.5%

      Ruler's diplomatic skill is less than 2.

      +0.5%

      Ruler's military skill is less than 2.

      War of the Roses

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      [Root.Monarch.GetName] has suddenly died! As the nation mourns, two junior branches of the old House of Plantagenet, the House of Lancaster and the House of York, both claim to be the rightful heir to the throne of England. Sporadic fights between their supporters have already broken out in a number of provinces around the country. Their heraldic symbols, a red rose and a white rose, have already become synonymous with the conflict now commonly known as the War of the Roses.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      the start of the ‘War of the Roses’ disaster.

      Support the House of Lancaster

      England:

      • loses 2 stability.
      • loses manpower worth 2.5 years of manpower.
      • supports the House of Lancaster during the ‘War of the Roses’.
      • gets a new ruler of the Lancaster dynasty, aged 31, with ‘Well Connected’ personality.

      If England uses Statists vs Orangists mechanics then:

      • strengthen Orangists in power by 100%.

      pretender rebels (size 3) led by ‘Richard York’ rise in revolt and occupy:

      • if owned and there are no units in the province: Yorkshire (245).
      • else: a random province of England in Britain (region).

      The revolting province gets the modifier “Supports House of York” for 10 years, giving the following effect:

      • +8 local unrest.
      Support the House of York

      England:

      • loses 2 stability.
      • loses manpower worth 2.5 years of manpower.
      • supports the House of York during the ‘War of the Roses’.
      • gets a new ruler of the York dynasty, aged 31, with ‘Tactical Genius’ personality.

      If England uses Statists vs Orangists mechanics then:

      • strengthen Orangists in power by 100%.

      pretender rebels (size 3) led by ‘Edward Lancaster’ rise in revolt and occupy:

      • if owned and there are no units in the province: Lancashire (244).
      • else: a random province of England in Britain (region).

      The revolting province gets the modifier “Supports House of Lancaster” for 10 years, giving the following effect:

      • +8 local unrest.

      Modifiers[edit | edit source]

      While the disaster is ongoing, England gets:

      • +5 global unrest.
      • +12.5% Mercenary manpower

      Also the following events are triggered by a monthly pulse.

      The End of the War of the Roses

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The armed conflict over the succession to the English throne is finally over as the ruling dynasty defeats the rebels!

       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      the end of the ‘War of the Roses’ disaster.

      Immediate effects

      England[3]:

      • loses the modifiers “Low Fertility”, “Importing Food”, “Plundering Soldiers” and “Legitimacy Questioned”.

      All provinces owned by England:

      • lose the modifiers “Supports House of York”, “Supports House of Lancaster”, “Local Support”, “Rebel Refugees” and “Rebel Stronghold”.
      Order has finally been restored

      England:

      • gains 50 prestige.
      If England has a stability of less than +3,
      • then it gains 1 stability,
      • else it gains 50 administrative power.

      If England supported the House of York at the start of the disaster:

      • every country that had the event ‘Support House of Lancaster’ and chose to support the rebels
        • gets the event ‘Relations Deteriorate with England (Lancaster lost)’ the next day.

      If England supported the House of Lancaster at the start of the disaster:

      • every country that had the event ‘Support House of York’ and chose to support the rebels
        • gets the event ‘Relations Deteriorate with England (York lost)’ the next day.

      If the rebels win the War of the Roses one of the following events will occur.
