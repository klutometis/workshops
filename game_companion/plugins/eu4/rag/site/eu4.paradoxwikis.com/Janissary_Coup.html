      Janissary Coup
      Discontent with what their lack of direct influence over the government, the Janissary Corps have revolted against their sovreign and taken control over the state.

      The Janissary Coup[1][2] is a disaster for countries that have enacted the ‘Ottoman Government’ (tier 1 monarchy) government reform (only available for the Ottomans) from the 'Age of Reformation' onwards. With Cradle of Civilization, it triggers from over-reliance on Janissary regiments. It cant happen if Domination is enabled

      Prerequisites[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.31.
      • The game is set to use normal or historical nations.
      • The country:
        • Has enacted "Ottoman Government" reform
        • did not have this disaster before.

      If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

      Monthly progress starts if Monthly progress halts if
      • It is at least the 'Age of Reformation'
      • The country:
        • does not have an ongoing disaster.
        • has stability of less than 0
        • If Cradle of Civilization is active:
          • has Janissary units comprising at least 20% of the force limit.
          • has at least 20 Janissary regiments.
        • If Cradle of Civilization is not active:
          • has the modifier “The Janissaries”.[3]
        • Ruler's administrative skill is less than 5.
        • Ruler's diplomatic skill is less than 5.
        • Ruler's military skill is less than 5.
      Monthly progress Condition
      +2.0% Is bankrupt.
      +1.0% Has at least 50 loans.
      +1.0% Is not a great power.
      +1.0% Has an army tradition of less than 70.
      +1.0% Has an army tradition of less than 50 and Cradle of Civilization is not active.
      +0.5% Janissary units comprises at least 10% of the force limit.
      +1.0% Janissary units comprises at least 20% of the force limit.
      +2.0% Janissary units comprises at least 30% of the force limit.
      +1.0% Has ruler modifier “Janissaries Denied Reward”.
      +1.0% Has country modifier “Janissary Landholders”.
      +1.0% Has country modifier “Janissaries Upset”.
      • At least one of the following is true for the country:
        • has an ongoing disaster.
        • has +3 stability.
        • If Cradle of Civilization is active:
          • has Janissary units comprising less than 5% of the force limit.
        • If Cradle of Civilization is not active:
          • has an army of less than 15% of the force limit.

      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      the start of ‘Janissary Coup’ disaster.

      Most worrisome.

      The country:

      • loses 20 prestige.
      • loses 10 legitimacy.
      • loses 2 stability
      • loses all hired advisors.
      • loses the modifiers:
        • “Janissaries Denied Reward”,
        • “Janissary Landholders”,
        • “Janissaries Upset”.
        • “Enforced Discipline Among the Janissaries”.
        • “Strictly Enforced Discipline”.
      • If the Janissaries estate exists :
        • Janissaries loses 80 loyalty

      Modifiers[edit | edit source]

      While the disaster is ongoing the country gets:

      • +25% technology cost
      • +25% idea cost
      • −1 yearly legitimacy

      Also the following events are triggered by a monthly pulse.

      Finishing conditions[edit | edit source]

      At least one of the following is true:

      • All of the following:
        • The disaster has been active for at least 5 years
        • Has no active revolt[6]
        • Has at least +2 stability
      • Has disbanded the Janissaries.

      Then the country loses the above-mentioned effects and the following event is triggered:

      id

      The following description is used if the janissaries were disbanded while the country didn't have the modifier The Janissaries:

      By forcing the inspection of all rolls of membership in the Janissaries [Root.Monarch.GetName] has finally managed to impose control over the corps again. All bad elements and non-soldiers are being weeded out of the system and will no longer receive state salaries or the status associated with being Janissaries. This is not something the former Janissaries take lightly however, while the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] has the authority to carry out these measures some are taking up arms against their former monarch.
