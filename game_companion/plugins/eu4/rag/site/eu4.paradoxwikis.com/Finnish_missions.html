      National missions[edit | edit source]

      Independence and expansion[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.34.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Raise Our Army
      Our Duchy may be of no significance as it's overshadowed by its neighbors. Nevertheless we must raise our armed forces in order to push for and protect our independence!
      		 Finland:
      • Has an army size of at least 100% of its force limit
      • At least one of:
        • Is employing any Military advisor
        • Owns at least 1 cavalry regiment
      • At least one of:
        • Has at least 100% liberty desire
        • Is not a subject nation
        • Is a subject of Russia which has completed the "Slay the Lion of the North" mission
      		 Finland:
      • Gains “Savage Tactics” for 15 years, giving:
        • +33% Movement speed
        • +15% Morale damage
      • Unlock mercenary company “Hakkapeliitta Cavalry” (This mercenary company does not cost army professionalism when hired.)
      • Gains a permanent claim on South Karelia area
      • If at least 10 provinces are core of Sweden:
        • Gains a permanent claim on every province with Sweden's core
      • Else:
        • Gains a permanent claim on the Scandinavia region
      Defend Independence
      Liberty is most sweet to those who have tasted the bitterness of servitude. Let us strive for our autonomy and undo the shackles of our so-called sovereign! Finland shall be free.
      		 Finland:
      • Has at least 0 stability
      • Has no loan
      • Is not a subject nation (unless they are a subject of Russia which has completed the "Slay the Lion of the North" mission)
      • Has at least 1 ally (unless they are a subject of Russia which has completed the "Slay the Lion of the North" mission)
      		 Finland:
      • Gain ruler modifier “Era of the Great Thunder” until ruler's death, giving:
        • +1 Monthly military power
        • −20% Core-creation cost
      • Gain permanent claims on every province within the Baltic Trade Node
      • If total develoment has increased by:
        • At least 50: Ruler gains 3 Military skill.[2]
        • At least 25: Ruler gains 2 Military skill.[2]
        • Less than 25: Ruler gains 1 Military skill.[2]
      • If stability is:
        • At least +3: Ruler gains 3 Administrative skill.[3]
        • At least +2: Ruler gains 2 Administrative skill.[3]
        • Less than +2: Ruler gains 1 Administrative skill.[3]
      • If the number of allies is:
        • At least 3: Ruler gains 3 Diplomatic skill.[4]
        • At least 2: Ruler gains 2 Diplomatic skill.[4]
        • Less than 2: Ruler gains 1 Diplomatic skill.[4]
      		 Raise Our Army
      Break Stockholm
      The capital of the decadent Kingdom of Sweden is nothing but a trinket, yet another important city to fall, facing our mighty army. Its addition to our collection of coastal ports will certainly serve to increase our legitimacy and influence in the region.
      		 Finland or its non-tributary subjects owns province Stockholm (1) Province Stockholm (1):
      • Renames province to Tukholma
      • Renames provincial capital to Tukholma
      • Gains 1 base tax
      • Gains 1 base production
      • Gains 1 base manpower

      Finland:

      • Gets access to a discounted trader advisor (50% to employ)
      • If is a Duchy:
        • Set government rank to Kingdom
      • Else:
        • Gains +25 power projection
      		 Defend Independence
      Seize the Means
      The former King of Sweden employed the mines of Dalaskogen for his own profit. Controlling the production of Europe's prime source of copper is a profitable venture. Let us liberate the province and its people from Swedish rule, they have nothing to lose but their chains!
      		 Finland or its non-tributary subjects owns province Dalaskogen (8) Finland:
      • Gains 10 years of production income from Copper or Iron production
      • If province Dalaskogen (8) great project Falun Copper Mine is not magnificent:
        • Province Dalaskogen (8) great project Falun Copper Mine gains 1 tier
      • Else:
        • Province Dalaskogen (8) gains 3 base production
      • If the ruler has less than 3 traits:
        • Ruler gains the personality trait Industrious
      • Else:
        • Ruler gains 1 Diplomatic skill.[4]
      		 Defend Independence
      St. Olav Tower
      The East territories of our domain are inhabited by wild and bloodthirsty principalities, vying to control the throne of the Rus people. We must secure a frontline and develop adequate defenses lest we fall pray to their expansionist tendencies.
      • All provinces in the South Karelia area are owned by Finland or its non-tributary subjects (unless they are a subject of Russia which has completed the "Slay the Lion of the North" mission)
      • Province Viborg (30):
        • Is owned by Finland (unless they are a subject of Russia which has completed the "Slay the Lion of the North" mission)
        • Has any Fort building
        • Has at least 8 Base manpower
      		 Province Viborg (30):
      • Gains 2 base manpower
      • Gains “Viipurin Linna” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +2 Local defender dice roll bonus
        • +3 Maximum attrition
        • +3 Attrition for enemies

      If province Åland (1930) has any Fort building and is owned by Finland or its non-tributary subjects:

      • Finland gains “Finnish Militarization” for 20 years, giving:
        • +2 Attrition for enemies
      		 Defend Independence
      A Baltic Empire
      Scaninavia and the seas of the Baltic have been the home of major empires across the ages. Due to recent attempts of foreign powers, we must strive to protect our coasts and assert our dominance over our coasts!
      		 Finland or its non-tributary subjects owns every province within the Baltic Trade Node Finland:
      • Trigger country event “Finnish Imperial Ambitions”
      • If is not an Empire
        • Set government rank to Empire
      • Else:
        • Gains 50 prestige
      • Gains “Karoliinit” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • Enable the ability to recruit Caroleans
      • If has had a golden age:
        • Enables a second Golden Age
      • Else, if is in a golden age
        • Extend the current golden age by 240 months
      • Else:
        • Gains 100 administrative power
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
        • Gains 100 military power

      Break Stockholm
      Seize the Means
      St. Olav Tower

      Control the Tar Trade
      Shipbuilding is a stable step towards developing a maritime presence in the Baltic seas. Tar, a substance used in ships, is commonplace in the Northern shores yet incredibly vital for our naval ventures in the future.
      • Finland controls at least 50% of the trade in the Novgorod trade node (unless they are a subject of Russia which has completed the "Slay the Lion of the North" mission)
      • Province Neva (33):
        • Is owned by Finland or their non- tributary subjects
        • Has any Shipyard building
        • Has any Trade building
      		 Province Neva (33):
      • Changes province's goods produced to Naval Supplies
      • Builds a Naval equipment manufactory
      • Gains “Commercial Tar” for 25 years, giving:
        • +2 Local goods produced
        • −25% Local ship cost
      		 St. Olav Tower
      The White Death
      It's time for our great nation to shed the attire of a modest domain of provinces and unleash our great might upon the unsuspecting giants of the old continent. Tales of our military might will be born from our many campaigns to come.
      		 Finland:
      • Is Defender of the Faith (unless they are a subject of Russia which has completed the "Slay the Lion of the North" mission)
      • Has at least 100% religious unity
      • Has an army of at least 100 regiments
      • Has +3 stability
      • Has at least 40 development in its capital
      		 Finland gains “White Death Upon Europe” for the rest of the game, giving:
      • +20% Fort defense
      • +1 Attrition for enemies
      • +1 Max hostile attrition
      • +1 Combat bonus in terrain of capital

      The country of Scandinavia no longer needs an Administrative Technology of 20 to be formed.

      Turku Academy
      Control the Tar Trade
      A Baltic Empire

      Internal development[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.34.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Agricultural Communities
      Our nation's economy is primarly based on hunting wild game and agriculture. It would be sensible to improve our production sector and thus build a strong foundation for a prosperous nation.
      		 Finland:
      • Has increased its total development by at least 30
      • Has at least 0 stability
      • Owns any province producing Fish or Grain which:
        • At least one of:
          • Has been developed at least 4 times
          • Has any Production building
      		 Finland gains “Abundance of Food” for 15 years, giving:
      • −15% Development cost
      • −25% Construction time

      Every owned province with a Production building and either Fish or Grain:

      • Gains 1 base production
      Celebrate our Culture
      Despite our success in military and administrative matters, the question of our national identity is slowly formulating within the chambers of the royal residence. We must soon choose the path of our nation, be it of tolerance or supremacy of culture.
      		 At least one of:
      • Finland owns at least 12 provinces with 'Finnish' culture
      • Finland has a cultural union
      • Is a subject of Russia which has completed the "Slay the Lion of the North" mission
      		 If Finland completes the mission via cultural union:
      • Gains “A Celebrated Melting Pot of Cultures” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +2 Max promoted cultures
        • −1 National unrest

      Alternatively:

      • Gains “Accelerated Assimilation” for 25 years, giving:
        • −33% Culture conversion cost
        • −10 Years of separatism
      		 Agricultural Communities
      Integrate the Sami
      The Sami are a people living in the dense forests of the North, where no other Scandinavian - be it Swede, Norwegian or Dane - lives. They do not trade with the world outside their territory, and are completely isolated. They are, however, living in territory, which formally belongs to us. We should integrate them into our society so we can benefit from these forests.
      		 Every province in the Laponia, Norrland and Ostrobothnia areas:
      • Is owned by Finland
      • Has Finland's religion
      • Has a culture accepted by Finland (unless they are a subject of Russia which has completed the "Slay the Lion of the North" mission)
      		 Finland:
      • Unlocks the “Forest Expansion” estate privilege

      Every province in the Laponia, Norrland and Ostrobothnia areas:

      • Loses 100% local autonomy
      • Gains 2 base manpower
      		 Agricultural Communities
      Finetune Our Finances
      The Finnish court must be known as frugal and responsible. We cannot build a credible reputation in the financial markets of our region without tending to our fiscal ledgers and making sure that our finances are just fine.
      		 Finland:
      • Has increased its monthly income by 500% of the starting monthly income
      • Is employing any Administrative advisor of at least level 2 (unless they are a subject of Russia which has completed the "Slay the Lion of the North" mission)
      • Is not running a deficit
      • Has no loan
      • If the ruler has less than 3 traits:
        • Ruler gains the personality trait Well Connected
      • Else:
        • Ruler gains 2 Administrative skill.[3]

      Every new Administrative advisor will grant +5% National Tax Modifier per level.

      		Agricultural Communities

      City improvment[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.34.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Build Turku
      The centralization of a nation commences as the ruler, with a steady hand, guides his people towards a common center. For us, this center must be a prosperous town overlooking the bountiful Baltic coast.
      		 Province Åbo (27):
      • Has at least 3 buildings
      • Has been developed at least 5 times
      • Has less than 1 devastation
      		 Province Åbo (27):
      • Renames province to Turku
      • Renames provincial capital to Turku
      • Gains 2 base tax
      • Gains 100 progress towards prosperity
      City of Helsinki
      Continuing on the path to urbanize our nation, we must strive forward and found new settlements buzzing with merchant and peasant activity, as a symbol of our unstoppable and unquenchable growth!
      		 Finland:
      • Has embraced all available institutions
      • Onw province Nyland (28) which:
        • Has at least 30 development
        • Has any manufactory
        • Has at least 4 buildings
      		 Finland:
      • Gains 3% mercantilism

      Province Nyland (28):

      • Creates a level 2 center of trade
      • If has a Dock
        • Replaces Dock by a Drydock
      • Else:
        • Builds a Dock
      • If has a Shipyard
        • Replaces Shipyard by a Grand Shipyard
      • Else:
        • Builds a Shipyard
      		 Build Turku
      Turku Academy
      Despite our tending to administrative and military matters in a proficient manner, it is abundantly clear that we have largely neglected the advanced education of our people. Let the growth of our minds be the guiding principle of our nation.
      		 Finland:
      • Has at least +2 stability
      • Owns province Åbo (27) which:
        • At least one of:
          • Has University building
          • Has been developed at least 12 times
        • Has less than 1 devastation
        • Has at least 50% Prosperity
      		 Province Åbo (27):
      • Gains 25 progress towards the next non-present institution
      • If does not have a University
        • Build a University
      • Else:
        • Gains 2 base production

      Finland:

      • Gains 2 innovativeness
      • If the ruler has less than 3 traits:
        • Ruler gains the personality trait Scholar
      • Else:
        • Ruler gains 2 Administrative skill.[3]

      St. Olav Tower
      City of Helsinki
      Finetune Our Finances
