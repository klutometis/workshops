      Formation decisions[edit | edit source]

      Main articles: Formation decisions, Formable countries

      Country formation decisions are kept on the pages of each formable country. All formable countries are listed below.

      Forming a country through decision[edit | edit source]

      Generally, a nation must meet the following requirements to form most formables:

      • Target formable does not exist
      • Current nation is not an end-game tag
      • At peace
      • Is not a non-tributary subject
      • Is not a Steppe Horde
      • Own and core certain provinces (Some provinces can also be owned by non-tributary subjects)

      And forming a country will have the following effects:

      • A new name and color; often some prestige, permanent claims and the "Increased Centralization" modifier.
      • Most formable countries have unique national ideas. An event triggered by the formation decision gives a choice between switching ideas or keeping the existing set. Note that custom nations do not get the event this way.
      • Formation of a new country changes "historical rival" attitudes regarding other countries. For example, if Christian Ottomans form Roman Empire, they will no longer be historical rivals with Austria. This also applies inversely; if Burgundy form France, they will become historical rivals with Austria.
      • Forming another nation while being the Emperor of China resets all reforms made as the previous nation. This is important to keep in mind if looking to form Qing or Yuan.
      • Forming a country may require your capital to be in a region, or it may move your capital. This may void your trade company investments, and if you don't have a main trade port it may move your home node.
      • Forming a country resets any expanded infrastructure done in any provinces. Centralized states remain centralized however.
      • All separatism in provinces owned at the time of formation is removed as soon as they are cores - instantly if they already are cores. This is very useful to get rid of rebels when expanding rapidly.

      Declare Statute in Restraint of Appeals

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      Officially demote the Pope and instead raise the king to be the final legal authority in all matters of religion.

       
      Potential requirements

      The country:

      • does not have the modifier “The Statute in Restraint of Appeals”.
      • is Catholic.
      • is not The Papal State.

      The Papal State exists.

      		Allow

      The country:

      • ruler's administrative skill is at least 2.
      • ruler's military skill is at least 2.

      The Papal State has an opinion of less than −50 towards the country.

      Effects

      The country:

      • gains 10 prestige.
      • gets the modifier “The Statute in Restraint of Appeals” until the decision ‘Revoke Restraint of Appeals’ is enacted or the religion is changed to Protestant or Reformed, with the following effects:
        • −10% stability cost modifier,
        • −1 national unrest,
        • +0.5 yearly prestige,
        • −10 yearly papal influence,
        • +5% Clergy loyalty.

      Catholicism gains 1% reform desire.
      Every neighbouring Catholic countries:

      • gets the opinion modifier “Demoted the Pope and raised the king to have religious authority” ( −60 opinion / +1 yearly decay / 10 years) towards the country for 10 years.

      The Papal State:

      • gets the opinion modifier “The Pope does not appreciate the demotion” ( −100 opinion / +1 yearly decay / 10 years) towards the country for 10 years.
