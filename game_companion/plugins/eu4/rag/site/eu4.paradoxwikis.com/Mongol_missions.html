This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

Available only with the Winds of Change DLC enabled.
See also: Mongol missions without winds of change

This is a list of all missions for Mongol nations (see table below) and for Yuan when not formed by Tatar or Moghulistan nations[1]. For the missions without Winds of Change, see Mongol missions without winds of change. The Golden Horde, Ilkhanate and Mongol Empire also have these missions when formed by a country which was a Mongol nation and which was never a Tatar or Moghulistan nation. The branching missions can be previewed and selected with the buttons right of the mission window after completing the mission Matters of Religion (the AI chooses the branch randomly).

Yuan missions include most of these as well.
