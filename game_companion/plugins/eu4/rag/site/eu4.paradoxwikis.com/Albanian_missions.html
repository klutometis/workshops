      National missions[edit | edit source]

      Resist the Turkish invasion[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Skanderbeg's Rebellion
      Skanderbeg has taken up the cause of resisting the Ottoman invasion of Albania. His heroic efforts have so far kept us safe from Turkish incursions, but he will need more men if he is to continue the insurrection.
      		 Albania:
      • Is not a subject nation
      • Has at least 1 stability
      • Has at least 100% of its land force limit
      • Owns province Lezhë (4175)
      		 Albania gains “Skanderbeg's Rebellion” for 20 years, giving:
      • +10% infantry combat ability
      • +10% movement speed
      Stand Against the Turks
      We must resist the Turks by every means we have available. To even survive this onslaught would be a feat of military and diplomatic genius.
      		 Albania:
      • Is not a subject nation
      • Owns core province Lezhë (4175)

      The year is at least 1468

      		Albania gets “Tales of Defiance” for 20 years, giving:
      • +1 diplomatic reputation
      • +20% improve relations

      Each of the following countries (if catholic):

      • Bohemia, Hungary, Lithuania, Poland and Teutonic Order gain opinion modifier “Legacy of Varna” ( +50 opinion / −1 yearly decay) towards Albania
      • The Papal State gains opinion modifier “Heroic Defenders” ( +100 opinion / −2 yearly decay) towards Albania
      • Naples, or its overlord if subject ( Aragon in 1444) gains opinion modifier “Nominal Fealty” ( +100 opinion / −2 yearly decay) towards Albania
      		 Skanderbeg's Rebellion
      Inspire Resistance
      We must resist the Turks by every means we have available. To even survive this onslaught would be a feat of military and diplomatic genius.
      		 If the Ottomans exist:
      • Albania wins a battle against the Ottomans

      Otherwise, Albania owns at least 10 provinces
      If the Ottomans exist and own any province in the Balkans region:

      • Albania has at least 50 spy network on the Ottomans

      Otherwise, Albania has at least 50 spy network on any rival

      		Albania gains “Inspiring Resistance” until the end of the game, giving:
      • +25% rebel support efficiency

      Every Ottomans' Christian provinces in the Balkans region gains +10 unrest

      		Stand Against the Turks

      Expand into the Balkans[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Lord of Albania
      The Turks are not the only unjust occupiers of Albanian lands. We must also expel the Italian merchant princes who have seized much of the coast. Albania will not be free until every last acre of land has been returned!
      		 All provinces in the Albania area are owned and cored by Albania Albania:
      • Gains 200 military power
      • Gains a claim on all Ottomans provinces in the Balkans region
      End Turkish Rule
      It is time to take the fight to our enemy. Skanderbeg's rebellion shall become the beginning of a Crusade, uniting the peoples of the Balkans against a common foe.
      		 All provinces in the Balkans region are not owned by the Ottomans Albania:
      • Ruler gains +1 monarch military skill
      • Gains “Legacy of Skanderbeg” until the end of the game, giving:
        • +1 monarch military skill
      		 Lord of Albania

      Regional missions[edit | edit source]

      European missions[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Global Dominance
      The acquisition of wealth is one thing; but being wealthier than all others, is a sign of true mastery. Grow our home trade node, and own a controlling stake in it.
      		 The country:
      • Is the strongest trade power in its home node which:
        • Is the highest valued trade node in the world
      		 Country:
      • Gains 30 convertible[2] prestige
      • Gains “World Renowned” for 20 years, giving:
        • +15% Trade efficiency
      Faith's Bastion
      Heresy must not be tolerated. The [Root.Religion.GetName] Church needs a pious role-model to light the way.
      		 The country:
      • Is the Defender of the Faith
      • Has at least 100% religious unity

      It is NOT the Age of Discovery

      		The country:
      • Gains a 50% cheaper skill 3 theologian advisor of our culture and religion
      • Gains “Armor of Righteousness” for 15 years, giving:
        • +5% Morale of armies
        • +1% Missionary strength vs heretics

      Generic missions[edit | edit source]

      Military missions[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Build to Force Limit
      Any growing country must maintain a reasonably sized army. By expanding our forces we can both further our political interests abroad and stimulate our domestic economy.
      		 The country has an army of at least 100% of its force limit The country:
      • Gains “Thriving Arms Industry” for 25 years, giving:
        • +5% Morale of armies
        • −5% Land maintenance modifier
      • Gains a claim on every neighboring province
      Expand Nation
      We have been given a strong and viable country by those that came before us. It is time we expand upon our legacy and grow [Root.GetName] so that we may leave a stronger and more influential country to our children.
      		 The country has increased its total development by at least 50 The capital:
      • Gains 1 base tax
      • Gains 1 base production
      • Gains 1 base manpower
      • Every owned provinces within the area gains “Growth of Capital” for 25 years, giving:
        • −10% Local construction cost
        • −10% Local development cost

      The country:

      • Gains a claim on every neighboring province
      		 Build to Force Limit
      Conquer New States
      As we expand we must ensure that we can truly integrate the new lands we conquer into our administrative apparatus. After conquest comes the hard work of establishing true [Root.GetAdjective] Rule over the conquered territories.
      		 The country has assigned at least 5 new states The country:
      • Gains 100 military power
      • Gains “Expansionist Policies” for 25 years, giving:
        • −25% State maintenance
        • −33% Harsh treatment cost
      • Gains a permanent claim on every neighboring area
      		 Expand Nation
      Assemble an Army
      Our territorial ambitions require a grand army. We must build and maintain a strong standing army that can defend and expand the lands under our rule.
      		 The country has an army with at least 80,000 troops The country:
      • Gains 20 convertible[3] army tradition
      • Gains “Army Enthusiasm” for 25 years, giving:
        • +10% Manpower recovery speed
        • +0.5 Yearly army tradition
      • Gains a permanent claim on every province in its home region
      		 Conquer New States
      Unite Home Region
      The time has come to put all our hard work to build a viable military machine to the test. We must rise to be the true hegemon of our region, respected and feared by all.
      		 All provinces in the region of its capital is owned by the country The country:
      • Gains 25 power projection
      • Gains “Hegemonic Ambition” for 25 years, giving:
        • −0.05 Monthly autonomy change
      • Gains a permanent claim on every neighboring area

      Every owned province in country's home region:

      • Loses 10 years of separatism
      		 Assemble an Army


      Diplomatic missions[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Trustworthy Allies
      No country can stand alone in this world. We must build lasting relationships with other countries if we are to be able to grow and prosper long term.
      		 The country has at least 2 allies with at least +150 opinion of us The country:
      • Gains a 50% cheaper skill 1 diplomat advisor of our culture and religion
      • Gains “Influential Diplomats” for 25 years, giving:
        • +1 Diplomatic reputation
      Acquire Subjects
      What good is power if no one recognizes it? We must acquire subject states that pay us the kind of respect one would expect from a country like [Root.GetName].
      		 The country has at least 2 non-tributary subjects The country:
      • Gains 100 diplomatic power
      • Gains “Obedient Subjects” for 25 years, giving:
        • −15% Diplomatic annexation cost

      Every subject:

      • Loses 10% liberty desire
      		 Trustworthy Allies
      Create a Grand Fleet
      In order to be able to project power overseas a high seas navy is a must. Warships may be expensive but it is an investment that is well worth it if we can find new markets to dominate and peoples to conquer.
      		 The country has either 10 heavy ships or 30 galleys The country:
      • Gains an admiral with at least 40 navy tradition
      • Gains “Naval Enthusiasm” for 25 years, giving:
        • +10% Morale of navies
        • +20% National sailors modifier
        • +1 Yearly naval tradition
      		 Acquire Subjects
      Dominate Trade
      Our merchant communities have been growing stronger and stronger for a long time now. It is time we give them the help they require to truly dominate our domestic trade. No foreign power must be allowed to influence the well being of our subjects.
      		 The country has at least 75% trade power in its home trade node The country:
      • Gains 3% convertible[4] mercantilism

      A random owned province in the home trade node gets “Dominant Trade Hub” for the rest of the game, giving:

      • −5% Local development cost
      • +10 Local trade power
      		 Create a Grand Fleet
      Expand Overseas
      As big as the world is it is still interconnected through a number of vital trade routes. In an ancient order cloth, spices, metals, bullion, Chinaware and all kind of manufactured goods are brought across deserts and oceans to those who would pay the most for them. Taking a share of this trade, through acquiring useful ports in trade regions far abroad, could make even the smallest power richer than Croesus.
      		 The country owns at least 3 trade companies which:
      • Are not on the same continent as the capital
      • Have at least 5 provinces
      		 The country:
      • Gains an explorer with at least 40 navy tradition
      • Gains “Growing East-Indian Trade” for 25 years, giving:
        • +15% Trade efficiency
        • +20% Global trade power
      		 Dominate Trade


      Administrative missions[edit | edit source]

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      High Income
      We must increase state income by any means necessary. Money is a requirement for any endeavor of the state, from the daily expenses of the government to the maintenance of armies to invade our enemies.
      		 The country has increased its starting monthly income by at least 150% The country:
      • Gains a 50% cheaper skill 1 treasurer advisor of our culture and religion
      • Gains “Growing Economy” for 25 years, giving:
        • −10% Construction cost
        • −25% Construction time
      Build Buildings
      Let us never sit idle, even in peaceful times. Instead we must use our resources with industry, in order to make sure that we have funds available in time if misfortune should strike upon us.
      		 The country:
      • Owns at least 5 owned provinces with any Tax building
      • Owns at least 5 owned provinces with any Production building
      		 The country:
      • Gains 50 administrative power
      • Gains “Building Spree” for 25 years, giving:
        • +10 Yearly tax income
      		 High Income
      Build a Treasury
      A large treasury is an imperative buffer for all things that could befall us through. If this is seen as miserly, it is because some do not realize that this vice is the very essence of what it means to reign.
      		 Country has at least 2 000 ducats The country:
      • Loses 2 corruption
      • Gains “Strong Treasury Department” for 25 years, giving:
        • +0.10 Yearly inflation reduction
      		 Build Buildings
      Build Manufactories
      Great advances are being made in the field of production, both technological and organizational. Let us invest greatly in new manufactories, a great industrious revolution might soon be upon us.
      		 Country has at least 20 manufactories The country:
      • Gains 1.5 years of income
      • Gains “Proto-Industrialization” for 25 years, giving:
        • +10% Production efficiency
      		 Build a Treasury
      Imperial Conquest
      Securing wealth must always be the foremost priority of our state. Let us make use of the expensive armies and fleets that our economic prosperity has bought us to acquire even more resources and subjects for an aspiring empire.
      		 Country has at least 1 000 total development The country:
      • Gains 30 convertible[2] prestige
      • Gains “Imperial Ambition” for 25 years, giving:
        • −5 Years of separatism
      • Gains a permanent claim on every neighboring area
      		 Build Manufactories
