This is a list of election events.[1]These events are used to choose a new ruler in a republic when the current ruler dies, is forced out of office by event or subject interaction, or their term ends.

 Election!

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

If the country has a ruler that is marked as pushed out[2] then:

With $MONARCH$ being forced out of office by the revolting factions a new [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] will have to be elected. As the votes are tallied there is an unusual excitement in the air. The three primary candidates are all acutely aware of the events that has transpired these last few days...

If the country has had "Committee of Public Safety" regent[3]

With the return of some degree of normalcy to the Republic, the Committee of Public Safety has agreed to stand down and allow elections to take place. Three primary candidates have been nominated to take over as [Root.Monarch.GetTitle].

else:

The term is up for $MONARCH$. With enough support [Root.Monarch.GetSheHe] can stay in office, but then [Root.Monarch.GetSheHe] must defeat [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] three primary competitors...''
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • does not use ‘Statists vs Monarchists’[4] mechanics.
  • does not use ‘Election by Lottery’ mechanics.
  • is not a Pirate Republic
  • is not a Military Dictatorship
  • is not a Presidential Dictator
  • is not a Stratocratic Administration
  • has not enacted Board of Admirals
  • does not have   "Bonfire of the Vanities disaster ongoing
  • if has had "Committee of Public Safety" regent[3]
    • has at least   50 Republican tradition
 Is triggered only by

one of the following:

  • the end of the election cycle in a republic,
  • choosing the option “Give in to their demands.” of the Violence in the Streets events.
  • the decision "force Re-election"
Option conditions

  Enabled if: the country:

  • does not have a regency.
  • does not have a ruler that is marked as pushed out[2].
  • if the reform "Civic Republicanism" is enacted :
    • This ruler hasnt been reelected before
 
Keep $MONARCH$

[Root.Monarch.GetName] ([Root.Monarch.GetAdm] £adm£ / [Root.Monarch.GetDip] £dip£ / [Root.Monarch.GetMil] £mil£ ) remains ruler.

The current ruler gains:

  •   1 administrative skill,
  •   1 diplomatic skill,
  •   1 military skill.

If the   Res Publica DLC is enabled and the country:

  • is not a tribe.
  • is a republic.
  • has less than   20 Republican tradition.
  • is not Revolutionary.
  • can fall under a Dictatorship

then

  • the country's government changes to Presidential Dictator.

If the country:

  • is a republic.
  • has less than   20 Republican tradition.
  • is Revolutionary.

then

  • the country becomes a   Monarchy
  • the country's government changes to Revolutionary Empire.

If the   Res Publica DLC is not enabled and the country:

  • is not a tribe.
  • is a republic.
  • has less than   20 Republican tradition.
  • can fall under a Dictatorship

then

  • the country becomes a   Monarchy

If the country has flag strengthened_signoria_flag[5]:

  • Remove flag strengthened_signoria_flag

Else, the country:

  • If increased_election_cost is set :
    • loses   3.75% republican tradition per election cycle year.
    • remove increased_election_cost flag
  • Else :
    • loses   2.5% republican tradition per election cycle year.

The country loses   5 karma.
One of the following happens at random:

  • The country gains   50 administrative power.
  • The country gains   50 diplomatic power.
  • The country gains   50 military power.

If the country has a government where Reelections reduce estate Influence :

  • All estates gain "Re-elected same Ruler" modifier for 4 years, reducing their influence by 10

If the country has a government where Reelections influence the estates :

  • All estates gain 5% Loyalty

If the country has a government where Reelections raise Absolutism :

  • Raise Absolutism by 10, multiplied by the amount of times the current Ruler has been reelected

If the country has a government where Reelections influence the factions :

  • add 20 influence to a random faction

  Always enabled:

 
Bureaucrat Candidate

A new ruler comes to power with the following (fixed) attributes:

  •   4 administrative skill,
  •   1 diplomatic skill,
  •   1 military skill,
  • a random gender if the country has the   policy ‘Mandatory Service’ or the government reform Meritocratic Rule or the modifiers Votes For Women or Burgundian Inheritance Reforms.

The country gains   10 karma.
If the country has a government where Reelections influence the factions :

  • add 20 influence to the   adm related faction

If the country has a government where Reelections influence the estates :

  • The   adm related estate gains 10% Loyalty

If the country has Civic Republicanism :

  • Gain a random modifier from the Administrative list

  Always enabled:

 
Diplomat Candidate

A new ruler comes to power with the following (fixed) attributes:

  •   1 administrative skill,
  •   4 diplomatic skill,
  •   1 military skill,
  • a random gender if the country has the   policy ‘Mandatory Service’ or the government reform Meritocratic Rule or the modifiers Votes For Women or Burgundian Inheritance Reforms.

The country gains   10 karma.
If the country has a government where Reelections influence the factions :

  • add 20 influence to the   dip related faction

If the country has a government where Reelections influence the estates :

  • The   dip related estate gains 10% Loyalty

If the country has Civic Republicanism :

  • Gain a random modifier from the Diplomatic list

  Always enabled:

 
Military Candidate

A new ruler comes to power with the following (fixed) attributes:

  •   1 administrative skill,
  •   1 diplomatic skill,
  •   4 military skill,
  • a random gender if the country has the   policy ‘Mandatory Service’ or the government reform Meritocratic Rule or the modifiers Votes For Women or Burgundian Inheritance Reforms.

The country gains   10 karma.
If the country has a government where Reelections influence the factions :

  • add 20 influence to the   mil related faction

If the country has a government where Reelections influence the estates :

  • The   mil related estate gains 10% Loyalty

If the country has Civic Republicanism :

  • Gain a random modifier from the Military list

  Enabled if: the country uses ‘Can re-elect from ruling family.’ mechanics.

 
The beloved relative of [Root.Monarch.GetName].

A new ruler of the current dynasty comes to power with the following attributes:

  • with 2 less skill in all categories (0 less if is an Italian Signoria)
  • an age between 30 and 50,
  • a random gender if the country has the   policy ‘Mandatory Service’ or the government reform Meritocratic Rule or the modifiers Votes For Women or Burgundian Inheritance Reforms.

If the country has flag strengthened_signoria_flag[5]:

  • Remove flag strengthened_signoria_flag

Else, the country:

  • loses   1% republican tradition per election cycle year.

If the country has a government where Reelections influence the estates :

  • All estates gain 10% Loyalty

  The Aristocrats faction gains   10 influence. If the country has Civic Republicanism :

  • Gain a random modifier

Colonial overlordsEdit

id

 New [From.Monarch.GetTitle] in [From.GetName]

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

We have chosen to replace [From.Monarch.GetName] as Governor of [From.GetName]. Whom shall we appoint in [From.Monarch.GetHerHis] place?

 
Trigger conditions
  • None
 Is triggered only by

using the replace governor subject interaction on a colonial nation.

 
Bureaucrat Candidate

The colonial nation gets a new Governor, marked as appointed by overlord, with the following (fixed) attributes:

  •   4 administrative skill,
  •   2 diplomatic skill,
  •   2 military skill.
 
Diplomat Candidate

The colonial nation gets a new Governor, marked as appointed by overlord, with the following (fixed) attributes:

  •   2 administrative skill
  •   4 diplomatic skill
  •   2 military skill
 
Military Candidate

The colonial nation gets a new Governor, marked as appointed by overlord, with the following (fixed) attributes:

  •   2 administrative skill
  •   2 diplomatic skill
  •   4 military skill

 Colonial Assembly

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

Our colony [From.GetName] has decided to form their own assembly in order to take control of tax legislation and budgets. They claim that these matters are better handled locally, but it seems suspiciously like a first step towards independence...

 
Trigger conditions
  • None
 Is triggered only by

a colonial subject taking the   decision ‘Form Colonial Assembly’.

 
Suspicious indeed...

The colonial nation gains   +10% liberty desire.

Dutch RepublicEdit

id

 Election in the [Root.GovernmentName]

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

  The description below is one of several available for this event.
The strengthening of the Statists has forced $MONARCH$ to accept the election of a new [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]. Both the Statists and the Orangists have put forward a new candidate.

 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • uses ‘Statists vs Monarchists’[4] mechanics.
  • is not the lesser partner in a personal union or the Statists are in power.
 Is triggered only by

the end of the election cycle in a republic.

Option conditions

  Enabled if: does not have "Open Public Elections" or "The Feuillant System" reforms

 
A Statist Candidate

The country:

  • gets a new ruler.
  • strengthen Statists in power by 33.

  Enabled if: has "The Feuillant System" reform

 
A Girondist candidate.

The country:

  • gets a new ruler.
  • strengthen Statists in power by 33.

  The Girondists faction gains   20 influence.

  Enabled if: has "The Feuillant System" reform

 
A Jacobin candidate.

The country:

  • gets a new ruler.
  • strengthen Statists in power by 33.

  The Jacobins faction gains   20 influence.

  Enabled if: the country has Dutch Election

 
An Orangist Candidate

The country:

  • gets a new ruler of the “van Oranje” dynasty.
  • strengthen Orangists in power by 33.

  Enabled if: does not have "Open Public Elections" or Dutch Election

 
A Monarchist Candidate

If has "The Feuillant System" reform and is   France:

  • The country:
    • gets a new ruler of the "Bonaparte" dynasty.
    • strengthen Monarchists in power by 33.

Else:

  • The country:
    • gets a new ruler of the former dynasty.
    • strengthen Orangists in power by 33.

If has "The Feuillant System" reform:

  •   The Imperials faction gains   20 influence.

  Enabled if: has "Open Public Elections" reform

 
A Militarist candidate.

The country:

  • gets a new ruler.
  • strengthen Statists in power by 33.

  Enabled if: has "Open Public Elections" reform

 
A Theocratic candidate.

The country:

  • gets a new ruler.
  • strengthen Orangists in power by 33.

Lottery electionEdit

id

 Election in the [Root.GovernmentName]

 
Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

The unfortunate passing of $MONARCH$ forces us to hold an election. After several rounds of secret allotment, three candidates have been presented each representing powerful factions within [Root.Country.GetName]. Officially the election should be decided by lottery, however we could pull some strings...

 
Trigger conditions

The country's government form uses lottery elections.[6]
Does not have   "Bonfire of the Vanities disaster ongoing

Is triggered only by

the death of the ruler in a republic.

Immediate effects
  • Define   administrative,   diplomatic, and   military ruler candidates:
    • with random skills, but their corresponding skill is raised by +2, while both other skills are reduced by -1
      • If the   Venetian   Mission "Promissione Ducale" has been completed, their respective skill is increased by +3 instead.
    • aged between 50 and 75 years
    • if the country has enacted the policy “Mandatory Service”, they have a random gender, otherwise male.
Option conditions

  Enabled if: Always enabled

 
Have [name of the administrative candidate] elected.
  • The   administrative candidate becomes ruler.
  • Lose   25 republican tradition.
  • The   Guilds faction, if it exists, gains 20 influence.
  • If the country has a government where Reelections influence the estates :
    • The   adm related estate gains 10% Loyalty

  Enabled if: Always enabled

 
Have [name of the diplomatic candidate] elected.
  • The   diplomatic candidate becomes ruler.
  • Lose   25 republican tradition.
  • The   Traders faction, if it exists, gains 20 influence.
  • If the country has a government where Reelections influence the estates :
    • The   dip related estate gains 10% Loyalty

  Enabled if: Always enabled

 
Have [name of the military candidate] elected.
  • The   military candidate becomes ruler.
  • Lose   25 republican tradition.
  • The   Aristocrats faction, if it exists, gains 20 influence.
  • If the country has a government where Reelections influence the estates :
    • The   mil related estate gains 10% Loyalty

  Enabled if: Always enabled

 
Let the lottery decide.
  • A random one of the three candidates becomes ruler.
  • The corresponding faction, if it exists, gains 20 influence.
  • If the country has a government where Reelections influence the estates :
    • The corresponding estate gains 10% Loyalty

  Enabled if: government reforms allow re-electing a ruler from the ruling family

 
Elect the beloved nephew of [Root.Monarch.GetName].
  • Lose   15 republican tradition
  • Elect a new ruler:
    • an age between 30 and 50,
    • with 1 less skill in all categories (0 less if is an Italian Signoria)
    • with the same surname as the previous ruler
    • with a random gender if the “Mandatory Service” policy is enacted, otherwise male.
  • The   Aristocrats faction, if it exists, gains 10 influence.
  • If the country has a government where Reelections influence the estates :
    • All estates gains 10% Loyalty
Effects after selecting any option
  • If the country has a Government where "all skills of the elected Ruler change by 1" :
    • increase all skills of the elected candidate by +1
