      Oman is a landlocked country in eastern Arabia, surrounded by Hormuz and Yas. It starts as an Arabian Plutocracy. Oman follows the Hanbali school of Islam.

      Arabia does not exist.
      The country:

      • was never an end-game nation.
      • is not the Papal State or the Holy Roman Empire.
      • has not yet enacted this decision.
      • is in the Levantine culture group, except Turkish culture.
      • is not a colonial nation.

      If the country is AI-controlled then it:

      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not a custom nation
      • is not a former colonial nation.

      Effects

      The country:

      • becomes Arabia.
      • gains 25 prestige.
      • gets new missions.
      • gets the event ‘New Traditions & Ambitions’, unless it has custom ideas.
      • becomes an empire.
      • removes all provinces from the Holy Roman Empire, if it is a member and not an elector or the emperor.
      • gains permanent claims on:
        • Mashriq region
        • Arabia region
        • Egypt except Lower Nubia area
      • gets “Increased Centralization” for 20 years, giving:
        • −0.05 monthly autonomy change,
        • +1 national unrest.

      • AI will always take this decision, unless it has an administrative technology of less than 20.
      • AI gives "high priority" (400) to this decision

      Strategy[edit | edit source]

      The below is one of many player suggested strategies for Oman. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

      Religion and Government[edit | edit source]

      Oman is a Muslim country with Ibadi religion under a Arabian Plutocracy government.

      Arabian Plutocracy has a unique government mechanic in "Arabian Mercantilism", giving up to +15 and +10% at 100. Arabian Mercantilism is increased monthly by, among other factors, being at peace, having high naval tradition and having a ruler with good diplomatic skill, while war, corruption and a trade income below 20% give a monthly decrease. As Oman starts without a coast and in need of some more provinces and therefore war, Arabian Mercantilism will remain low at the start of the game. Later in the game however, when Naval Tradition, Trade companies and (through Missions) Trader Advisors increase Arabian Mercantilism, the monthly change can be positive even while at war.

      In addition to giving a bonus for simply having it, points of Arabian Mercantilism can also be spent on temporary upgrades. "Support Overseas Merchants" is the cheapest at only 10, giving more trade power to every trade node you have a merchant in (+50 Trade power, +10, and +10% ) for 15 years and a 50% cheaper Trader advisor (+10%). These bonuses are almost strictly better than even a full meter of Arabian mercantilism, and should be activated liberally.

      "Strengthen Overseas Outposts" costs 15 points and increases defensiveness, trade power and center of trade upgrade costs of provinces with a Level 2 center of trade for 10 years. The trade power gain is comparable to "Support Overseas Merchants" if every trade node with a merchant has 2 of those provinces, or 1 in a trade company. As such, coupled with the higher price and shorter duration make it weaker in the early game compared to it, but better in the late game.

      "Empower Religious Propagation" cost 35 points and is arguably the weakest of the options. For 15 years, it allows the "Propagate State Religion" Trade Policy with only 35% trade power in a node, ignores the dominant religion requirement for said policy, and gives a 50% cheaper inquisitor (+2%). By the time you can spend 35 points regularly, you should be able to reach 50% trade power with your trade ships and/or the previous 2 options, which together cost 10 less points. Still, removing the dominant religion requirement can be useful since there are a lot of different Religions in your starting area, and Omani Ideas make tolerating them easier than converting them.

      As Ibadi religion is a minority Religious unity falls quickly with conquest. Choosing the religious idea group will increase Missionary strength to convert newly conquered provinces. Furthermore, the Deus Vult idea can turn the minority position into a huge advantage.

      Conquest[edit | edit source]

      At 1444 start, Oman needs to get its cores from Hormuz; it's advisable to declare soon rather than later, to prevent their alliance network grows too strong. Supporting Omani rebels is however a viable alternative, due to Hormuz's low religious unity. This requires a fabricated Spy Network of 30 and about 75 ducats. Once Hormuz is annexed, every other country in the peninsula can be defeated unless they find a strong ally.

      After coring some coastal provinces, a stack of galleys should help to keep a safe access to Timurids land. Timurids start in a weak position facing several revolts and disloyal vassals. The overlord is losing manpower and his vassals are not likely to defend his territory and join any battle. That is an opportunity to declare war and eventually defeat Timurids and change ruler into Timurid in order to win permanent claims in the whole Persia region. Additionally, forming Mughals by pushing the conquest to the east provides more permanent claims in the Indian subcontinent.

      Finally, Oman can form Arabia to reach empire rank and eventually conquer the rest of the Islamic world. At this point, idea groups like Diplomatic, Administrative, and Influence will all be very useful.

      Alternatively one could no CB a minor Indian nation and by building powerbase in India form Hindustan

      Trading[edit | edit source]

      Omani national ideas focus on trade and naval bonuses. Moreover, the country has quick access to several coastal provinces which make Oman a good candidate for trade oriented gameplay using exploration idea group early[1].

      After conquering Zanzibar, it is worth rushing the Spice Islands and conquering a province that produces cloves. This in return will give a decision that makes Zanzibar produce cloves.

      Dar al-Islam

      Start as an Ibadi nation and eliminate all rival schools of Islam (do not convert to another religion).
