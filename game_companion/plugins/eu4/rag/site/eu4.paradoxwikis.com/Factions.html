      Factions

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.

      Factions represent powerful and influential groups within the country. Each faction has an influence rating associated with it, and the faction with the highest influence will be in power, granting a set of bonuses (and penalties) to the country. Faction details and interactions can be accessed via the unique faction icon in the lower right corner of the map, next to the HRE and papacy icons.

      The influence of a faction can either be increased by spending 10 monarch power from the faction's category, or will automatically change due to certain modifiers. As the total influence of all factions is always equal to 100, a increase in one faction's influence is at the expense of the two others'.

      Revolutionary Republics, Revolutionary States, Revolutionary Empires and Pirate republics have their estates replaced by their factions.

      Celestial empire[edit | edit source]

      Only available without the Mandate of Heaven DLC.

      The unique T1 government reform Celestial Empire ( Ming has this in 1444) has access to the following factions:

      Name Type Effects Description & notes
      Commanderies of the Five Armies
      • +10% Manpower recovery speed
      • −25% Unjustified demands
      • −1 Diplomatic relations
      		 The Commanderies are the system of the generals and commanders of the Imperial armies. Eager to prove their valor and loyalty they seek to promote war over diplomacy to be able to advance in rank.

      Gains influence from positive Stability and Army tradition. Loses influence from War exhaustion.

      Offices of Maritime Trade
      • +10% Provincial trade power modifier
      • +1 Diplomatic reputation
      • +10% Stability cost modifier
      		 The office organizing the provincial bureaucrats in charge of trade and seafaring. Seeking to promote profitable trade they are often opposed to the militaristic ambitions of the Commanderies.

      Gains influence from Navy tradition.

      Grand Secretariat
      • −10% Construction cost
      • +1 Possible advisors
      • −5% Discipline
      		 The corps of administrators promoted through the Imperial Examination system. Brilliant Confucian scholars, these men are the diplomats and bureacrats of the Empire. They promote policies to build a stable and strong Empire.

      Gains influence from Overextension and Bankruptcy.

      The factions influence changes monthly with the following modifiers:

      The Commanderies influence modifiers
      +0.1 At 100 army tradition (scaling from 0 at 0)
      +0.02 Per point of positive stability
      −0.05 Per point of war exhaustion
      The Offices influence modifiers
      +0.1 At 100 navy tradition (scaling from 0 at 0)
      The Secretariat influence modifiers
      +0.5 At 100% overextension (scaling from 0 at 0)
      +0.25 In bankruptcy

      Merchant republics[edit | edit source]

      See also: Republic factions events

      Merchant republics, Veche republics, and their trading cities aswell as Trade Companys have access to their own factions system. Merchant republics with a very dominant ruling faction can have the other two factions revolt, forcing the ruler to resign or face a hefty hit to republican tradition. The factions are:

      Name Type Effects Description & notes
      The Aristocrats
      • 0+5% Morale of armies
      • +25% National sailors modifier
      • −10% Trade power abroad
      		 Representing the aristocratic Patrician families that dominate the political life in the capital. Their main concern is ensuring that the Republic is victorious in war and achieves the glory that they view as their own.

      Gains influence through positive Stability and Army tradition. Loses influence through War exhaustion.

      The Traders
      • +10% Global trade power modifier
      • −10% Naval maintenance modifier
      • 0−5% National tax modifier
      		 Representing the Merchants and Ship Captains that are the lifeblood of the Republic's trade. Their main concern is the control of lucrative maritime trade routes.

      Gains influence through Navy tradition.

      The Guilds
      • −10% Construction cost
      • +10% Goods produced modifier
      • −10% National manpower modifier
      		 Representing the Artisans and Master Craftsmen inhabiting our cities that have banded together in Guilds for mutual economic gain and assistance. Their main concern is the economic well-being of the cities.

      Gains influence through Overextension and Bankruptcy.

      Elections will increase the elected candidate's faction influence by 20.

      The factions influence changes monthly with the following modifiers:

      The Aristocrats influence modifiers
      +0.1
      • At 100 army tradition (scaling from 0 at 0)
      • With the modifier "Aristocrats' Military Focus" (from Gotlander missions)
      +0.05 With the modifier "Strengthened Council of Ten" (from Venetian events)
      +0.03 Per point of positive stability
      −0.05
      • Per point of war exhaustion
      • With the modifier "Restrained Council of Ten" (from Venetian events)
      The Traders influence modifiers
      +0.1
      • At 100 navy tradition (scaling from 0 at 0)
      • With the modifier "Traders' Commercial Focus" (from Gotlander missions)
      +0.05
      • With the modifier "Expansion of the Great Council of Venice" (from Venetian events)
      • With the modifier "Barnabotti" (from Venetian events)
      +0.02 With the modifier "Nobility for Sale" (from Venetian events)
      The Guilds influence modifiers
      +0.5 At 100% overextension (scaling from 0 at 0)
      +0.25 In bankruptcy
      +0.1 With the modifier "Guilds' Bureaucratic Focus" (from Gotlander missions)
      +0.02 With the modifier "Murano Glass Industry" (from Venetian events)

      Revolutionary republics[edit | edit source]


      See also: Revolutionary republic factions events

      Revolutionary Republics, Revolutionary States and Revolutionary Empires have access to their own factions system. They are:

      Name Type Effects Description & notes
      The Jacobins
      • −2 National unrest
      • −15% Construction cost
      • +15% National tax modifier
      • −2 Diplomatic reputation
      		 The Jacobins are the staunchest supporter of the popular revolution. They believe that we must forcefully do away with the old ruling classes and seize their property. This faction has the support of the masses in our capital and will not shy away from inciting them into violence for the good of the Revolution.

      Influence scales with Republican tradition and positive Stability.

      The Imperials
      • +1 Diplomatic reputation
      • −25% State maintenance
      • −20 Subject liberty desire
      • −0.5 Yearly republican tradition
      		 The Imperials wish to bring the revolution under control and to centralize authority again. They claim to respect the will of the people but they are abhorred by the excesses and terror that uncontrolled popular opinion can bring. If the Imperials manage to seize control they will eventually try to create a Revolutionary Empire.

      Gains influence from War exhaustion.

      The Girondists
      • +20% Land force limit modifier
      • +20% Manpower recovery speed
      • +5% Discipline
      • +20% Aggressive expansion impact
      		 Rather than let the Revolution spiral out of control internally the Girondists want to focus on spreading it beyond our borders. They believe in an active foreign policy and in ending the counter-revolutionary regimes on the continent.

      Gains influence from being at War and from Army tradition. Loses influence from War exhaustion and being at Peace.

      If the T3 government reform National Assembly is enacted, elections will increase the elected candidate's faction influence by 20.

      The factions influence changes monthly with the following modifiers:

      The Jacobins influence modifiers
      +0.03 Per point of positive stability
      +0.1
      • At 100 republican tradition (scaling from −0.1 at 0)
      • With the T3 / T2 government reform Egalité Ideals
      • With the / T4 government reform Cult of Reason
      • With the T7 / T10 government reform Equality Principle
      • With the T11 government reform Equal Electorate
      The Imperials influence modifiers
      +0.05 Per point of war exhaustion
      +0.1
      • With the T2 government reform Nobility of the Emperor
      • With the T3 / T2 government reform Fraternité Ideals
      • With the T4 government reform Conserve the Religion
      • With the T7 / T10 government reform Imperial Principle
      • With the T10 / T11 government reform Military Electorate
      +0.2 With a loyal and influential nobility estate (depending on loyalty and influence, from −0.1 when disloyal)
      The Girondists influence modifiers
      +0.15 At war
      +0.1
      • At 100 army tradition (scaling from 0 at 0)
      • With the T3 / T2 government reform Liberté Ideals
      • With the T4 government reform Secularization of the State
      • With the T7 / T10 government reform Revolutionary Principle
      • With the T11 government reform Three Social Classes
      −0.05 Per point of war exhaustion
      −0.1 At peace

      Influential Imperials will bring the popular revolution under control by establishing a more authoritarian revolutionary empire. This is loosely modelled on the Brumaire 18th coup in France.

      Pirate republics[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Golden Century DLC enabled.

      Pirate republics have access to their own factions system:

      Name Type Effects Description & notes
      The Buccaneers
      • +20% Sailor recovery speed
      • +1 Yearly republican tradition
      • −5% National tax modifier
      		 The Buccaneers are the desperate and disenfranchised. Ex-slaves, mutineers and impressed sailors all find sanctuary from the law in the Republic of Pirates.

      Gains influence through Coastal raiding.

      The Smugglers
      • +10% Trade efficiency
      • +10% Global trade power
      • −0.5 Yearly prestige
      		 The Smugglers are clandestine merchants willing to risk capture to sell their illegitimate wares. They provide a semi-legitimate front for the fencing of plundered goods.

      Gains influence through being at peace.

      The Captains
      • +10% Morale of navies
      • +1 Naval leader fire
      • +10% Stability cost modifier
      		 Pirate Captains are often former privateers gone rogue, either out of greed or with a political motive. They have the experience, education and charisma to lead the pirates.

      Gains influence through Privateering.

      Elections will increase the elected candidate's faction influence by 20.

      The factions influence changes monthly with the following modifiers:

      The Buccaneers influence modifiers
      +1 Raiding coasts (each time the interaction is used)
      +0.1
      • With the T2 government reform Articles of Agreement
      • With the T4 government reform Crew before Faith
      • With the T6 government reform War Against the World Doctrine
      • With the T8 government reform Open Naval Services
      • With the T9 government reform Expand Buccaneers' Power
      • With the T13 government reform Unified Piratical Confederacy
      +0.2 With the modifier "The Buccaneers' Focus of Piracy" (from Gotlander missions)
      The Smugglers influence modifiers
      +0.1
      • At peace
      • With the T2 government reform Smugglers Haven
      • With the T4 government reform Complete Religious Freedom
      • With the T6 government reform Black Market Consortium
      • With the T8 government reform A Smuggler's Deal
      • With the T9 government reform Power to the Smugglers
      • With the T13 government reform Neutral Ground Policy
      +0.2 With the modifier "The Smugglers' Focus of Piracy" (from Gotlander missions)
      The Captains influence modifiers
      +0.1
      • While privateering (as a fraction of naval force limit)
      • With the Barbary Corsair idea 7: Board of Captains
      • With the T2 government reform Council of Captains
      • With the T4 government reform Respect for the Sea
      • With the T6 government reform Pirate King
      • With the T8 government reform Bounty Hunting
      • With the T9 government reform Captains in Charge
      • With the T13 government reform Scourge of the Seven Seas
      +0.2 With the modifier "The Captains' Focus of Piracy" (from Gotlander missions)
