      Biapas is a small nation located in the north-west of the Persia region. It borders Gilan, Ajam, Ardabil, and Shirvan.

      Decisions[edit | edit source]

      id

      Form Mughal Empire

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

      $MONARCH$ has begun to lay the foundation of his new empire. Although inferior in numbers, his well-disciplined troops have managed to achieve several decisive victories against the Sultans of India. The battle of $CAPITAL$ became the first great victory in a series of territorial expansions and, with time, the empire of the Mughals could stretch over the larger part of the Indian plateau.

       
      Potential requirements

      The country:

      • was never an end-game nation.
      • is not Persia, Mughals, Ottomans, or Holy Roman Empire.
      • has not yet enacted this decision.
      • is not a colonial nation.
      • does not have 'Celestial Empire' Government reform.
      • is in the Iranian culture group or has Turkmeni, Uzbek or Kyrgyz as primary culture.

      If the country is AI-controlled then it:

      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not a custom nation
      • is not a former colonial nation.
      • has at least 5 cities
      • Mughals does not exist

      Playing with normal or historical nations.

      		Allow

      Mughals does not exist
      The country:

      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not at war
      • is Muslim
      • owns its core provinces:
        • Delhi (522)
        • either Doaba (2075), Lahore (507) or Central Doab (524)

      Delhi (522) is part of a state.

      Effects

      The country:

      • becomes Mughals.
      • restore its name
      • gains 25 prestige.
      • gets new missions.
      • gets the event ‘New Traditions & Ambitions’, unless it is a custom nation.
      • changes primary culture to Hindavi.
      • changes technology group and unit type to Indian.
      • becomes an empire
      • removes all provinces from the Holy Roman Empire, if it is a member and not an elector or the emperor.
      • gets a permanent claim on Hindustan region
      • removes all unowned cores and permanent claims in the following regions:
        • Persia
        • Khorasan
        • Central Asia
        • Caucasus.
      • becomes a Monarchy, if is not revolutionary
      • adds 'Indian Sultanate' government reform.
      • If Dharma is enabled, then:
        • adds 'Mughal Diwan' government reform,
        • adds 'Mansabdari System' (tier 5) and 'Zabt System' (tier 7) government reform, if government reform tier 3 was previously enacted[1] and the country was a Monarchy[2].
        • removes 'States General' if previously enacted.

      If Delhi (522) is part of the HRE, but its owner is not a member then:

      • the province is removed from the HRE.
      • the Holy Roman Emperor:
        • gets the opinion modifier “Removed provinces from the Empire” towards the owner, worth −50 opinion with a yearly decay of 1.
        • loses 1 imperial authority.

      Delhi (522):

      • becomes the new capital of the owner country.
      • gains 1 base tax
      • gains 1 base production
      • gains 1 base tax

      • AI will always take this decision.
      • AI gives "very high priority" (600) to this decision
      • Ai will only take this decision if they are the Timurids, Afghanistan, Baluchistan or have at least 350 dev.

      Form Persian Nation

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.36.

      The lands of Persia have always been the seat of powerful empires since the times of Cyrus the Great. It is time for [Root.Monarch.GetName] to reclaim that rightful glory and the title Shah of Iran, as several of the neighboring regions have been conquered and the possibility of a resurrected Iranian nation doesn't seem so absent anymore.

       
      Potential requirements

      The country:

      • was never an end-game nation.
      • is not the Mughals, Persia, Eranshahr, the Holy Roman Empire, the Roman Empire, the Papal State the Ottomans, Byzantium, or Arabia.
      • has not yet enacted this decision.
      • is not a colonial nation.
      • Either:
        • is in the Iranian culture group or has Azerbaijani as the primary culture
        • is Aq Qoyunlu and the King of Kings DLC is not active
      • Playing with normal or historical nations

      If the country is AI-controlled then it:

      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not a custom nation
      • is not a former colonial nation.
      • Persia does not exist

      If the King of Kings DLC is active and the country is Aq Qoyunlu or Qara Qoyunlu then it:

      • Has country flag turkoman_can_form_persia (from event Persianization of the Turkomans after completing the mission Shahanshahs of Eranshahr)
      		 Allow

      Persia and Eranshahr do not exist The country:

      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not at war
      • is not a nomad nation
      • is not' 'Tribal'
      • owns the core provinces:
        • Hamadan (414)
        • Isfahan (429)
        • Yazd (433)
        • Qom (2213)
        • Qazvin (2215)
        • Amol (426) or Shiraz (2218)
        • Tabriz (416), Kerman (432), or Mashhad (2221)

      If the country is the Timurids:

      • Transoxiana, Afghanistan, Sistan, Fars, Khorasan, Ajam, Luristan, and Ardalan all do not exist
      Effects

      The country:

      • becomes Persia.
      • gains 25 prestige.
      • gets new missions.
      • becomes an empire
      • changes primary culture to Persian, unless the King of Kings DLC is active and the primary culture is Mazandarani
      • becomes a monarchy if is not already and not revolutionary
      • enacts 'Persian Government' government reform if is not revolutionary and not Zoroastrian
      • enacts 'Asha Monarchy' government reform if is not revolutionary and is Zoroastrian
      • removes 'States General' government reform, if has enacted it
      • gets the event ‘New Traditions & Ambitions’, unless it has custom ideas.
      • If the the King of Kings DLC is active:
        • Unlocks the Qizilbash estate
      • removes all provinces from the Holy Roman Empire, if it is a member and not an elector or the emperor.
      • gains permanent claims on Persia (region)
      • gets “Increased Centralization” for 20 years, giving:
        • −0.05 monthly autonomy change,
        • +1 national unrest.

      • AI will always take this decision.
      • AI gives "high priority" (400) to this decision

      Enthrone Timurid Prince

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.31.

      Amir Timur once crushed all realms in this region and entrusted their rule to his relatives. Ever since they have been torn apart by the Timurid princes' lust to rule it all, and there are many princes who could yet stake their claim to Timur's empire. Some local rulers have been known to invite Timurid claimants to their states, propping them up as leaders, and using the strength Timur's name to extend their rulership over all lands he once ruled.

       
      Potential requirements

      The year is before 1490.
      The country:

      • was never an end-game tag.
      • is not Persia.
      • does not have a ruler of the ‘Timurid’ dynasty.
      • is a monarchy or a tribal government.
      • has not enacted the ‘Feudal Theocracy’ or ‘Tribal Federation’ government reform.
      • has its primary culture in the Iranian group or it is Uzbek or Turkmeni.
        • is Muslim.

      If the country is AI-controlled then it must be a custom nation, Ajam, Transoxiana, Fars, Khorasan, Afghanistan or Yazd.

      		Allow

      The year is before 1490.
      The country:

      • does not have a ruler of the ‘Timurid’ dynasty
      • is not a subject nation.
      • owns and cores one of the following provinces: Teheran, Isfahan, Herat, Kabul, Ferghana, Samarkand, Qom, Astarabad or Shiraz.
      Effects

      The country:

      • loses 2 stability.
      • gains 20 legitimacy.
      • gets a ruler and a heir of the ‘Timurid’ dynasty.

      This will make it possible for us to form the Timurid Empire.
