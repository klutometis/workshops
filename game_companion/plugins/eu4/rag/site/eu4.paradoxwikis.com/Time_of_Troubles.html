      Time of Troubles
      A time of both social and economic problems, our nation is struggling with both famine and war as well as the opposition against the Church and aristocracy.

      The Time of Troubles[1] is a disaster in Russia or Muscovy during the ‘Age of Reformation’.

      Prerequisites[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      It is currently the ‘Age of Discovery’ or ‘Age of Reformation’ and this country:

      • is either Russia or Muscovy if Russia does not exist.
      • did not have this disaster before.
      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • does not have the government reform Russian Empire

      If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

      Progress starts if Progress halts if
      Russia/ Muscovy:
      • has a stability of less than 0.
      • is a monarchy.
      • does not have an ongoing disaster.
      • One of the following:
        • does not have its original dynasty
        • the ruler's , , stats add up to less than 9

      It is currently the ‘Age of Reformation’.

      Monthly progress The country:
      +1.0% • has a stability of less than 0.
      +1.0% • has a stability of −3.
      +1.0% • has a war exhaustion of at least 8.
      +1.0% • has a war exhaustion of at least 12.

      Note: If the requirements for a progress are fulfilled, then it is at least 1% due to stability.

      		At least one of the following is true for the country:
      • has an ongoing disaster.
      • is not a monarchy.

      If the progress reaches 100%, the disaster will start with the following event:

      Time of Troubles

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The Time of Troubles stretched from the death of the last Tsar of the 700-year-old Rurik dynasty, Feodor Ivanovich, in 1598, and the ascension of Mikhail Fyodorovich of the Romanov dynasty in 1613. Famine, plague, public unrest and the appearance of false claimants plunged the country into a decade of turmoil as neighboring powers like Sweden and Poland saw their chance to interfere.

       
      Trigger conditions
      • None
      		 Is triggered only by

      the start of “Time of Troubles” disaster.

      Immediate effects

      The country gets the flag time_of_troubles

      Hard times are ahead of us

      The country:

      • loses 1 stability.

      Modifiers[edit | edit source]

      While the disaster is ongoing the country gets:

      • +10 national unrest
      • +20% stability cost modifier
      • −2 Monthly modernization

      Also the following events are triggered by a monthly pulse.

      Monthly events[edit | edit source]

      The weight that no event is triggered is 1000 and for each monthly event it is 100.

      The Insanity of [Root.Monarch.GetName]

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The Insanity of [Root.Monarch.GetName] Over the last couple of months [Root.Monarch.GetName]'s erratic behavior has become an increasing problem. Some even say [Root.Monarch.GetSheHe] has gone completely insane...

       
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • is not in a regency.
      • does not have an heir.
      • has a male ruler.
      • has not had this event before.
      		 Is triggered only by

      the monthly pulse of the ‘Time of Troubles’ disaster.

      [Root.Monarch.GetName] will rule until his death!

      The country:

      • gets the modifier “Insane Ruler” until the ruler changes with the following effects:
        • −75% chance of new heir,

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 25% of the cases.

      Let his trusted advisor take over

      The country:

      • gets ‘Boris Godunov’ as ruler.

      If the country uses Statists vs Monarchists mechanics then:

      • strengthen Monarchists in power by 20000%.

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 75% of the cases.

      Famine Strikes

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The Russian famine of 1601-1603 was caused by three consecutive poor harvests. Widespread starvation followed, killing around two million people or nearly a third of the Russian population. Refugees flocked to the capital, increasing economical and political disorganization across the country.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • has not had this event before.
      		 Is triggered only by

      the monthly pulse of the ‘Time of Troubles’ disaster.

      There is nothing we can do

      The country:

      • gets the modifier “Severe Famine” for 10 years with the following effect:
        • −0.5 goods produced.

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 25% of the cases.

      Try to ease the suffering

      The country:

      • loses ducats equal to 0.25 years of income.
      • gets the modifier “Famine” for 10 years with the following effect:
        • −0.25 goods produced.

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 50% of the cases.

      Use all our resources to aim those in need

      The country:

      • loses ducats equal to 0.4 years of income.
      • gets the modifier “Fighting Famine” for 5 years with the following effect:
        • −0.10 goods produced.

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 25% of the cases.

      Recent famines and outbreaks of plague have left much of the country without functioning authorities. The province of [Root.GetName] has become a lawless land where bands of armed brigands roam the countryside terrorizing local population.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • had the event ‘Famine Strikes’.
      • had the event ‘Plague’.
      • has not had this event before.
      		 Is triggered only by

      Restore authority

      • loses 10 administrative power.
      • loses ducats equal to 10% years of income.

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 75% of the cases.

      Leave it be

      • −50% local tax modifier,
      • −50% local manpower modifier.

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 25% of the cases.

      First Dimitri

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      In 1603, during the Time of Troubles, a man called Dimitri appeared and claimed to be the supposedly dead younger brother of former Tsar Feodor Ivanovich - and thereby the rightful claimant to the Russian throne. This Dimitri quickly attracted supporters throughout Russia and especially in the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth. A few months later Dimitri crossed the border into Russia together with a force of Poles, Lithuanians and Russian exiles. It seems he already has strong support from our enemies.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • has not had this event before.
      • is not the lesser partner in a personal union.

      A neighboring country exists that:

      • is a rival of the country, has rivaled the country or has an opinion of less than 0 towards the country.
      • is not an ally of the country.
      • is not a subject of the country.
      • has at least 10000 manpower as reserve.
      		 Is triggered only by

      the monthly pulse of the ‘Time of Troubles’ disaster.

      Recognize him as the legitimate ruler

      The ruler and the heir of the country die.
      The country:

      • gets ‘Dimitri I Rurikovich’ as ruler.
      • gets the modifier “Usurper of the Throne” until the ruler changes with the following effects:
        • +5 national unrest,
        • +33% stability cost modifier,
        • +50% advisor cost,
        • +33% technology cost.
      • loses −50 legitimacy.

      If the country uses Statists vs Monarchists mechanics then:

      • strengthen Monarchists in power by 20000%.

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 10% of the cases.

      Expel him

      A random neighboring country that:

      • is a rival of the country, has rivaled the country or has an opinion of less than 0 towards the country.
      • is not an ally of the country.
      • is not a subject of the country.
      • has at least 10000 manpower as reserve.

      gets:

      • if this country is Muscovy the event False Dimitri in 30 days.
      • if this country is Russia the event False Dimitri in 30 days.

      Dimitri travels to [This.GetName] in search of support.
      The AI ​​chooses this option in 90% of the cases.

      id

      False Dimitri Overthrown

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.35.

      The false Dimitri wasn't able to hold on to the title of Tsar for even a year. A prince named Vasily Shuisky from another branch of the old Rurik dynasty conspired against him. Fuelled by the rumors that Dimitri was planning to convert Russia to the Catholic faith, Shuisky and his supporters stormed the Kremlin in the morning of 17 May 1606. Dimitri tried to escape through a window but broke his leg and was soon captured and shot. His body was then cremated, and the ashes reportedly shot from a cannon towards Poland.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • has the modifier “Usurper of the Throne”.
      • has not had this event before.
      		 Is triggered only by

      the monthly pulse of the ‘Time of Troubles’ disaster.

      Resist these usurpers!

      The country:

      • loses 2 stability.

      Pretender rebels (size 3) with “Vasily Shuisky” rise up in revolt in the capital of the country.
      The AI ​​chooses this option in 10% of the cases.

      Let the rebels seize power

      The ruler and the heir of the country die.
      The country:

      • gets ‘Vasily Shuisky’ as ruler.

      If the country uses Statists vs Monarchists mechanics then:

      • strengthen Monarchists in power by 20000%.

      A random known country that had the event ‘False Dimitri’ or ‘False Dimitri’ and chosen the option “Support him with troops”:

      • gets the opinion modifier “Overthrow Dimitri” towards this country worth −50 opinion with a yearly decay of 5.
      • gets a Obscure Documents casus belli against this country for 2 years.

      The AI ​​chooses this option in 90% of the cases.

      Finishing conditions[edit | edit source]

      If this country:

      • has no province controlled by rebel.
      • does not have the modifier “Usurper of the Throne”.
      • has a stability of at least +1.
      • controls its capital.

      the disaster ends and the following event is triggered.
