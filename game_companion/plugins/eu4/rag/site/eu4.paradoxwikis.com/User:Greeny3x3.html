      Peace terms

      Demand Warscore Prestige Aggressive Expansion Effect
      Annex province Province cost 0.25 per development[Note 1]; some overseas provinces give less prestige 0.6 per development[Note 2] The selected province of the country on the losing side is annexed by its current occupier, or by the country making peace if not occupied. If it is occupied, the occupier must be one of the parties to the peace. If a CB with the "Take province"(e.g. conquest or reconquest) or "Take colony" wargoal is used, the warscore cost for the wargoal is lowered by ⅓ if it is occupied(this does not apply to CBs with the "Take capital" wargoal). In a coalition war, members of the coalition may only take their own cores. AI won't accept giving away unoccupied forts, or unfortified provinces that are near forts if none of them are occupied.
      Revoke core Half province cost 0.1 per development[Note 1] 0 The loser revokes their core on the selected province that they don't own before the peace. Revoking cores of third parties gives them a small relations bonus towards the winner. Never costs diplomatic power. Cannot be used to force the loser to revoke cores on provinces which have the loser's primary culture.
      Return core Province cost 0.25 per development[Note 1] 0 The loser returns the selected core province of an existing country, and loses their core if the province is not of their primary culture. The victor may lose claims on provinces returned to other nations.
      Concede Colonial Area Sum of province costs 2[Note 1] 0.2 per development[Note 2] Requires The Cossacks and a colonial nation with a province in that colonial region. The loser cedes all provinces in the selected area to the winner or the winner's colonial subject if one exists. Furthermore, the loser may not establish any additional colonies in the region unless they overturn the peace treaty in another war with the victor. Can't be used against unreformed natives.
      Give up claims 20% 2 0 The loser revokes all claims on the selected country, who need not be involved in the war. If it's a third party, this gives them a small opinion bonus towards the winner.
      Cancel subject Half of the sum of the province costs of the subject 0.25 per development of the subject[Note 1] 0 The selected subject of the loser becomes independent. Cannot cancel trade companies or colonial nations.
      Release nation sum of province costs 0.25 per development[Note 1] 0 A new nation will be formed out of the loser's provinces with cores of that nation. The new nation will have a 15 year truce with the loser and the victor. The victor guarantees their independence for 15 years, which does not take up a diplomatic relations slot (unlike normal guarantees). The loser also loses their cores except on provinces of their primary culture.
      Force religion Sum of province costs[Note 3] 10 0 Loser converts state religion and religion of their capital to that of the winner.[Note 4] This will destroy a center of reformation if one is present there. Only possible within the same religious group (except when using the Great Holy War CB); that is, only heretics can be converted, not heathens.
      Form Personal Union 60% 20 0.2 per development[Note 2] Loser becomes lesser partner of a personal union with the winner. Only available with "Claim on Throne" and "Restoration of Union" CBs.
      Become vassal Sum of province costs 1 per 2 development 0.5 per development[Note 2] Cost capped at 200 diplomatic power. Loser becomes a vassal of the winner.
      Become tributary Sum of province costs 1 per 2 development 0.5 per development[Note 2] Requires Mandate of Heaven. Loser becomes a tributary of the winner. Only available for countries capable of making tributaries.
      Pay tribute 5% per loan 0.5 per loan 0 Loser pays an immediate lump sum of ducats to the winner, measured in amount of loans. Limited to the amount of 5 loans, and results in inflation for the winner. Quick rule of thumb is 2.75 ducats × total development of target nation (as can be seen in the ledger under the Country tab).
      Concede defeat 10% 10 0 A "white peace plus", the only change being a larger gain and loss of prestige than other peace deals such as paying gold (±10 instead of ±2). Not compatible with any other terms. Can only be used between the war leaders.
      Annul treaties 10% 1 0 Loser cancels all treaties (including military access) with the selected country, and is not allowed to sign any new treaties with that country for a duration of 10 years. Royal marriages are unaffected.
      Change government 50% Loser's government type changes to that of the winner. Only available with Government Type and (unused) Revolutionary War CBs.
      Transfer trade power 30% 1 0 Requires Wealth of Nations. Establishes a "Transfer Trade Power" relationship from the loser to the winner, amounting in 50% of the trade power in all trade nodes where both countries have trade power. This lasts until cancelled, which the loser can't do until the truce expires. Transfer Trade Power does not occupy a relationship slot.
      Steer trade 60% 2 0 Requires Rule Britannia. Establishes a "Steer Trade" relationship from the loser to the winner, forcing them to use their merchants to steer trade towards the winner.
      Humiliate 40% 5 0 Winner gains 30 power projection, loser loses 30 power projection. Note that the power projection bonus from humiliating any one rival caps out at 30; and doesn't stack, instead refreshing the bonus. Only available if the loser is the winner's rival.
      Show strength 100% 0 0 Winner gains 30 power projection plus 100 of each monarch power, loser loses 30 power projection and 20 prestige. Note that the power projection bonus from humiliating any one rival caps out at 30; and doesn't stack, instead refreshing the bonus. Only available with the "Humiliate Rival" casus belli.
      War reparations 10% 2 0 The losing party is forced to pay 10% of their income to the winning party as war reparations. This lasts for 10 years. It is canceled if the parties go to war again.
      Force migrations 100% 2 0 Requires Conquest of Paradise. Force a primitive nation to migrate elsewhere. Only possible when the nation is small enough.
      Enforced military access 15% 0 0 Force a nation to give military access until truce expires.
      Enforced fleet basing rights 25% 0 0 Force a nation to give fleet basing rights until truce expires.
      Enforce rebel demands 50% 2 0 Force a nation to accept the rebels' demands. Only works with the "Support Rebels" casus belli.
      Religious Supremacy 50% 37 0 Attacker's religion becomes the official religion of the Holy Roman Empire. Available only with the "Religious League" casus belli.
      Claim Mandate of Heaven 50% 25 0 Attacker becomes the Emperor of China. Available only with the "Take Mandate of Heaven" casus belli (requires Mandate of Heaven).
      Dismantle the Revolution 100% 25 0 Defender stops being the revolution target and becomes a monarchy. Available only with the "Crush the Revolution" casus belli and as a defender against the "Spread the Revolution" casus belli.
      Extinguish the Revolution 20% 3 0 Only available if a Revolutionary war was declared but the target ceased being revolutionary during the war. Removes the Revolution from all provinces of the target and awards 100 of each monarch power.
      Spread the Revolution 60% 0.1 per target development 0 Requires the Emperor DLC. Available only with the "Spread the Revolution" casus belli and as a defender against the "Crush the Revolution" casus belli. Gives 0.1 revolutionary zeal per target development and ⅓ monarch point in every category per target development. The revolution will spread to all provinces of the target.
      Expand Empire Provinces × 1.25 0.06 per development 0.08 per development Only available to the Emperor after the "Absolute Reichstabilität" reform has been passed. Forces defender, all their subjects, and all their provinces to join the empire. Attacker gains imperial authority equal to 0.1 × defender's development.
      Reintegrate into the Empire 80 1 per warscore 0 Only available to the Emperor during the Swiss War for Independence. Forces defender, all their subjects, and all their provinces to join the empire. Attacker gains 25 imperial authority.
      Grant Independence 30%[Note 5] 0 0 Only available for subjects with the Independence, Colonial Independence, and War for the Emperor CBs.
      Raid Heretic Churches 20% 2 0 Only available for countries with the "Salvific Plutocracy" government reform. Destroys the church or cathedral in a province. Gain 50 church power or 25 fervor (as appropriate to the religion) and loot the province. The province to raid is the first province in the following list which has a church or cathedral:
      • Roma (118)
      • a random province which has at least 40 development
      • a random province which has at least 30 development
      • a random province which has at least 20 development
      • a random province which has at least 10 development
      • a random province
      Claim Norwegian Throne 60% 60 0.35 per development[Note 2] Forms a personal union with Norway. Only available to Sweden via mission.
      Claim Electorate 40% 40 0.35 per development[Note 2] War is declared against the Emperor to take the electorate from one random subject of the attacker.
      Establish Eyalet Sum of province costs (maximum of 60) 1 per warscore 0.4 per development Loser becomes an eyalet of the winner. Only available to the Ottomans and Rûm.
      Annex Migratory Tribe Sum of province costs (maximum of 100) 0.1 per development 0.1 per development Annexes all currently inhabited provinces of the tribe. The tribe will not be able to migrate and ceases to exist.

      Egyptian Westernization

      Egyptian Westernization works mainly like regular Bars with Buttons, with one big difference. Its Buttons grant three ever increasing permanent modifiers. Each Button can only be pressed 5 times, maximizing its respective modifier and disabling the button. Thus after the Buttons have been pressed a total of 15 times, the mechanic only has access to its bar. Pressing the Buttons also costs Innovativeness and monarch power, in addition to reducing the bar.

      -3 Innovativeness

      Russian Rule

      Russian Rule works very similar to "Power buttons" mechanics. Like them, it has three buttons which gather power depending on associated ruler skills. However unlike regular Power Buttons, where there is no reason to accumulate government power besides to press the buttons, Russian Rule also gains three sets of modifiers that scale up with how much power their respective button has gathered.

      Russian Rule Russian Empire, Great Russian Federation 0 - 100 50
      Three Royal fires

      The Three Royal Fires mechanic has both a Bar and Buttons. The Bar scales from -100 to 100. They are different from "Bars with Buttons" in the fact that their Buttons arent solely dependant on the Bar. Instead their Buttons work like "Power Buttons", gathering individual power depending on Ruler skill.
      Still, the Bar and Buttons are linked; no Button can be pressed while the Bar is not at at least -50. Additionally, while the Bar is at above 0, it will grant increasing amounts of yearly government power to the buttons. Finally, pressing a button reduces the bar by -50 and grants a modifier which reduces the Bars power gain.
