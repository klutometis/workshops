Court And Country
Resistance to the growing centralization in our country has culminated in a period of general crisis and conflict between Court and Country. Depending on how we handle this time of crisis we might come out of it a stronger country than before.

Court And Country[1] is a disaster that can occur in the Age of Absolutism.

PrerequisitesEdit

 
Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.35.

It is the   Age of Absolutism and this country:

  • has not had this disaster before
  • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
  • has at least 8 cities
  • is not currently in the "Internal Power Struggle" disaster

If these prerequisites are met, the monthly progress can begin.

Monthly progress starts if Monthly progress halts if
This country:
  • Does not have an ongoing disaster.
  • Has at least   +1 national unrest
  • Has less than   +3 stability
  • Is at war
  • Has at least   50 absolutism
Monthly progress This country:
+1% • Has at least   50 absolutism
+1% • Has at least   60 absolutism
+1% • Has at least   +1 national unrest
+1% • Has   0 stability or less
−1%[Note 1] • Has   +3 stability
−1%[Note 1] • Has less than   50 absolutism
−1%[Note 1] • Has less than   +1 national unrest
At least one of:
  • Has an ongoing disaster
  • Has less than   +1 national unrest
  • Has   +3 stability
  • Has less than   50 absolutism
  • It is not the   Age of Absolutism
  1. ↑ 1.0 1.1 1.2 The three modifiers that can give −1.0% progress per month are irrelevant because they have the same conditions as halting, which resets progress to 0.

StartEdit

If the progress reaches 100%, the disaster will start with the following event:

id

 Conflict of Court and Country

The encroachment of the [Root.GetAdjective] state on domains that have traditionally been dominated by regional aristocrats, local courts or other interest groups has led to a widespread resentment of the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] and the [Root.Capital.GetCapitalName] court. These groups have now united and formed alliances to combat central authority.
Today we have received a number of objections to our latest tax decree, citing ancient rights and immoral conduct by the representatives of the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]. It is clear that continued attempts to strengthen our power will be obstructed at every opportunity.
 
Trigger conditions
  • None
 Is triggered only by

The start of the   "Court And Country" disaster

 
I am the State.

The country:

  • Gains   5 absolutism
  • Gains “Hardline Centralization Attempts” for the rest of the disaster, giving:
    •   +2 National unrest
    •   −0.05 Monthly autonomy change
 
Attempt to negotiate with these Frondeurs.

The country:

  • Loses   5 absolutism
  • Gains “Negotiations to Increase Central Power” for the rest of the disaster, giving:
    •   −1 National unrest
    •   +0.05 Monthly autonomy change

ModifiersEdit

While the disaster is ongoing, the country gets:

  •   −20% National goods produced modifier
  •   −20% National tax modifier
  •   −20 Maximum absolutism

Also the following events are triggered by a monthly pulse.

Monthly eventsEdit

The weight that no event is triggered is 1000 and for each monthly event is 50.

id

 Refusal to Pay Taxes

Local [Root.GetLocalPowerStructure]s in [local_court_location.GetName] and surrounding provinces are refusing to acquiesce to a number of recent ordinances and taxes. Instead the [Root.GetLocalPowerStructure]s have sent a formal request to [Root.Capital.GetCapitalName] asking us to replace a number of [Root.GetLowLevelOfficialForCountry]s in the state, citing abuse of power and a disrespect of their old privileges.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Owns any province which:
    • Is not the country's capital
    • Is part of a state
    • Does not have the “Refusal to Pay Taxes” modifier
    • Has not had this event before
 Is triggered only by

Monthly pulse of   "Court And Country" disaster

 
Arrest their leaders!

The country:

  • Gains   5 absolutism

The chosen province:

  •   Particularist rebels (size 1.5) rise in revolt
  • Every owned province in the same area, except the country's capital:
    • Gains “Enforcement of Ordinances” for   5 years, giving:
      •   +4 Local unrest
 
Send a negotiator to the [local_court_location.GetName] [Root.GetLocalPowerStructure].

Every owned province in the same area as the chosen province, except the country's capital:

  • Gains “Refusal to Pay Taxes” for   10 years, giving:
    •   +0.13% Monthly autonomy change
id

 [separatist_province.Culture.GetName] Revolt

Our attempts to unify the legal traditions, taxes and obligations of the provinces in the [Root.GovernmentName] of [Root.GetName] has led to the leaders of the [separatist_province.Culture.GetName] people to call their compatriots to arms. They claim we have violated the rules we swore to uphold when the [separatist_province.Culture.GetName] lands came under our control and that they have no choice but to once again raise the [separatists_wanted_country.GetAdjective] banners.
Our local representatives implore us to strike a deal before it is too late. If we guarantee to uphold some of the [separatist_province.Culture.GetName] privileges they may yet be convinced the abolition of others.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Owns at least 15 cities
  • Owns any province which:
    • Has a non-accepted culture
    • Has a primary culture of any other country who has cores on at least 5 provinces in our country

Can happen only once per country

Is triggered only by

Monthly pulse of   "Court And Country" disaster

 
This rebellion must be crushed!

The country:

  • Gains   10 absolutism

The chosen province:

  •   Nationalist rebels (size 2) rise in revolt
  • This and every other owned province with the same culture:
    • Gain   10 years of separatism
    • Gain “Rooting out Separatism” for   5 years, giving:
      •   +8 Local unrest
 
We will buy their loyalty.

The country:

  • Gains   5 absolutism

The chosen province and every other owned province with the same culture:

  • Gain   40% local autonomy
  • Lose   5 years of separatism
  • Gain “Local Concessions” for   10 years, giving:
    •   −2 Local unrest
    •   +0.15% Monthly autonomy change
id

 The League of [city_league_demand_rights_province.GetName]

Recent years have seen a number of new taxes and obligations put on many [Root.GetAdjective] cities and guilds. So far we have received all what has been asked for but our [Root.GetLowLevelOfficialForCountry]s report an increasing dissatisfaction among them.
With the growing resistance to the [Root.Capital.GetCapitalName] edicts and taxes the most powerful cities in the province of [city_league_demand_rights_province.GetName] have banded together around the mayor of [city_league_demand_rights_province.GetCapitalName]. They now demand their rights to levy their own taxes and privileges to uphold their own laws and they have violently evicted our [Root.GetLowLevelOfficialForCountry] from the area.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Owns any province which:
    • Is not the country's capital
    • Has at least   10 development or a   center of trade
    • Has not had this event before

No more than 3 owned provinces have had this event before

Is triggered only by

Monthly pulse of   "Court And Country" disaster

Immediate effects

Choose a random owned province that:

  • Is not the country's capital
  • Has not had this event before
  • Matches one of the following, in descending order of priority:
    • Has at least   60, 50, 40, 35 development
    • Either has at least   30, 26, 23, 20, or 18 development or a   level 2 or 3 center of trade
    • Either has at least   15 development or a   level 2 or 3 center of trade
    • Either has at least   10 development or any   center of trade
 
Let us crush these troublemakers!

The country:

  • Gains   5 absolutism
  •   Particularist rebels (size 2) rise in revolt in and occupy the chosen province
 
We can make exceptions for the cities of [city_league_demand_rights_province.GetName].

The country:

  • Loses   5 absolutism

Every owned provinces in the area containing the chosen province, except the country's capital:

  • Gains   40% local autonomy
id

 The Quarrelsome Nobles of [quarrelsome_nobles_province.GetName]

Some nobles have come to resent the new ideals of nobility. They have lost influence and power in [Root.Capital.GetCapitalName] but they still command considerable local influence, with vast estates to their name and deep pockets. Longing for the time when they were the pillars of the realm instead of the servants of the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] and [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] ministers, they have grown increasingly quarrelsome and obstinate.
It seems one such group has been preparing a plot to restore their old privileges and bring tradition and dignity back to the [Root.Capital.GetCapitalName] court. Using their countryside holdings as a base they have assembled an army to force their will upon us.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Owns any province which:
    • Does not border the capital
    • Has not had this event before

No more than 2 owned provinces have had this event before

Is triggered only by

Monthly pulse of   "Court And Country" disaster

 
We will meet them with sword in hand.

The country:

  • Gains   5 absolutism
  •   Noble rebels (size 2) rise in revolt in the chosen province
  •   Noble rebels (size 1) rise in revolt in up to 3 additional non-capital provinces connected to the capital if has at least 30, 40, or 60 cities
 
Let us see try to meet their demands.

The country:

  • Loses   5 absolutism
  • Loses   10 legitimacy (or equivalent)

The chosen province:

  • Gains   40% local autonomy

 Disloyal Officers

In times of war the army allows advancement like few other places. In a period of inward strife such as this, however, we must not forget that these men of ability still have ties to the disenfranchised and disloyal groups of the realm.
Can we really trust our armed forces? Close ministers to the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] are asking us to purge all men with ties to the groups and families that oppose us.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Has not had this event in the last   5 years
 Is triggered only by

Monthly pulse of   "Court And Country" disaster

 
We need loyal soldiers.

The country:

  • Gains   5 absolutism
  • Loses   20 army tradition
  • Loses   1 year's worth of manpower
 
We cannot afford that.

The country:

  • Loses   5 absolutism
  • Loses   10 prestige
id

 Supporters of the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]

Not everyone stands to lose from the new order of the [Root.GovernmentName], some can now make a salaried career out of military service or as a functionary in one of the many new institutions of the state. Others yet remain loyal to the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] out of their sense of honor and tradition.
While the resistance to the increasingly ironclad rule of the [Root.Capital.GetCapitalName] court is widespread in our country we have recently seen a [Root.GetGovernmentSupporters] surge.
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Has not had this event in the last   3 years
 Is triggered only by

An excellent opportunity to strengthen our ranks!

The country:

  • Gains   5 absolutism
  • Gains   1 year's worth of manpower
 
Their loyalty will be rewarded.

The country:

  • Loses   ducats equal to 0.25 years of income
  • Gains   5 absolutism
  • Gains   10 legitimacy (or equivalent)
  • Gains   10 prestige

End eventEdit

If this country:

  • Has no rebel controlled provinces
  • Has at least   0 stability
  • Has less than   2 war exhaustion
  • Has had this disaster active for   10 years

Or one of the following rebel types enforce their demands:

  •   Particularist
  •   Noble rebels
  •   Eunuch rebels
  •   Janissary rebels

Then it loses the above-mentioned effects and the following event is triggered:

id

 An End to Hostilities between Court and Country

The period widespread obstruction of authority in the provinces has finally come to an end. The way we have handled this crisis will shape the way local power structures interact with the government in [Root.Capital.GetCapitalName] for the foreseeble future.
 
Trigger conditions
  • None
 Is triggered only by

The end of   "Court And Country" disaster

Option conditions

  Enabled if:

  • Has at least   65 absolutism
  • Did not end this disaster by rebels enforcing their demands
 
The state is Triumphant!

    •   +20 Maximum absolutism

  • Has at least   30 absolutism, but less than 65
  • Did not end this disaster by rebels enforcing their demands
 
Perhaps we can now finally work together.

The country:

  • Gains   5 prestige
  • Gains “Central Authority Asserted” for the rest of the game, giving:
    •   +10 Maximum absolutism

  Enabled if: Either:

  • Has less than   30 absolutism
  • Ended this disaster by rebels enforcing their demands
