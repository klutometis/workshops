      The Plight of province

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

      All of nation suffers in war, but those provinces which lie in the path of the enemy suffer worst. What appears to a soldier or commander as a convenient source of supplies is often all the common people have. The outskirts of capital have been plundered in this war to the point of starvation. They are now reaching out for the government to aid them in recovering, lest the suffering of country drag on for years past the eventual peace.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Has been looted
      • Does not have province modifier “Famine Refused Aid”

      The country:

      • Is at war
      		 Mean time to happen

      240 months
      Modifiers
      Any owned province has province modifier “Famine Refused Aid” : ×1.5

      Whatever is necessary, we will provide.

      The country:

      • Lose ducats equal to 0.1 years of income
      That money is better spent winning this war.

      The province gets “Famine Refused Aid” for 10 years, giving:

      • −25% Trade goods produced

      Spread of Pestilence

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

      The war has worsened the spread of diseases, and what once started as a local epidemic centered around a siege has now spread further. Dislocated civilians as well as troop movements, both difficult to avoid in wartime, has facilitated this and now province has also fallen victim to the illness.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Neighbours any province with province modifier “Lingering Plague”

      The country:

      • Is at war
      		 Mean time to happen

      100 months

      Curse this war...

      The province gets “Lingering Plague” for 3 years, giving:

        • −50% Supply limit
        • −50% Local manpower
        • −50% Local tax
        • −50% Trade goods produced

      Cruelty of Mercenaries

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

      War is seldom without brutality. Soldiers are not known for their mercy, but this war has seen some exceptionally vile behavior on part of our occupying forces. It appears that mercenaries, unsatisfied with the wages we provide them with, have resorted to robbing the civilian population. Extortion by means of terror and torture has become commonplace, our soldiers are reviled as well as feared and stories of their atrocities spread beyond the borders of the war.

        • Is owned by defender

      240 months

      We should prevent this from happening in the future.

      • Lose 10 Prestige

      • Hidden: 25% chance of: Gets the event "Mercenary Bands Raid a province" in 30 days
      • Gets “Terrorized by Mercenaries” for 3 years, giving:
        • +8 Local unrest

      The Families Left Behind

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

      The recent war took a heavy toll on our people. Many were called, few returned. The families of nobles and commoners alike toiled as they waited for soldiers to return home, but the war ended and they never did. Now the toiling continues, many being so preoccupied with the work to make up for the lost pair of hands that they barely have time to grieve. Of the ones who have time to think, many are increasingly thinking that we have been throwing away lives too carelessly.

       
      Trigger conditions

      • Has less than 10% maximum manpower reserves

      • Is not at war

        • “Post-War Tax Relief”
        • “Decreased Drafting”
        • “Suffering Bereaved”

      240 months

      • −15% Global tax modifier
      Let us be more lenient drafting for the army in the future.

      The country gets “Decreased Drafting” for 5 years, giving:

      • −20% National manpower modifier
      This is the price that must be paid for our glorious nation.

      The country gets “Suffering Bereaved” for 5 years, giving:

      • +2 National unrest

      Witchcraft

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

      The people of the war-torn capital are looking for reasons for the misfortunes that have befallen them. Fortunately, they have not found them to be related to our rulership, but unfortunately, what they have found has caused quite a stir. The prolonged effects of the war and how they seem to affect some more than others, they claim, can only be the work of those practicing witchcraft and sorcery.

      Accusations have been leveled generously, and there are local authorities willing to organize proper trials for these crimes. As proper as trials for magic get in our country, anyway.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Has at least one of following modifier:
        • “Lingering Plague”
        • “Plundered in War”
        • “Famine Refused Aid”
      • Does not have modifier “Local Witch Hunts”

      The country:

      • Is not at war
      • Is Christian
      • Does not have country modifier “Forbade Witch Hunts”
      • Does not have country modifier “Nationwide Witch Hunts”
      		 Mean time to happen

      480 months
      Modifiers

      • Is a Theocracy: ×0.75
      • It is the Age of Discovery or Age of Reformation: ×0.75
      • It is the Age of Revolutions: ×2
      Option conditions

       Always enabled:

      Allow them to proceed with the local trials.

      The province:

      • Gets “Local Witch Hunts” for 10 years, giving:
        • −3 Local unrest
        • +10% Local autonomy
      • Hidden: Gets the event "Witch-hunt" in 3 to 16 years
      • Hidden: Gets the event "A Witch from Foreign Lands" in 3 to 16 years

       Enabled if: Has an Inquisitor advisor

      Inquisitor can see to these matters in a more organized fashion.

      The country gets “Nationwide Witch Hunts” for 10 years, giving:

      • +2% Missionary strength
      • −1 National unrest
      • −3 Tolerance of heretics
      • −3 Tolerance of heathens

       Always enabled:

      Such foolish superstitions have no place in our country!

      The country gets “Forbade Witch Hunts” for 10 years, giving:

      • +2 National unrest

      Witch-hunt

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

      Since the long-suffering people in a province received the blessing of the state and church to prosecute those who allegedly brought famine and disease upon them by means of witchcraft, they have made good use of it. While once only the obviously suspicious were pointed out, witches now appear to be everywhere. The crowd calls for the spilling of peasant and noble blood alike, and even some of the local clergy may not be trustworthy.

      This abundance of satanic practices certainly goes a long way to explain the post-war hardships of a province, but the mass trials that have followed are going out of hand. We might consider the possibility that some are innocent.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The province:

      • Has province modifier “Local Witch Hunts”

      The country:

      • Is not at war
      		 Is triggered only by

      The event "Witchcraft" - option "Allow them to proceed with the local trials."

      Option conditions

       Always enabled:

      Cease this madness!

      The country:

      • Lose 80 administrative power

      The province:

      • Remove province modifier "Local Witch Hunts"

       Always enabled:

      Intervene on behalf of the nobles.

      Peasant rebels (size 1) rise in revolt in the province

       Always enabled:

      Intervene on behalf of the commoners.

      Noble rebels (size 1) rise in revolt in the province

       Enabled if: The province:

      • Is not the capital
      • Has less than 80% local autonomy
      Let them exact justice as they see fit.

      The province:

      • Gain 25% local autonomy

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

      Under the watchful eye of inquisitor, trials for sorcery and heresy have proceeded under relatively controlled circumstances. Inquisitor would not be at our court if we did not trust the advisor, after all. A recent event has complicated this, however, as inquisitor has challenged our trust of another. Advisor, who has aided our country greatly in foreign relations, has been accused of witchcraft, a testament to no one being truly unquestionable.

      The Fires of Hell

      Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for version 1.33.

      Inquisitor has been diligent. With the ruler's support, those who practice witchcraft are condemned and burned, on Earth as it is in Hell. Our merciless prosecution of these crimes has put our religious devotion beyond reproach.

      Still, there are moments of doubt. One day, your young heir watches the public execution of a witch for the first time. You see heir's eyes fill up with terror in the firelight, and before it's through, your child is begging for the witch to be spared. Your attempts to explain do nothing to stop the tears, and eventually you find yourself at a loss to justify this to an innocent mind. Perhaps the convicted is innocent too, and you will once burn yourself, eternally.

       
      Trigger conditions

      The country:

      • Has country modifier “Nationwide Witch Hunts”
      • Has an Inquisitor advisor
      • Has an heir of 6 to 16 years old
      		 Mean time to happen

      240 months

      The child is right. We have been blinded by our zeal.
      • Inquisitor leaves the court
      • Remove country modifier "Nationwide Witch Hunts"
      • Gain 100 Diplomatic power
      Hush child, and hope no one heard that...
      • Gain 100 Administrative power
      • Heir loses 10 Claim strength
