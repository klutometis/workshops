 The Rights of the $ESTATE_NOBLES$

[Root.GetRulerTitleAndNameOrRegencyCap] has striven to reduce the privileges of the aristocracy and centralize power under [Root.Monarch.GetHerHis] rule. Recent developments have raised concerns among the $ESTATE_NOBLES$ that [Root.Monarch.GetSheHe] is preparing a coup to abolish many of their most cherished liberties - most significantly, their right to elect the next [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]. Rural landholders are already mobilizing against this development and will surely rise in revolt should we attempt to further curtail their privileges.
 
Trigger conditions
  • None
 Is triggered only by

Removing the privilege "Golden Liberty" as an 'Elective Monarchy' without being   Poland/the   Commonwealth

 
The elective monarchy is an integral part of our nation.

The country:

  • Loses   10 prestige
 
We shall abolish the elective monarchy.

The country:

  • Removes the "Elective Monarchy" or "Polish Elective Monarchy" government reform
  • Removes the current heir
  • Gains   10 absolutism
  •   Noble rebels (size 3) rise in revolt in a random owned core province
  • If owns at least 8 non-colony provinces:
    •   Noble rebels (size 2) rise in revolt in another random owned core province
  • If owns at least 15 non-colony provinces:
    •   Noble rebels (size 1) rise in revolt in another random owned core province

If   Noble rebels enforce their demands in the next   10 years:

  • Re-enacts the "Elective Monarchy" government reform

 Estate Statutory Rights

The Estates of [Root.GetName] have come forth offering a compromise to [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] [Root.Monarch.GetName] to solve the weak control that the [Root.GovernmentName] exercises over the land. In exchange for control, the Estates demand certain liberties...
 
Trigger conditions

The country:

  • Has less than   11% crown land ownership
  • Has not granted the   Estates Statutory Rights privilege
  • At least one of:
    • Is a   monarchy and has the   Nobility estate with less than 6 privileges
    • Is a   republic and has the   Burghers estate with less than 6 privileges
    • Is a   theocracy and has the   Clergy estate with less than 6 privileges
    • Is   Hindu and has the   Brahmins estate with less than 6 privileges
 Mean time to happen

128 months
Modifiers
  Crownland share:

  • Is below 6%: ×0.5
  • Is below 4%: ×0.5
  • Is below 2%: ×0.5
  • Is below 1%: ×0.5
 
Honestly what did you expect when you sold out the entire country?

The country:

  • "Seize land will be unavailable as long as Estate Statutory Rights Privilege is in effect!"
  • Grants the   "Estates Statutory Rights" privilege, giving:
    •   +10% All estates influence
    •   −5 Max absolutism
    •   +30% Crown land ownership
    •   +25% Minimum autonomy in states
    • Can not be revoked for 20 years
