For many centuries, the ancient Persian empires and the Romans have been bitter rivals. Each empire has tried to dominate the other and claim their territories for themselves. As time passed on, so did both empires crumble to dust. However, unlike the Emerald Lion is once again ruling over the Zagros mountains while the Purple Phoenix is nothing but ash. It is time to finish what our ancestors started all those centuries ago, and claim the Roman capital for ourselves!

Qizilbash Loyalty
The Qizilbash are a branch of our military made of Shia militant Turkomans. They are organized in Turkic clans, and each of them has its own unique attributes which makes the collaboration of these tribes at times challenging. Still, the loyalty of the [Root.GetTurkomanOrHorseman] has to be bound to the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]. It would be best to remind them of this fact.
 The country:
  • If the   Qizilbash estate exists:
    • Has at least   65 Qizilbash loyalty
    • Has at least   3 Qizilbash privileges
  • Else:
    • Has at least   60 Army tradition
    • Has a   Commandant advisor of at least level 3
 The country:
  • Creates 5 Qizilbash Infantry in the capital
  • If the Nobility is exempted from land seizing:
    • Gains   10 Qizilbash loyalty
  • Else:

Create the Ghilman
The military consists mostly of irregular troops which are called in times of need, and the Qizilbash, who themselves have shown their discipline on the battlefield. While the Qizilbash have sworn their loyalty to the [Root.Monarch.GetTitle], it is obvious that they are a powerful entity within our politics. We must not allow them to be the dominant branch of our military. A new counterbalance has to be created!
 The country owns at least 12 provinces which:
