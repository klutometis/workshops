      Movement speed

      From Europa Universalis 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
      At least some were last verified for version 1.30.

      For the movement speed of fleets, see Naval units#Movement speed.

      Movement speed is a measure of how quickly an army can move between different provinces. Movement speed of a particular army is affected by local and global movement speed modifiers, as well as by the general leading it.

      Country-wide modifiers[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.30.

      There are several factors that increase the movement speed for all armies of a country.

      Conditions
      +20.0%
      • with ‘Steppe Nomads’ government reform (tier 1 tribal government)
      • with ‘Great Mongol State’ government reform (tier 1 tribal government)
      • with ‘Holy Horde’ government reform (tier 1 theocracy)
      +10.0%
      • as ‘Mughal Diwan’ government reform (tier 1 monarchy) with assimilated Sahelian culture group
      • as ‘Sich Rada’ government reform (tier 1 republic) with activated ‘Organise Raiding Parties’ government interaction
      +0.1% as military hegemon per percentage point of power

      Local modifiers[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.30.

      Local movement speed modifiers only affect movement into the affected province; movement out of the province is unchanged. The local modifiers of movement speed are divided into those for friendly armies and those for hostile armies.

      Local friendly movement speed
      Conditions
      +10%
      • in the Holy Roman Empire after enacting the reform ‘Expand the Gemeiner Pfennig’
      • producing Wool
      −0.25% per percentage point of devastation
      −25% during mild monsoon
      −25% during normal monsoon
      −50% during severe monsoon
      Local hostile movement speed
      Conditions
      −0.25% per percentage point of devastation
      −25% during mild monsoon
      −25% during normal monsoon
      −50%
      • during severe monsoon
      • after using the ‘Scorched Earth’ army interaction, for 5 years

      Army-specific modifiers[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.30.
      Available only with the Cradle of Civilization DLC enabled.

      Scaling up to 100, average of subunits drill for entire army gives:

      +20% Movement speed

      Leader-based modifiers[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.30.

      A general or conquistador increases the movement speed of the army they are attached to by +5% for each point of maneuver skill.

      Leader traits[edit | edit source]

      The “Goal Oriented” trait for land leaders gives +10% movement speed to the army which the leader commands.
