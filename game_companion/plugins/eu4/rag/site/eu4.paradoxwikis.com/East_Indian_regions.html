      East Indian regions are regions that are part of the East Indies subcontinent, which belongs to Asia. The regions of India and other parts of Asia have their own pages. The table content is out of the files in /Europa Universalis IV/history/provinces and refers to a game start on the 11th of November, 1444. Province names listed are the ones they have with the "Dynamic Province Names" option off; names found in game may be different.

      Note: Continents, regions and areas of Europa Universalis 4 do not always match reality.

      This article has been verified for the current version (1.37) of the game.
