      Baltic control[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.36.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Retake Gotland
      Our previous king, Eric VII Gryf, ruled over Denmark and over the entire Kalmar Union from 1412 until 1439. Due to the economical issues caused by Eric's war with the Hansa he eventually was deposed, and his nephew, "Arch-"King Chistopher III, took over the throne.

      Although Eric is no longer ruling the Kalmar Union, he still made himself a home on the island of Gotland, where he continues his existence as a pirate.

      While he used to be our king, Gotland still is Danish territory. It is time for him to abdicate entirely.
      		 The country:
      • Owns core province Gotland (25)
      • Has no rebel armies present
      • Has no province controlled by rebels

      Gotland does not exist

      		The country:
      • If it is before 1460:
        • Gets “The Fate of Eric Gryf” event
      • Else:
        • If has a stability of less than +3,
          • then it gains 1 stability,
          • else it gains 50 administrative power.
      • Gain a permanent claim on Lübeck (45)
      • If is AI-controlled and Sweden is our subject:
        • Sweden gains opinion modifier “Early Victories” ( +200 opinion / −2 yearly decay) towards us
      Expand the Military
      We have many enemies such as the Holy Roman Empire to our South, the English to our West and the Republic of Novgorod to our East and, worst of all, Sweden in the North.We should build our land and naval forces and prepare them for the worst to come.
      		 The country:
      • Has an army size of at least 100% of its force limit
      • Has an navy size of at least 100% of its force limit
      • Has at least 12 galley ships
      		 The country:
      • Gains an admiral with 50 navy tradition
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Livonia
        • Estonia
        • Curonia
      Humiliate Lübeck
      In the year 1426, King Eric established the Sound Toll which was one of the main sources of Danish income. Every non-Danish ship which traversed the Öresund had to pay us.

      Unfortunately, the Hanseatic League had no interest in Danish prosperity, and embargoed the entire Kalmar Union. Eventually, in 1426, their audacity led to an open war between Denmark and the Hansaetic League. To our humiliation, these good-for-nothing merchants won the war in 1435, and enforced liberation from the toll.

      We should repay our humiliation to the Hansa by either conquering the city of Lübeck, or overtaking their grip over the Lübeck trade node.
      		 At least one of:
      • Lübeck (45) is owned by the country or its non-tributary subjects
      • Lübeck does not exist
      • No known country is in a trade league with Lübeck
      • The country controls at least 30% of the trade in the Lübeck trade node
      		 The country:
      • Enables the decision "Provoke Hanseatic Trade Conflict"
      • Gains “Humiliated the Hansa” for 30 years, giving:
        • +20% Trade efficiency
        • +20% Global trade power
        • +10% Trade steering
      		 Retake Gotland

      Expand the Military

      Reconquer Estonia
      The great King Valdemar II led the Danish armies to great victories in Estonia against the Pagans, however, since the St George's Night uprising these lands have become part of the Livonian order. We must follow in his footsteps and reconquer what was taken by the Livonian Order.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns:
      • All provinces in the Estonia area
      • Province Ösel (35)
      		 The country:
      • Gains “Estonian” as an accepted culture.[2]
      • Gains “Estonian Integration” until 'Estonian' is no longer an accepted culture, giving:
        • +1 Max promoted culture
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Province Neva (33)
        • South Karelia
        • Novgorod
        • Pskov

      Every owned Estonian province gets “Support of the Estonian People” until Estonian is no longer an accepted culture, giving:

      • +10% Local goods produced modifier
      		 Expand the Military
      Eliminate Novgorod
      The relations with the Veche Republic of Novgorod have always been frosty at best. Their intention to control the eastern flow of the trade as well as expanding their borders into Scandinavia did not help improve these relations either. But even if Novgorod was not a threat to us, the rising Principality of Muscovy is. We should take the strategic provinces of [274.GetName], [33.GetName] and [310.GetName] for the sake of protecting the Scandinavian people.
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns the following provinces with the highest level fort available:
      • Neva (33)
      • Pskov (274)
      • Novgorod (310)

      • Novgorod
      • Kazan
      • Kiev

      The ruler:

      • Gains 1 Administrative skill[3]
      • Gains 1 Diplomatic skill[4]
      • Gains 1 Military skill[5]
      		 Reconquer Estonia
      The Cities of the Hansa
      The coastal cities of the Holy Roman Empire consists mostly of members of the Hanseatic League. We should blockade, occupy or annex these cities if we ever want to disband this league which threatened our trade dominance in Öresund for so long.
      		 At least 3 of the provinces Stralsund, Stettin, Lübeck, Hamburg and Bremen:
      • At least one of:
        • Is owned by the country or its non-tributary subjects
        • Is controlled by the country
        • Is blockaded while the owner is at war with the country
        • Its owner submitted to our country's Trade Dominance when enacts the Provoke Hanseatic Trade Conflict decision
      		 The country:
      • Gains 20 prestige
      • Gains 2% mercantilism
      • Gains 4 years worth of trade income
      • Gains “Danish Trade Influence in Germany” ( +10 Trade power) in the Rheinland and Saxony trade nodes for the rest of the game
      		 Humiliate Lübeck
      Expand the Sound Toll
      The Sound Toll is one if not the most important source of income for the Danish crown. It ensures the royal independence from the nobility, and grants us an iron grip on the trade flow between the Baltic Sea and the North Sea. It would be wise to expand the Sound Toll by constructing additional naval batteries and markets where merchants have to pay their toll.
      		 The country:
      • Has at least 15% mercantilism
      • Owns the following core provinces with any Coastal Defense and Trade buildings:
        • Sjælland (12)
        • Østjylland (4142)
        • Holstein (1775)
        • Lund (6)
      		 Provinces Sjælland (12), Østjylland (4142), Holstein (1775), and Lund (6):
      • Gain 2 base production
      • Gain “Sound Toll Protection” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +3 Local trade power
        • +3 Hostile fleet attrition
        • +2 Possible number of buildings
        • −5% Cost to promote mercantilism
        • Active as long the province has any kind of naval defense and market building and is owned by us
      		 Reconquer Estonia

      The Cities of the Hansa

      Control the Channel
      Due to the discovery of the New World in the west, goods and profitable trade flows into the English Channel. It is a fool's errand to hope that either the British or the Dutch would steer their invaluable goods into the Sound Toll. Instead we should expand our own trade influence in the English Channel, and siphon as much trade as possible from our naval rivals.
      		 The country:
      • Controls at least 30% of the trade and is the strongest trade power in the English Channel trade node
      • Has at least 50% of its income from trade income
      		 The country gains “Controller of the English Channel” until we have no longer any Trade Power in the English Channel, giving:
      • +25% Trade efficiency
      		 Expand the Sound Toll

      Unification of Scandinavia[edit | edit source]

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.36.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      The Crown of Norway
      The Kalmar Union unifies all of Scandinavia under one crown, and the aristocrats of Sweden and Norway obey our [Root.Monarch.GetTitle]. But even before the creation of the Kalmar Union, Denmark and Norway had a long history together - reaching all the way back to Cnut the Great and his North Sea Empire. We should strengthen our ties with Norway even further to ensure that they remain loyal to the Kalmar Union.
      		 At least one of:
      • Norway does not exist
      • Norway is our subject and:
        • At least one of:
          • Has at least +150 opinion of us and less than 20% liberty desire
          • It is at least the Age of Absolutism
      		 The country:
      • If Norway does not exist:
        • Gains 100 administrative power
      • Else:
        • Gets the “Norway in the Kalmar Union” event
      The Crown of Sweden
      The Kalmar Union by itself is not a very old union as it was created in 1397. Yet, the nobility of Sweden expressed again and again their discontent with a [Root.Culture.GetName] [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] as their monarch. During 1434 - 1436, the Swedish miners, peasants and even nobles gathered behind Engelbrekt Engelbrektsson and started an open rebellion against the [Root.GetAdjective] Crown during our war with the Hansa. Only Engelbrekt's unfortunate yet very convenient death managed to break this rebellion, and normality has returned in Sweden. Still, unlike Norway, the Swedes and their nobility are a powder keg, waiting for a moment to explode into yet another rebellion. We have to secure the good relations with the Swedish nobility as fast as possible.
      		 At least one of:
      • Sweden does not exist
      • Sweden is our subject and:
        • At least one of:
          • Has at least +150 opinion of us and less than 20% liberty desire
          • It is at least the Age of Absolutism
      		 The country:
      • If Sweden does not exist:
        • Gains 100 administrative power
      • Else:
        • Gets the “Sweden in the Kalmar Union” event
      Integrate Holstein
      King Valdemar II invaded and conquered the lands of the Duchy of Holstein in 1203. However, his conquest was not permanent as Duke Adolf IV of Holstein and a coalition of North German states managed to defeat Valdemar in the Battle of Bornhöved in 1227. It is time for us to retake Holstein for ourselves again, and integrate it into our land.
      		 The country owns the following core provinces:
      • Slesvig (13)
      • Holstein (1775)
      		 The country:
      • Loses the “Annexed Subjects” modifier if present
      • Gains 50 administrative power
      • Gains 50 diplomatic power
      • Gains 50 military power
      		 The Crown of Norway
      The Nobles of Sweden
      The nobles of Sweden need to accept that they are part of the Kalmar Union - part of the [Root.GetAdjective] Crown. However, to make them accept this fact is easier said than done as they elect any peasant before our [Root.Monarch.GetTitle] for the Swedish Throne. We must secure the loyalty of the Swedish nobility - either by monetary means or by the sword.
      		 If Sweden exists:
      • Sweden is our subject and has and less than 10% liberty desire
      • At least one of:
        • Has completed the Gotlander "Secure Swedish Loyalty mission
        • Has completed the "Swedish Dream of Independence event chain
        • It is at least the Age of Reformation

      Else:

      • Sweden does not exist
      		 The country:
      • If Sweden is our subject:
        • Swedish Pretender Rebels will no longer rise against us[6]

      The ruler:

      • Gains 2 Military skill[5]
      		 The Crown of Sweden
      Ratify the Kalmar Union
      Although the Kalmar Union is de facto a real alliance between Denmark, Norway and Sweden, and bounded all three kingdoms to one single monarch, it was never really ratified by either of its members. It is time to change this, and convert this union into a tighter relationship between all of the Scandinavian Kingdoms.
      		 The country:
      • Has +3 stability
      • Has 100 legitimacy
      • Does not have an ongoing disaster
      • Has no rebel armies present
      • Has no province controlled by rebels
      • No subject has any rebel armies present
      • Owns or non-tributary subjects owns the following provinces:
        • Sjælland (12)
        • Lund (6)
        • Østjylland (4142)
      • If has enacted the “Kalmar Union” government reform:
        • Sweden and Norway are our subjects and:
          • At least one of:
            • Have less than 10% liberty desire
            • It is at least the Age of Absolutism
      • Else:
        • Owns at least 50 provinces in the Scandinavia region
      		 The country:
      • Creates a level 2 center of trade in Lund (6)
      • Increases the center of trade level by 1, to a maximum of Level 2, in Østjylland (4142)
      • If has enacted the “Kalmar Union” government reform:
        • Gets “The Ratification of the Kalmar Union” event
      • Else:
        • Gains 100 administrative power
        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
        • Gains 100 military power
      		 Integrate Holstein

      Integrate Norway

      		 The country owns the following areas as core province:
      • Trøndelag
      • Vestlandet
      • Østlandet

      Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.36.
      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Rival England
      The English to our West are one of the greatest naval powers in Europe. Unfortunately, their interests and our own are in direct conflict as our both nations intend to dominate the North Sea trade.
      		 The country:
      • If Great Britain exists, else England:
        • At least one of:
          • Is embargoing Great Britain/ England and is rivaled by or rival of Great Britain/ England
          • Is allied to Great Britain/ England who has at least +100 opinion of us
          • Has at least as much total development as Great Britain/ England
      • Else:
        • Great Britain and England do not exist
      		 The country gains “Naval Preperations” for 30 years, giving:
      • −15% Shipbuilding time
      • −15% Ship cost
      Challenge the English Navy
      The Royal Navy of the English is one of the most formidable powers on the sea. There is no chance for us to end their dominance in the North Sea as long as our own navy cannot match theirs. We should build more heavy ships and advance our knowledge in the arts of shipbuilding so we can face them in the near future.
      		 The country:
      • Has a flagship
      • Has at least 30 Naval forcelimit
      • If Great Britain exists, else England:
        • Has at least as many ships as Great Britain/ England
        • Navy size is at least as large as that of Great Britain/ England
        • At least one of:
          • Has at least +15% Ship Durability
          • Has at least as many heavy ships as Great Britain/ England
      • Else:
        • Great Britain and England do not exist
        • Has at least 30 ships
        • At least one of:
          • Has at least +15% Ship Durability
          • Has at least 20 heavy ships
      		 The country:
      • Gains 20 navy tradition
      • Gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
        • Northern England
        • Yorkshire
      		 Rival England
      Invade North England
      The time has come to put an end to the English threat. We shall sail westwards and land into England in their nothern territories of Northumberland!
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 4 provinces in the following areas:
      • Northern England
      • Yorkshire
      		 The country gains a permanent claim on the following areas:
      • East Midlands
      • West Midlands
      • Wales
      • Wessex
      • East Anglia
      • London
      		 Challenge the English Navy
      Conquer England
      In the year of 1016, the Danish King, Cnut the Great, led an invasion of England and conquered the kingdom within a few months. His achievement resulted into the creation of the North Sea Empire. Today, we shall repeat what did done so many centuries ago, and conquer all of England yet again for the Danish glory!
      		 The country or its non-tributary subjects owns at least 20 provinces in the following areas:
      • Northern England
      • Yorkshire
      • East Midlands
      • West Midlands
      • Wales
      • Wessex
      • East Anglia
      • London, including at least province London (236)
      		 The country:
      • Gains “The Second North Sea Empire” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • +1 Yearly legitimacy
        • +1 Monthly splendor
      • If owns province London (236):
        • Gets “A new Capital for the North Sea Empire?” event
      • Else, if province London (236) is owned by our subject:
        • Our capital:
          • Gains 3 base tax
          • Gains 3 base production
          • Gains 2 base manpower
      		 Invade North England

      Mission Completion requirements Effects Prerequisites
      Dansk Ostindien
      The trade between Europe and India is an ancient business which brought wealth and prosperity to many empires in the past. Of course we have our own interests to benfit from the trade of India. We should increase our influence in India, and get access to the markets of the Far East.

      Every province in the Laponia, Norrland and Ostrobothnia areas:

      • Loses 100% local autonomy
      • Gains 2 base manpower
      Forest Expansion
      The forests of Scandinavia are mostly uncharted territory. Apart from the crafty Sami living there, the land is mostly empty of inhabitants due to the harsh winters and the lack of resources. However, the forests are great hunting grounds for fur, and some have goods like iron in the ground. It might be advisable to expand into the forests, and settle even the harsher parts of Scandinavia.
      		 Every Forest and Woods province in Scandinavia region:
      • Is owned by Scandinavia
      • Has at least 6 development
      		 Every owned province in Scandinavia:
      • Gains +10% progress towards prosperity

      Every owned Arctic province in Scandinavia:

      • Gains “Arctic Expansion” for the rest of the game, giving:
        • −40% Local development cost
        • +1 Possible number of buildings
      		 Integrate the Sami

      Invest in the Workforce
      No other estate is as crucial for the development of a country as the Burghers are. It is them who invest into architectural projects, who keep the money flowing into our [Root.GovernmentName]. We should invest into their services as we might need them in the near future.
      		 Scandinavia:

        • Gains 100 diplomatic power
      Scandes Infrastructure
      The Scandes are a mountain range rich with iron and copper. But due to the nature of mountainous terrain they are difficult to traverse. We should develop the infrastructure of the Scandes provinces so we start with our search for new iron and copper nodes.
      		 Scandinavia:
      • Has embraced the Renaissance institution
      • Owns the Trøndelag, Vestlandet and Østlandet areas which:
        • Is a core province
        • Has at least 10 development
      		 Scandinavia:
      • Gets the “In Search for Iron and Copper” event
      		 Invest in the Workforce
      Intensify Metal Production
      Scandinavia is rich in iron and copper - both essential materials for a strong army and highly sought after in the European market. If we expand our mining and smelting of iron and copper we can not only equip our troops with higher quality weapons, but also make a large profit in the European trade.
      		 Scandinavia:
      • Owns at least 7 core provinces which:
        • Produces Copper or Iron
        • Has a Weapons manufactory building
        • Has any Production building
      		 Scandinavia:
      • Gets the “The Iron and Copper of Scandinavia” event
