      Zulu Government[edit | edit source]

      Zulu has a unique government in the Zulu tribe:

      • −2 National unrest
      • −0.02 Montly war exhaustion
      • +2 Monarch military skill
      • −50% Governing capacity modifier

      Note: Zulu Tribe doesn't set a minimum of 2 for rulers, but directly increases their military stat by 2. Due to the way ruler stats are calculated, it gives a 6/16 or 37.5% chance of getting a 6 ruler.

      Potential requirements

      Zulu does not exist
      The country:

      • is not the Holy Roman Empire or The Papal State.
      • has not yet enacted this decision.
      • if 'Origins' DLC is active and has the “Recruit the Nguni” mission
        • has selected the option "I think it is better to have them integrated in our state instead." during the "Nguni Militarization" event
      • else, is in the Southern African culture group

      If the country is AI-controlled then it:

      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not a custom nation
      		 Allow

      Zulu does not exist
      The country:

      • has an administrative technology of at least 15.
      • is not a subject nation other than a tributary state or a cultural influenced state.
      • is not at war
      • owns at least 3 provinces

      Provinces Mtetwa (789) and' Natal (1181):

      • are owned by us
      • have at least 8 Base manpower
      Effects

      The country:

      • gains 2 years' worth of manpower
      • Set country flag zulu_attracted_flag. This flag enables the event Rise of the Zulu which can spawn Zulu

      Provinces Mtetwa (789) and' Natal (1181):

      • gain province modifier “Nguni Unrest” for 25 years, giving:
        • +5 Local unrest
      AI will always take this decision, unless its before 1810.
